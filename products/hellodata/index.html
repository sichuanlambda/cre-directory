About HelloData

HelloData is a multifamily analytics platform that automates market surveys, rent comps, unit-level pricing, expense benchmarking, and underwriting from publicly sourced listing data. The company reports surveying more than 38.5 million units daily across 250,000+ property websites, capturing unit-level rents, concessions, and amenities with daily updates. AI-driven comp selection, rent roll parsing, development feasibility analysis with custom survey regions, and a map-based market analytics report builder round out the toolset. APIs provide real-time property data for proptech integrations, and the platform serves underwriters, investors, property managers, developers, and brokers.

✓ Pros

✓Daily-refreshed data across a very large multifamily listing footprint

✗ Cons

HelloData Features

✦ Automated Market Surveys Unit-level rents, concessions, and amenities refreshed daily from public listing sources.
✦ AI Rent Comps AI-powered comparable property selection for subject properties and submarkets.
✦ Expense Benchmarking Operating expense benchmarks to sanity-check budgets and underwriting assumptions.
✦ Financial Analysis AI-assisted multifamily deal underwriting built on live market data.
✦ Rent Roll Parsing Upload rent rolls and have them parsed and formatted automatically.
✦ Development Feasibility Custom survey regions and analysis for evaluating new development.
✦ Market Analytics & Reports Map-based trend analysis with a report builder, plus Excel and PDF exports.
✦ APIs Real-time property data APIs for proptech and internal tooling integrations.

About QuantumListing

QuantumListing is a commercial real estate listing service for brokers, agents, and owners across the United States, used to search, post, and share commercial properties for sale or lease. All members' active listings are publicly viewable without payment or registration, and listings can be shared directly to social media accounts. Search covers criteria such as location, property type, and price, and premium members get real-time analytics on listing views, profile views, and shares. Basic members can save listings and post Wants for space they are seeking, while premium members can post unlimited property and business-for-sale listings. Enterprise memberships are offered for higher-volume brokers and whole organizations.

QuantumListing Features

✦ Open Listing Visibility All active member listings are viewable by anyone without payment or registration.
✦ Listing Search Search commercial properties for sale or lease by location, property type, price, and other criteria.
✦ Wants Listings Members can post Wants describing the real estate they are searching for.
✦ Social Sharing Share listings to social networks such as LinkedIn and Facebook to widen distribution.
✦ Listing Analytics Premium members see real-time analytics on listing views, profile views, and shares.
✦ Enterprise Accounts Organization-level memberships for higher-volume brokers and brokerages.

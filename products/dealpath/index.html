About Dealpath

Dealpath is real estate's leading deal management software and AI-powered operating system for real estate investing. Trusted by 300+ firms powering over 0 trillion in transactions, Dealpath provides a comprehensive platform that centralizes data, surfaces insights, and enables efficient execution from sourcing to close. The platform combines AI-driven automation with comprehensive deal management capabilities to help investment teams identify opportunities, execute confidently, and maximize returns.

✓ Pros

✓Purpose-built for CRE investment workflows — not a generic CRM adapted for real estate
✓AI-powered data ingestion dramatically reduces manual data entry from OMs and flyers
✓White-glove implementation with dedicated Customer Success Manager
✓Trusted by top-tier institutional investors (Blackstone, New York Life, CBRE IM)
✓Strong integration ecosystem with CompStak, Esri, Microsoft Office, and data providers

✗ Cons

✗Minimum of 5 users required — not suitable for solo practitioners or very small teams
✗Quote-based pricing lacks transparency for budget planning
✗Implementation takes 6-8 weeks which may feel slow for firms wanting quick deployment
✗Focused on deal management — not a full property management or accounting solution
✗Best suited for institutional investors; may be over-engineered for smaller owner-operators

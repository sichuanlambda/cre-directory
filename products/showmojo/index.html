About ShowMojo

ShowMojo is a leasing automation platform for residential property managers, owner-operators, and leasing agents. It automates the rental leasing workflow end to end, including listing syndication to more than 50 rental sites, instant inquiry response, showing scheduling for in-person and self-guided tours, and tenant screening with credit, background, and ID verification. Smart lock integrations provide digital access for self-guided tours, and real-time reports track the leasing funnel from leads through showings to placements. An AI virtual agent engages prospects around the clock, backed by an optional live answering service. The platform integrates with Yardi, Buildium, AppFolio, Rent Manager, and Zapier.

✓ Pros

✓Covers the full leasing funnel from ad syndication through screening in one system
✓Self-guided tour support with smart lock integrations reduces agent time per showing
✓Integrates with the major property management systems (Yardi, Buildium, AppFolio, Rent Manager)

✗ Cons

✗Pricing is not published and requires contacting sales
✗Focused on leasing operations, not a full property management or accounting suite
✗Aimed at residential rentals rather than commercial leasing workflows

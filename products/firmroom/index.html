About FirmRoom

FirmRoom is a virtual data room platform for secure document management and collaboration during high-stakes transactions, including property deals and other corporate transactions. Rooms can be set up in under five minutes with drag-and-drop uploads, and the platform includes a built-in document viewer, smart search, granular permission controls, custom watermarks, and real-time activity analytics with access tracking. It is SOC 2 certified with AES-256 encryption on AWS infrastructure. Pricing is a published flat monthly fee by storage tier ($395/month for 2 GB, $695/month for 5 GB, $995/month for 10 GB) with no per-page charges, and a free trial is available. The site says it is used by more than 1,000 dealmakers, including teams at major financial institutions.

About MRI Angus

MRI Angus (formerly Angus Systems) helps commercial property teams manage service requests, control access, manage maintenance, gather feedback, and book spaces in one platform. Winner of the 2025 IDC SaaS CSAT Award for Facilities Management. Features include CMMS, visitor management, occupant communication, insurance document management, and shared resource booking.

MRI Angus Features

✦ Tenant Requests Capture and monitor service requests via mobile-friendly branded portal.
✦ Preventive Maintenance Establish maintenance schedules, capture asset data, dispatch work orders.
✦ Notify & Response Targeted two-way notifications with real-time tracking across channels.
✦ Resource Reservation Online calendar for booking shared resources with billing integration.
✦ Visitor Security Manage registrations, capture photos, print badges, activate/deactivate access cards.
✦ Building Directory Interactive touchscreen directory with tenant lists, floor plans, and amenities.
✦ Certificate of Insurance Collect, certify, track, and manage tenant and vendor COIs.
✦ Customizable Surveys Property-specific or corporate surveys with flexible configuration.
✦ Property Websites Professional web presence with links to handbooks and service portals.
✦ Tenant Handbook Online building policies, procedures, and emergency plans.

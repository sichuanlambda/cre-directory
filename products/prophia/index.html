About Prophia

Prophia is an AI-powered lease abstraction and management platform purpose-built for commercial real estate owners and operators. The platform transforms static lease documents into dynamic, living abstracts that allow teams to verify, analyze, and manage lease obligations with confidence. Prophia processes 493M+ square feet across 4,425+ buildings and 22,000+ tenants. Key use cases include leasing (stacking plans, benchmarks, critical dates), acquisitions (due diligence, rent roll reconciliation), property management (CAM reconciliation, budgeting), and investor reporting. Trusted by firms like Nuveen, RXR, and Spear Street Capital.

Prophia Features

✦ AI-Powered Abstraction Drag-and-drop lease upload with instant AI abstraction and data structuring.
✦ Quality Assurance CRE expert audit process ensuring accuracy through detailed review.
✦ Living Abstracts Dynamic abstracts that consolidate dozens of documents into one verifiable source of truth.
✦ Prophia Abstract (Instant) Free self-serve tool for instant, accurate lease summaries — no setup required.
✦ Self-Updating Stacking Plans Real-time visual stacking plans that update automatically as lease data changes.
✦ Critical Date Tracking Automated alerts for expirations, renewals, and option exercise deadlines.
✦ Lease Benchmarking Compare lease terms against portfolio and market benchmarks.
✦ Encumbrance Analysis Track and analyze encumbrances across the portfolio.
✦ CAM Reconciliation AI-driven CAM reconciliation with automated identification of caps, exclusions, and audit rights.
✦ Rent Roll Reconciliation Automated rent roll verification reducing material financial errors.

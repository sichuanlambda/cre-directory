About Prophia

Prophia is an AI-powered lease abstraction and management platform purpose-built for commercial real estate owners and operators. The platform transforms static lease documents into dynamic, living abstracts that allow teams to verify, analyze, and manage lease obligations with confidence. Prophia processes 493M+ square feet across 4,425+ buildings and 22,000+ tenants. Key use cases include leasing (stacking plans, benchmarks, critical dates), acquisitions (due diligence, rent roll reconciliation), property management (CAM reconciliation, budgeting), and investor reporting. Trusted by firms like Nuveen, RXR, and Spear Street Capital.

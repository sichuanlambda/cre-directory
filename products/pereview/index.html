About Pereview

Pereview is commercial real estate asset management software for institutional investors, built to visualize and optimize the performance of both equity and debt investments across the life of the asset. It combines equity and debt portfolio tracking in a single environment, with automated reporting, KPI visualization for assets, funds, and the overall business, and AI-powered aggregation of rent rolls, financial statements, and budgets. Workflow automation includes custom forms and checklists, and an investor portal handles stakeholder communication. The cloud platform integrates with tools such as Argus, Entrata, VTS, QuickBooks, and AppFolio, and Pereview also offers data management and middle office services for portfolio teams.

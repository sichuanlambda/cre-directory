About LoopNet

LoopNet is the most heavily trafficked commercial real estate marketplace in the United States, owned by CoStar Group. It serves as the primary listing platform where brokers market commercial properties for sale and lease to a massive audience of investors, tenants, and buyers. LoopNet receives over 10 million unique monthly visitors and lists hundreds of thousands of commercial properties. While basic listings are free, premium listing tiers (Diamond, Platinum, Gold) provide enhanced visibility, photography, video, and 3D tours. LoopNet is the consumer-facing complement to CoStar's professional platform.

✓ Pros

✓Largest CRE marketplace audience — 10M+ monthly visitors
✓Owned by CoStar Group — backed by the most comprehensive CRE data
✓Free basic listings lower the barrier to entry
✓Strong SEO — LoopNet listings rank highly in Google searches
✓Recognized brand trusted by buyers, tenants, and investors

✗ Cons

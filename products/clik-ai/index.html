About Clik.ai

Clik.ai provides AI-automated underwriting, servicing, and data-operations software for commercial real estate institutions. Its AutoUW module extracts rent rolls and operating statements into underwriting models, and companion modules cover investment analysis (InvestAssist), Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac agency workbook population, lease abstraction and audit, loan onboarding and digitization, and portfolio analytics dashboards. The platform is aimed at lenders, banks, brokerages, and institutional investment teams. Alongside the software, Clik.ai offers professional services such as custom financial modeling and CRE technology consulting. Pricing is not published.

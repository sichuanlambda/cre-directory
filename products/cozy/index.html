About Cozy

Cozy was a free online property management platform for independent landlords, providing rent collection, tenant screening, and rental application tools. It was acquired by Apartments.com (CoStar Group) in 2018, and the standalone product was shut down in July 2021, with accounts migrated to Apartments.com Rental Manager. The Cozy brand no longer operates.

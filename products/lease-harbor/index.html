About Lease Harbor

Lease Harbor is an integrated lease administration and lease accounting platform that manages the full lease lifecycle, from tracking critical dates and portfolio obligations to automating journal entries, rent payments, invoicing, and financial reporting. It supports compliance with FASB, IFRS, and GASB lease accounting standards with audit-ready outputs. The system includes a configurable reporting library, configurable data fields, and ERP and data integrations. The company reports more than 25 years in business serving 1,800+ clients ranging from small portfolios to Fortune 100 enterprises, with turn-key implementations deployable in days and customized rollouts in a few weeks.

Lease Harbor Features

✦ Automated Accounting Automates journal entries, rent payments, and invoicing with audit-ready compliance for FASB, IFRS, and GASB standards.

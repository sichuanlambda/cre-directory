About PlanRadar

PlanRadar is a construction and real estate management platform for digital documentation, project tracking, quality assurance, and asset management across the building lifecycle. Teams pin defects, tasks, and observations directly onto digital plans and BIM models, then manage snagging, checklists, safety audits, site diaries, and subcontractor communication from web and mobile apps. The platform also covers document and plan management, Gantt schedule management, handovers, claims, fire safety compliance, and 360-degree reality capture via SiteView. PlanRadar reports over 200,000 users across 75+ countries spanning construction, real estate, retail, infrastructure, hospitality, and public sector projects. Subcontractors and watchers can be invited to projects at no charge on every plan.

PlanRadar Features

✦ Defect Management & Snagging Pin defects to digital plans or BIM models, assign them to subcontractors, and track resolution to closeout.
✦ Inspections, Checklists & Safety Audits Standardized digital checklists for inspections, quality assurance, and site safety audits.
✦ SiteView Reality Capture 360-degree site capture that documents progress and conditions for remote review.
✦ Site Diaries & Daily Reports Structured daily documentation of site activity, weather, and workforce for an audit trail.
✦ Task & Subcontractor Management Assign tasks with evidence attached; unlimited free subcontractor and watcher accounts on every plan.
✦ Document & Plan Management Version-controlled plans and documents, including BIM model viewing on Pro plans.
✦ Gantt Schedule Management Project schedules connected to on-site progress data.
✦ Reports & Project Insights Configurable report templates and dashboards for handovers, claims, and compliance.

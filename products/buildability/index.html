About Buildability

Buildability scores any U.S. parcel from 0 to 100 using nine factors drawn from public data: zoning (city and county code), flood (FEMA NFHL), seismic (USGS), soil (USDA SSURGO), radon (EPA), wildfire (USFS Wildfire Hazard Potential), utilities, terrain (USGS 3DEP) and lot shape from the county parcel record. Each factor shows its source and an as-of date, and a factor with no data is left out of the score rather than estimated. Constraints such as a flood zone, septic limits or a use restriction cap the score, and factor weights vary by region, though the weights are not published. The free score needs no account. A paid Property Report adds a buildable envelope, risk findings, a lender-ready PDF, a verifiable certificate with a QR code, and an AI assistant that answers questions about the parcel. Cities and counties can use it to inventory undeveloped land, with custom pricing and a free 30-day pilot. A keyless MCP server exposes the scoring tools to AI assistants. Buildability is operated by ReadyPermit.AI, Inc. and launched in 2026.

Buildability Features

✦ Buildability Score Free 0 to 100 score for any U.S. parcel with no account or card required.
✦ Nine Cited Factors Zoning, flood, seismic, soil, radon, wildfire, utilities, terrain and lot shape, each with its source and as-of date.
✦ Constraint Caps Flood zones, septic constraints and use restrictions cap the score instead of being averaged away.
✦ Property Report Buildable envelope, risk findings and a lender-ready PDF for a single parcel.
✦ Verifiable Certificate QR-coded certificate that recomputes the score from the parcel record.
✦ Parcel Q&A An AI assistant answers questions about the report's parcel.
✦ Land Inventory Jurisdiction-wide scoring for cities, urban counties and CDBG grantees inventorying undeveloped land.
✦ Pilot Program Free 30-day pilot with custom per-jurisdiction pricing afterwards.

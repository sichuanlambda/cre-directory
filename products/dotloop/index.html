About Dotloop

Dotloop, a Zillow Group company, is a leading transaction management platform for real estate professionals. It provides an all-in-one solution for managing real estate transactions from offer to close, including document editing, legally-binding eSignatures, compliance tracking, and collaboration tools. Dotloop has partnered with 190+ local and state MLS associations for comprehensive forms coverage and integrates with 75+ real estate platforms. Used by hundreds of thousands of agents, teams, and brokerages, dotloop streamlines paperwork with features like document scanning, SMS signing, clause management, and automated audit trails.

Dotloop Features

✦ Robust Document Editor Fill out, edit, and annotate PDFs with a full-featured document editor.
✦ Document Templates Create reusable document templates and save common contract clauses.
✦ Document Scanner Mobile app turns your phone into a scanner, converting photos to editable PDFs.
✦ Split and Rotate PDFs Upload multi-page files and split them into separate documents.
✦ Legal eSignatures Secure, legally-binding electronic signatures trusted by banks and brokerages.
✦ SMS Signing Send documents for signature via text with full activity tracking.
✦ Audit Trail Complete compliance record of every action taken during a transaction.
✦ In-Person Signing Have clients eSign directly from your mobile device or computer.
✦ MLS & Association Feeds Partnered with 190+ associations for comprehensive forms coverage and MLS feeds.
✦ 75+ Integrations Connect with real estate CRMs, MLSs, and platforms through seamless integrations.
✦ Easy-Offer Links Share secure public links to offer documents for listings.
✦ Real-Time Notifications Instant email and push notifications customizable for each transaction.
✦ Brokerage Dashboard Overview and compliance management for all agent transactions.
✦ Clause Manager Store and reuse commonly used real estate contract clauses.
✦ Task Templates Set up reusable task lists for consistent transaction workflows.

About Actovia

Actovia is a commercial real estate and mortgage intelligence platform providing property data, ownership information, verified contact details, and market analytics. Coverage is deepest in New York City, with mortgage data available across the US and expansion into additional markets. Users search property and ownership records to reach the people behind LLCs, build and export custom lead lists, and manage outreach with built-in CRM functionality. The platform sends weekly email alerts on portfolio changes and permit pulls, tracks violations, and includes AI-powered research tools alongside market trends and historical property data. It serves brokers, lenders, investors, owners, and insurance professionals.

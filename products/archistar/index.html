About Archistar

Archistar is an AI platform that assesses the development potential of sites by analyzing zoning regulations, planning overlays, and constraints such as setbacks and height limits. Its generative design engine automatically produces code-compliant 3D building concepts and massing studies in minutes, helping developers test what is feasible on a property before engaging consultants. AI PreCheck (formerly eCheck) evaluates architectural plans against digitized zoning and building codes at the feasibility and design stages. The platform consolidates planning data from more than 25,000 government and property sources, and serves property developers, architects, real estate agents, and government and town planners.

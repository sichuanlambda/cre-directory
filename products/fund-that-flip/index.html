About Fund That Flip

Fund That Flip, rebranded as Upright in September 2023, is a lending and investment platform for residential real estate investors. It originates short-term bridge loans for fix-and-flip, new-construction, and rental projects, and lets accredited investors fund that loan portfolio to earn interest on real estate debt. Following its 2022 acquisition of FlipperForce, the platform also includes project and construction management software for rehabbers and builders.

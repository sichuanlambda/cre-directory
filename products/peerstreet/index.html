About PeerStreet

PeerStreet was a real estate lending marketplace that connected investors with short-term real estate loans. The company filed for bankruptcy in 2023 after experiencing financial difficulties. PeerStreet allowed accredited investors to invest in real estate debt, providing an alternative to traditional fixed-income investments. The platform is no longer operational and investors should consult with the bankruptcy proceedings for information about existing investments.

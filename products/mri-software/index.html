About MRI Software

MRI Software is one of the most comprehensive real estate technology platforms in the industry, providing solutions for commercial and residential property management, accounting, lease management, investment management, and facilities management. MRI serves over 50,000 clients globally with an open and connected platform that integrates with 200+ partners. Key products include MRI Platform X for AI-powered property management, MRI Investment Management, MRI Angus for facilities, and MRI Lease Intelligence. Founded in 1971, MRI is one of the longest-standing CRE technology companies.

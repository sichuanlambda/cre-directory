About Buxton

Buxton is a customer analytics company that helps businesses understand their customers and identify the best locations for growth. The platform uses consumer behavior data, demographic analysis, and predictive modeling to inform site selection, marketing strategies, and real estate decisions. Note: The Buxton website blocked automated access during research (403 error). Buxton is known for providing location intelligence solutions to retailers, restaurants, healthcare systems, and other multi-unit businesses seeking data-driven expansion strategies.

Buxton Features

✦ Customer Profiling Detailed analysis of customer demographics, behaviors, and spending patterns.
✦ Trade Area Analysis Define and analyze customer trade areas for existing and potential locations.
✦ Market Segmentation Segment customers into actionable groups for targeting and strategy.
✦ Location Scoring Score potential sites based on customer match and market potential.
✦ Predictive Modeling Forecast performance for new locations using customer analytics.
✦ Market Planning Identify optimal markets and locations for expansion.
✦ Marketing Targeting Data-driven targeting to reach the right customers in the right areas.
✦ Campaign Analytics Measure marketing campaign effectiveness and ROI by location.
✦ Competitive Insights Understand competitive landscape and market share.

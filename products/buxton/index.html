About Buxton

Buxton is a customer analytics and location intelligence platform used for site selection, market planning, and location performance optimization. Drawing on household-level consumer intelligence and more than 50 data sources, it scores potential sites, defines trade areas, analyzes foot traffic, and supports whitespace and expansion planning through its web-based SCOUT analytics tool. Since 1994 it has served multi-unit operators in retail, restaurants, healthcare, and hospitality, along with the real estate teams planning their expansion. Buxton is now part of audience-intelligence company Audiense and is offered as Audiense In-Person.

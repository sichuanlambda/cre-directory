About OpenCounter

OpenCounter is a modern permitting and licensing platform that helps cities and municipalities streamline the development approval process. The platform provides online zoning verification, permit applications, and licensing services for businesses and developers, reducing processing times and improving transparency. OpenCounter is deployed in 40+ cities across the United States including Colorado Springs, Indianapolis, Orlando, Atlanta, Salt Lake City, and Miami. The platform digitizes and simplifies the traditionally complex and opaque process of understanding zoning requirements, applying for permits, and obtaining business licenses.

OpenCounter Features

✦ Online Permit Applications Submit and track permit applications digitally through municipal portals.
✦ Zoning Verification Instantly check zoning and permitted uses for any address.
✦ Requirement Checklists Auto-generated checklists of requirements for specific project types.
✦ Application Tracking Track permit status and review progress online.
✦ Business License Applications Streamlined online business license applications.
✦ Use Type Verification Verify that proposed business uses are permitted at a given location.
✦ Fee Transparency Clear display of applicable fees and requirements upfront.
✦ GIS Integration Integration with municipal GIS data for accurate zoning and parcel information.
✦ Code Digitization Digital representation of municipal codes and regulations.
✦ Analytics Dashboard Data on application volumes, processing times, and user engagement.

About SmartZip

SmartZip is a predictive analytics and marketing platform, operated by Constellation1, that identifies the homeowners in an agent's territory who are most likely to sell. Its Smart Targeting product pairs those predictions with automated direct mail, email, and online advertising so agents can reach likely sellers before competitors. Supporting tools include Smart Data for homeowner contact information, Market Pulse for local market analytics, and Reach150 for agent branding and referrals. A companion CheckIn app gives agents a daily action plan and lead alerts. The platform is aimed at agents and brokers running geographic farming strategies.

✓ Pros

✓Purpose-built for seller-lead farming with predictive targeting
✓Bundles data, direct mail, and digital ads into one workflow
✓Companion CheckIn app turns predictions into a daily prospecting plan

✗ Cons

✗Pricing is not published and requires a sales conversation
✗Focused on residential seller leads rather than commercial workflows

About SmartPM

SmartPM, from Atlanta-based SmartPM Technologies, is a construction project controls platform that automatically analyzes CPM schedules to surface data quality problems, delays, compression, and risk. It ingests schedule files from Primavera P6 (XER/XML), Microsoft Project, Asta Powerproject, and Phoenix, and integrates with Procore, turning raw schedules into standardized analytics without manual analysis. Executive and company dashboards aggregate KPIs and trends across portfolios, while a project workspace and web-based Gantt visualize progress, critical path shifts, and end-date movement over time. The platform is used by general contractors, CM firms, owners, and government agencies, and is ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certified with FedRAMP authorization. Pricing is by demo and custom quote.

SmartPM Features

✦ Automated Quality & Risk Checks Grades schedule quality and flags logic issues, compression, and risk automatically.
✦ Delay Analysis Quantifies delays, accelerations, and critical path changes across schedule updates.
✦ Web-Based Gantt Dynamic timeline showing progress and end-date movement without desktop tools.
✦ Executive & Company Dashboards Aggregated KPIs and interactive trends across all projects.
✦ Project Workspace Customizable analytics layouts for deep-dive schedule review.
✦ Scheduling File Support P6 (XER/XML), Microsoft Project, Asta Powerproject, and Phoenix imports, plus a Procore integration.

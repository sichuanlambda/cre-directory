About Radix Underwriting

Radix Underwriting is the multifamily underwriting platform formerly known as redIQ, which Radix acquired and is rebranding into its data ecosystem. The platform ingests deal financials and applies AI-driven underwriting models trained on industry data so investors, brokers, and lenders can test scenarios, forecast returns, and analyze market impacts. Features include scenario stress-testing with real-time recalculation, rehab and repositioning analysis comparing as-is versus post-renovation value, and machine-learning forecasting that fills gaps in financial data. It connects to the wider Radix ecosystem (Intelligence, Analytics, RealRents) and supports Excel export. Pricing is not published; demos are sales-led.

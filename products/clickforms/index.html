About ClickFORMS

ClickFORMS is form-filling appraisal software from Bradford Technologies, a San Jose-based vendor that has focused exclusively on the appraisal industry for 39 years. The product pairs an intuitive form-filling interface with a comprehensive forms library and supports converting legacy UAD XML files. It extends beyond the desktop with the Inspect-a-Lot mobile inspection app and a cloud-based ClickFORMS Online option, and offers team appraising workflows plus dedicated student and trainee editions. Bradford sells subscription memberships with a 30-day free trial and a 100-day money-back guarantee, and is developing NightHawk, a next-generation end-to-end appraisal platform.

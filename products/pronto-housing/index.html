About Pronto Housing

Pronto Housing is compliance software for affordable housing owners and property managers that automates income qualification and certification workflows. The platform manages the full process - resident portal, configurable questionnaires and forms, applicant communication, document collection, income calculations, and final signed certification packages. For annual recertifications it runs a proactive outreach cycle starting 120 days out so notices, documents, and calculations are completed on time. It supports LIHTC, HUD, HOME, and local overlay programs, has been used across 20+ states, integrates with AppFolio, and includes portfolio-level compliance status and ESG reporting. The company also offers compliance services alongside the software.

Pronto Housing Features

✦ Resident Portal Residents complete questionnaires, upload documents, and sign forms online.
✦ Configurable Forms Use any form set - housing authority, HUD, or an owner's own templates.
✦ Income Calculations & Cert Packages Automates income calculations and delivers signed certification files.

About Prospect by Buildout

Prospect by Buildout, formerly ProspectNow, is an AI-powered property and owner database used for real estate prospecting. It covers 155 million US properties and 18 million businesses, layering ownership, sales, listing, and loan origination data with owner contact information. Machine-learning models flag properties likely to sell within the next 12 months - 34 million predicted sellers - so brokers, lenders, and investors can focus outreach where deals are most likely. The platform supports commercial and residential filtering, regional or nationwide plans, recent-transaction tracking with new owner contacts, and database enrichment, with mobile apps for iOS and Android. It is part of Buildout's broker software suite.

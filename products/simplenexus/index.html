About SimpleNexus

SimpleNexus, now part of the nCino Mortgage Suite, is a digital mortgage platform that increases financial institutions' mortgage profitability by automating loan processing and closing, delivering modern customer convenience, and surfacing data insights. The platform has achieved a 29% increase in application pull-through rates, 80% decrease in loan closing time, and 49% improvement to average cycle time for its users. Trusted by leading lenders including credit unions and banks, the nCino Mortgage Suite includes mortgage origination, incentive compensation management, and mortgage analytics. The platform is used by over 2,700 financial institutions for end-to-end mortgage simplicity.

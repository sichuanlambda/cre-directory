About nCino Mortgage

nCino Mortgage is the mortgage suite of nCino, built from SimpleNexus, which nCino acquired in January 2022 and rebranded as nCino's Mortgage Suite in September 2023 (simplenexus.com now redirects to nCino). It is residential mortgage software for banks, credit unions and mortgage lenders: a mortgage point of sale with borrower and loan officer apps, document collection and eClose, integrations with loan origination systems plus Freddie Mac AIM and dual automated underwriting, mortgage analytics, and incentive compensation management. nCino says the mortgage point of sale is trusted by more than 2,700 institutions. Pricing is quote-based.

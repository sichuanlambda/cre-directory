About Azira

Azira is a location intelligence platform that turns real-world consumer movement into analysis, audiences, and campaign measurement. The company cites 13 trillion observed global signals and 43 million mapped places of interest across more than 44 countries. Its product suite spans Azira Insights for data analysis, Azira Audience for building behavior-based audiences, Azira Campaigns for activation, and Azira Measurement for tracking real-world outcomes. Real estate is a named industry alongside retail, restaurants, hospitality, automotive, and travel, with teams using the data for trade-area analysis, competitive benchmarking, and site evaluation. Pricing is not published and is handled through sales.

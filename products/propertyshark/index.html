About PropertyShark

PropertyShark is a commercial real estate data and research platform owned by Yardi Systems. It provides comprehensive property data, owner information, comparable sales, building permits, zoning details, and market analytics across major US markets. PropertyShark is known for its deep coverage in New York City and other major metros, offering detailed property reports, foreclosure data, and ownership lookup tools. The platform serves CRE brokers, investors, appraisers, and researchers with data for prospecting, due diligence, and market analysis.

✓ Pros

✓Deep data coverage in major metros especially NYC
✓Owned by Yardi — reliable data infrastructure
✓Good for prospecting with owner lookup and list building
✓Foreclosure and distressed property tracking
✓More affordable than CoStar for individual users

✗ Cons

✗Coverage varies significantly by market — strongest in NYC
✗Smaller dataset than CoStar
✗No property management or transaction features
✗Interface can feel dated

PropertyShark Features

✦ Property Reports Comprehensive property reports with ownership, sales history, tax assessments, and building details.
✦ Owner Lookup Identify property owners including contact information for outreach.
✦ Comparable Sales Recent transaction data with price, cap rate, and price per square foot.
✦ Zoning & Permits Building permits, zoning designations, and development activity tracking.
✦ Market Analytics Market-level data on pricing trends, transaction volumes, and supply pipeline.
✦ Foreclosure Data Track pre-foreclosures, lis pendens, and distressed property opportunities.
✦ Neighborhood Data Demographics, schools, transit, and walkability scores for property locations.
✦ Map-Based Search Interactive map search with property overlays and data layers.
✦ List Building Build targeted property and owner lists for prospecting campaigns.
✦ Data Exports Export property data and lists for CRM import and analysis.

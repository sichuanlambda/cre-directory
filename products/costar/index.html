About CoStar

CoStar is the world leader in commercial real estate information and marketplaces, providing comprehensive data, analytics, and online marketplace services to commercial real estate professionals. As a publicly-traded company, CoStar maintains the largest database of commercial real estate information in the United States, including detailed building and ownership data, lease comps, sales transactions, and market analytics. The platform serves over 150,000 CRE professionals with critical market intelligence and research capabilities.

✓ Pros

✓Most comprehensive and accurate commercial real estate database available
✓Research team of 1,000+ professionals ensures data quality and timeliness
✓Powerful analytics and forecasting tools for market intelligence
✓Integrated marketplace reach through LoopNet and Apartments.com
✓Covers all major property types with submarket-level granularity

✗ Cons

✗Premium pricing makes it cost-prohibitive for smaller firms and individual brokers
✗Steep learning curve due to the depth and complexity of the platform
✗Annual contracts with limited flexibility on plan adjustments
✗Can be overwhelming for users who only need basic property search
✗Some international market coverage is less comprehensive than US data

CoStar Features

✦ Property Database Access data on over 6 million commercial properties including ownership, tenancy, and building specs
✦ Sales Comparables Verified sales transaction data for valuation and market analysis
✦ Lease Comparables Detailed lease comp data including rates, terms, and concessions
✦ Tenant Information Track tenant movements, lease expirations, and expansion/contraction activity
✦ Market Analytics Submarket-level analytics on vacancy, absorption, rent trends, and construction pipeline
✦ Analytics Suite Custom reporting and visualization tools for portfolio and market analysis
✦ CMBS/Loan Data Access to commercial mortgage data for lending and investment analysis
✦ Forecast Models Proprietary forecasting models for rent growth, vacancy, and cap rates

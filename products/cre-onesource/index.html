About CRE OneSource

CRE OneSource is a brokerage operating system built specifically for commercial real estate brokers, founded by veteran broker Craig Coppola. The platform combines three core modules: OS Prospects for lead management with automated follow-up cadences, Deal View for tracking deals from letter of intent through closing, and OS Back Office for commission and payment processing with automated vouchers, invoices, and reconciliation. AI features automate data entry and administrative tasks, and the platform is accessible on mobile. The company reports use by 500+ companies across 48 U.S. states.

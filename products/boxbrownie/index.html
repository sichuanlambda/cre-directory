About BoxBrownie.com

BoxBrownie.com is a self-serve online platform where agents, photographers, and property developers order professional editing of listing imagery from a global team of editors. Services include image enhancement, virtual staging, day-to-dusk conversion, item removal and virtual cleaning, floor plan redraws, virtual renovations, 3D renders, and 360 image editing for virtual tours. Pricing is published per image with no subscription, such as image enhancement from 2 euros and virtual staging at 30 euros, and standard turnaround is 24 hours (48 hours for virtual staging) with an unlimited change guarantee. New accounts receive free trial edits, including three image enhancements and one day-to-dusk edit.

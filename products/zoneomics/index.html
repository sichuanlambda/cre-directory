About Zoneomics

Zoneomics is an AI-driven zoning analysis and site search platform preferred by real estate industry leaders for efficient zoning due diligence and accelerated site acquisition. The platform digitizes and aggregates zoning and land use data from fragmented city sources across thousands of cities, providing the largest nationwide zoning data coverage available. Zoneomics serves thousands of end-users including Redfin, Cushman & Wakefield, Moody's, Stanford University, and Keller Williams. The platform offers zoning search, prospecting tools for filtering properties by zoning and land use, a Zoning API for data integration, and various report products including Zoning Briefs, Summary Reports, Full Reports, and Certified Zoning Letters.

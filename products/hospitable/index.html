About Hospitable

Hospitable is a cloud-based property management system that positions itself as an operating system for short-term rental professionals, from solo hosts to portfolios of 1,000+ properties. It syncs calendars and listings across Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Agoda, Google Vacation Rentals, and direct bookings, with AI-powered guest messaging and auto-replies across channels. Operational tools include dynamic pricing, task management with role-based auto-delegation, automated owner statements and payouts, and portfolio analytics with an AI copilot for natural-language questions. Direct booking sites come with damage coverage and chargeback protection. A free Essentials plan covers basic messaging and channel syncing, and paid tiers add AI messaging, dynamic pricing, direct booking sites, and owner portals.

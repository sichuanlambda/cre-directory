About Mashvisor

Mashvisor is a real estate investment data analytics platform specializing in short-term rental (Airbnb) and long-term rental analysis. The platform helps investors find profitable investment properties by providing market data, rental revenue estimates, and property analytics across the United States. Key tools include the Market Finder for discovering top-performing markets, Property Finder for searching investment opportunities across multiple locations, Dynamic Pricing powered by AI for Airbnb hosts, and a comprehensive real estate data API. Mashvisor provides cap rates, cash-on-cash returns, occupancy rates, and rental comps to support data-driven investment decisions.

Mashvisor Features

✦ Market Finder Discover top-performing rental markets nationwide using Mashmeter score, revenue, cap rate, and crime filters.
✦ Neighborhood Analysis In-depth neighborhood data with rental revenue, occupancy rates, and market trends.
✦ Migration Data Track population migration trends to identify emerging rental markets.
✦ Short-Term Regulations Stay updated on current STR regulatory landscape by market.
✦ Property Finder Search for investment properties across up to five locations simultaneously with customizable criteria.
✦ Airbnb Calculator Estimate potential short-term and long-term rental income for any property.
✦ Rental Comps Compare similar properties to validate revenue projections and pricing.
✦ Mashmeter Score Proprietary scoring system evaluating investment potential based on multiple criteria.
✦ Dynamic Pricing AI-powered pricing optimization considering seasonality, demand, and booking trends.
✦ Competition Analysis Analyze competing listings to position your rental strategically.
✦ Revenue Forecasting Data-driven revenue projections for short-term rental properties.
✦ Real Estate Data API Integrate Mashvisor's rental market data and analytics into your own website or app.
✦ Investment Metrics Cap rates, cash-on-cash returns, occupancy rates, and ROI calculations.
✦ Market Reports Downloadable market reports with trends and investment insights.

About Guesty

Guesty is an AI-powered property management platform for short-term and vacation rentals, sold in tiers for solo hosts (Guesty Lite, 1-3 listings), professional managers (Guesty Pro, 4-199 listings), and large operators (Guesty Enterprise, 200+ listings). Its channel manager syncs listings to 60+ platforms including Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com, with a multi-calendar and unified guest inbox at the core. The suite adds AI-driven messaging, pricing, and content generation, revenue management with dynamic pricing, GuestyPay payments, trust accounting, task automation, review management, damage protection, and a direct booking website builder. Published pricing starts at $9 per month, with 24/7 support and personalized onboarding.

✓ Pros

✓Scales from a single listing to enterprise portfolios on one platform

✗ Cons

About ParkMobile

ParkMobile is the leading smart parking platform in North America, enabling users to find and pay for on-street and off-street parking through its mobile app. The platform serves both consumers who need convenient parking payment and businesses that want to manage employee/fleet parking expenses. ParkMobile also provides solutions for parking operators and municipalities to manage parking infrastructure, including garage management, event parking, and fleet management. With millions of users across 1,000+ cities, ParkMobile offers features like parking spot reservations, real-time availability, session extensions, and detailed expense reporting for businesses.

ParkMobile Features

✦ Mobile Parking Payment Pay for on-street and garage parking directly from the ParkMobile app.
✦ Parking Reservations Reserve parking spots in advance for events, airports, and popular locations.
✦ Session Extensions Extend your parking time remotely without returning to the meter.
✦ Real-Time Availability Find available parking spots near your destination in real time.
✦ ParkMobile for Business Portal for managing company vehicles, users, tracking expenses, and generating reports.
✦ Fleet Management Manage parking for company fleets with centralized billing and controls.
✦ Expense Reporting Detailed monthly reports for parking expense tracking and reimbursement.
✦ Garage Solutions Digital payment and management tools for parking garage operators.
✦ Event Parking Specialized solutions for managing high-volume event parking.
✦ Municipal Partnerships Technology solutions for cities to modernize on-street parking management.

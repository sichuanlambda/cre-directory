About Dynamo Software

Dynamo Software is an alternative investment management platform used by real estate funds alongside private equity, venture capital, and fund-of-funds firms. For real estate managers it integrates acquisition pipeline management, deal tracking, investor relations, fund accounting, and portfolio monitoring in one system built around a purpose-built CRM. Investor relations tooling automates capital calls, manages disclosures, and delivers tailored reports, while a secure investor portal gives LPs real-time access to performance metrics, financial statements, and documents, and a fundraising data room shares marketing and subscription materials with prospects. DynamoAI adds tagging and summarization across communications, documents, and research.

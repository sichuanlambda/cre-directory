About FlipperForce

FlipperForce is an all-in-one platform for managing residential real estate investment projects from acquisition through completion, operating since 2018. Deal analysis tools include flip and BRRRR calculators, comps research, and rehab cost estimators. Project management covers scheduling, task management, photo logging, and material catalogs, with a mobile app for field updates. Accounting features include an AI-powered receipt analyzer, expense tracking, and budget-vs-actual reporting. The company reports more than 125,000 active projects hosted on the platform.

About CRED iQ

CRED iQ is a commercial real estate data, analytics, and valuation platform built on the universe of securitized CRE debt, spanning CMBS, SASB, CRE CLO, and GSE/Agency multifamily loans. Founded in 2020, it covers roughly $2.3 trillion in loan data and over 150 million properties, including loan balances and maturity dates, borrower and ownership contact data, special-servicing status, and property-level operating statements. Investors, lenders, brokers, and analysts use it to monitor loan performance, track distress, model valuations, and source leads for lending, refinancing, and acquisition opportunities. Data is delivered via web application, API, and bulk feeds.

CRED iQ Features

✦ Loan-Level Data Detailed CMBS loan data including terms, rates, and performance metrics.
✦ Property Financials Operating statements and financial data for CMBS-financed properties.
✦ Delinquency Tracking Monitor CMBS loan delinquencies and special servicing status.
✦ Maturity Analysis Track upcoming CMBS loan maturities and refinance risk.
✦ Market Reports Regular reports on CRE market conditions and trends.
✦ Property Search Search commercial properties with detailed financial data.
✦ Comp Analysis Comparable transaction data for property valuation.
✦ Portfolio Monitoring Track and monitor CMBS portfolio performance.
✦ Alert System Notifications on loan events, delinquencies, and market changes.
✦ Data Export Export data for custom analysis and reporting.

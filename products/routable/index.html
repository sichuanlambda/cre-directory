About Routable

Routable is an accounts payable automation platform that helps businesses automate invoice processing, payments, vendor onboarding, and tax management. While not CRE-specific, Routable is used by property management companies and real estate firms to manage vendor payments, contractor invoices, and owner distributions. The platform enables payments to hundreds of thousands of vendors in 220+ countries and 140+ currencies, 2x faster and for 30% less than competitors. Routable features customizable approval workflows, stress-free compliance, and audit-ready data. Key metrics include 80% reduction in manual payment tasks, 25% faster reconciliation, and $100K+ saved per year.

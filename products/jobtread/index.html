About JobTread

JobTread is construction business management software from Dallas-based JobTread Software that runs sales, project delivery, and job finances in one platform. Sales and estimating tools include a construction CRM, proposals, takeoffs, cost catalogs, and bid management, while project management covers task scheduling, daily logs, time tracking, photo and file management, subcontractor portals, and automated workflows. Job finance features span budgeting, job costing, change orders, purchase orders, AIA-style payment applications, invoicing, and financial dashboards, with client portals, online payments, and e-signatures on the customer side. It serves home builders, remodelers, specialty contractors, and commercial contractors, and integrates with QuickBooks Online, CompanyCam, EagleView, Gusto, and Zapier. Published per-user pricing starts at $16 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited jobs and documents and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

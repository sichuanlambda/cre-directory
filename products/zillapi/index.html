About Zillapi

Zillapi is a REST API that returns Zillow-sourced U.S. property data for developers and AI agents: price, Zestimate, rent estimate, photos, schools, taxes, agent contact, and full price history — 300+ typed fields across a stated 160M+ U.S. parcels in a single call. It offers nine sub-resources (such as /zestimate, /nearby comps, and /price-history), geo search by bounding box with filters, address- or zpid-based lookups, and JSON, CSV, and NDJSON output with HMAC-signed webhooks for async jobs. The service ships an OpenAPI 3.1 spec, llms.txt files, and MCP-readiness for use with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, LangChain, and workflow tools like Zapier and n8n. Pricing is credit-based: a free tier with 100 credits and no card, $5/month for 1,000 credits, or $54/year for 12,000 credits, with most calls costing 1 credit per record. Zillapi states it is an independent service not affiliated with or endorsed by Zillow Group.

Zillapi Features

✦ Full Property Records 300+ typed fields per property: address, price, Zestimate, rent estimate, beds/baths, lot size, taxes, schools, agent contact, photos, and price history.
✦ Sub-Resources & Field Projection Nine sub-resources (zestimate, nearby comps, price history, photos, schools) and ?fields= projection to slim payloads.
✦ Geo & URL Search Bounding-box search with price/beds/baths/sqft/type filters, or paste a Zillow search URL; async jobs with signed webhooks.
✦ OpenAPI 3.1 Spec Stable v1 REST contract that drops into Postman, Cursor, and code generators.
✦ AI-Agent Ready Function-call-ready spec, llms.txt/llms-full.txt, and MCP readiness for Claude, ChatGPT, and LangChain.
✦ Multiple Output Formats JSON, CSV, and NDJSON output for pipelines including Google Sheets, BigQuery, and Snowflake.

About Parcl Labs

Parcl Labs provides real-time US housing market data, with pricing, supply, sales, rental, and seller-motivation metrics updated daily across more than 70,000 markets down to the zip-code level. The platform covers roughly 150 million properties and prices 1.6 million+ active listings daily. Parcl HQ surfaces live price feeds, mortgage and rental data, watchlists, and metrics such as the Motivated Seller Index and price-cut percentages by market, while the Data Vault delivers institutional-grade property and market data via API and MCP. The company also publishes data-driven housing research and tracks housing prediction-market resolutions verified against its indices. It is aimed at institutional investors, operators, and real estate professionals.

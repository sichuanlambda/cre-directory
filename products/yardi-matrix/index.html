About Yardi Matrix

Yardi Matrix is the market intelligence division of Yardi Systems, providing data, analytics, and research to support institutional real estate investment and asset management decisions. Coverage spans multifamily, affordable housing, student housing, single-family rental and build-to-rent, office, industrial, self storage, and vacant land across more than 180 US markets. Property-level data is developed through original field research with continuous near real-time updates, supplemented by anonymized Yardi platform data. The platform includes monthly market reports on rents and occupancy, ownership transparency, loan intelligence, transaction history, and supply and absorption insights, delivered through dashboards and benchmarking tools, and a Matrix Connector for Claude, included on every plan, lets the assistant query Matrix data. It is used by equity investors, lenders, and property managers underwriting US real estate.

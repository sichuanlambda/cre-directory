About IDX Broker

IDX Broker displays live MLS listing data on real estate websites with customizable search tools, lead capture, and lead routing for agents, teams, broker offices, and web developers. Map-based search templates return unlimited results, and configurable signup prompts capture visitors as leads with real-time text notifications. Higher tiers add market reports, sold listing data, AI-powered natural language search, and an AI room redesign tool. A WordPress plugin (IMPress for IDX Broker) integrates search into WordPress sites, and the Elite plan bundles professional website design and hosting. An IXACT Contact CRM add-on is available for $50/month.

About SyndicationPro

SyndicationPro, part of the SponsorCloud suite, is real estate syndication software for sponsors and fund managers raising and managing investor capital. It combines a branded GP and LP investor portal with an SEC-compliant CRM, email marketing with segmentation, and fundraising tools including digital PPMs, built-in eSign, and a soft reservation module. Distribution features cover calculations, ACH payments, and investor statements, alongside K-1 and document management and a co-sponsor module for secure collaboration on joint deals. Pricing is based on equity under management, and the wider SponsorCloud suite adds fund administration (SponsorAdmin), syndication document preparation (SponsorDocs), and a GP networking community (SponsorNetwork).

About Northspyre

Northspyre is a cloud platform that unifies deal, project, and portfolio data across the real estate development lifecycle, from acquisition and early planning through construction and stabilization. It replaces spreadsheet-based workflows with automated budget, cost, and vendor tracking, giving teams real-time financial visibility on every project. Modules include Northspyre Deal for financial modeling and pipeline management, Northspyre Development for project and financial management, and Analytics for portfolio performance monitoring. The platform connects to existing systems, standardizes cost and vendor data across portfolios, and includes a mobile companion app for on-site access. It serves executives, development leaders, project managers, acquisition teams, and accountants working across asset classes including data centers, commercial, and affordable housing.

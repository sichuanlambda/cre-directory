About Deepblocks

Deepblocks (Deep Blocks Inc.) builds AI-powered software for early-stage real estate development analysis. Its Developer product produces interactive feasibility studies with assumptions, 3D massing, and return metrics - including pro forma generation, scenario testing, unit mix adjustment, real-time return calculations, and PDF/CSV export. The AIM deal-flow service layers automated property screening, zoning analysis, comparables, and residual land value calculations onto a pipeline CRM, delivering on-market development and value-add opportunities matched to a buyer's criteria at published monthly tiers. A ChatDB tool lets users query demographic and economic data across cities, zip codes, neighborhoods, and census tracts, and the company also offers AI strategy advisory for real estate workflow automation.

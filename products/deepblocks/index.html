About Deepblocks

Deepblocks (Deep Blocks Inc.) builds software for early-stage real estate development analysis in US cities. Its DEV product produces interactive feasibility studies: live 3D massing sits beside an editable pro forma whose return metrics update as assumptions change, with tutorials covering multifamily, condo, retail, office, hotel and industrial schemes. Studies export as branded PDFs, assumptions export to CSV, and models can be shared by link. City coverage is built on request rather than published as a map. A second product, AIM, for site selection, is listed as coming in October 2026, and further modules (market analysis, highest and best use, zoning reports, a data API) are marked coming soon. Pricing is published per month by the number of cities included, from $99.99 (about 5 cities for DEV) to $499.99, with overage billed at the standard rate.

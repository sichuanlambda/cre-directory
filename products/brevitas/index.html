About Brevitas

Brevitas is the world's leading platform for investment real estate, connecting agents, brokers, and investors with commercial and investment property opportunities globally. The platform hosts 200,000+ global members, 350,000+ property listings created, 8,000+ email templates built, and 5,000+ landing pages created. Brevitas offers free property listing for sale or lease, enterprise solutions for brokerages to create branded platforms, a member search directory of professionals, and advanced email marketing campaigns. The platform serves agents and brokers looking to sell or lease, brokerages needing enterprise tools, investors finding exclusive opportunities, and marketing professionals.

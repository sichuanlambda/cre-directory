About Plotzy

Plotzy is an AI-powered parcel and zoning research platform for commercial real estate brokers, land acquisition teams, developers and investors. It covers 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ US counties, updated bi-weekly, and attaches a two-level zoning designation to every parcel so you can search by permitted use, lot size, land use, assessed value, owner type and sale date instead of decoding municipal code parcel by parcel. A Zoning AI chat answers code questions in plain language, FEMA flood layers and satellite basemaps sit on the same map, and owner contact lookup, folders, deal notes and CSV export turn a search into a prospect list. Pricing is published and month to month: Starter at $180/month, Production at $300/month with sub-level zoning filters and 100 owner contacts a month, and a custom Scale plan with API access and integrations. A 7-day free trial on the Production plan needs no credit card. The product is still labeled beta, and sub-level zoning depth varies by municipality.

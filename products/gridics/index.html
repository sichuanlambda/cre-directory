About Gridics

Gridics converts zoning ordinances into parcel-level data using a patented rules engine, powering a suite of zoning products for cities and real estate professionals. CodeHUB integrates code text, maps, and self-help tools; MuniMap adds 3D mapping and data visualization; ZoneCheck virtualizes a zoning front counter with 24/7 remote access; and PropZone streamlines property research and site selection. ZoneIQ analyzes development potential in real-time 3D, a Zoning Data API exposes hundreds of parcel-level zoning and real estate data points, and expert-prepared Property Zoning Reports cover feasibility. Customers include municipal planning departments, developers, and CRE firms such as CBRE, Colliers, KW Commercial, and RE/MAX.

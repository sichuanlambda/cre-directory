About TheAnalyst PRO

TheAnalyst PRO by CRE Tech is the #1 CRE investment modeling and DCF software platform, combining investment analysis, lease analysis, demographic analysis, offering memorandums, flyers, brochures, and infographics in one subscription. The platform creates sophisticated financial analysis reports in minutes, not hours, replacing clunky spreadsheets with professionally designed, branded output. It includes 5 and 10-year cash flow analysis (before and after tax), demographic mapping with infographics, comprehensive offering memorandums, and lease vs. own analysis. Priced at $699.99/year or $89.99/month.

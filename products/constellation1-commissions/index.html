About Constellation1 Commissions

Constellation1 Commissions is the commission management module within the Constellation1 real estate technology suite, built to model caps, splits, deductions, referral fees, and team structures for modern brokerages. Constellation1 organizes its platform around three pillars: Win (lead generation and marketing), Close (transaction and conversion tools including eSign), and Run (operations and back office, where Commissions sits). The broader suite includes IDX websites and CRM, a digital marketing suite, and MLS and association portals, and the company reports 400+ team members across North America processing 450K+ transactions annually. It serves brokerages from regional independents to multi-office franchises, along with MLSs, associations, and teams.

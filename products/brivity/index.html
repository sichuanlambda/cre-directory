About Brivity

Brivity is an all-in-one real estate software platform, part of the Ben Kinney/PLACE family of companies, that lets agents and teams generate leads, manage clients, market listings, and close deals. The suite includes Brivity CRM for client relationship management, Brivity AI, lead capture and routing through Brivity Lead Gen and the KWKLY text lead capture tool, custom IDX websites, Brivity Marketer for marketing, Brivity CMA for comparative market analyses, and Recruiter.ai for recruiting. Plans are organized for solo agents through large teams, and its site shows use by agents at major brokerages including Keller Williams, Century 21, Coldwell Banker, and Compass. The platform is cloud-based with mobile apps.

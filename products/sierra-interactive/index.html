About Sierra Interactive

Sierra Interactive is a real estate platform focused on IDX websites, CRM, and marketing automation that helps agents and teams differentiate their brand and personalize lead nurture. The platform stands out with proprietary IDX website integration that boosts SEO rankings, a campaign builder for personalized messaging sequences triggered by lead behavior, and easy integration with favorite tools. Sierra Interactive has helped teams generate 2,500 monthly leads, cut cost per conversion by 37%, and increase leads by 1,000%. The platform includes an embedded dialer, lead ponds and router, action plans with reporting, agent accountability dashboards, and digital ad management for Google and Facebook.

About CRE Data Extractor

CRE Data Extractor is a simple AI-powered tool that converts rent roll images into structured CSV data. Users upload an image of their rent roll (JPG, JPEG, PDF, or PNG), and the platform uses AI to extract and structure the data into a downloadable CSV file. Processing takes approximately 30 seconds to 2 minutes. The tool is designed for commercial real estate professionals who need to quickly digitize paper or image-based rent rolls for analysis in spreadsheets or financial models.

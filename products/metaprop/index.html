About MetaProp

MetaProp is the world's most active venture capital and growth equity firm focused on the built world. The firm has made 150+ investments, created $107B+ in enterprise value, facilitated $1.9B+ raised across its portfolio, and manages real estate totaling over 20B square feet through its global investor group. MetaProp invests in technology companies transforming real estate, construction, and the built environment, with a 40% diversity rate among portfolio founders. The firm provides 900+ industry introductions annually for portfolio companies, combining capital with deep industry connections to accelerate proptech innovation.

About OwnerRez

OwnerRez is cloud-based vacation rental property management software used by both small homeowners and larger property management companies. It provides direct channel integrations with booking platforms such as Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, and Google Vacation Rentals, alongside a unified inbox for email, SMS, and channel messaging. The platform covers payment processing, rental agreements with digital signatures, guest CRM, automation triggers, reporting, and a website builder for direct bookings. A property management module adds commission calculation and monthly owner statements, and premium add-ons include QuickBooks integration, hosted websites, SMS messaging, and Rezzy AI. Pricing is per property on a sliding scale with unlimited bookings and no setup fees, booking fees, or contracts.

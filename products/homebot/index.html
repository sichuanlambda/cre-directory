About Homebot

Homebot is a homeownership platform that keeps loan officers, real estate agents, title agents, and insurance agents engaged with past clients between transactions by delivering personalized home finance insights. Clients receive ongoing digests about their home's finances, and professionals get Partner Intel behavioral insights that flag high-intent buyer and seller activity in their database. The platform includes a client home search experience, lead generation from transaction-intent signals, performance tracking, and retention and referral tools. Plans are offered separately for loan officers, real estate agents, title agents, insurance agents, and enterprise teams such as lenders, banks, and credit unions. Homebot is cloud-based and accessible on smartphones and tablets.

✓ Pros

✓Long-horizon nurture keeps clients engaged for years between transactions
✓Serves lenders, agents, title, and insurance from one platform, enabling co-branding
✓Surfaces intent signals from existing databases rather than selling new leads

✗ Cons

✗Specific pricing is not detailed publicly; plans differ by role

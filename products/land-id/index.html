About LandID

Land id (formerly AcreValue) is a real estate mapping software platform trusted by over 40,000 real estate and industry professionals. The platform provides extensive parcel data, property lines, and interactive map creation capabilities across all 50 US states. Land id enables users to create branded, interactive property maps with 40+ basemap overlays, comparable sales reports, 3D property tours, and embedding capabilities. The platform serves rural and land real estate professionals particularly well, offering detailed property data including ownership, year built, and front footage. Over 3 million maps have been created on the platform.

LandID Features

✦ Nationwide Parcel Data Property boundaries and detailed data from all 50 US states.
✦ Property Information Ownership details, year built, property type, front footage, and more.
✦ Smart Search Search properties by location, owner name, or parcel number.
✦ Comparable Sales Reports Generate professional comp reports for pricing and listings.
✦ Interactive Property Maps Create branded, interactive maps with property lines, boundaries, and features.
✦ 40+ Basemap Overlays Contextual layers including aerial, topographic, soil, and environmental data.
✦ 3D Property Tours Stunning fly-around 3D property showcases from your maps.
✦ Custom Branding Add your name, logo, and contact info to maps and reports.
✦ Map Embedding Seamlessly embed interactive maps on websites and listing services.
✦ Map Sharing Share branded maps via web, email, and text.
✦ Matterport Integration Integrate Matterport 3D tours into your property maps.
✦ CSV Mailing Lists Export property owner data as CSV mailing lists for marketing.

About Ten-X

Ten-X is an online transaction platform for buying and selling commercial real estate through digital auctions and negotiated bids. Sellers and brokers use it to market assets to a national buyer pool with standardized due-diligence packages, while buyers get transparent access to deal documents and defined bidding and closing timelines. CoStar Group acquired Ten-X in 2020 and integrated it with LoopNet and CoStar data, exposing its auctions to CoStar's large commercial real estate audience. The platform serves brokers, owners, and investors across all major CRE asset types.

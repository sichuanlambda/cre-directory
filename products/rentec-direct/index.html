About Rentec Direct

Rentec Direct is a cloud-based property management software trusted by landlords and professional property managers for managing rental properties of all types including single-family, multi-family, apartments, student housing, commercial, and affordable housing. Starting at $2/unit/month for landlords, the platform offers complete accounting with general ledger, tenant screening with instant credit, criminal, and eviction reports, online rent payments (free ACH), vacancy publishing to 20+ listing sites, secure file management, and custom rental websites. Rentec Direct has won the American Business Awards for Customer Service Department of the Year multiple times.

Rentec Direct Features

✦ General Ledger Accounting Full bank, property, and tenant accounting with general ledger for accurate records.
✦ Financial Reporting Comprehensive reports including P&L, balance sheet, rent rolls, and 1099 generation.
✦ Bank Reconciliation Sync and reconcile bank accounts securely via Plaid integration.
✦ Owner Statements Generate and distribute owner statements and reports.
✦ Free Online Payments Free ACH/eCheck rent collection for tenants with automated processing.
✦ Cash Payments Accept cash payments via PayNearMe at retail locations nationwide.
✦ Tenant Screening Instant credit, criminal, and eviction reports for applicant screening.
✦ Income Verification Verify tenant income with Payscore integration.
✦ Vacancy Publishing Automatically publish listings to 20+ rental websites including Zillow and Realtor.com.
✦ Custom Rental Website Create branded website for tenants to discover vacancies and owners to find your business.
✦ Leasing Automation Streamline showing scheduling and leasing with ShowMojo integration.
✦ Electronic Signatures Digital lease signing via Dropbox Sign integration.
✦ Maintenance Management Track and manage maintenance requests from submission to completion.
✦ File Management Securely store and share lease agreements, photos, and documents online.
✦ Rental Inspections Conduct and document property inspections via ZInspector integration.
✦ Communication Tools SMS and email communication with tenants via Twilio integration.

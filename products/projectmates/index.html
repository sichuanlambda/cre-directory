About Projectmates

Projectmates is construction project management software built for owners, centralizing document management, cost tracking, workflow automation, scheduling, capital planning, and reporting in one platform. Configurable workflows digitize RFIs, submittals, change orders, punch lists, and approvals, replacing spreadsheets and manual processes with real-time visibility across a program. The platform includes bid management, risk management, custom apps and forms, more than 100 pre-built reports, and native iOS and Android mobile apps for field teams. It is used by owners in government, healthcare, higher education, K-12, retail, and commercial real estate, alongside the contractors, architects, and engineers on their projects. Projectmates reports more than 100,000 active projects on the platform.

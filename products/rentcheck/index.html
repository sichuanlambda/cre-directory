About RentCheck

RentCheck is a property inspection platform that shifts routine inspections to residents: step-by-step mobile flows walk tenants through move-in, move-out, and periodic inspections with time-stamped photos, so property managers avoid site visits. Automated scheduling, reminders, and pre-built templates keep inspections on cadence, and the platform generates professional reports with side-by-side comparisons, maintenance issue tracking, and real-time dashboards. AI damage detection helps teams identify critical issues and prioritize review. RentCheck integrates with property management systems including AppFolio, Rent Manager, and Buildium. Published pricing starts at $1 per unit per month on the Launch plan, with Accelerate at $1.25 and Velocity at $1.75, and a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

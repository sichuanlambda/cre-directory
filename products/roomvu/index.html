About Roomvu

Roomvu is an AI content creation and social media management platform that produces personalized, localized videos and auto-posts them daily to an agent's channels, including Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube. It generates AI avatars from a photo and can use the agent's own voice and branding, creates listing and market-update videos tied to the agent's city, and runs weekly email campaigns. The platform also includes a lite CRM with SMS and email plus AI-powered lead follow-up. Published plans run from $89.99/month (Omni-Presence) to $399.99/month (Lead Magnet, which bundles Meta ad spend and managed lead generation), each with a 7-day money-back guarantee.

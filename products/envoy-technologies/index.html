About Envoy Technologies

Envoy Technologies provides electric car sharing as a service for properties, offering on-demand electric vehicle mobility as a premium amenity for multifamily, commercial, and hospitality properties. The platform positions properties as hubs of sustainable, on-demand mobility, providing residents, guests, and employees with convenient access to electric vehicles. Envoy handles all details of the car sharing program, helping properties increase occupancy, reduce parking headaches, and differentiate with a forward-thinking amenity. The service transforms everyday convenience into an eco-luxury experience while supporting sustainability goals.

✓ Pros

✓Turnkey service—Envoy handles everything from vehicles to maintenance
✓Electric vehicles align with sustainability and ESG goals
✓Premium amenity differentiates properties in competitive markets
✓Reduces parking pressure at properties
✓Mobile app provides convenient on-demand access

✗ Cons

✗Requires dedicated parking spaces for vehicles and chargers
✗Limited to markets where Envoy operates
✗Usage-based revenue model requires sufficient resident adoption

About PropertyRadar

PropertyRadar is a property data and marketing platform that helps real estate professionals identify motivated owners and automate outreach. It combines public-records data on more than 160 million properties and 318 million mortgages across 3,000+ counties with over 30 distress signals, skip tracing, and list building. Built-in campaigns reach owners by phone, text, email, direct mail, and online ads, with automated alerts and actions as property status changes. The company started with foreclosure tracking in 2007 and now serves investors, residential and commercial agents, mortgage professionals, and property service businesses through industry-specific playbooks.

✓ Pros

✓Deep public-records coverage with long-running foreclosure and distress data
✓Data and outreach live in one platform instead of separate list and mailing tools
✓Transparent published pricing with a 5-day free trial
✓Serves both residential and commercial prospecting use cases

✗ Cons

PropertyRadar Features

✦ Public-Records Database 160M+ properties and 318M+ mortgages across 3,000+ counties with owner information.
✦ Distress Signals Over 30 signals, including foreclosure tracking dating back to 2007, to surface motivated sellers.
✦ Skip Tracing Owner phone numbers and emails with searchable filters for list building.
✦ Multi-Channel Campaigns Reach owners via phone, text, email, direct mail, and online advertising.
✦ Automated Alerts & Actions Monitor properties and trigger outreach automatically when status changes.
✦ Industry Playbooks Prebuilt targeting playbooks for investors, agents, lenders, and home-service businesses.

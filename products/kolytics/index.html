About Kolytics

Kolytics is an investment research platform for listed real estate that consolidates REIT financial data, company models, and analytics into a single collaborative workspace. Pre-built financial models and assumptions let investors screen listed property companies in minutes instead of building spreadsheets by hand. Scenario analysis tools apply proprietary algorithms to evaluate performance at both the sector and individual REIT level. The platform is aimed at real estate investors, portfolio managers, and research professionals covering global listed property, and is delivered as a cloud application at app.kolytics.com by Kolytics Ltd.

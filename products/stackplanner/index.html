About StackPlanner

StackPlanner is cloud-based stacking plan software for commercial real estate teams that replaces spreadsheet-based occupancy tracking. Users import existing spreadsheet, lease, or portfolio data, and the platform turns it into color-coded, interactive visual stacking plans showing tenants, vacancies, and floor distribution building by building. A real-time dashboard tracks occupancy, lease expiries, square footage, and portfolio-wide trends, with alerts and notifications for upcoming renewals, expiries, and move-outs. Plans can be exported to PDF or Excel, and the platform includes contact management, multi-property views, role-based access, and CRM integration. It is aimed at asset managers, property managers, leasing teams, property owners, and brokers.

StackPlanner Features

✦ Interactive Stack View Color-coded visual stacking plans showing tenants, vacancies, and floor distribution, updated in real time.
✦ Multi-Property View Manage multiple buildings and compare occupancy trends across a portfolio in one place.
✦ PDF/Excel Export Export stacking plans for presentations and reporting.
✦ Lease & Expiry Management Track lease timelines, tenant details, and square footage; spot upcoming renewals and expiries.
✦ Alerts & Notifications Proactive notifications for upcoming expiries, renewals, and move-outs.
✦ Real-Time Dashboard Portfolio dashboard tracking occupancy rates, lease expiries, revenue indicators, and custom KPIs.
✦ Data Import & Sync Upload spreadsheet, lease, or portfolio data from existing workflows.
✦ Role-Based Access & Contacts Team collaboration with role-based access controls and built-in contact management.
✦ CRM Integration Connects stacking plan data with CRM workflows.

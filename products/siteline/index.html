About Siteline

Siteline is billing software for trade contractors that manages the pay application process from generation through collection. It produces pay apps on the custom forms required by specific general contractors, drawing on a large library of GC-specific pay app and lien waiver forms. Lien waivers, compliance documents, and change orders are tracked alongside each bill, with state-specific lien rights management. A/R reporting, collections management, and cash flow forecasting give finance leaders visibility into outstanding payments, and the platform integrates with accounting systems and GC payment portals.

✓ Pros

✓Purpose-built for subcontractor billing rather than general construction management
✓Large library of GC-specific pay app and lien waiver forms
✓Combines billing with lien rights, compliance, and A/R visibility

✗ Cons

✗No published pricing; demo required
✗Aimed at trade contractors, not owners, developers, or GCs
✗Focused on billing; not a full accounting or project management system

Siteline Features

✦ Pay App Generation Generates and submits payment applications on the custom forms each GC requires.
✦ Change Order Tracking Tracks change orders so billed amounts stay current.
✦ GC Portal Submission Submits through GC payment portals and accounting integrations.
✦ Lien Waiver Management Automates collection and tracking of lien waivers.
✦ Lien Rights Management State-specific tracking of lien deadlines and rights.
✦ Compliance Document Tracking Keeps insurance and compliance documents current per project.
✦ A/R Reporting Reporting and analytics on receivables across projects.
✦ Collections & Forecasting Collections management and cash flow forecasting for finance teams.

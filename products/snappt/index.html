About Snappt

Snappt is an AI-enabled fraud detection and verification platform for property management and tenant screening workflows. It authenticates application documents such as pay stubs and bank statements through metadata analysis to catch manipulated financials, backed by a dedicated Fraud Forensics team. Verification services span income (including non-traditional sources and real-time payroll connections), identity with biometric checks, employment, and rental payment history. The platform integrates with major property management systems including Entrata, RealPage, Yardi, and ResMan, and is SOC 2 Type II certified and Fair Housing compliant.

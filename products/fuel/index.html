About FUEL

Fuel CRE has been acquired by RealPage and is now part of the RealPage Investment Management solutions suite. The platform provides AI-powered, data-driven solutions for real estate investment management including asset investment management (AIM), data management services, financial modeling, business intelligence, investor management, and investment accounting. The combined platform offers a single source of truth across financial, operational, valuation, loan, and metric data with automated data collection and AI/ML-driven ingestion. RealPage Investment Management helps firms make confident decisions with portfolio-wide visibility and enhanced investor interactions.

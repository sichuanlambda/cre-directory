About Quarem

Quarem is a lease management platform based in Houston, TX that combines technology with dedicated client services for comprehensive lease administration. Unlike fully automated platforms, Quarem believes the best lease management strategy comes from technology and people working together. All core components — technology, client services, abstracting, and leadership — are under one roof, meaning clients work with real people who know their leases. Quarem provides lease abstraction, critical date management, payment tracking, and portfolio analytics.

✓ Pros

✓Combines technology with dedicated human client services
✓All operations under one roof — no outsourced call centers
✓Strong lease abstraction services
✓Personal relationship model preferred by many corporate tenants

✗ Cons

✗Pricing not publicly available
✗Service model may not scale as efficiently as pure SaaS
✗Smaller company with limited technology innovation vs. pure tech platforms
✗Houston-based — less geographic diversity in team

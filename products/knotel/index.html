About Knotel

Knotel is a flexible workspace provider offering headquarters-as-a-service and custom office space for companies. After rapid venture-fueled expansion, it filed for bankruptcy in January 2021 and its assets were acquired by Newmark Group. Newmark continues to operate Knotel as its flexible office and workspace brand. The company no longer operates independently but the brand and service remain active under Newmark.

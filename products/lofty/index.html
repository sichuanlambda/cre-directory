About Lofty

Lofty (formerly Chime) is an integrated real estate platform combining CRM, lead generation, IDX websites, and AI automation for agents, teams, and brokerages. Its agentic AI lineup includes a 24/7 Sales Agent that captures and converts leads into appointments, a Homeowner Agent that identifies likely sellers in an existing database, a Social Agent that manages social media activity, and Cowork, an AI business partner that handles tasks like contact imports and lead enrollment. Smart Plans run automated multi-step workflows that nurture leads across email, text, calls, and direct mail, alongside a power dialer and SEO-optimized IDX websites. Lofty also sells multi-channel lead generation services across Google, Facebook, and Microsoft ads. The platform is cloud-based with native iOS and Android apps plus a client-facing app.

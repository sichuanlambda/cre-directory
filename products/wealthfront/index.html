About Wealthfront

Wealthfront is a leading automated investment platform (robo-advisor) managing over $90 billion in client funds for 1.3+ million clients. While not CRE-specific, Wealthfront provides exposure to real estate through diversified portfolios that include REITs and real estate ETFs. The platform offers automated investing with portfolios built to each client's risk profile, a high-yield Cash Account with up to $8M in FDIC insurance, and both managed and self-directed investment options. Named the 'best overall robo-advisor' by Investopedia in 2025, Wealthfront has been simplifying personal finance since 2011 with low-cost, tax-optimized portfolio management.

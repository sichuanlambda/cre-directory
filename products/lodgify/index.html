About Lodgify

Lodgify is vacation rental software for hosts and small property managers, built around creating a direct-booking website with an integrated booking engine and payment processing. A channel manager syncs rates and availability with Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Expedia, and Google Vacation Rentals, while a unified inbox, automated guest communications, rate management, and housekeeping and task tools cover day-to-day operations. Plans are published in four tiers (Basic, Starter, Professional, Ultimate) starting at $13 per month, all with 0% booking fees on the top tier and a 7-day free trial with no credit card required.

✓ Pros

✓Strongest at direct-booking websites, reducing dependence on OTA commissions
✓Low published entry price with a 7-day free trial and no credit card required
✓Covers channel sync, messaging, and payments for small portfolios

✗ Cons

✗Lower-priced tiers add a booking fee on direct reservations
✗Less operational depth than enterprise short-term rental platforms
✗Website templates favor speed over full custom design

About CINC

CINC (Commissions Inc) is an all-in-one real estate lead generation and CRM platform designed for agents, teams, and brokerages. The platform provides a complete lead generation cycle from capture to close, managing over $30 million in annual ad spend across Google, Facebook, and Instagram on behalf of clients. CINC features IDX websites optimized for high GCI lead capture, behavioral messaging for automated follow-up, an AI add-on for identifying ready-to-convert leads, and the Etta home shopping app for enhanced buyer engagement. The platform automates lead nurturing through AutoTracks drip campaigns while providing training and masterminds for agent development.

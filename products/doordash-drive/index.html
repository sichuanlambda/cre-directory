About DoorDash Drive

DoorDash Drive is a last-mile delivery logistics platform from DoorDash that enables businesses to offer delivery services through DoorDash's network of drivers. Not CRE-specific, but relevant for commercial real estate properties with food service or retail tenants. DoorDash Drive provides white-label delivery capabilities for businesses to integrate delivery into their own ordering systems.

About Market Leader

Market Leader is a real estate CRM and marketing platform for residential agents and teams, known for pairing its software with a guaranteed volume of exclusive buyer and seller leads delivered directly into the CRM each month. The platform includes customizable IDX websites with MLS integration, automated lead nurturing, daily prioritized to-dos, marketing campaign automation, and a library of customizable marketing materials. Lead engagement features include behavioral tracking and listing alerts to keep prospects active. A mobile app provides real-time notifications for new leads along with contact information and lead insights.

✓ Pros

✓Guaranteed monthly volume of exclusive leads bundled with the software
✓Mature CRM with automated nurture and a large marketing library

✗ Cons

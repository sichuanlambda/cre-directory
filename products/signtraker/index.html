About SignTraker

SignTraker is cloud software for real estate sign post installation companies and the brokerages they serve. It manages online ordering, flexible scheduling, and multi-level inventory, and is built to scale from startup operations to hundreds of installations per day. Field crews use iOS, Android, and Windows mobile apps with image verification of installed panels and riders, while clients place orders through custom-hosted portals with team ordering and cost center support. The platform integrates with QuickBooks, calculates sales tax, and handles import/export of operational data. Pricing is not published on the site.

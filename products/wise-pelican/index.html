About Wise Pelican

Wise Pelican is a direct mail platform that lets real estate agents design, customize, and send postcards, letters, brochures, door hangers, and flyers with no minimum order and no long-term contracts. A drag-and-drop Express Editor and a library of pre-designed templates handle creative, while a built-in mailing list builder targets recipients by radius, home value, and equity. The platform supports automated seller valuation postcards, MLS integration, and real-time campaign tracking. Pricing is per piece: postcards cost $1.04 each including printing, postage, and mailing, and creating an account is free. Though rooted in real estate, it also serves other small businesses.

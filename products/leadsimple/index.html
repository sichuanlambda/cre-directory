About LeadSimple

LeadSimple is sales and operations software for property management companies, unifying leads, calls, processes, and work orders in one system. Its CRM provides lead pipelines, owner prospecting, and conversion playbooks with speed-to-lead routing and SLA escalation, while Phone + Inbox consolidates calls, texts, and email with recording and assignment. The Operations module automates workflows with SLAs, PMS-triggered processes, and automated owner communications, and a Maintenance module covers triage, dispatch, and tenant updates. An AI engine adds voice agents, lead scoring, intake triage, and message drafting, and the platform integrates with AppFolio, Buildium, Rentvine, Propertyware, and Rent Manager. Published pricing includes Phone + Inbox from $29/user/month, CRM from $99/month, Operations at $1.35/door/month, and an all-in Platform plan at $2.99/door/month, with 14-day free trials on most plans.

About iHomefinder

iHomefinder combines IDX property search, lead capture, CRM, and marketing automation into one platform for real estate agents, teams, brokerages, and website developers. Its IDX tools embed branded MLS property search into websites, while the CRM tracks lead activity and engagement. Marketing automation includes MLS-powered listing alerts, drip campaigns, and an AI content assistant for blog posts, social media, and email. A mobile app adds push notifications and daily task management, and plans scale from a single agent up to multi-agent teams with multiple MLS feeds.

✓ Pros

✓IDX, CRM, and marketing automation bundled in one subscription

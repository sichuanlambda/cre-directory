About SingleKey

SingleKey is a tenant screening and rent protection platform for landlords, property managers, and real estate agents. Screening reports combine Equifax and TransUnion credit data with eviction and public record searches, landlord and employment reference checks, AI-powered income document analysis, and identity verification with liveness checks. Beyond screening, SingleKey offers a rent guarantee that covers missed payments, lease break and property damage protection, and rent collection services. Digital lease signing and an enterprise platform for larger operators round out the product, and the service is strongest in the Canadian market.

✓ Pros

✓Combines screening with an actual rent guarantee, not just reports
✓Dual-bureau credit data (Equifax and TransUnion) in one report
✓Free account creation with pay-as-you-go reports suits small landlords

✗ Cons

✗Strongest coverage is Canadian; US depth is more limited
✗Guarantee and premium services add per-policy costs on top of screening
✗Not a full property management system - no accounting or maintenance

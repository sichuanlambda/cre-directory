About Accruent

Accruent is a comprehensive facilities, asset, and real estate management software platform serving organizations across healthcare, retail, education, government, and commercial real estate. The platform includes IWMS capabilities for space management, lease administration and accounting, capital planning, maintenance management, and IoT-based monitoring. Accruent's solutions help organizations optimize their physical resources, manage lease portfolios for compliance (ASC 842, IFRS 16), track assets throughout their lifecycle, and streamline facilities operations. Acquired by Fortive Corporation.

Accruent Features

✦ Lease Administration Centralized lease management with critical date tracking, clause management, and portfolio analytics.
✦ Lease Accounting ASC 842 and IFRS 16 compliance automation with journal entry generation and disclosure reporting.
✦ Space Management Floor plan visualization, space allocation, and occupancy tracking.
✦ Capital Planning Long-term capital expenditure planning with condition assessments and project prioritization.
✦ Maintenance Management Work order management, preventive maintenance scheduling, and vendor coordination.
✦ Asset Lifecycle Management Track assets from acquisition through disposal with condition monitoring.
✦ IoT Monitoring Real-time equipment monitoring with automated alerts and predictive maintenance.
✦ Regulatory Compliance Healthcare, education, and government-specific compliance tracking and documentation.
✦ Analytics & Dashboards Customizable dashboards and reports for operational and financial performance.
✦ Document Management Centralized document storage with workflow automation and audit trails.

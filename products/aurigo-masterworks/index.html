About Aurigo Masterworks

Aurigo Masterworks is an AI-native capital program management platform for planning, building, and maintaining capital infrastructure programs. It connects teams, data, and workflows across the full lifecycle, with modules for capital planning and portfolio management, federal aid reimbursement, construction project management, estimation and e-bidding, right-of-way acquisition, materials management, real property management, and asset condition management. The Lumina AI platform, an advanced workflow engine, analytics and reporting, document management, and mobile-native apps round out the suite. Masterworks is aimed at federal, state, and local government agencies, metropolitan planning organizations, and large owners running multiyear capital programs that require governance, project controls, and transparency. It is delivered as a cloud platform with enterprise-grade security.

✓ Pros

✓Purpose-built for public-sector capital programs, including federal aid workflows
✓Unusually broad scope spanning right-of-way, materials, and real property
✓AI capabilities delivered through the dedicated Lumina platform

✗ Cons

About Oracle Construction and Engineering

Oracle Construction and Engineering is a suite of software for construction, engineering, infrastructure, and capital project management, supporting planning, costs, contracts, documentation, collaboration, and project controls. Its core products are Primavera P6 EPPM, one of the most widely used enterprise planning and scheduling tools; Oracle Primavera Cloud, which covers planning and scheduling plus resource, risk, portfolio management, and capital planning; Primavera Unifier for project and asset lifecycle processes connecting capital delivery with downstream operations; and Oracle Aconex for construction document management and project collaboration. The products integrate with each other and with Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP as well as other finance, procurement, and business intelligence systems. The suite is used on major capital projects by owners, contractors, and engineering firms worldwide.

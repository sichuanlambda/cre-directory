About Sertifi

Sertifi, now part of Flywire, is the leading digital agreements and payment platform for the hospitality industry. While not exclusively CRE-focused, Sertifi powers electronic signatures, payment processing, and credit card authorizations for over 20,000 unique hospitality locations across 150+ countries. The platform is the digital authorizations provider for all Marriott properties worldwide and brand standard for Hilton, Hyatt, Accor, Sonesta, and Red Roof Inn. Sertifi is also a strategic partner to major hospitality management companies including Aimbridge, Highgate, and Hersha, and exclusive provider for Topgolf, Dave & Busters, and Main Event.

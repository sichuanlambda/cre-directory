About LeaseUp

LeaseUp is a deal platform for tenant representation brokerage teams that streamlines survey building, touring, and client collaboration. Teams create digital surveys and market reports from imported CSV market data, generate automated PDF reports on custom templates, and share everything through a white-labeled client portal available on web, PDF, and mobile, including mobile touring. The platform adds centralized collaboration, deal flow analytics, and CRM integrations. The site cites customers at firms including JLL, Cresa, Lee & Associates, Colliers, Savills, Transwestern, and Cushman & Wakefield. Published pricing starts at $100 per user per month on the Team plan with no long-term commitment, with custom enterprise contracts for larger firms.

✓ Pros

About CRE Retail Maps

CRE Retail Maps is cloud-based mapping software for commercial real estate brokers who need presentation-quality retail maps for offering memorandums, lease proposals, and flyers. Users search for brand artwork, place logos on a map, and auto-discover nearby businesses by category such as restaurants, banks, grocery, and fitness. Maps can be customized with nine map styles, custom color schemes, text overlays, and shape drawing tools, then exported in five PDF layouts or as high-resolution images up to 8000 pixels. It targets landlord reps, tenant rep brokers, investment sales professionals, and brokerage marketing teams. Pricing is $39/month or $379/year, and a free trial allows building and previewing up to three maps without payment.

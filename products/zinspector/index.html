About zInspector

zInspector, from Scaled Tech Inc., is property inspection software for property managers, maintenance teams, contractors, and independent inspectors, spanning portfolios from a handful of units to enterprise scale. Its zAssistant AI lets field staff dictate observations while walking a property and automatically assembles reports with photos and conditions, while the zTenant app guides residents through move-in, move-out, and periodic self-inspections with digital signatures. The platform includes a PM Desk operations hub that connects inspections to workflows, centralized dashboards, zData analytics, 360-degree camera support, and access to the OnSight PROS third-party inspector network. It runs in the cloud with native iOS and Android apps. A free tier is available for portfolios under 5 units, and all core features are free to try for 15 days without a credit card.

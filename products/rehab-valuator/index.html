About Rehab Valuator

Rehab Valuator is integrated software for analyzing real estate deals, estimating rehab costs, marketing wholesale properties, raising funding, and managing construction projects. It calculates max offers, ARV, and profitability across exit strategies including wholesale, fix-and-flip, BRRRR buy-and-hold, commercial, and ground-up development, with nationwide sales and rental comps plus owner contact data. Project tools cover rehab and new-construction budgets, scopes of work, drag-and-drop scheduling, expense tracking, and budget-versus-actual monitoring, while marketing tools generate wholesale deal flyers and lender-ready funding presentations. A free Lite tier covers basic deal analysis; Premium runs $49/month and Pro $99/month for up to 6 users, with a $1 two-week Premium trial.

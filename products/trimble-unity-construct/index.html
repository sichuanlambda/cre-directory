About Trimble Unity Construct (e-Builder)

Trimble Unity Construct is an enterprise solution for capital program and construction project management, powered by the capabilities of Trimble e-Builder and positioned as its next evolution in digital project delivery. Built for capital program owners, it spans capital planning, funding allocation, bidding, business process automation, schedule management, document management, cost management, and asset handover. Reports and dashboards give owners portfolio-wide visibility across design teams, construction teams, and stakeholders through every project phase. The platform is cloud-based and offered across multiple regional instances, including US regions, GovCloud, and Canada. Trimble states the goal as reducing risk, enhancing collaboration, and improving outcomes across digital project delivery.

✓ Pros

✓Built on e-Builder, one of the longest-established owner-side capital program platforms
✓Covers the full owner lifecycle from capital planning through asset handover
✓Regional cloud instances including GovCloud for public-sector requirements

✗ Cons

✗No published pricing; enterprise sales process
✗Owner-focused scope; general contractors need separate field tools

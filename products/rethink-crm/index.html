About Rethink CRM by Buildout

Rethink is a commercial real estate CRM owned by Buildout, which acquired the company in February 2021. It is built on the Salesforce platform and covers contact management and activity tracking, deal pipeline management, a property and listing database with comps tracking, dashboards, branded client activity reports and map-based search of CRM data, with a marketing integration to Buildout Showcase. Rethink+, launched in 2025, adds property ownership data, intelligent property and owner search, AI owner research, predictive analytics and property records fueled by Prospect by Buildout, plus 10 hours of premium support. Buildout publishes pricing of $129 per month for Rethink and $299 per month for Rethink+ and provides onboarding that imports contacts, companies, properties and historical deals. It is used by individual brokers, teams and brokerages across investment sales, leasing, tenant rep and capital markets. The old rethinkcrm.com domain no longer belongs to the product.

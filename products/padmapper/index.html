About PadMapper

PadMapper was a rental apartment and house search platform that aggregated listings from multiple sources into an interactive map-based search experience. PadMapper was acquired by Zumper and its functionality has been integrated into the Zumper platform. The PadMapper brand is no longer independently operational. Former PadMapper users are redirected to Zumper for apartment and rental search.

About Key Data

Key Data provides performance analytics and benchmarking for the short-term rental and lodging industry, combining verified reservation data from more than 80 direct property management system integrations with hotel benchmarks and OTA market coverage from Airbnb and Vrbo. Dashboards track occupancy, revenue, pacing, and booking-window metrics against market comp sets, backed by more than five years of historical booking data and AI-powered forecasting. Products include ProData for property manager portfolio benchmarking, DestinationData for destination marketing and tourism organizations, and EnterpriseData custom feeds delivered via API, flat files, or S3. The company states its dataset draws on 17,000+ property managers, 10M+ vacation rental properties, and 4.5M+ hotel rooms.

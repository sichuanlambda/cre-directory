About Testfit

TestFit is a real estate feasibility platform that enables developers, architects, and brokers to rapidly test-fit building configurations on sites. The platform automates site planning by allowing users to map parcels, configure road layouts, and generate 3D building massing in hours instead of days. TestFit provides real-time financial analysis, automatic takeoffs for parking, infrastructure, and earthwork costs, and the ability to compare multiple design schemes instantly. With 6,200+ users, TestFit helps teams produce 2-3x more design iterations, validate pro formas against actual layouts, and make confident investment decisions 30x faster than traditional methods.

Testfit Features

✦ Parcel Mapping Map parcels or draw custom boundaries for any building typology.
✦ Road Layout Configuration Build site layouts with customizable roads, turning radius, and topography data.
✦ 3D Visualization Visualize site plans in 3D context for faster stakeholder approvals.
✦ Multi-Typology Support Plan multifamily, office, retail, industrial, and mixed-use developments.
✦ Pro Forma Validation Validate financial pro formas against actual physical layouts before design begins.
✦ Real-Time Deal Prototyping Compare design schemes instantly with live financial analysis.
✦ Automatic Takeoffs Auto-generate cost takeoffs for parking, infrastructure, and earthwork.
✦ Unit Mix Optimization Test different unit mixes and parking ratios to maximize site value.
✦ AI-Optimized Solutions Generate optimized building configurations instantly, then refine to match design intent.
✦ Zoning Compliance Test against zoning requirements including setbacks, height limits, and FAR.
✦ Highest and Best Use Analysis Analyze maximum development potential for any parcel.
✦ Revit Export Push approved layouts directly to Revit for detailed design and documentation.
✦ Multi-Format Export Export to AutoCAD, SketchUp, Excel, or PDF for consultant collaboration.
✦ Live Feasibility Reports Share interactive feasibility reports and markups with stakeholders.

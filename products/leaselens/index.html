About LeaseLens

LeaseLens is an AI-powered commercial lease analysis service for tenants and tenant-rep brokers. Users upload a lease PDF and get a free instant preview (a lease grade, risk flag count, and one detailed finding), then can purchase the full PDF report for $75. Reports check every clause against 14 risk categories and identify 15+ risk flag types, including uncapped CAM charges, personal guarantee scope, holdover rates, rent escalations, and renewal notice deadlines, quoting the exact clause language and suggesting negotiation changes. The report also calculates year-by-year occupancy costs and total personal guarantee exposure, and detects prompt-injection tampering hidden in PDF documents. A $199/month plan gives brokers unlimited analyses across their client base, and the site offers a free 10-question lease-risk quiz, a sample report, and a money-back guarantee.

LeaseLens Features

✦ 14 Risk Category Review Every clause is checked against 14 risk categories, flagging 15+ risk types such as uncapped CAM, personal guarantees, and holdover traps.
✦ Quoted Clause Findings Findings quote the exact lease language and rate it Critical/High/Medium/Low with specific negotiation recommendations.
✦ Financial Exposure Math Calculates year-by-year occupancy costs, escalation impact, and total personal guarantee exposure over the lease term.
✦ Free Instant Preview Upload a PDF and see a lease grade, risk flag count, and one finding before paying.
✦ Structured PDF Report A shareable report with critical dates, financial breakdown, rights and restrictions, renewal/exit options, and a plain-English summary, delivered by email in minutes.
✦ Document Integrity Check Detects hidden prompt-injection text and tampered PDFs and flags them as a high-severity risk; no lease data stored.

About Comp Crunch

Comp Crunch is a Chrome extension that allows real estate professionals to easily analyze and export Zillow search results. The extension provides unlimited CSV and PDF exports of Zillow data, market overview statistics for quick market understanding, and sale price trend visualizations to understand market conditions over time. Comp Crunch saves time by enabling one-click data extraction from Zillow searches, including rental market data for investors. The extension is popular with real estate agents, appraisers, and investors.

✓ Pros

✓Simple one-click Zillow data export
✓CSV and PDF format options
✓Market statistics and trend visualization

✗ Cons

✗Dependent on Zillow—only works with Zillow data
✗Chrome extension only—no standalone platform
✗Zillow may change access policies

Comp Crunch Features

✦ CSV/PDF Export Export Zillow search results to CSV or PDF with one click.
✦ Market Statistics Quick market overview statistics from Zillow search data.
✦ Price Trends Sale price trend visualizations over time.

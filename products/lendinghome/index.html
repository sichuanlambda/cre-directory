About LendingHome

LendingHome, now rebranded as Kiavi, is a technology-powered lending platform providing financing for real estate investors. Kiavi leverages cutting-edge technology for faster, simpler access to fix-and-flip, rental, and new construction loans. With a 4.8/5 star rating, the platform serves non-owner-occupied investment properties across 40+ US states plus Washington D.C. Kiavi has funded billions in real estate investment loans, offering competitive rates and a streamlined digital process that eliminates traditional lending hassles.

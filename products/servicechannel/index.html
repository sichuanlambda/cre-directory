About ServiceChannel

ServiceChannel is a facilities management platform for multi-location businesses that coordinates maintenance, compliance, and service provider networks across distributed sites. It automates work order management, tracks assets and equipment, and monitors refrigerant compliance, with analytics and reporting for strategic decisions. A service provider marketplace lets facilities teams source contractors based on performance data, and a facilities-management-as-a-service option is available for teams that want managed operations. Founded in 1999 and owned by Fortive, the company reports serving more than 600 brands globally across retail, restaurants, grocery, hospitality, healthcare, education, and financial services.

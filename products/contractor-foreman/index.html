About Contractor Foreman

Contractor Foreman is an all-in-one construction management suite positioned as an easy, affordable alternative for small and mid-size general, trade, and service contractors. It bundles project management (scheduling, daily logs, inspections, permits, work orders, punch lists, and a client portal) with financial tools including estimates, invoices, change orders, purchase orders, bid management, and takeoffs backed by a cost database. People and document modules cover crew scheduling, time cards, team chat, safety meetings, incident tracking, submittals, PDF markup, and forms, alongside a built-in CRM. Integrations include QuickBooks, Stripe, Gusto, CompanyCam, Zapier, Microsoft Project, and Google and Outlook calendars. Pricing starts at $49 per month with a 30-day free trial and a 100-day money-back guarantee.

About Dodge Construction Network

Dodge Construction Network provides construction market intelligence and project data, letting users discover commercial construction projects early in their lifecycle - before they hit bid boards - with specs, contacts, and lead time to pursue the work. Its products include Dodge One, The Blue Book, Bid Board, Sweets, IMS, and Principia, alongside market forecasting and analysis by segment and geography. The company tracks more than 700,000 unique projects annually and holds over 10 million archived plans and specifications built on 50+ years of historical construction data. The cloud platform offers CRM integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Microsoft Dynamics, a mobile app, and API and data feeds for custom integration. It serves building product manufacturers, suppliers, distributors, contractors, architects, and engineers.

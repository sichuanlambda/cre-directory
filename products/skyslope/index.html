About SkySlope

SkySlope is a real estate transaction management platform built around broker compliance, with document management, customized smart checklists, automated auditing, and analytics that flag missing paperwork and deal issues early. The suite spans digital forms (SkySlope Forms), e-signature (DigiSign), offer management (Offers), disclosures (Breeze), transaction coordination services (SkyTC), and a back-office accounting and commissions product (Books). Differentiators include unique transaction email addresses for automatic email logging, file sharing for brokerage collaboration, and auto-population that pulls matching data in from the MLS. It serves brokerages from small firms to large enterprises that need transaction management, back-office accounting, and audit-ready recordkeeping in one platform. Pricing is not published; brokerages request a demo.

SkySlope Features

✦ Smart Checklists & Auditing Customized checklists with automated auditing and analytics that surface missing documents and issues early.
✦ Document Management Audit-ready document storage with unique per-transaction email addresses for automatic email logging.
✦ Broker Review File sharing and review workflows for brokerage-wide collaboration and compliance sign-off.
✦ SkySlope Forms & Breeze Digital forms and disclosures with auto-fill that updates matching fields and pulls data from the MLS.

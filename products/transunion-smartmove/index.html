About TransUnion SmartMove

TransUnion SmartMove is a tenant screening service powered by TransUnion, one of the three major credit bureaus. The platform provides landlords with comprehensive tenant background checks including credit reports, criminal history, eviction records, and income verification. SmartMove is designed for independent landlords who want quick, reliable screening without needing a business account or subscription. Reports are available within minutes and can be paid for by either the landlord or the applicant. The service leverages TransUnion's massive consumer database for accurate, up-to-date screening data and is widely integrated with property management platforms.

TransUnion SmartMove Features

✦ Credit Reports Full TransUnion credit report with credit score and payment history.
✦ Criminal Background Check National criminal database search covering all 50 states.
✦ Eviction History Nationwide eviction records from court databases.
✦ Income Insights Income verification using TransUnion data to validate applicant earnings.
✦ ResidentScore Proprietary scoring model designed specifically for rental risk assessment.
✦ OFAC Check Screening against the Office of Foreign Assets Control terrorism watchlist.
✦ Recommendation Engine Accept/review/deny recommendations based on screening results.
✦ Instant Results Reports available within minutes of applicant completion.
✦ Applicant-Pays Option Applicants can pay for their own screening to save landlords money.
✦ No Subscription Required Pay-per-use model with no monthly fees or long-term commitments.

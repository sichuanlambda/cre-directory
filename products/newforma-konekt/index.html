About Newforma Konekt

Newforma Konekt is the cloud-based project information management (PIM) platform from Newforma, built for architecture, engineering, and construction teams. It centralizes project email, files, RFIs, submittals, and contract changes to create a single connected project record. A built-in 2D/3D viewer supports BIM coordination and issue tracking across models and drawings, alongside document control, aggregate search, construction administration workflows, and a mobile companion app. Konekt connects to tools such as Procore through Newforma's app marketplace, and sits alongside the server-based Newforma Project Center for firms that need on-premises deployment.

✓ Pros

✓Creates a single connected record spanning email, files, RFIs, submittals, and model issues
✓Serves architects, engineers, contractors, and owners with role-specific workflows
✓Vendor also offers on-premises Newforma Project Center for firms that cannot use cloud

✗ Cons

✗No published pricing; a demo is required to evaluate cost
✗Broad AEC platform that can be more than small CRE teams need

Newforma Konekt Features

✦ Communication & Email Management Files project email and correspondence against the project record for a single source of truth.
✦ Document Control & Aggregate Search Centralized file management with search across project documents, email, and records.
✦ Contract Change Management Tracks contract changes as part of the connected project record.
✦ RFI & Submittal Management Manages RFIs and submittals through review and response workflows.
✦ BIM Coordination & 2D/3D Viewer Views models and drawings in 2D/3D and coordinates issues across disciplines.
✦ Mobile Companion App Access to project information and workflows from the field.
✦ App Marketplace Connectors to tools such as Procore, with a Microsoft Teams integration announced.
✦ Data Analytics as a Service Analytics offering built on centralized project information.

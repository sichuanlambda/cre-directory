About InEight

InEight is an integrated project controls and management platform for complex capital construction, unifying scope, cost, schedule, and execution data across the project lifecycle from planning through completion. Modules span project controls (Control, Contract, Change, Plan & Progress), pricing and scheduling (Estimate, Schedule), project information management (Document, Model), and construction intelligence (Report, Explore), plus billings, completions, compliance, and design. It serves capital project owners, EPCs, self-perform contractors, design-builders, and consultants across transportation, power and renewables, nuclear, water, oil and gas, and mining. Enterprise pricing is custom-quoted by users and products, and per-user monthly or annual subscriptions are available through InEight NOW.

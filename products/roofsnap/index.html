About RoofSnap

RoofSnap is the #1 roofing software platform designed for small crews and solo roofers, providing everything needed to measure, estimate, and get paid fast. The platform delivers accurate roof, gutter, and lighting measurements with detailed reports in as little as 30 minutes, an estimation suite for creating professional Good/Better/Best quotes, and integrated payment processing powered by Stripe. RoofSnap's measurement reports include detailed diagrams and waste calculations for asphalt roofs, gutter material bins with downspout placement, and lighting-specific reports for eaves and rakes. Rated 4.5/5 stars, the platform also includes a Draw-It-Yourself sketch tool, contract creation, and professional business documents.

RoofSnap Features

✦ Standard Measurement Reports Detailed roof measurements with diagrams and waste calculations, delivered in under 4 hours.
✦ Gutter Measurements Reports with material bins for end caps, miters, and downspout placement suggestions.
✦ Lighting Reports Focused reports for eaves and rakes for lighting layout planning.
✦ Rush Orders Receive measurement reports in less than 30 minutes with rush delivery.
✦ Instant Estimates Generate Good/Better/Best pricing based on materials and measurements instantly.
✦ Detailed Estimate Builder Customize line items, apply markups or discounts, and control margins.
✦ Professional Documents Branded estimates and contracts with company logo, photos, and notes.
✦ Contract Creation Create professional roofing contracts ready for client signing.
✦ Payment Processing Collect credit card (2.9% + $0.30) or ACH (1%) payments via Stripe.
✦ Draw-It-Yourself Tool In-app sketch tool for unlimited DIY measurements without site visits.

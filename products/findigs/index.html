About Findigs

Findigs is an end-to-end rental application platform that automates screening, underwriting, and lease decisioning for property operators. Applications are automatically approved or declined against configurable risk policies covering credit, income ratios, employment history, and eviction history, with human escalation specialists handling fraud and complex cases. Screening combines identity verification, income and employment verification, document verification, and fraud detection. Portfolio-level reporting covers decisioning, leasing, and payment analytics, and operators can tune policies against lease performance data. The platform is SOC 2 Type II certified and FCRA-compliant, and serves multifamily, single-family, build-to-rent, and student housing operators.

Findigs Features

✦ Identity Verification Verifies applicant identity as part of the application flow.
✦ Income & Employment Verification Confirms income and employment from source data rather than applicant-supplied claims alone.
✦ Document Fraud Detection Flags manipulated or fraudulent application documents, with escalation to fraud specialists.
✦ Policy-Based Decisioning Automatically approves or declines applications against configurable risk policies (credit score, income ratio, employment, eviction history).
✦ Policy Optimization Tunes screening policies against actual lease performance data.
✦ Portfolio Analytics Portfolio-level decisioning, leasing, and payment reporting.
✦ 24/7 Human Support Around-the-clock support team with escalation specialists for complex or flagged cases.

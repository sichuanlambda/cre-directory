About Cherre

Cherre is a real estate data management and intelligence platform that connects, cleans, and unifies data from 150+ sources for asset managers and investment managers. The platform ingests data from internal systems and external providers, normalizes it through a Universal Data Model, and delivers validated, enriched data for analytics, AI, and reporting. Cherre's Agent STUDIO enables users to build AI agents for automating CRE workflows. With 100+ petabytes of data and 3.5B+ connected data points, Cherre powers data-driven decision-making for some of the largest real estate investors globally. Raised $30M Series C in 2024.

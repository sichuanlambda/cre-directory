About Intex

Intex Solutions provides cashflow modeling and data analytics software for global securitization markets, including CMBS, RMBS, ABS, and CLO securities. The company maintains what it describes as the most accurate, independent, and complete cashflow model library, with models for over 50,000 deals used to analyze and value structured securities. Its models and data are accessible through user interface platforms, client-resident and web-service APIs, and Excel plug-ins, supporting trading, research, portfolio management, risk management, and deal structuring workflows. For commercial real estate professionals, the CMBS deal library underpins bond and loan-level analysis by investors and issuers. Founded in 1985, Intex is privately held and based in Boston, with offices in London and Shanghai.

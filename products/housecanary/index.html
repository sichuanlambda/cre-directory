About HouseCanary

HouseCanary is the most accurate AI-powered residential real estate data and analytics platform, covering 136M+ properties across the US. It provides instant property valuations (AVMs), CMAs, market forecasts, and investment analytics for single-family rental operators, mortgage lenders, investment banks, and prop-tech companies. HouseCanary's AI algorithms analyze data from thousands of sources with image recognition for property condition assessment. Six of the top 10 SFR REIT operators and major mortgage lenders rely on HouseCanary. The platform also includes a 50-state brokerage and digital banking/customer engagement tools.

HouseCanary Features

✦ Automated Valuation Models (AVMs) Industry-leading AVMs providing instant property valuations across 136M+ US properties.
✦ CMA Generation Automated comparable market analysis with adjustable comp selection.
✦ Property Data Comprehensive property characteristics, ownership, transaction history, and tax records.
✦ Image Recognition AI that identifies room types and assesses property condition from photos.
✦ Market Forecasts Block-level price forecasts and market trend predictions powered by machine learning.
✦ Investment Analytics Rental yield estimation, cap rates, and investment scoring for SFR portfolios.
✦ Risk Scoring Property and market risk assessment including crime, climate, and economic factors.
✦ Neighborhood Intelligence Granular neighborhood data including demographics, schools, and walkability.
✦ HouseCanary Platform 50-state property search platform with customizable investment criteria and filters.
✦ Portfolio Analysis Bulk property analysis and portfolio-level valuation for institutional investors.
✦ API & Data Feeds Well-documented API for integrating property data and valuations into applications.
✦ Origination Support Property data and valuations integrated into mortgage origination workflows.
✦ ComeHome Platform Digital banking and customer engagement platform for mortgage lenders.
✦ Portfolio Monitoring Ongoing property value monitoring for loan servicing and risk management.

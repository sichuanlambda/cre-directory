About Rabbu

Rabbu provides free short-term rental data tools that help investors evaluate Airbnb potential before buying. Its Airbnb Calculator estimates revenue and returns for any US address, projecting occupancy, average daily rate (ADR), revenue per available night (RevPAN), and seasonalized monthly revenue. Market-level Airbnb data pages cover occupancy rates, ADR, supply growth, and seasonal trends, with an ROI Score for quickly comparing markets on revenue potential, pricing, and demand. Data is built from live Airbnb and short-term rental listings, historical performance, and real estate indicators, and a Chrome extension brings projections into listing browsing. It fits buyers evaluating STR markets, agents comparing Airbnb potential, and operators planning expansion.

✓ Pros

✓Core calculator and market data are free to use
✓Address-level projections with seasonality, not just metro averages
✓Chrome extension integrates analysis into listing browsing

✗ Cons

✗Limited to short-term rental analysis; no long-term rental or commercial coverage
✗Projections are estimates from listing data and should be validated before purchase

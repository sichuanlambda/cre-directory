About Giraffe

Giraffe is a collaborative, map-based design platform for real estate development that combines GIS data, 3D massing, and feasibility analysis in one workspace. Users sketch schemes with drawing tools and see instant metrics as designs change, layering spatial data onto each site for analysis and selection. The platform covers site analysis, generative design, and real estate feasibility, with workflow automation and AI-enabled analysis across a portfolio of sites. It serves developers, designers, corporate real estate teams, and governments working on projects from masterplans and commercial buildings to data centers and renewable energy sites. An SDK supports custom apps and integrations, and the platform is SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified.

About Rentals369

Rentals369 is cloud-based property management software for independent landlords and small property management teams. It tracks rent with automatic late-fee application, manages maintenance requests with vendor dispatch, and stores documents with audit trails. Tenant and vendor portals are included at no extra cost, and optional add-ons provide QuickBooks Online synchronization and email sync. Pricing is usage-based: a base plan at $19.99/month includes the first 2 retained tenant records, with additional records priced on a declining per-record tier (from $5.99 down to $0.79 per record at higher volumes). The site offers a money-back guarantee and a live pricing calculator.

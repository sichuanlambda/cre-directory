About Mytraffic

Mytraffic is a geospatial analytics platform, merged with Geoblink, that provides footfall and location intelligence across 18 countries in Europe plus the United States. Its products include Gini, an AI assistant for geospatial analytics; DataLibrary for alternative data; AudienceLabs for digital-to-in-store attribution; and SmartMonitor for shopping center analytics. Teams use it for footfall pattern analysis, competitor benchmarking, location suitability assessment, market gap identification, and cannibalization risk evaluation. The platform serves retailers, restaurant and franchise brands, commercial real estate professionals, cities, and advertisers, with more than 600 client companies including Carrefour, Decathlon, Sephora, and Goldman Sachs.

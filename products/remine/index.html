About Remine

Remine is a residential real estate data and analytics platform that combines public records, MLS data, and consumer behavior data to give agents and lenders predictive prospecting tools such as Buy and Sell Scores. After years of losses under its MLS-consortium owners, real estate tech company Place bought Remine at auction in April 2025. The platform remains live at remine.com under Place ownership.

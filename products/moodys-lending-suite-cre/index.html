About Moody's Lending Suite for CRE

Moody's Lending Suite for CRE packages Moody's origination workflow, underwriting, and risk monitoring tools for commercial real estate lenders, mainly banks. Lenders can run cash flow analysis, underwriting, scoring, and stress testing on CRE deals, then monitor risk at both the loan and portfolio level after closing. Monitoring includes covenant compliance tracking, early warning models and signals, and refinance risk tools, which are relevant as loans approach maturity. The suite draws on Moody's analytics and market data, and Moody's describes generative AI features for evaluating loans and watching CRE portfolios. Moody's previously marketed CRE origination and monitoring through its CreditLens platform, which added a CRE module in 2019.

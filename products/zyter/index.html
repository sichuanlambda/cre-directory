About Zyter

Zyter is a technology company that once offered TruIOT smart building solutions, including space management, occupancy analytics, air quality monitoring, and energy management for commercial buildings. The company has since pivoted away from smart spaces entirely and now operates at zyter.ai, focused on AI-driven workflow orchestration for regulated industries, primarily healthcare. It no longer markets CRE or smart building products.

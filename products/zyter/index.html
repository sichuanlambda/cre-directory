About Zyter

Zyter is a digital health and smart spaces technology platform that provides IoT-enabled solutions for healthcare and commercial buildings. Zyter TrueIOT offers smart building solutions including space management, occupancy analytics, indoor air quality monitoring, and energy management. While not exclusively CRE, Zyter's smart building technology is relevant to commercial real estate operations and tenant experience management.

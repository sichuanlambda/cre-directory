About Curb Hero

Curb Hero is a digital open house sign-in app that replaces paper sheets with QR-code visitor capture. Sign-in pages use branded, customizable templates, work offline, and push leads to more than 2,000 CRMs through direct integrations and Zapier. Beyond sign-in, the product generates listing microsites optimized for social sharing, reusable dynamic QR codes for marketing materials, lead verification that appends background information, and digital disclosures and waivers. Solo agents use it free, with premium plans adding team and brokerage coordination, and lenders and builders use it for co-branded sign-in.

About ResMan

ResMan is a cloud-based property management software platform designed for multifamily operators and their unique needs. The platform provides an all-in-one solution for accounting, reporting, traffic management, leasing, and unit information management. ResMan integrates with payment processing platforms like Paylease for resident portal functionality. The platform is known for being easy to navigate with comprehensive features that save property management companies thousands in bookkeeping fees. ResMan serves property management companies managing conventional, affordable, and student housing portfolios, delivering tools for financial reporting, lease management, and resident communication.

ResMan Features

✦ Property Accounting Full accounting system with AP/AR, general ledger, and bank reconciliation.
✦ Financial Reporting Comprehensive reports for owners, management, and compliance.
✦ Budget Management Create and track budgets across properties and portfolios.
✦ Owner Reporting Automated owner statements and distribution management.
✦ Traffic Management Track prospects, tours, and applications through the leasing pipeline.
✦ Online Leasing Digital lease creation and e-signature for streamlined move-ins.
✦ ILS Syndication Push listings to major apartment listing services.
✦ Screening Integrated tenant screening for background and credit checks.
✦ Work Order Management Create, assign, and track maintenance work orders.
✦ Unit Management Track unit status, availability, and make-ready processes.
✦ Resident Portal Online portal for residents to pay rent and submit requests.
✦ Compliance Tools Tools for affordable housing compliance and reporting.

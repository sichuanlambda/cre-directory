About RealNex

RealNex is the most complete integrated software suite for commercial real estate brokers, combining CRM, financial analysis, presentation/marketing, transaction management, and marketplace in one platform. RealNex Navigator includes a market-leading CRM with RX Data (property records, ownership, loan details, tenant data), predictive analytics for identifying likely buyers/sellers, investment and lease analysis engine, elegant presentation builder with 60+ templates, collaborative transaction management, and a dynamic marketplace with virtual deal rooms. Used by individual brokers through multi-office brokerages.

RealNex Features

✦ RealNex CRM Full-featured CRM for managing relationships, properties, and deals across desktop, cloud, and mobile.
✦ RX Data Property records with ownership, loan details (lender, amount, rate, maturity), and recent transaction history.
✦ NavigatorPRO Predictive Analytics AI-powered analysis of buying history, hold cycles, and loan maturities to identify likely buyers and sellers.
✦ Tenant Database Known tenants listed at property addresses for prospecting and market intelligence.
✦ Investment Analysis Complete investment analysis engine with DCF, IRR, cash-on-cash, and cap rate calculations.
✦ Lease Analysis Comprehensive lease analysis comparing tenant and landlord perspectives.
✦ BOV Generator Broker Opinion of Value reports generated from CRM property data.
✦ MarketEdge Presentation Builder Create branded flyers, proposals, OMs, and BOVs from 60+ professional templates.
✦ eMarketing Campaigns Email marketing campaigns targeted to specific buyer/tenant profiles from CRM data.
✦ Listing Syndication Distribute listings to up to 20 syndication sites directly from the platform.
✦ Virtual Deal Rooms Private, secure deal rooms with automated NDA and 7 levels of security access control.
✦ Transaction Manager Online portal and mobile app for tour management, client collaboration, and deal tracking.
✦ MarketPlace Dynamic marketplace for marketing properties and driving buyer traffic to deal rooms.

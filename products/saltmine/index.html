About Saltmine

Saltmine is a modern workplace planning and design platform built by architects and real estate professionals for corporate real estate (CRE) teams. The platform enables space programming, test fitting, visualization, and project delivery with intelligence woven throughout. Saltmine helps CRE teams plan workspaces 70-90% faster, reduce change orders by 5-10%, and optimize space use by 3-5%. A global financial services firm used Saltmine to test-fit 10 sites in a single week, and a global insurance firm avoided a planned expansion by identifying 4% latent capacity—saving over $6M in rent. The platform offers unlimited users with no special skills required and is SOC 2 compliant.

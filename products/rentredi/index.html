About RentRedi

RentRedi is an all-in-one property management platform designed for landlords of all sizes, from first-time owners to investors with large portfolios. The platform is the official property management software for BiggerPockets' 3 million landlords and provides exclusive member access for NAR's 1.5 million realtors. RentRedi was recognized on the Inc. 5000 list of fastest-growing companies for two consecutive years. Key features include AI-powered onboarding, rent collection via ACH, card, and cash at 90,000+ retail locations, TransUnion tenant screening, and maintenance management with photo/video requests. RentRedi manages $34B in assets with 99% on-time rent payments using autopay.

RentRedi Features

✦ Multi-Method Payments Collect rent via ACH, credit/debit card, or cash at 90,000+ retail locations.
✦ Autopay Automated recurring payments achieving 99% on-time rent collection rate.
✦ Instant Notifications Real-time payment notifications, deposit tracking, and automatic receipts.
✦ Late Fee Automation Automatic late fees based on customizable rules and grace periods.
✦ TransUnion Screening Credit, criminal, and eviction checks with verified reports paid by applicants.
✦ Online Applications Digital rental applications with instant processing.
✦ Lease Management Create, sign, and store leases with built-in e-signature.
✦ Listing Syndication Publish listings to major rental websites to fill vacancies faster.
✦ Maintenance Management Tenants submit photo/video maintenance requests via the app.
✦ Partner Repair Network Coordinate repairs through RentRedi's network of service providers.
✦ Accounting Dashboard Track income, expenses, and financial performance across your portfolio.
✦ AI-Powered Onboarding Guided setup for adding properties, connecting banks, and inviting tenants.
✦ Mobile-First Design Full-featured landlord and tenant mobile apps for iOS and Android.
✦ 24/7 Live Chat Support Round-the-clock customer support via live chat.

About EliseAI

EliseAI is an agentic AI platform that automates communications and operational workflows for housing operators, handling messages, calls, and tasks across voice, SMS, email, and web chat. The AI schedules tours, answers complex prospect questions, and follows up with leads, and extends into resident workflows such as maintenance request automation, renewals, delinquency management, and move-in assistance. It includes EliseCRM and integrates with operators' existing systems, and the company also offers healthcare modules for scheduling, reminders, and intake. EliseAI targets multifamily, single-family rental, student housing, and affordable housing operators, and is built by Elise A.I. Technologies Corp. in New York.

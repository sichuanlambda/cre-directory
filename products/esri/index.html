About Esri

Esri develops ArcGIS, a comprehensive geospatial platform that integrates and connects data through the context of geography. ArcGIS Online provides ready-to-use mapping, analysis, and data sharing, while the wider platform covers spatial analytics, 3D modeling and digital twins, data integration, and real-time operational monitoring. Retail and real estate teams use it for site selection and market analysis, such as store network expansion planning. Esri serves organizations across retail, government, infrastructure, energy, and port operations, and backs the platform with training programs and partnerships including Microsoft.

✓ Pros

✓Industry-standard GIS platform with unmatched depth of spatial analysis tooling
✓Scales from ready-to-use ArcGIS Online to full enterprise deployments
✓Large ecosystem of training, developer tools, and partner integrations

✗ Cons

✗General-purpose GIS rather than a CRE-specific product, so real estate workflows require configuration
✗No pricing published on the main site
✗Full platform carries a significant learning curve for non-GIS users

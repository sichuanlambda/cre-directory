About OnComply

OnComply is a vendor onboarding and compliance platform that automates collecting, validating, and tracking vendor documentation through a single shareable link. It handles W-9s and tax-exempt certificates, certificates of insurance with automatic validation against customizable rules, professional and business licenses with expiration tracking, encrypted bank/ACH details with e-signature, signed agreements, and custom forms. Each vendor carries live "Can Work" and "Can Pay" eligibility statuses that update in real time as documents change, expire, or renew, with automated renewal reminders, grace period management, and audit-ready exports. Vendors upload documents through a portal without creating accounts, and an eligibility API, webhooks, and SFTP delivery connect compliance data to other business systems. The platform targets general contractors managing subcontractors, property managers tracking multi-location vendors, and accounts payable teams.

OnComply Features

✦ W-9 & Tax Documents Collects W-9s and tax-exempt certificates and extracts key details like tax IDs; optional real-time TIN verification against IRS records.
✦ COI Validation Certificates of insurance validated automatically against customizable rules, including insurance limits.
✦ Licenses & Agreements License collection with expiration tracking, plus fill-and-sign for agreements and any PDF.
✦ ACH & Banking Details Encrypted collection of bank account and ACH authorization with e-signature.
✦ Can Work / Can Pay Eligibility Live per-vendor eligibility decisions that update the moment a document changes, expires, or renews.
✦ Renewal Reminders & Grace Periods Automated expiration-based reminders with grace period management for lapsed documents.
✦ Audit Trail & Exports Timestamped logs of user actions and document changes with audit-ready exports.
✦ No-Account Vendor Portal Vendors upload documents, complete forms, and e-sign via a secure link without creating accounts.
✦ API, Webhooks & SFTP Eligibility API, webhook integrations, and SFTP delivery for real-time compliance data exchange.
✦ Security Controls Role-based access, step-up authentication, AES-256-GCM encryption, and database-enforced tenant isolation.

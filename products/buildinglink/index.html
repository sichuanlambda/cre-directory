About BuildingLink

BuildingLink is a cloud-based community management platform that centralizes operations, communications, and resident engagement for residential buildings, including condominiums, co-ops, HOAs, multifamily communities, and mixed-use properties. It consolidates work orders, preventive maintenance, package tracking, amenity reservations, visitor and contractor management, and front-desk operations into a single dashboard used by managers, board members, and building staff. Residents get a mobile app and web portal for service requests, amenity bookings, package alerts, documents, and community bulletin boards, while communication tools support email, SMS, and voice broadcasting, surveys, and event calendars. Add-ons include KeyLink electronic key control, TippingLink digital tipping, custom websites, and virtual meetings.

✓ Pros

✓Long-established platform widely used in condos, co-ops, and luxury residential buildings
✓Deep front-desk and concierge workflows (packages, keys, visitors) that generic PM tools lack
✓Covers staff, board, and resident needs with dedicated portals and apps

✗ Cons

✗Pricing is not published and requires contacting sales
✗Operations and engagement focus; it is not an accounting or leasing system
✗Feature depth is oriented to staffed urban buildings, which can be overkill for small communities

BuildingLink Features

✦ Work Order Management Work orders, preventive maintenance scheduling, equipment tracking, inspections, and a vendor directory.
✦ Front Desk Tools Package tracking, visitor and contractor management, key management, incident reporting, and shift logs.
✦ Resident App & Portal Service requests, amenity bookings, package alerts, document library, and community bulletin board.
✦ Communications Email, SMS, and voice broadcasting, announcements, surveys, and event calendars.

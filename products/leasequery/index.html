About LeaseQuery

LeaseQuery (now rebranded as FinQuery) is the G2-leading lease accounting software built for compliance with ASC 842, IFRS 16, and GASB 87 lease accounting standards. The platform automates lease data management, journal entry generation, disclosure reporting, and compliance workflows. FinQuery serves organizations of all sizes — from small businesses to Fortune 500 enterprises — and is recognized for top satisfaction scores in quality of support, ease of use, and implementation accuracy. It also handles prepaid expenses and accrual management beyond leases.

✓ Pros

✓G2 leader across small, mid-market, and enterprise categories
✓Top satisfaction scores for support quality, ease of use, and accuracy
✓Supports all major lease accounting standards (ASC 842, IFRS 16, GASB 87)
✓Extends beyond leases to prepaids and accruals — broader value proposition
✓Strong implementation support with dedicated onboarding

✗ Cons

✗Pricing not publicly available — must contact sales
✗Primarily focused on accounting compliance — limited operational lease management
✗Rebranded from LeaseQuery to FinQuery which may cause market confusion
✗Less comprehensive for real estate operations compared to full property management platforms

LeaseQuery Features

✦ ASC 842 Compliance Full compliance automation for the ASC 842 lease accounting standard.
✦ IFRS 16 Compliance Automated IFRS 16 lease accounting calculations and reporting.
✦ GASB 87 Compliance Government accounting standards compliance for public sector leases.
✦ Journal Entry Generation Automated generation of lease-related journal entries for seamless accounting.
✦ Lease Portfolio Dashboard Centralized view of all leases with key metrics, expirations, and alerts.
✦ Lease Abstraction Extract and organize key terms from lease documents into structured data.
✦ Critical Date Tracking Automated alerts for lease expirations, renewals, and option exercise dates.
✦ Document Storage Centralized repository for all lease documents with version control.
✦ Financial Disclosures Automated disclosure reports meeting audit requirements for all major standards.
✦ Footnote Generation Automated footnote generation for financial statements.
✦ Custom Reporting Flexible reporting tools for internal analysis and management reporting.
✦ Prepaid Expense Management Automated tracking and amortization of prepaid expenses.
✦ Accrual Management Streamlined accrual calculations and journal entry automation.
✦ ERP Integration Seamless integration with major ERP systems for automated data flow.

About PlanSwift

PlanSwift is desktop takeoff and estimating software that converts architectural plans into measurable quantities and bid-ready estimates. Estimators use point-and-click and drag-and-drop tools to measure areas, lengths, and counts directly on digital plans. Its Takeoff Boost AI suite adds Auto Takeoff for preset-driven initial measurements, Auto Count for symbol recognition across multiple plans, Auto Scale for batch scaling entire plan sets, and Auto Bookmark for AI-generated plan navigation. The product is operated by ConstructConnect and serves general contractors and specialty trades including concrete, drywall, electrical, flooring, framing, HVAC, landscape, painting, and plumbing. A 14-day free trial is available with no credit card required.

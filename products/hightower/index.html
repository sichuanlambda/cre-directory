About Hightower

Hightower was a commercial real estate leasing and asset management platform that has been acquired by VTS. The platform provided CRE landlords and brokers with tools for leasing pipeline management, deal tracking, stacking plans, and portfolio analytics. Note: The Hightower website is no longer accessible as the product has been fully integrated into VTS. Former Hightower users should contact VTS for continued service. The VTS platform continues to provide comprehensive leasing and asset management capabilities that incorporate Hightower's original functionality.

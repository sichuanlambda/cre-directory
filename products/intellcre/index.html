About IntellCRE

IntellCRE is an AI platform that combines commercial real estate underwriting with automated marketing production. It generates offering memorandums, brochures, pitch decks, listing websites, and flipbooks from deal data, while the underwriting side covers financial modeling, sensitivity analysis, equity waterfall modeling, and broker opinions of value. Comparable analysis draws on a database of 150 million property records, and deal sourcing tools (Dealfinder and Dealbox) surface opportunities. Data imports support Excel and PDF, brand customization templates keep output on-brand, and a concierge service can produce materials hands-on. A free trial is offered; full pricing requires a demo.

About PERQ

PERQ is an AI-powered marketing platform for multifamily that unifies traffic generation, website conversion, and portfolio-wide marketing analytics to drive lease conversions. Traffic and lead tools include paid media, Google Business Profile management, and lead capture with attribution. Conversion websites add embedded lead capture, technical SEO with geo-targeting, and real-time integration with property management systems. Pulse, its marketing intelligence layer, provides unified reporting, budget visibility, and automated reports across a portfolio. PERQ integrates with Yardi, RealPage, Entrata, and Apartments.com, and is aimed at owner-operators and third-party property management companies.

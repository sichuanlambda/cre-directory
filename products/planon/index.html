About Planon

Planon is a market-leading Integrated Workplace Management System (IWMS) and smart building management platform, recognized by Verdantix, Gartner, IDC, and Frost & Sullivan. The platform enables connected places where people work, live, play, and learn, covering real estate portfolio management, space and workplace management, facilities and maintenance management, lease accounting, sustainability tracking, and field services. Planon serves global enterprises including universities (LSE), construction firms (Royal BAM Group), and corporate occupiers.

Planon Features

✦ Space Management Occupancy planning, space allocation, and utilization analytics for hybrid workplaces.
✦ Workplace Experience Room booking, desk reservation, wayfinding, and workplace services.
✦ Move Management Plan and execute office moves with automated workflows and checklists.
✦ Portfolio Management Manage real estate portfolios with lease tracking, financial analysis, and strategic planning.
✦ Lease Accounting IFRS 16, ASC 842, and GASB 87 compliance with automated calculations and reporting.
✦ Capital Project Management Manage construction and renovation projects with budgets, schedules, and milestones.
✦ Facilities Management Work order management, SLA tracking, and resource planning for FM operations.
✦ Preventive Maintenance Schedule-based and condition-based maintenance with asset lifecycle management.
✦ Sustainability Management Energy monitoring, carbon accounting, and ESG reporting.
✦ IoT Integration Connect building systems and sensors for real-time monitoring and automation.
✦ Digital Twin Create digital representations of buildings for visualization and simulation.
✦ Analytics & AI Predictive analytics and AI-driven insights for optimizing building performance.

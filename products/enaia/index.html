About Enaia

Enaia is a CRM and workflow platform that helps commercial real estate brokers organize prospecting, manage deals, and collaborate with other brokers. It combines deal and prospecting management on drag-and-drop kanban boards with contact enrichment drawing on a large base of digital sources, integrated email, and real-time news insights about prospects and clients. Brokers get lease expiration reminders, portfolio account management, and transaction financial analysis, while Controlled Collaboration lets them selectively share information with teammates without exposing their whole book. The platform serves tenant and occupier reps, landlord reps, investment sales brokers, and brokerage teams. Enaia publishes flat pricing at $129 per month or $1,299 per year with a 10-day free trial and no credit card required.

Enaia Features

✦ Kanban Deal Boards Drag-and-drop boards for organizing prospecting activity and tracking deal pipelines by stage.
✦ Lease Expiration Reminders Automatic reminders on upcoming lease expirations so brokers reach tenants at the right time.
✦ Transaction Financial Analysis Built-in financial analysis on deals and transactions.
✦ Contact Enrichment Company and contact database with enrichment drawing on around one million digital sources.
✦ News Insight Enrichment Real-time news aggregated from digital sources to surface intelligence on prospects and clients.
✦ Email Integration Send and receive email inside the platform, tied to contacts and deals.
✦ Controlled Collaboration Selectively share deals and contacts with teammates while keeping the rest of the book private.

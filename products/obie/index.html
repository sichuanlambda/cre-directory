About Obie

Obie is a technology-driven insurance platform designed specifically for landlords and real estate investors. The platform uses data-driven insights to provide faster, more accurate insurance quotes with a streamlined online experience. Obie offers customizable insurance solutions tailored to unique investment properties and goals, with expert guidance available at every step. The platform is trusted by thousands of property owners and investors, with customers reporting getting landlord insurance policies online in as little as 15 minutes. Obie handles various property types and investment strategies from fix-and-flip to long-term rentals, meeting lender insurance requirements for all loan types.

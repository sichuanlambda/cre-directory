About CityBldr

CityBldr is an AI-powered site selection and development analysis platform for multifamily and commercial real estate developers. The platform identifies development sites 1,000x faster, provides 50x faster workflows at half the price of traditional methods. CityBldr automatically calculates buildable units for any site, shows market rents for every location, includes a built-in CRM for lead management, and offers comprehensive zoning information, environmental indicators, parking requirements, and cost analysis. The platform also features Assemblage AI for identifying land assembly opportunities, competitor analysis, and neighborhood analysis. Used by major developers including Essex Property Trust.

About Storefront

Storefront is a marketplace for short-term retail space, offering 10,000+ pop-up shops, showrooms, event venues, and flexible retail spaces for rent in cities worldwide including New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Paris, London, Amsterdam, Milan, Hong Kong, and Dubai. The platform serves brands looking for temporary retail experiences by the day, week, month, or year. Storefront offers a Priority Pass premium service with bespoke space propositions, dedicated account managers, and access to exclusive offline spaces. The marketplace covers multiple use cases including retail/pop-up stores, showrooms, art exhibits, corporate events, product launches, sample sales, and photo shoots.

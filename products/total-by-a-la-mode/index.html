About TOTAL by a la mode

TOTAL is a la mode's appraisal report writing suite for licensed residential appraisers, sold by Cotality (a la mode technologies, LLC). The desktop formfiller is complemented by cloud products: Titan Reports for browser-based forms, Titan Office for business management, Titan Analytics, and Titan Drive for file storage and collaboration. TOTAL for Mobile handles on-site data collection, photo organization, and sketching on iOS and Android, with ScanToSketch for floor plans and QuickSource for comp data. The ecosystem also includes flood maps, a mileage estimator, XSites appraiser websites for marketing, and the TOTAL Store marketplace of third-party integrations. Annual bundles such as the Elite System are offered with a 30-day free trial.

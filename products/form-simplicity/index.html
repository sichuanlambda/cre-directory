About Form Simplicity

Form Simplicity is a cloud-based real estate forms and transaction management platform owned and operated by Florida Realtors and provided through numerous state Realtor associations and brokerages. It lets users create transactions quickly and compliantly, keeping forms, documents, and everything related to a transaction organized in one location. The Ultimate Edition includes Sabal Sign, an integrated e-signature tool for sending, signing, and tracking documents without leaving the platform. Dynamic forms can be programmed to specific workflows, and MLS data imports directly into forms to reduce rekeying, with cloud storage the company describes as bank-level security. Pricing is not published; access typically comes through an association membership or a brokerage agreement, with demos available.

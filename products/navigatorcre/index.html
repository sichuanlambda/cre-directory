About Navigator CRE

NavigatorCRE is the #1 CRE data analytics platform that centralizes commercial real estate data, accelerates reporting, and unlocks analytics with speed. The cloud-based platform connects data across organizational systems from accounting, leasing, collections, debt, construction, work orders, and more into a centralized CRE intelligence experience. NavigatorCRE serves asset management, development, brokerage, investments, and occupiers across all asset classes including multifamily, office, industrial, retail, land, and hospitality. The platform includes NAVI AI for intelligent insights and the HELM product for strategic portfolio management.

About Condo Control

Condo Control is a cloud-based property management platform for condominiums, HOAs, co-ops, and community associations that consolidates accounting, operations, resident services, payments, and governance into one connected system. Financial tools cover billing, collections, payables, receivables, bank reconciliation, budgeting, and audit-ready reporting, while operations features track maintenance requests, tasks, architectural approvals, and preventive maintenance. Residents get a self-service portal for payments, requests, amenity booking, document access, and voting, and governance tools handle e-voting, proxy voting, meeting agendas, and audit trails. Communication features include announcements, discussion forums, virtual meetings, surveys, and branded community websites, with AI-powered communications and financial summaries layered on top. It scales from single communities to multi-property portfolios.

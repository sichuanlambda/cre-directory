About RentPath

RentPath was a digital marketing solutions provider for the rental housing industry, operating rental listing sites Rent.com, ApartmentGuide.com, and Rentals.com. RentPath was acquired by Redfin in 2021 out of bankruptcy. The platform connected renters with apartments and rental properties while providing landlords and property managers with marketing tools, lead generation, and online advertising solutions. RentPath's listing sites continue to operate under Redfin's ownership.

About Covercy

Covercy is a CRE investment management platform that uniquely combines investment management software with embedded banking. It enables GPs and syndicators to manage fundraising, investor relations, distributions, and banking — all from one platform. Covercy's embedded banking eliminates the need for separate bank accounts and manual wire transfers, allowing automated distribution payments directly from the platform. The system covers investor CRM, fundraising automation, waterfall distributions, reporting, and compliance.

Covercy Features

✦ Deal Management Track and manage deals from sourcing through closing with pipeline visibility.
✦ Fundraising Digital fundraising with subscription documents, e-signatures, and payment collection.
✦ Waterfall Distributions Automated waterfall calculations and distribution processing.
✦ Investor Portal Self-service portal for investors to view performance, documents, and tax forms.
✦ Banking Platform FDIC-insured bank accounts embedded within the investment management platform.
✦ Automated Payments Send distributions and collect capital calls directly from the platform without wires.
✦ Multi-Entity Banking Separate bank accounts per entity with consolidated management.
✦ Investor Reporting Automated performance reports, K-1 distribution, and investor communications.
✦ Cap Table Management Real-time cap table tracking with ownership percentages and waterfall modeling.
✦ Compliance Tools KYC/AML verification and accreditation management.

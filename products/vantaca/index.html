About Vantaca

Vantaca is a community association management platform that serves as the operational and accounting backbone for HOA and condo management companies. The core platform runs back-office operations and workflows, while HOAi Fleet adds an AI workforce that automates routine tasks. Vantaca Home gives residents and board members a portal for their community, and Vantaca Vendor handles vendor management and payment automation. The company pairs the software with implementation, onboarding, accounting services, and strategic account management. It is built for management companies, boards, homeowners, and the vendors that serve their communities.

✓ Pros

✓Purpose-built for professional association management companies rather than adapted from general PM software
✓AI automation (HOAi Fleet) targets repetitive back-office work
✓Covers management company, board, resident, and vendor sides in one ecosystem

✗ Cons

✗Pricing is not published and requires a sales conversation
✗Aimed at management companies, so it is heavy for small self-managed associations
✗Migrating association accounting onto a new system of record is a significant implementation project

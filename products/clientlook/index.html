About ClientLook (LightBox)

ClientLook, now part of LightBox, is a commercial real estate CRM designed specifically for CRE brokers and professionals. It provides contact management, property tracking, deal pipeline management, and activity logging in a simple, ready-to-use interface. As part of LightBox, ClientLook integrates with LightBox's comprehensive property data platform including RCM (Real Capital Markets) for deal marketing, property data intelligence, and environmental data. The CRM is known for its ease of use: brokers can be up and running quickly without elaborate setup or training.

ClientLook (LightBox) Features

✦ Contact Management Organize contacts with relationship tracking, activity history, and categorization.
✦ Property Tracking Track commercial properties with details, photos, and linked contacts.
✦ Activity Logging Log calls, meetings, and activities with automatic reminders and follow-ups.
✦ Pipeline Management Track deals through stages with visibility into the sales pipeline.
✦ Property Data Access LightBox's comprehensive property characteristics, tax parcels, and spatial data.
✦ Real Capital Markets Integrated deal marketing through LightBox RCM for investment sales.
✦ Environmental Data Access environmental risk data and reports through LightBox PARCEL.
✦ Email Integration Sync with Outlook and Gmail for automatic email logging and tracking.
✦ Mobile App Full CRM access on mobile for field brokers.
✦ Reporting Activity reports, pipeline summaries, and team performance tracking.

About Visual Lease

Visual Lease is an enterprise lease management and accounting platform with 25+ years of experience, recently acquired by CoStar Group. It provides end-to-end lease lifecycle management — from acquisition to termination — combined with robust lease accounting compliance for ASC 842, IFRS 16, and GASB 87. VL serves Fortune 500 companies and large enterprises, unifying real estate, finance, procurement, and legal teams around centralized lease data. With AI-supported tools for data extraction, it manages millions of lease records across its customer base with 97%+ customer retention.

Visual Lease Features

✦ Lifecycle Management Manage leases from acquisition to termination with automated workflows and renewals.
✦ Critical Date Alerts Automated notifications for lease expirations, options, and renewal deadlines.
✦ Portfolio Dashboard Centralized view of entire lease portfolio with key metrics and real-time insights.
✦ Clause Library Standardized lease clause templates for consistent lease negotiation.
✦ ASC 842 / IFRS 16 / GASB 87 Full compliance automation for all major lease accounting standards.
✦ Journal Entries & Disclosures Automated journal entry generation, disclosure reports, and footnotes.

About Wise Agent

Wise Agent is an all-in-one real estate CRM that consolidates lead generation, contact management, marketing automation, transaction management, and team collaboration. Lead tools include website visitor identification, custom IDX websites and landing pages, Facebook and Google ad integration, and lead pipelines with distribution rules. Communication features cover bulk email up to 2,500 sends daily, built-in texting with automatic logging, automated drip campaigns, and an AI writing assistant. Transaction checklists, client portals, commission reporting, and goal tracking round out the back office, with shared calendars, flexible permissions, and 100+ integrations for teams. Published plans start at $49/month covering up to five team members, with a 14-day free trial and no contracts.

About Follow Up Boss

Follow Up Boss is a CRM and operating system for real estate teams that centralizes leads, contacts, tasks, deals, and team coordination. Organization tools include lead distribution and routing, Smart Lists, deal tracking, calendar integration, mobile apps, and the FUB Pixel for website tracking. Engagement features cover built-in calling, texting, email automation, workflow automations, a team inbox, and a company phone number, with a Zillow Pro integration. Reporting includes advanced analytics, deal tracking, and a team leaderboard, and the open platform connects to over 250 lead sources and business tools. Plans come with a 14-day free trial and 7-day-a-week phone and email support; the company operates under Zillow Group ownership.

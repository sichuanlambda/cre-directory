About DealCloud

DealCloud by Intapp is a deal and relationship management platform purpose-built for professional and financial services firms, including real estate investment managers, private equity, investment banking, and law firms. Far more than a CRM, DealCloud centralizes firm and market intelligence, delivers real-time insights powered by Applied AI, and connects deal sourcing, pipeline management, relationship tracking, and business development workflows. With AI-powered Intapp Assist, teams get zero-entry relationship insights, deal summaries, and tailored content generation. DealCloud is used by firms like Hamilton Lane, Haven Capital, and Raymond James.

✓ Pros

✓Purpose-built for deal-driven firms — not a generic CRM adapted for real estate
✓AI eliminates 95% of manual data entry via automated relationship capture
✓No-code configuration — business users can customize without IT
✓Proven results: Hamilton Lane replaced 28 systems with DealCloud
✓Enterprise-grade security with ISO 27001 and SOC-2 compliance

✗ Cons

✗Enterprise pricing — geared toward mid-to-large firms
✗Can be complex to implement and configure for maximum value

✦ Deal Sourcing AI-powered opportunity identification and target company recommendations.
✦ Deal Execution Task management, document collaboration, and approval workflows for closing deals.
✦ Fund Allocation Allocate deals to funds and track capital deployment across strategies.
✦ Contact & Relationship CRM Comprehensive contact management with relationship history and interaction tracking.
✦ Network Intelligence Uncover relationship paths and connections across your firm's network.

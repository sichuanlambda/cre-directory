About IBM Envizi

IBM Envizi is an enterprise ESG data management and reporting suite that IBM acquired in 2022. It captures data from ERP systems, metering platforms, utility providers, spreadsheets and supplier portals, keeps data health checks and audit trails on every change, and calculates Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions. For buildings, utility bill analytics and interval meter analytics consolidate consumption data, a Building Ratings and Benchmarks module calculates and tracks building ratings, the ENERGY STAR module uploads data to Portfolio Manager through its web services API to return scores, and Envizi sends performance indicators to GRESB as a data partner. Planning Analytics models emissions reduction targets. Pricing depends on data volume, with Essentials, Standard and Premium account bundles quoted through IBM.

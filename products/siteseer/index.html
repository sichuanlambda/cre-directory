About SiteSeer

SiteSeer is a full-featured site selection and market intelligence platform designed by retail analysts for real world business decisions. The platform combines cutting-edge technology with easy-to-use design, turning complex analysis into clear, actionable insights. SiteSeer empowers organizations with tools for consumer analysis, site selection, competitive intelligence, sales forecasting, tenant prospecting, and market potential analysis. The platform uses visitor foot traffic data to understand customer behavior and is trusted by major brands including Mattress Firm, i9 Sports, and Purosystems. SiteSeer's territory planning tools help businesses prioritize markets and rank potential locations.

SiteSeer Features

✦ Location Analysis Evaluate potential sites with demographics, traffic, and competitive data.
✦ Sales Forecasting Predict sales performance for potential new locations.
✦ Void Analysis Identify underserved markets and gap analysis for expansion.
✦ Heat Maps Visual heat maps showing market density and opportunity.
✦ Foot Traffic Data Visitor foot traffic analytics to understand customer behavior.
✦ Consumer Analysis Profile and segment customers for targeted expansion.
✦ Market Potential Assess total addressable market for any location.

About OutboundEngine

OutboundEngine, an Elm Street Technology brand, is a done-for-you marketing automation platform for real estate agents and other small businesses. The service writes industry-specific content, sends email newsletters on a schedule, manages social media posting, and runs referral and online review programs with minimal hands-on time from the user. A dashboard and mobile app track engagement in real time, and the platform also offers a website builder plus Listings Pro, an IDX property search add-on for real estate. Compliance-aware content is available for regulated verticals such as real estate, mortgage, insurance, and financial services. Pricing is not published and is provided through a demo.

✓ Pros

✓Truly hands-off model for agents who will not write their own campaigns
✓Covers reviews and referrals alongside email and social
✓Industry-specific content with compliance awareness for regulated fields

✗ Cons

✗Serves many SMB verticals, so it is not real-estate-exclusive
✗Pricing is not published
✗Standardized automated content is less personal than self-authored outreach

About Rentvine

Rentvine is cloud property management software for professional residential managers, positioned for teams managing roughly 30 or more units across single-family, multifamily, or mixed portfolios. Its accounting module covers trust accounting, owner statements, tenant payments, and portfolio management, while operations add maintenance coordination and vendor, tenant, and owner portals. Leasing tools include a website builder, tenant screening, document signing (Rentsign), and RentFinder.ai, with a platform layer offering a mobile app, AI assistant, dashboards, global search, custom fields, and an open API with an MCP server. Pricing is per active unit, advertised as low as $1.50 per unit with a monthly platform minimum and every feature included from day one. Onboarding includes full-service migration with a dedicated specialist over a 45-day implementation.

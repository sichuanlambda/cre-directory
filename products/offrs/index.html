About Offrs

Offrs is a predictive analytics platform that forecasts which homeowners are likely to sell within the next twelve months. The company says its models analyze more than 250 data points drawn from public records and third-party data providers to score properties in an agent's chosen territory. Agents subscribe to territories and receive seller leads with owner contact data, plus marketing support such as branded home-valuation landing pages used to capture inbound prospects. A built-in CRM with follow-up tools, including a voicemail-drop system, helps agents work the leads. Territories can be shared or purchased exclusively depending on the plan.

✓ Pros

✓Focused specifically on seller listings rather than buyer leads
✓Exclusive territory option limits competition inside a farm area
✓Includes lead capture pages and a CRM, not just raw data

✗ Cons

✗Predictive leads still require long nurture cycles to convert
✗Costs scale with territory size and exclusivity
✗Residential focus with little relevance to commercial property workflows

Offrs Features

✦ Listing Predictions Models score homeowners by likelihood to sell in the next twelve months using 250+ data points.
✦ Territory Subscriptions Agents claim regions or ZIP codes, with exclusive territory options available.
✦ Owner Contact Data Seller leads include property and homeowner contact information for outreach.
✦ Home Valuation Landing Pages Branded home-value report pages capture inbound seller prospects from ads.
✦ Built-in CRM Lead tracking with follow-up tools such as voicemail drop to work predictions into appointments.

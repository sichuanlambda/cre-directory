About Roofstock

Roofstock is a real estate investment platform specializing in single-family rental (SFR) properties. The platform enables investors to buy, sell, and manage investment properties remotely with data-driven tools. Roofstock has facilitated over $10 billion in transactions, manages 20,000+ homes, and serves 400,000+ users. The platform combines decades of property management expertise with purpose-built technology and proprietary data analytics. Roofstock offers both individual property purchases and institutional-scale portfolio management, making residential real estate investing accessible regardless of investor location.

Roofstock Features

✦ Property Marketplace Browse and purchase tenant-occupied single-family rental homes across US markets.
✦ Neighborhood Ratings Proprietary ratings system scoring neighborhoods on multiple investment factors.
✦ Financial Projections Detailed pro forma analysis including cap rates, cash-on-cash returns, and appreciation.
✦ Roofstock Guarantee Money-back guarantee if a property doesn't meet certified standards.
✦ Full-Service Management Professional property management for single-family rentals with 20K+ homes managed.
✦ Tenant Placement Find and screen quality tenants for investment properties.
✦ Maintenance Coordination Handle all maintenance requests and vendor management.
✦ Market Analytics Proprietary data on rental yields, appreciation trends, and market fundamentals.
✦ Portfolio Tracking Dashboard to monitor investment performance across multiple properties.
✦ Rent Estimates Data-driven rental income estimates for properties in the marketplace.

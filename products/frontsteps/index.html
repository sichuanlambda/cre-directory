About FRONTSTEPS

FRONTSTEPS is an HOA and community management platform that consolidates operations, financial management, payment processing, and security into one integrated system. Its Caliber module automates accounting workflows with AI-powered reporting, customizable rules, and document organization, while the Community module provides mobile apps and communication tools for managers, boards, and residents. Payments handles online assessment collection with PCI DSS compliance, Manager supports field work from mobile devices, and Dwelling covers visitor management and community access control. It is sold to community management companies, community association managers, board members, and the residents they serve.

✓ Pros

✓Unusually broad scope for HOA software, spanning accounting through gate and visitor access
✓Dedicated modules for each stakeholder: managers, boards, residents, and field staff
✓PCI DSS compliant payment processing built in

✗ Cons

✗Pricing is not published and requires contacting sales
✗Multiple named modules can mean buying and learning several products to get the full value
✗Community association focus; not designed for commercial or mixed portfolios

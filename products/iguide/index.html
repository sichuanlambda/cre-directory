About iGUIDE

iGUIDE, made by Planitar Inc., pairs the PLANIX scanning camera with a cloud platform that turns property captures into 3D virtual tours, accurate floor plans, and property reports. A capture takes under 15 minutes and yields ANSI/RMS-compliant floor plans with stated measurement uncertainty of 0.5 percent, room-by-room measurements, and site plans. Deliverables export to PDF, DWG, CAD, SVG, RVT, ESX, and Xactimate-compatible formats for real estate marketing, appraisal, inspection, insurance, and construction workflows. Projects are paid per capture with no subscription required, and users retain ownership of their data; the PLANIX R1 camera is sold at $3,499 CAD, or teams can hire trained iGUIDE Pros for capture.

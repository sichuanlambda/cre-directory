About Nakisa

Nakisa is an AI-driven enterprise solutions provider offering products for HR (workforce planning, org design), Finance (lease accounting, financial forecasting), and Real Estate (IWMS, capital projects, portfolio management, facility management). For CRE, Nakisa provides a comprehensive IWMS suite covering capital project management, portfolio management, lease accounting (ASC 842, IFRS 16, GASB 87), and facility management. Nakisa's AI platform delivers decision intelligence across all modules, helping enterprises optimize real estate portfolios and streamline operations.

✓ Pros

✓Comprehensive IWMS covering lease accounting, FM, and capital projects
✓Strong lease accounting compliance for all major standards
✓AI-powered decision intelligence across modules
✓Integrates real estate with HR and finance for enterprise-wide visibility

✗ Cons

✗Enterprise pricing only — not accessible to small firms
✗Broad product portfolio may lack depth in CRE-specific areas vs. specialists
✗Less CRE market recognition than Planon or Accruent
✗Complex implementation for full IWMS deployment

About U-Rite

U-Rite is commercial real estate underwriting software that works inside Excel while executing calculations in the cloud. Users model deals from single-tenant acquisitions to multi-tranche developments, entering assumptions in their own workbooks or the U-Rite Engine, with DCF modeling, debt analysis, expense recovery calculations, and dashboard reporting. The platform is compatible with ARGUS files and adds AI-powered task automation, version control, and deal tracking. Alongside the software (U-Rite Pro), the company offers on-demand analyst services (U-Rite Plus) for institutional owners, developers, lenders, and advisors. A free trial is available; pricing requires contacting sales.

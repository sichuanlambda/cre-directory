About MotionCRE

MotionCRE is deal management software for commercial real estate teams that replaces spreadsheets, shared drives, and email threads with a single workspace per deal. A real-time pipeline offers drag-and-drop stage management with days-in-stage tracking and filtering by asset class, market, or assignee, while each deal workspace centralizes files, contacts, tasks, key dates, financing quotes, and notes. Per-workspace email forwarding addresses automatically file attachments and extract context, and an AI associate summarizes documents, drafts investment committee memos, and answers questions about the pipeline. Supporting tools include password-protected data rooms, a financing tracker for lender quotes, and leadership reporting. Published plans run from $249/month for three users to $699/month for ten, all with a 14-day free trial.

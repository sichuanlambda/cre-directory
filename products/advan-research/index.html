About Advan Research

Advan Research combines anonymized mobile location data with card transaction data to measure foot traffic, estimate sales, and track performance for properties and brands. The company cites coverage of 9 million+ points of interest, 150 million mapped buildings, 10,000+ tracked brands, 40 billion+ card transactions, and daily observations from tens of millions of US and global devices, with over 10 million hand-drawn geofences. Its product line includes FIT (foot traffic dashboard and dataset), REI (traffic and spend platform), REVEAL (property-level intelligence), SPENDVIEW (consumer spend signals), Patterns+ (foot traffic dataset), and REP (migration and demographic trends). Customers span commercial real estate, investment firms and hedge funds, retail corporations, and public agencies. Data is delivered via dashboards or direct feeds over FTP, AWS S3, and Google Cloud.

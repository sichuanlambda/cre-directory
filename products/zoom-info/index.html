About Zoominfo

ZoomInfo is a leading B2B data and intelligence platform providing comprehensive company and contact information for sales, marketing, and recruiting teams. While not CRE-specific, ZoomInfo is widely used in commercial real estate for prospecting, tenant research, and market analysis. The platform maintains a database of 100M+ business contacts and 14M+ companies with firmographic, technographic, and intent data. ZoomInfo offers AI-powered tools for sales automation, lead scoring, and account-based marketing, helping CRE professionals identify decision-makers at target companies for leasing, investment, and brokerage opportunities.

✓Largest B2B contact database with 100M+ profiles

Zoominfo Features

✦ Contact Database Access 100M+ business professional profiles with verified emails and direct dials.
✦ Company Profiles Detailed firmographic data on 14M+ companies including revenue, employee count, and technology stack.
✦ Intent Data Identify companies actively researching topics related to your offerings.
✦ Scoops & News Real-time company news, funding events, and executive changes.

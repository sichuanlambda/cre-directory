About Stessa

Stessa (by Roofstock) is the #1 free app for rental property investors, covering the entire investment lifecycle from acquisition to exit. It replaces spreadsheets with automated bookkeeping, real-time financial dashboards, rent collection, tenant screening, maintenance tracking, eSigning, and landlord banking. Stessa's free Essentials plan includes unlimited properties and automatic bank feeds, while paid plans ($12-$35/month) add features like maintenance management, Schedule E reports, budgeting, and a 3.24% APY banking account. Investors report saving $4,000 and 100+ hours annually.

Stessa Features

✦ Automated Bank Feeds Live connections to bank accounts for automatic transaction categorization.
✦ Smart Receipt Scanning AI-powered receipt scanning and automatic expense categorization.
✦ Tax Package Reports Schedule E, CapEx reports, and CPA-ready tax packages for simplified tax filing.
✦ Financial Dashboards Real-time income statements, net cash flow, and property performance dashboards.
✦ Online Rent Collection ACH, credit/debit card payments with automated reminders and late fees.
✦ Tenant Screening Integrated RentPrep screening with 25% lower default rates.
✦ eSigning via DocuSign Send leases and documents for electronic signature directly from Stessa.
✦ 50+ Legal Templates State-specific lease agreements and legal forms included.
✦ Maintenance Requests Tenant-facing maintenance request portal with tracking and vendor scheduling.
✦ Maintenance Tracking Log repairs, schedule vendors, and tie expenses to property financials.
✦ Document Storage Unlimited document and receipt storage organized by property.

✦ Investment Marketplace Browse and evaluate investment properties powered by Roofstock institutional analytics.

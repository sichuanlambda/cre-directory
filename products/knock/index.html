About Knock

Knock CRM is the leading front office technology platform for the multifamily industry, delivering tools for leasing teams to efficiently guide renters from lead to lease to retained resident. The platform helps owners and operators improve operational efficiency by profitably acquiring and retaining high-value, long-term residents to maximize NOI. Knock features self-service tour scheduling (Knock Now) that increases tour volume 2.5x, multi-channel messaging across email, text, voice, and chat from a single screen, AI-powered source attribution for marketing optimization, gamified task management, centralized leasing operations, and Knock Insights analytics with billions of data points.

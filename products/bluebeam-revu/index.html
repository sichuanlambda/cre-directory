About Bluebeam Revu

Bluebeam is construction document collaboration software built around Revu, its PDF markup and measurement tool that is widely used for drawing review in architecture, engineering, and construction. Teams mark up PDFs with industry-standard tools, run design review and QA/QC workflows, and collaborate in real time through Studio sessions from desktop, web, and mobile. The platform also covers drawing and document management, takeoffs and estimation, and workflows for RFIs, submittals, punch lists, and project handover. Subscriptions are sold in tiers (Basics, Core, Complete, and the AI-focused Max) billed annually per user, each including Revu for Windows plus web and mobile access. Bluebeam is part of the Nemetschek Group.

Bluebeam Revu Features

✦ PDF Markup Tools Industry-standard markup for communicating project information on drawings, including specialty 2D and 3D markup tools.
✦ Measurement & Takeoff Length, area, perimeter, count, angle, and volume measurements, with Dynamic Fill and Excel integration via Quantity Link on higher tiers.
✦ Design Review & QA/QC Structured review workflows for design and quality assurance.
✦ Studio Real-Time Collaboration Host and join live document collaboration sessions across desktop, web, and mobile.
✦ Drawing & Document Management Centralized management of construction drawings and documents with unlimited cloud storage.
✦ Project Workflows RFIs, submittals, punch lists, and project handover workflows.
✦ Bluebeam Max AI Premium tier with AI drawing reviews and comparisons, multi-view stitching, and Magic Markups.

About DealGro

DealGro tracks commercial real estate listings and emails buyers when a new deal matches their buy box: asset type, target states (all states or up to 20), price range and size criteria such as minimum lots. Coverage centers on mobile home parks, self-storage, RV parks and campgrounds, multifamily, and flex or small-bay industrial, with retail and office marked as coming soon. Listings come from listing sites, broker pages and off-market sources including Craigslist, are updated daily, and each match is labeled confirmed, likely or off-market. Brokers, wholesalers and owners can post deals for free, including pocket listings with controlled visibility. Paid members get an alert as soon as a deal matches; free members receive one email a month. Paid plans cover one asset type, with extra types as add-ons. DealGro LLC operates the service on a domain registered in 2019.

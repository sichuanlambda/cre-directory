About Searchland

Searchland is a UK land sourcing and planning data platform that combines planning history, ownership records, policy constraints, and market comparables in a map-based interface for sourcing and assessing development sites. Its sourcing tool uses AI-powered site discovery with custom filters, alongside strategic land data such as SHLAA sites, settlement boundaries, and housing supply figures. The platform also covers ownership and title reports, energy and DNO data, biodiversity net gain analysis, residential and commercial comparables, and traffic, footfall, and demographic layers. Built-in letter sending supports direct outreach to landowners, and appraisal tools help value sites before acquisition. It is used by developers, land agents, architects, planners, energy providers, and housing associations, with more than 2,000 users cited including Connells, SSE, and Strutt & Parker.

Searchland Features

✦ AI-Powered Site Discovery Source development sites with AI-driven filters tuned to custom acquisition criteria.
✦ Strategic Land Data SHLAA sites, settlement boundaries, and housing supply data for strategic sourcing.
✦ Landowner Letter Sending Send letters directly to landowners for off-market outreach from within the platform.
✦ Planning Applications & Constraints Around 30 years of planning history plus policy constraints on any site.
✦ Ownership & Title Reports Land ownership records and title information for site due diligence.
✦ Biodiversity Net Gain Analysis Assess BNG implications of a site before committing to it.
✦ Residential & Commercial Comparables Market comps to support valuations and appraisals.
✦ Appraisal & Land Valuation Tools Run site appraisals and land valuations inside the platform.

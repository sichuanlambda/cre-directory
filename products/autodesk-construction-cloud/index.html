About Autodesk Construction Cloud

Autodesk Construction Cloud is Autodesk's connected construction management platform, linking teams, workflows, and data across design, preconstruction, field execution, and operations. It covers document management, project management, quality and safety, takeoff and estimating, model coordination, and bid management, with Autodesk now presenting these products under its Forma brand (including Forma Build, Forma Takeoff, Forma Estimate, Forma Data Management, and Construction Connect). Autodesk cites use on more than 2 million projects, 400+ pre-built integrations, and mobile field apps on iOS and Android. The platform serves general contractors, specialty contractors, owners, and designers on commercial, industrial, institutional, and infrastructure work.

✓ Pros

✓End-to-end suite connected to Autodesk's design tools (Revit, AutoCAD ecosystem)
✓Large integration ecosystem and mobile apps for field teams
✓Scales from preconstruction through operations on large commercial projects

✗ Cons

✗Pricing not published; sold via demo and quote
✗Suite breadth can exceed the needs of small contractors

Autodesk Construction Cloud Features

✦ Document Management Centralized drawings, models, and project documents as a common data environment.
✦ Project Management RFIs, submittals, and field collaboration workflows for build execution.
✦ Cost & Schedule Insights Collaboration on costs, schedules, and real-time project insights from design through operations.
✦ Takeoff & Estimating Quantification and estimating tools (Forma Takeoff, Forma Estimate).
✦ Bid Management Bid and qualification workflows through Construction Connect.
✦ Model Coordination Clash detection and multi-discipline model coordination before work goes to the field.
✦ Integrations 400+ pre-built integrations with other construction and business tools.
✦ Mobile Field Apps iOS and Android apps for plans, tasks, and field data capture.
✦ AI-Powered Workflows AI capabilities embedded across the platform's project workflows.

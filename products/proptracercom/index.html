About PropTracer

PropTracer is an AI-powered property intelligence platform that delivers accurate owner contact information for commercial and residential properties. Trusted by industry leaders including CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, Newmark, and Marcus & Millichap, the platform offers map-based search with bulk export, parcel boundary overlays, interactive parcel selection, and advanced filtering by ownership type, purchase date, equity, and more. PropTracer specializes in identifying true ownership behind LLCs and corporate entities, providing verified phone numbers, emails, and mailing addresses for property owners. Features include dynamic list building, viewport property discovery, and clean CSV exports ready for CRMs and direct mail.

PropTracer Features

✦ Map Search Draw, filter, and segment properties in real-time on an interactive map.
✦ Advanced Filters Filter by ownership type, purchase date, equity, occupancy, corporate vs individual, and more.
✦ Parcel Boundaries Toggle parcel boundary overlays to see lot shapes and sizes.
✦ Viewport Discovery See all properties in your current map view and refine with filters.
✦ True Ownership Data AI-powered platform identifies real owners behind LLCs and corporate entities.
✦ Corporate Entity Breakdown Go beyond the LLC to find the individuals behind commercial properties.
✦ Verified Contact Info Phone numbers, emails, and mailing addresses verified for accuracy.
✦ Ownership History Track ownership length and history for targeting long-term holders.
✦ Dynamic Lists Save and reuse lists that stay current as data updates automatically.
✦ Bulk Export Clean CSV exports with verified owners, phones, emails, and addresses.
✦ Interactive Selection Click, draw shapes, or multi-select parcels for granular list building.

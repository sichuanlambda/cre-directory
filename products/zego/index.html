About Zego

Zego (formerly PayLease) is property management software focused on payments and utilities for management companies and community associations. Zego Pay automates rent and HOA dues collection with flexible payment options, check scanning, payouts, and credit reporting. Zego Utility handles utility billing and expense recovery, including RUBS and submeter methodologies, plus vacant unit cost analysis. A resident engagement app, Zego Mobile Doorman, adds self-service tasks, work orders, and community communications. The company states its platform is used by more than 7,000 management companies across multifamily, single-family, HOA, student, and other residential portfolios.

Zego Features

✦ Automated Rent & Dues Collection Collects rent and HOA dues online with flexible resident payment options.
✦ Check Scanning & Payouts Digitizes paper payments and manages disbursements to owners and residents.
✦ Credit Reporting Reports resident rent payments to help residents build credit.
✦ Utility Billing & Expense Recovery Bills residents for utilities using RUBS or submeter methodology to recover costs.
✦ Vacant Unit Cost Analysis Tracks utility costs in vacant units to reduce leakage.
✦ Resident App Self-service resident app for payments, work orders, and community communications.

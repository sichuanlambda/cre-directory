About Rezi

Rezi was a New York-based residential leasing startup that leased apartments from landlords upfront, then listed and rented the units itself, using machine-learning pricing to fill vacancies faster. Despite raising roughly $30M in equity and over $200M in debt financing, the company shut down in early 2024 after failing to secure further investment. The rezi.com domain now belongs to an unrelated resume-builder company.

About JustPark

JustPark is the UK's favourite parking platform, trusted by 13 million+ drivers with over 100,000 reservable parking spaces. The platform connects drivers seeking parking with space owners looking to monetize their driveways, garages, and unused parking spaces. JustPark offers pre-bookable parking near popular destinations including airports, train stations, sports stadiums, and city centres. The platform also provides car park management solutions for businesses to maximize yield from underused car parks and vacant land. With a 96% average satisfaction rating and 5-star app reviews, JustPark covers EV charging space rentals alongside traditional parking.

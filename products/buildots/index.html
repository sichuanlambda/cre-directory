About Buildots

Buildots is an AI-powered construction intelligence platform that uses helmet-mounted 360-degree capture to compare actual site conditions against BIM models and the project schedule. It delivers up-to-date progress reporting, advanced risk detection for schedule-threatening activities, and objective data for managing contractors and trades, along with complete site documentation usable in dispute records. Executives get portfolio-wide visibility across projects, and Buildots Field adds workforce visibility, safety, and logistics intelligence, with optional managed Capture Services. The platform is used on data center, commercial, multifamily, healthcare, industrial, education, and airport projects, and the company states projects using it have reduced delays by up to 50%.

✓ Pros

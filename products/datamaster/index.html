About DataMaster

DataMaster, a Class Valuation product, automates data collection and market analysis for real estate appraisal reports. It pulls MLS data, public records, and comparable property information directly into an appraiser's report software, with Smart Field Technology handling data input and CompTracker maintaining a personal comparables database. Analysis tools cover market conditions and neighborhood analysis, automated 1004MC form population, and adjustment calculations, with workfile documentation for consistency. The company claims it saves appraisers over 60 minutes per report. Single-user unlimited pricing is published at $89/month, with Express and Pro versions and multi-user or annual pricing by inquiry.

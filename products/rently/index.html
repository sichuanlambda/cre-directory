About Rently

Rently is a resident lifecycle management platform providing smart home solutions and self-guided touring technology for property managers and owners. The platform combines IoT smart home devices with leasing automation tools to create a complete proptech solution. Rently's self-guided tours allow prospective renters to tour properties safely and securely without staff present, expanding showing hours and accelerating leasing velocity. Smart home solutions include energy management, damage prevention, activity monitoring, and access control. The platform also offers vacancy advertising through its own Internet Listing Site (ILS), increasing property traffic by 25%. Rently powers organizations like RangeWater, Fundrise, and American Residential Group.

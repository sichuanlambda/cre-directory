About Mynd

Mynd, a Roofstock company, is a tech-enabled property management platform managing 20,000+ single-family rental homes across 40+ US markets. The platform provides full-service property management including tenant placement, maintenance coordination, lease management, and financial reporting through a technology-powered Investor Portal. Mynd's expert turn service ensures homes are leased quickly without compromising on rental rates or resident quality, achieving a less than 2% delinquency rate. The platform offers institutional-grade management services for portfolios of 100+ homes as well as individual investors, with features like the Mynd Eviction Protection Plan, 24/7 emergency coverage, and integration with Stessa for portfolio tracking.

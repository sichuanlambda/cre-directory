About Entrata

Entrata is a comprehensive property management operating system serving multifamily, student, affordable, military, commercial, and manufactured housing operators. It provides a unified platform covering accounting, leasing, marketing, resident services, maintenance, and utility management. Entrata's Layered Intelligence (ELI) infuses AI across the platform for automated communications, review responses, and workflow automation. With 32,000+ properties, 3.2M+ unit spaces, and 700+ PM companies, Entrata is one of the largest property management platforms in the industry.

✓ Pros

✓True single-login unified platform — everything integrated without third-party dependencies
✓Proven at massive scale — 32,000+ properties and 3.2M+ units
✓Strong AI with ELI handling 1,000+ simultaneous conversations
✓Covers diverse property types including multifamily, student, affordable, military, and commercial

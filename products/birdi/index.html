About Birdi

Birdi is a collaborative geospatial platform that helps teams work from the same map by uploading, processing, and sharing geospatial insights from one centralized platform. The platform bridges the gap between GIS experts, operations teams, and decision-makers with real-time map collaboration, commenting directly on maps, activity feeds, role-based permissions, and view-only sharing links. Birdi offers AI-powered feature detection for inspection photos and aerial imagery, automated reporting, and digital twin capabilities. While not CRE-specific, Birdi is used in construction, infrastructure, agriculture, and real estate for site documentation, progress tracking, and property analysis.

Birdi Features

✦ Real-Time Map Collaboration Multiple team members work on the same map simultaneously.
✦ Map Commenting Add comments and annotations directly on map locations.
✦ Role-Based Access Roles and permissions for secure, appropriate data access.
✦ View-Only Sharing Share maps via links without requiring recipients to sign up.
✦ AI Feature Detection AI-powered detection and labeling of features in aerial imagery.
✦ Automated Reporting Generate reports from map data with AI-assisted summaries.
✦ Image Segmentation Segment and analyze inspection photos and orthomosaics.
✦ Orthomosaic Processing Upload and process drone imagery into maps and 3D models.
✦ Elevation Models Digital elevation models for terrain analysis.
✦ Centralized Storage All geospatial data, annotations, and reports in one place.

About RealtyBackOffice

RealtyBackOffice is a cloud-based back office platform for real estate brokerages that combines transaction management, e-signature, CRM, and commission tracking in one system. Transaction workflows use customizable templates with financial tracking, broker review, and automated commission letters, while the integrated e-signing module includes form libraries and audit logs. Brokerage management tools cover commission plan creation, office management, sales reports, agent profiles, permissions, and production reports, and the built-in CRM adds lead assignment, custom pipelines, and telephony/SMS. The platform runs in the browser on Windows, Mac, and mobile, and integrates with tools such as Google Apps, QuickBooks Online, Mailchimp, and Dropbox. Six published plans are priced by agent count, all with the same feature set and a 30-day free trial.

About Tango

Tango is an industry-acclaimed real estate and facilities management platform that powers every stage of the property lifecycle — from site selection to sustainability. The platform serves corporate and retail real estate teams with solutions for lease management, space utilization, project and maintenance management, energy and sustainability tracking, and workplace optimization. Tango helps organizations reduce their second-largest expense (real estate) by optimizing portfolios, improving space utilization, and driving sustainability compliance. Strong presence in retail with store performance prediction and market analysis.

Tango Features

✦ Corporate Real Estate Execute real estate strategy leveraging utilization data to inform lease decisions.
✦ Retail Real Estate Find market opportunities, predict store performance, and manage lease portfolios.
✦ Lease Administration Centralized lease management with critical dates, payment tracking, and compliance.
✦ Site Selection Market analysis and site evaluation tools for location strategy.
✦ Workplace Management Optimize hybrid workplaces with space utilization insights and occupancy data.
✦ Space Planning Visualize and plan space allocations across the portfolio.
✦ Occupancy Analytics Track real-time utilization to right-size the portfolio and reduce costs.
✦ Project Management Streamline construction and maintenance work across locations and the portfolio.
✦ Maintenance Management Work order management and preventive maintenance across all locations.
✦ Capital Planning Long-term capital expenditure planning and budgeting.
✦ Energy Management Track, manage, and reduce energy consumption across the portfolio.
✦ Carbon Accounting Measure and report carbon emissions for ESG compliance.
✦ Sustainability Reporting Automated sustainability reporting with waste, water, and energy metrics.

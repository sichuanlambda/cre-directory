About Placester

Placester is the #1 AI-powered website builder for real estate agents, teams, and brokerages. The platform provides codeless website creation with 5,000+ customization options, IDX integration with 600+ MLSs, built-in CRM and email nurturing, lead capture tools (landing pages, forms, pop-ups, banners), and agent management capabilities. Placester's AI website builder creates custom sites in minutes that are personalized to the agent's brand and market. With 4.9/5 ratings from 1,600+ reviews, the platform offers straightforward pricing starting at $59/month for agents with no hidden fees. The platform also includes a mobile app, live chat, and native integrations with popular real estate tools.

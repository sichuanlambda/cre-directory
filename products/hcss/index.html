About HCSS

HCSS (Heavy Construction Systems Specialists) is a construction software suite for winning, planning, and managing projects, built for heavy civil and infrastructure contractors. Its products cover the full project lifecycle: HeavyBid for estimating and bidding, HeavyJob for project management and job costing, HCSS Safety for crew safety, HCSS Plans for digital plan management, and HCSS Dispatcher for job dispatch. Fleet tools include Equipment360 for maintenance and HCSS Telematics for GPS asset tracking, while HCSS Insights delivers reporting through more than 80 standard Power BI reports. The platform is cloud-hosted by HCSS and the company states it is trusted by over 4,000 heavy civil and infrastructure contractors, with 24/7/365 support from its Texas headquarters.

✓ Pros

✓Deep specialization in heavy civil and infrastructure workflows rather than generic construction management

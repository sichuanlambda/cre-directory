About Janover Connect

Janover Connect (formerly Groundbreaker) is a real estate syndication software platform that makes syndication ridiculously easy for sponsors and GPs. The platform automates fundraising, investor portal management, distribution processing, K-1 sharing, and investor communications. Janover also offers Janover Engage, which connects sponsors with a network of 52,549+ investors representing $1B+ in confirmed investments. The platform helps sponsors impress investors and close deals on autopilot, with strong customer testimonials highlighting ease of use and exceptional customer service.

✓ Pros

✓Simple and easy to use — designed to be ridiculously easy
✓Janover Engage network provides access to 52K+ investors
✓Strong customer service with dedicated success managers
✓Handles the full syndication lifecycle end-to-end

✗ Cons

✗Smaller platform than InvestNext or Juniper Square
✗Pricing not publicly available
✗Limited features for complex fund structures
✗Relatively newer brand — less market recognition

About The Broker List

TheBrokerList is the first online CRE broker directory designed to help commercial real estate brokers get found, build their network, and promote their expertise. The platform allows brokers to create SEO-optimized profiles that attract clients and referrals, share expertise through blogging, vlogging, or podcasting, syndicate property listings to reach real prospects, boost visibility through backlinks, connect with other brokers through search and filtering, and highlight professional affiliations. TheBrokerList serves as a networking and marketing hub for CRE professionals.

✓Free tier available for basic networking

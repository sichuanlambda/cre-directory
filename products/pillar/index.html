About Pillar

Pillar is a portfolio management platform for institutional commercial real estate teams that centralizes relationships, capital, and asset data in one system. Pillar CRM tracks investor and lender relationships with Outlook integration and AI enrichment, capturing activity through email and calendar connections. The Equity module streamlines fundraising with investor-ready offerings, unified data rooms, and real-time analytics, while the Debt module centralizes loan tracking, including balances, rates, and covenants, with AI document extraction. Asset Intelligence aggregates property data via API into portfolio dashboards for performance tracking. Pillar Markets Inc. is based in Indianapolis and was founded by real estate and fintech professionals.

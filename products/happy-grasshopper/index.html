About Happy Grasshopper

Happy Grasshopper is a relationship-nurturing platform that keeps real estate databases and recruiting pipelines engaged with professionally written, conversational content. Campaigns go out across email, SMS, ringless voicemail, social posts, handwritten cards, and gifts, with replies routed straight to the agent's inbox or phone. Messaging is pre-written by the company's copywriters based on fifteen years of performance data, and voice profiling tailors the tone to each sender. The platform integrates with Follow Up Boss, Lofty, BoldTrail, and more than 100 other CRMs. The company has operated since 2011 and publishes simple pricing of 19 dollars for the first month and 119 dollars per month after, alongside its free-entry BUILT coaching program.

✓ Pros

✓Content is written for you, removing the biggest follow-up bottleneck

✗ Cons

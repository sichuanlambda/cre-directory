About Noda (formerly Aquicore)

Noda (formerly Aquicore) is the first AI-powered building orchestration platform for the commercial built environment. The closed-loop solution helps real estate teams cut energy waste, costs, and carbon through intelligent workflows. The platform clarifies building data, prioritizes energy opportunities using agentic AI, executes automation and demand management, and validates results for continuous savings.

✓ Pros

✓First AI-powered building orchestration platform
✓Closed-loop from insight to action to validation
✓Agentic AI for prioritizing energy opportunities
✓Digital twin capabilities

✗ Cons

✗Specialized in energy/sustainability—not general property management
✗Requires BMS/sensor data infrastructure

Noda (formerly Aquicore) Features

✦ Data Foundation Ingest, normalize, and model building data from BMS, meters, sensors, and utility bills.
✦ Digital Twins Create digital representations of buildings for visibility and lifecycle context.
✦ API & MCP APIs and Model Context Protocol to embed insights into existing tools.
✦ Energy & Carbon Tracking Track energy and carbon from portfolio to equipment level.
✦ Anomaly Detection Context-aware diagnostics identifying inefficiencies.
✦ Benchmarking Benchmark buildings to surface underperformance.
✦ Agentic AI AI agents ranking economically justified energy opportunities.
✦ HVAC Optimization AI-driven control strategies for HVAC optimization.
✦ Demand Management Automate load shifting to reduce peak energy costs.
✦ Automated M&V AI-powered measurement and verification of savings.
✦ Impact Attribution Attribute savings to specific interventions.
✦ Sustainability Reporting Support ESG reporting with validated data.

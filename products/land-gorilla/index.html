About Land Gorilla

Land Gorilla provides cloud-based construction loan management software for banks, credit unions, and mortgage lenders. The platform manages draw processing, budget reviews, inspections (on-site, remote, and self-service), and loan servicing across consumer and commercial construction portfolios. AI-powered document processing tracks invoices and lien waivers, while risk scoring, real-time alerts, and lien monitoring help protect first lien position. A branded OneSite portal keeps borrowers, builders, and contractors updated, and X-RAY applies AI to pre-close due diligence. Supported programs span new construction, renovation, factory-built housing, fix and flip, builder finance, multifamily, acquisition and development, and SBA lending.

About Corefact

Corefact is a direct mail marketing platform for real estate agents and brokers that combines a design library of postcards, mailers, flyers, brochures, business cards, and yard signs with print fulfillment and campaign management. Mailers carry QR codes and unique URLs so agents can track engagement and capture seller leads, feeding a built-in leads dashboard, contacts manager, and listing manager. The Corefact Elite service runs automated, set-and-forget monthly farming campaigns, and the platform supports Every Door Direct Mail and USPS Informed Delivery for digital exposure alongside physical mail. It is used by agents at major brokerages including Century 21, Keller Williams, RE/MAX, and Compass.

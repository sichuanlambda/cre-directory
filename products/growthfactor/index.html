About GrowthFactor

GrowthFactor is an AI-powered site selection and deal management platform for retail and restaurant expansion teams. It scores candidate addresses against comparable locations in a brand's own portfolio with explainable reasoning, maps whitespace across entire metros, and forecasts revenue potential, with custom forecasting models available through GrowthFactor Labs. A built-in pipeline with kanban, table, and map views carries deals from site evaluation through lease signing, and an MCP integration connects the platform to AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT. Self-serve onboarding is offered for the Pro tier, with custom-priced Platform and Enterprise options. Pro is published at $200 per month for a single seat, and a Labs Discovery assessment is offered at $5,000.

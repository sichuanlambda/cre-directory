About Agora Real Estate

Agora provides best-in-class software and services for real estate investment management. Trusted by 1,400+ customers worldwide, the platform covers fundraising and investor onboarding, investment management, investor portal and experience, real estate accounting services, and payment solutions including ACH and cross-border distributions.

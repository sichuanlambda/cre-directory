About ListedKit

ListedKit is an AI-assisted transaction coordination platform centered on Ava, an AI transactions assistant for real estate teams. Ava reads purchase agreements from any state, extracts key dates and contingencies, monitors connected email to match messages to deals, and surfaces next steps across active files. The platform generates task lists from contract data, syncs timelines and milestones to calendars, drafts email replies and updates, and organizes documents for audit readiness. It serves transaction coordinators, brokers, agents, teams, and real estate attorneys, with users at brokerages including Keller Williams, Compass, and eXp Realty. Pricing is per transaction at $14.99 per credit with no subscription or monthly fees; credits never expire, bulk purchases save up to 27%, and the first transaction is free.

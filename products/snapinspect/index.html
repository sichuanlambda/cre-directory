About SnapInspect

SnapInspect is cloud-based property inspection software with iOS and Android apps that lets teams capture photos and video, conduct mobile inspections online or offline, and produce customizable branded reports. It serves property managers, asset managers, and field inspectors across residential portfolios (multifamily, student housing, vacation rentals, single-family), commercial properties, associations, and even fleet assets. Interactive dashboards surface vacancies, maintenance costs, and critical inspection data, while auto-messaging keeps contractors and tenants informed. The platform includes a report builder with side-by-side comparisons, multi-language report support, AI-powered inspection tools, and API access. Pricing is not published; a free trial is available on request.

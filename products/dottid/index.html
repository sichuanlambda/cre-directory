About Dottid

Dottid is a modern leasing workflow management platform built specifically for commercial real estate asset managers and their teams. The platform aligns leasing teams around deal pipelines, capital projects, and portfolio performance for office, industrial, and retail properties. Dottid combines deal tracking, stacking plans, dynamic projections, ARGUS MLA integration, task management, file sharing, and team workspaces into one cohesive platform. It replaces email chains, spreadsheets, and disconnected CRE tools with real-time collaboration and actionable intelligence. SOC II compliant with strong data privacy commitments.

Dottid Features

✦ Deal Pipeline Visual pipeline management for tracking CRE leasing deals across stages.
✦ Deal Templates Standardized deal templates ensuring consistent processes across the team.
✦ Deal Terms Tracking Track and compare deal terms, rent structures, and concessions.
✦ ARGUS MLA Integration Direct integration with ARGUS for market leasing assumption analysis.
✦ Asset Visualization Property-specific visualizations for office, industrial, and retail assets.
✦ Dynamic Projections Real-time projections powered by live deal and asset data.
✦ Encumbrance Schedule Automated encumbrance schedules showing lease obligations over time.
✦ Leasing Performance Track leasing velocity, conversion rates, and team performance metrics.
✦ Team Workspaces Dedicated workspaces for teams to collaborate on deals and projects.
✦ Task Lists Task management with assignments, deadlines, and progress tracking.
✦ File Management Centralized document storage with deal-level organization.
✦ Mobile App Full-featured mobile app for on-the-go deal and asset management.

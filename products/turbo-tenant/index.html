About TurboTenant

TurboTenant is a free property management software platform that helps over 500,000 landlords streamline their rental process. The platform is designed for landlords with one to 1,000+ doors, offering tools for listing vacancies, screening tenants, collecting rent, managing leases, and tracking maintenance—all from a consolidated dashboard. TurboTenant's free plan includes core features while the Premium plan offers advanced capabilities. The platform emphasizes keeping passive income truly passive by automating repetitive tasks and eliminating the need for multiple apps and paper filing systems. TurboTenant serves both first-time landlords and experienced residential investors.

TurboTenant Features

✦ Rental Listings Create and syndicate listings to major rental websites to find tenants quickly.
✦ Tenant Screening Background checks, credit reports, and eviction history for applicants.
✦ Online Applications Accept and manage rental applications digitally.
✦ Lead Tracking Track and manage prospective tenant leads.
✦ Online Rent Payments Collect rent online via ACH and credit/debit card.
✦ Autopay Tenants set up automatic recurring rent payments.
✦ Payment Tracking Real-time payment status tracking and notifications.
✦ Late Fee Automation Automatic late fees and reminders for overdue payments.
✦ State-Specific Leases Create lease agreements customized for your state's regulations.
✦ E-Signatures Sign leases digitally with legally-binding electronic signatures.
✦ Document Storage Securely store and organize lease agreements and documents.
✦ Maintenance Tracking Receive, track, and manage tenant maintenance requests online.
✦ Expense Tracking Track property-related income and expenses for accounting.
✦ Landlord Dashboard Centralized view of all properties, tenants, and key metrics.

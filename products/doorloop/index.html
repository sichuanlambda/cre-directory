About DoorLoop

DoorLoop is a cloud-based property management software designed for landlords, property managers, and real estate investors. It offers end-to-end rental management including online rent collection via credit card, debit card, ACH, and cash, tenant screening through TransUnion, lease management with eSignatures, maintenance request tracking, and full accounting with QuickBooks sync. DoorLoop features an AI Assistant for task automation, AI-powered property inspections, customizable workflows, and listing syndication to Zillow, Trulia, and HotPads. Trusted by tens of thousands of customers with a 98.6% satisfaction rating.

DoorLoop Features

✦ Online Rent Collection Accept payments via credit card, debit card, ACH, Google Pay, Apple Pay, or cash.
✦ Automated Payment Reminders Send automated reminders for upcoming and overdue rent payments.
✦ Cash Payments via Western Union Collect and track cash rent payments at Western Union Quick Collect locations.
✦ Outgoing Payments Send payments to vendors, owners, and tenants with 1099 support.
✦ Tenant Screening Run criminal, credit, and eviction background checks through TransUnion.
✦ Tenant Portal Dedicated portal for tenants to make payments, submit work orders, and manage insurance.
✦ Lease Management Track payments and requests with automated reminders, tasks, and eSignatures.
✦ Listing Syndication Market properties on Zillow, Trulia, HotPads, Dwellsy, and more.
✦ Full Accounting Suite Chart of accounts, statements, and reports for each property with AP/AR management.
✦ QuickBooks Online Sync Sync income and expense transactions directly to QuickBooks Online.
✦ Live Bank Connect Securely sync and reconcile bank accounts with automatic transaction matching.
✦ Budgeting Create customizable budgets on properties and portfolios to manage cash flow.
✦ AI Assistant 24/7 AI assistant that resolves tenant issues, automates tasks, and provides instant insights.
✦ AI Inspections Complete hours of manual inspection work in minutes with AI-powered reports.
✦ Workflows Automate repetitive tasks with customizable templates and triggers.
✦ Maintenance Management Receive, assign, and track work orders with vendor management and payments.
✦ Two-Way SMS Communication Send and receive text messages from a dedicated phone number.
✦ Tenant Announcements Send bulk announcements to tenants by email or text.

About RealAtom

RealAtom is a commercial real estate debt marketplace and loan management platform that connects borrowers, capital markets advisors, and investment sales brokers with a network of more than 3,000 lenders. The Arlington, VA-based company provides loan origination, lender matching, and deal management tools, and has added a lender-facing SaaS product called Client Connect.

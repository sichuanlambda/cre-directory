About Join

Join is a cloud platform for preconstruction and design-phase management that aligns owners, design teams, and contractors in one shared workspace. It ties real-time cost and schedule data to design evolution so teams can see budget trending across design phases and make decisions on value-engineering options and design changes with current numbers. Modules cover stakeholder engagement, validation and programming, design management, cost analysis, procurement coordination, and transition to operations, plus firm-wide preconstruction intelligence. Founded in 2017 and based in San Francisco, Join states its platform is used by more than 1,000 companies across 10,000+ projects.

✓ Pros

✓Purpose-built for the messy design-phase collaboration that spreadsheets and email handle poorly
✓Gives owners and GCs a single live view of cost impacts as the design changes
✓Integrates with estimating tools such as Beck Technology's DESTINI

✗ Cons

✗No published pricing; sales-led process
✗Focused on preconstruction - not an estimating, takeoff, or field management tool
✗Most valuable on collaborative delivery models; less so for hard-bid work

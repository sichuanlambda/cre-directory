About Backshop

Backshop is a web-based commercial real estate loan platform from CMBS.com that covers a loan from pipeline and underwriting through asset management, servicing, and exit. Lenders manage deal workflow, tasks, approvals, documents, and contacts in one system, value commercial, multifamily, and hotel properties by direct capitalization and discounted cash flow, and model debt structures that include additional fundings, floating rates, modifications, and extensions. The platform calculates loan to value, debt service coverage, and debt yield, produces deal reports in PDF and industry standard XML, and connects to a library of CMBS loans and bonds. CMBS.com also markets special servicing tools for default management and loss scenario analysis, a borrower portal, and CMBS PRO, a lower cost version of the same tools for smaller owners and borrowers. In 2024 SitusAMC took a minority stake in CMBS.com and moved its CLOSER origination system to the company.

Backshop Features

✦ Deal Workflow Pipeline, tasks, approvals, documents, and contacts for each deal.
✦ Property Valuation Direct capitalization and DCF valuation for commercial, multifamily, and hotel properties.
✦ Debt Structure Modeling Models additional fundings, floating rates, leverage, modifications, and extensions, with LTV, DSCR, and debt yield.
✦ Loan Asset Management Tracks loans and equity positions after closing within the same record.
✦ Special Servicing Tools Default management and loss scenario analysis tied to the CMBS library.
✦ Borrower Portal Lets borrowers self-serve information on their loans.
✦ Integrated CMBS Library Database of CMBS loans and bonds for comps and lead generation.
✦ Deal Reports Counterparty deal reports in PDF and industry standard XML.

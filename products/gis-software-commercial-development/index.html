About Latapult GIS

Latapult is a GIS mapping software platform built on Esri and Cotality (CoreLogic) data, providing environmental, demographic, geospatial, and topographic insights through easy-to-use mapping tools. The platform simplifies complex GIS data for confident problem-solving and business decisions, offering seamless stakeholder collaboration with dynamic mapping, project tracking, and information sharing. Latapult serves land development, CRE, infrastructure, and environmental professionals with access to millions of dollars worth of regularly updated GIS data. The platform includes in-house geospatial experts for personalized support and can integrate with CRM or project management systems.

Latapult GIS Features

✦ Interactive Maps Cloud-based GIS mapping accessible anytime, anywhere with internet.
✦ Environmental Data Environmental overlays including wetlands, flood zones, and soil types.
✦ Demographic Data Population, income, and market demographic layers.
✦ Topographic Data Elevation, terrain, and topographic information for site analysis.
✦ Project Tracking Track projects and site status with dynamic mapping tools.
✦ Stakeholder Sharing Share plans and information with stakeholders easily.

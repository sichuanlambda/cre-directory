About Procore

Procore is the leading construction management software platform that connects project teams and provides real-time insights to help build with confidence from start to finish. The platform serves over 3 million projects across 150+ countries, offering comprehensive solutions for project management, cost management, quality & safety, and resource management. With the introduction of Procore Helix, an AI-powered intelligence layer, the platform helps construction professionals automate workflows, analyze data, and make informed decisions to improve project efficiency and reduce risks.

✓ Pros

✓Industry-leading construction management platform — 3M+ projects in 150+ countries
✓Unlimited users model — no per-seat pricing encouraging broad adoption
✓Exceptional mobile app for field teams
✓Massive integration marketplace with 300+ partners
✓Strong AI features via Procore Helix for automated insights

✗ Cons

✗Pricing based on construction volume can be expensive for large developers
✗Primarily construction-focused — not a full CRE lifecycle platform
✗Can be overkill for small renovation or tenant improvement projects
✗Steep learning curve for teams not used to project management software

About Innago

Innago is a free, cloud-based property management software designed for landlords and property managers of all sizes. The platform provides comprehensive tools for online rent collection, tenant screening, lease management, maintenance tracking, and accounting—all at no cost. Innago emphasizes communication as the foundation of tenant-landlord relationships and was built from the ground up with both landlords and tenants in mind. The platform includes a mobile app for on-the-go management and has earned consistently high reviews on Google, G2, and Capterra for its ease of use, responsive customer support, and the fact that it's genuinely free.

Innago Features

✦ Online Rent Payments Accept rent payments online via ACH, credit card, and debit card.
✦ Autopay Tenants can set up automatic recurring rent payments.
✦ Payment Tracking Track paid, pending, and overdue rent payments in real time.
✦ Late Fee Automation Automatically apply late fees based on customizable rules.
✦ Tenant Screening Run background checks, credit reports, and eviction history on applicants.
✦ Online Applications Accept and manage rental applications digitally.
✦ Lease Management Create, sign, and store lease agreements with e-signature support.
✦ Tenant Communication Built-in messaging for direct landlord-tenant communication.
✦ Maintenance Requests Tenants submit work orders online; landlords assign and track completion.
✦ Accounting & Reporting Track income, expenses, and generate financial reports for each property.
✦ Document Storage Store and organize lease agreements, receipts, and property documents.

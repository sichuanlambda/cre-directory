About Buildium

Buildium is an all-in-one property management software purpose-built for residential property managers and landlords. It handles the full property management lifecycle including leasing, tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance, accounting, and owner reporting. Buildium serves portfolios from small landlords to 15,000+ units, covering single-family, multifamily, community associations, student housing, and affordable housing. It features Lumina AI for automated communications and bill scanning, built-in insurance and resident services, and a robust marketplace of integrations.

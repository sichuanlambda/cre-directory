About Realhound

Realhound is a commercial real estate CRM built for Microsoft Office 365, embedding CRE workflows directly into Outlook, Excel, and Word so users never switch applications. Outlook contacts become the CRM database, Excel serves as the reporting engine, and Word generates proposals, all kept in sync automatically with over 2,200 specialized real estate fields. The system centralizes property data, tenant information, lease details, and transaction history, and a mobile app lets teams update property information and notes in the field. Reporting tools support analysis of market trends and property performance.

✓ Pros

✓No new interface to learn - works inside Outlook, Excel, and Word
✓Deep CRE field set (2,200+ fields) for properties, leases, and deals
✓Mobile app for field updates

✗ Cons

✗Pricing not published on the site

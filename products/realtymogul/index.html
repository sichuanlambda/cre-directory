About RealtyMogul

RealtyMogul is a real estate crowdfunding and investment platform that has been empowering investors for over 10 years. The platform provides access to private market offerings including private placements, 1031 Exchange properties, and Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs). RealtyMogul simplifies commercial real estate investing through cutting-edge technology, giving members access to professionally managed properties with potential to generate income. The platform has a formalized due diligence process reviewing sponsors, business plans, properties, and documentation for every investment opportunity. Members gain exposure to deals with detailed, transparent information to make personal investment decisions.

✓ Pros

✓10+ years of track record in real estate crowdfunding
✓Rigorous due diligence process for every investment
✓Multiple investment types: REITs, private placements, 1031 exchanges
✓Transparent reporting and dedicated support teams
✓Access to institutional-quality commercial real estate

✗ Cons

✗Investments are illiquid with long hold periods
✗Higher minimums ($5K-$25K+) may exclude smaller investors
✗Past performance not indicative of future results—substantial risk of capital loss

About Rentology

Rentology is a cloud property management platform for operators who run short-term, mid-term and long-term rentals side by side. One account covers a shared inbox for guest, tenant and owner messages with AI-drafted replies, turnover and maintenance scheduling, a revenue management module that recommends and pushes nightly rates, and monthly owner statements with an owner portal. For mid-term stays it answers Furnished Finder housing requests by text, prospects nearby employers for corporate housing, and issues proposals and a single monthly invoice. Cleaners, contractors and owners each get their own app view, and an MCP connection lets AI assistants such as Claude query portfolio data. The site lists Airbnb, Vrbo, Zillow, Furnished Finder, OwnerRez, PriceLabs, Stripe and Twilio as live integrations. Rentology was built by the founder of PeachHaus Group, an Atlanta rental operator that runs its own portfolio on it. Pricing is a flat monthly fee by property count, never a percentage of bookings.

Rentology Features

✦ Unified Inbox with AI Drafts Guest, tenant and owner messages in one inbox, with AI reply drafts written in the operator's voice.
✦ Turnovers & Maintenance Turnover scheduling, maintenance work orders with vendors attached, and team tasks and notifications.
✦ Role-Based Apps Separate app views for the management team, owners, cleaners and contractors; a tenant app is listed as coming soon.
✦ Revenue Management AI rate recommendations based on demand, seasonality and local events, synced across connected channels.
✦ Owner Statements & Portal Automated monthly statements showing income, expenses and payout, with a shareable owner portal.
✦ Rent Collection & Leases Rent collection, lease management with e-sign, and tenant screening for long-term units.
✦ Furnished Finder Follow-Up Texts each new housing request, follows up, and hands off leads that are ready to sign.
✦ Corporate Housing Prospecting Finds employers near each property and the housing contact, then sends proposals and one monthly invoice.
✦ Channel & Tool Integrations The site lists Airbnb, Vrbo, Zillow, Furnished Finder, OwnerRez, PriceLabs, Stripe and Twilio as live integrations.
✦ MCP Access for AI Assistants Connect Claude, Cursor or another MCP client to query properties, bookings and work orders; included on every plan.

About Enertiv

Enertiv is a portfolio-scale sustainability reporting and decarbonization platform for commercial real estate. The platform provides investor-grade ESG reporting, enabling CRE firms to capture utility data, monitor progress, and disclose environmental impact. Enertiv automates utility bill ingestion with quality assurance, provides on-demand greenhouse gas calculations, and seamlessly submits data to Energy Star, GRESB, SFDR, CSRD, and TCFD frameworks. The platform offers 100% utility data coverage through a combination of tenant portals, shadow metering hardware, and mobile apps for manual reads. Enertiv also provides asset-level decarbonization pathways, demand response, energy procurement, smart irrigation, and solar deployment solutions.

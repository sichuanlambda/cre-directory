About Geod

Geod is network scenario software for multi-unit restaurant, retail, and service operators that tests every site decision against the existing network. Users upload and baseline their location portfolio, then simulate openings, closures, and brand conversions while the platform models per-store demand capture and transfer to quantify cannibalization. Analysis is built on drive-time trade areas with time-of-day and day-type variants, demand quantification from census demographics and points of interest, and category-specific competition weighting for substitutability. Outputs are fully sourced and auditable, with one-click PDF reports that include methodology, timestamps, and data sources. The product is currently in a pilot phase with limited availability and offers a 14-day free trial of the Network plan with no credit card required.

Geod Features

✦ Network Scenarios Simulate store openings, closures, and brand conversions against a baselined portfolio.
✦ Cannibalization & Transfer Analysis Per-store demand capture and transfer modeling shows the network impact of each decision.
✦ White Space & Capacity Analysis Identifies market gaps and saturation across the network (Network plan).
✦ Drive-Time Trade Areas Catchments with time-of-day and day-type variants.
✦ Demand & Competition Census demographics and POI-based demand with category-specific competition substitutability scoring.
✦ Explainable Scores & Site Briefs Unlimited site briefs with explainable scoring and co-tenancy identification.
✦ Auditable PDF Reports One-click reports with full methodology, timestamps, and data sources.
✦ Custom Scoring & Model Versioning Custom scoring weights and versioned models on higher plans.

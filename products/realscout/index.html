About RealScout

RealScout is an AI-powered lead nurture platform that turns agent and brokerage databases into active buyers and sellers. It automatically imports contacts from CRMs, websites, and ad platforms, then enriches records by verifying emails and completing missing data to improve deliverability. Nurture runs through MLS-powered listing alerts and market intelligence that keep contacts engaged between transactions, while Scout Score analyzes intent signals to surface the leads most ready to act. RealScout offers Core, Build, and Enterprise subscription tiers with contact-based limits, integrates with existing CRM stacks through native connectors, and supports automation via Zapier and Make.

✓ Pros

✓Nurtures the existing database rather than requiring new lead purchases
✓Data enrichment improves email deliverability before campaigns run
✓Layers onto existing CRMs via native connectors, Zapier, and Make

✗ Cons

✗Dollar pricing is not published; tier limits are contact-based
✗Depends on MLS listing data, so it is residential-only

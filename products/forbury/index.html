About Forbury

Forbury is commercial real estate valuation software used by valuers, investors, asset managers, and advisors, and now operates as an Altus Group business. It pairs an Excel-based interface with a cloud calculation engine (averaging 1.57-second runs) for valuation and appraisal modeling, investment and transaction analysis, scenario modeling, and market analysis, with support for complex lease structures and custom reporting. The company reports more than 300 customers and 2,000 users globally across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas, naming firms such as Colliers, Charter Hall, Dexus, JLL, Knight Frank, and Savills. A free trial is available; pricing requires contacting the company.

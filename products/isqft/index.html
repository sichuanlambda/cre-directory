About iSqFt

iSqFt was a construction project lead and bidding network that connected general contractors with subcontractors and suppliers. iSqFt was acquired by ConstructConnect and its functionality has been integrated into the ConstructConnect platform. The service provided project lead databases, plan rooms, and bidding tools for the construction industry. The iSqFt brand is no longer independently operational.

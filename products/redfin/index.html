About Redfin

Redfin is a technology-powered residential real estate brokerage and one of the most-visited U.S. home search platforms, offering MLS-based listings, map search, 3D walkthroughs, and its Redfin Estimate valuation tool. It pairs its own agents with online tools for scheduling tours, making offers, and selling at reduced listing fees, and also operates rental search and mortgage services. Rocket Companies acquired Redfin in 2025 and it now operates as a wholly owned Rocket subsidiary. Its focus is residential rather than commercial real estate.

Redfin Features

✦ MLS Home Search Comprehensive home search with real-time MLS data, photos, and 3D walkthroughs.
✦ Redfin Estimate Proprietary home valuation tool providing estimated market values for properties.
✦ Neighborhood Insights Data on schools, walkability, transit, and local amenities for every listing.
✦ Saved Searches & Alerts Save search criteria and receive instant notifications for new listings.
✦ Redfin Agents In-house agents with local expertise and lower buyer commission rates.
✦ Online Tour Scheduling Schedule home tours directly through the app or website.
✦ Offer Management Submit and manage offers online with Redfin agent support.
✦ Redfin Mortgage Integrated mortgage services for a streamlined buying experience.
✦ Low Listing Fees Sell your home with lower listing fees compared to traditional 6% commissions.
✦ Professional Photography Professional photos and 3D virtual tours included with listing services.
✦ Comparative Market Analysis Data-driven pricing strategy based on comparable sales and market trends.
✦ Market Trends Detailed local and national housing market data and trend reports.
✦ Housing Market Reports Monthly and weekly reports on prices, inventory, and market conditions.
✦ Recently Sold Data Access to recently sold properties with sale prices and details.

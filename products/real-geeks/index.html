About Real Geeks

Real Geeks is an all-in-one growth platform for real estate agents, teams, and brokerages built around lead-generating IDX websites paired with a CRM. Branded property-search websites capture leads that agents own, and the CRM tracks prospect activity so follow-up can be prioritized. Nurture tools include automated email drip campaigns, SMS autoresponders, the Real Geeks Dialer for calling, and Geek AI, an AI auto-engagement assistant that qualifies leads. A Marketing Studio produces print, ad, and promotional materials, market reports generate listing and buyer leads, and the MoveTo app gives clients a branded property search experience. The platform is cloud-based SaaS with mobile app availability.

About Sage 300 Construction and Real Estate

Sage 300 Construction and Real Estate (formerly Sage Timberline) is an integrated accounting, project management, and estimating solution designed for real estate developers, property managers, and construction companies. It provides general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, job costing, property management, and project management in a unified platform.

✓ Pros

✓Industry standard for construction/RE accounting — decades of track record
✓Combines construction and property management
✓Robust job costing for development projects
✓Strong audit trail and compliance

✗ Cons

✗Dated interface — desktop-first
✗Sage shifting focus to Sage Intacct cloud
✗Steep learning curve
✗Limited modern integrations

Sage 300 Construction and Real Estate Features

✦ General Ledger Full GL accounting with multi-entity and multi-currency support.
✦ Job Cost Accounting Detailed job costing for construction with cost codes and budgets.
✦ Accounts Payable/Receivable AP/AR management with purchase orders and retention tracking.
✦ Financial Reporting Standard and custom financial reports with drill-down.
✦ Lease Management Track leases, tenants, rents, and common area expenses.
✦ CAM Reconciliation Common area maintenance expense allocation and tenant reconciliation.
✦ Service Management Work order and service request management.
✦ Project Planning Scheduling, budgeting, and resource management for construction.
✦ Document Management Centralized project documents with version control.
✦ Estimating Construction estimating tools with assemblies and cost databases.

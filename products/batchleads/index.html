About BatchLeads

BatchLeads is a cloud platform for finding, analyzing, and engaging real estate leads, combining property data search with built-in calling, direct mail, and CRM functionality. Lead generation uses 130+ filters and one-click lead lists, with BatchRank-AI scoring properties and the Reia AI chatbot assisting deal analysis. Outreach tools include Dialer-AI with real-time call guidance, skip tracing with owner contact information, and virtual or door-to-door canvassing workflows, alongside comping and property evaluation tools. Now powered by PropStream data, it serves investors, wholesalers, agents, lenders, title companies, and home services contractors, and reports more than 10,000 users.

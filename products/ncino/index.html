About nCino Commercial Real Estate Lending

nCino sells cloud software to banks and credit unions, and its Commercial Real Estate Lending solution covers the CRE loan lifecycle from origination through analysis and ongoing monitoring. Lenders capture loan and collateral details in one system, build net operating income statements with custom ratios and cash flow, and run single and multi-variable sensitivity analysis to stress vacancy and interest rate assumptions. A property list tracks tenants, lease terms, concentrations, and rollovers, and portfolio tools track loan performance through custom reports and dashboards. The CRE module sits alongside nCino's commercial lending, credit analysis, and automated spreading products, so it fits institutions that want CRE on the same platform as the rest of their loan book. nCino is listed on Nasdaq (NCNO) and is headquartered in Wilmington, North Carolina.

✓ Pros

✓Puts CRE lending on the same platform many banks already use for commercial lending
✓Sensitivity analysis on vacancy and rates supports refinance and maturity risk reviews
✓Publicly listed vendor (Nasdaq: NCNO), which lowers vendor risk for a long-term system

✗ Cons

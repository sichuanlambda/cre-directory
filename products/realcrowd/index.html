About RealCrowd

RealCrowd was a commercial real estate crowdfunding marketplace that connected accredited investors with institutional-quality real estate investment opportunities through a direct investment model. The platform has been transitioned to Trinity Investors, which continues to provide access to private equity real estate and operating company investments. Historical investments made through the RealCrowd Marketplace remain unchanged, a benefit of the direct investment model. Trinity works with a close network of investment managers to provide deal flow mirroring what investors experienced on RealCrowd. The platform emphasized access, transparency, and control for investors in private real estate.

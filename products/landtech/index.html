About LandTech

LandTech (Land Technologies Ltd) is a UK platform for land acquisition and development decisions, positioned as "All Your Land Decisions, Powered by One Intelligent Platform." LandInsight handles site sourcing and assessment with ownership lookups, planning applications, policy constraints, environmental data, and demographics; LandFund covers appraisals and funding; and Give My View runs community engagement. A LandTech AI Assistant generates site analysis with verifiable planning policy citations, and letter sending plus shared workspaces support outreach and collaboration. The company says it is trusted by over 5,000 UK developers, including residential, care home, and renewable energy developers, with partner data from AiDASH, RenKap, Hometrack, and AtkinsRealis.

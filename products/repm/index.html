About REPM

REPM is a unified real estate platform from Woods Business Solutions that runs development projects and a standing portfolio in one place. On the development side it offers DIN 276 cost calculation, phase management, financial metrics, and cash flow tracking; on the portfolio side it covers unit and lease administration, maintenance tracking, and rent and charge management. Centralized property records syndicate listings to ImmobilienScout24 and other channels via OpenImmo 1.2.7 connectors, and built-in lead management adds duplicate detection and prospect matching. The platform is multi-currency (EUR/USD), bilingual (German/English), and GDPR-compliant, positioning itself as a modern alternative to legacy brokerage software. A free trial is available; pricing is not published.

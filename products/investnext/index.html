About InvestNext

InvestNext is a people-centered real estate investment management platform built for General Partners, investor relations teams, and finance leaders to access, nurture, and manage capital. The platform covers the full capital lifecycle: raising capital (fundraising, payments, capital calls, co-sponsors, fund structures), nurturing investors (portal, CRM, integrations), managing investments (distributions, cap table management, documents, K-1s, fund administration), and compliance (accreditation, KYC/AML, security). InvestNext democratizes real estate investing by delivering streamlined solutions for sponsors.

✓ Pros

✓Full capital lifecycle management from fundraising to distributions
✓Built for GPs, not just investor portals — includes fund admin and compliance
✓Strong co-sponsor and fund structure support
✓Modern platform designed for real estate syndication

✗ Cons

InvestNext Features

✦ Fundraising Digital fundraising workflows with subscription documents, e-signatures, and payment processing.
✦ Capital Calls Automated capital call notices, tracking, and collection.
✦ Fund Structures Support for various fund structures including co-GP, fund-of-funds, and series LLC.
✦ Co-Sponsor Management Tools for managing co-sponsor relationships and capital allocation.
✦ Investor Portal Branded investor portal for documents, performance tracking, and communications.
✦ Investor CRM Built-in CRM for managing investor relationships and pipeline.
✦ Distribution Management Automated distribution calculations, waterfall modeling, and payment processing.
✦ Cap Table Management Real-time cap table tracking across deals and funds.
✦ K-1 Distribution Secure K-1 sharing and global tax center for investor tax documents.
✦ Fund Administration Comprehensive fund admin tools for NAV calculations, fee tracking, and reporting.
✦ KYC/AML Automated investor verification and compliance checks.
✦ Accreditation Verification Automated accredited investor verification workflows.

About Floored

Floored was a 3D visualization and virtual tour technology company for commercial real estate. Floored was acquired by CBRE in 2017 and its technology has been integrated into CBRE's digital and technology offerings. The Floored platform created interactive 3D models of commercial spaces for leasing and marketing purposes. The technology continues to be used within CBRE's brokerage and property marketing services.

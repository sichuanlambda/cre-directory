About Apto

Apto was a CRM and deal management platform purpose-built for commercial real estate brokers, providing tools for contact management, deal tracking, property data, and commission management. Note: The Apto website is no longer accessible, suggesting the product may have been discontinued or acquired. Apto was known for being one of the first CRMs designed specifically for CRE brokers, built on the Salesforce platform, and offering integrations with major CRE data providers.

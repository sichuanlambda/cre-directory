About Sitewise Analytics

Sitewise Analytics is market planning software that helps restaurant, retail, and service brands make data-driven real estate decisions. The platform combines predictive analytics models, whitespace analysis, site evaluation and scoring, franchise territory management, and Market Grid trade-area screening, with Sitewise Mobile providing field access and Site Submit streamlining site proposal collection. Integrated data partnerships cover demographics, mobility, and retail locations across more than 100 international markets, and a Location Intelligence API exposes the data to other systems. The company, based in Vancouver, BC, serves both established multi-unit brands and emerging brands with fewer than 100 stores, and the platform is SOC 2 Type II accredited.

About Lev

Lev is a commercial real estate financing platform that combines powerful tools with purpose-built AI to help CRE professionals close more deals faster. The platform provides lender matching, workflow automation, and AI-powered deal management. Leading commercial real estate brands including Time Equities and Alturas Capital Partners use Lev to streamline the financing process, manage lender relationships, and connect with new lenders. Lev has integrated AI into real estate mortgage lending to provide clarity and purpose for deal teams, enabling them to run more holistic financing processes with reduced headaches.

About Dealius

Dealius is a cloud-based brokerage management platform for commercial real estate firms, operating since 2018. It consolidates deal, property, and contact databases with personal dashboards for pipeline, earnings, and tasks, and automates workflows including commission calculations and invoice processing. A comps module provides a commercial comparable database and analysis, an Outlook integration syncs email and contacts, and an Assist feature adds AI-powered help. Clients include Lee & Associates, CORFAC, and TCN affiliates, and the platform supports multi-office brokerage operations from agents up to corporate leadership.

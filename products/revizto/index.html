About Revizto

Revizto is a cloud-based BIM collaboration platform that brings architects, engineers, contractors, and owners into one unified 2D and 3D environment with overlay and split-view modes. Teams run collaborative clash detection with automated dynamic groupings, and every issue is tracked at its exact spatial location in the model or sheet through integrated issue management. Interactive dashboards and an API provide real-time project intelligence, with AR, VR, and QR-code capabilities for the field and more than 100 integrations including Revit, Autodesk tools, Procore, and SharePoint. The company reports over 1,000 customers managing 80,000+ projects, including AECOM, Arup, DPR Construction, Skanska, and Stantec, and offers sovereign and localized hosting options.

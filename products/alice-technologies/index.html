About ALICE Technologies

ALICE is an AI-powered construction planning and scheduling platform from ALICE Technologies (founded 2012) that generates and compares thousands of sequencing scenarios for capital projects. ALICE Optimize imports existing P6 or Microsoft Project schedules and runs what-if scenarios that plot risk-reduced solutions on time-cost graphs, ALICE Plan turns schedules and drawings into a visual 2D canvas for sequence adjustments, and ALICE Model generates baseline schedules from 3D BIM models with integrated cost data. Additional capabilities include a conversational Schedule Insights Agent, schedule quality scoring, and risk management. The company reports the platform has been deployed on $297 billion of construction projects worldwide, spanning industrial facilities such as data centers and fabs, infrastructure like railways and tunnels, and commercial high-rises. Pricing is not published.

About Moody's REIS

Moody's Analytics CRE (formerly REIS) provides commercial real estate market data, econometric forecasting, and analytics for institutional investors, lenders, and developers. Covers rents, vacancies, absorption, and construction data across all major US metros and submarkets for office, retail, industrial, apartment, and flex/R&D. Combines historical data with proprietary forecasting models projecting market performance 5-10 years out.

Moody's REIS Features

✦ Market Fundamentals Rent, vacancy, absorption, and construction data across US markets.

✦ Historical Trends Long-term historical data for cycle analysis.

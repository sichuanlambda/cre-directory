About Avalara Property Tax

Avalara Property Tax brings real and personal property tax data, documents, notices, bills, payments and appeals into one cloud system with a due-date calendar and workflow engine. It extracts and validates data from uploaded bills and notices, compares assessments with deadline tracking for review dates to help find appeal opportunities, prepares personal property returns on current jurisdictional forms, tracks bill due dates, and reports liabilities and filing status for forecasting and accruals, with third-party payment integration. Avalara says it covers all 50 states for real property and the states where personal property returns are filed. The product grew out of Avalara's 2021 acquisition of CrowdReason and launched under the Avalara name in 2023, for in-house tax teams and accounting firms. Pricing is by quote; Avalara's published plan prices are for its sales tax products.

About TenantCloud

TenantCloud is a cloud-based property management software for landlords, property managers, and tenants. It provides online rent collection (credit, debit, ACH, cash, checks), tenant screening with 99.9% accuracy, listing syndication to Apartments.com, Realtor.com, and Rentler, full accounting with QuickBooks integration, maintenance request management, and e-signatures. The platform includes separate mobile apps for landlords, tenants, service providers, and owners. TenantCloud reduces late payments by 90% with autopay and saves landlords an average of 20 hours per week compared to manual management.

TenantCloud Features

✦ Online Payments Accept credit, debit, ACH, cash, or check payments with automatic tracking.
✦ Autopay Tenants set up automatic payments, reducing late payments by 90%.
✦ Late Fee Automation Set automatic late fees, grace periods, and payment notifications.
✦ Deposit Management Manage security deposits, pet deposits, and other deposit types.
✦ Background Checks Full tenant screenings including credit, criminal, eviction reports with 99.9% accuracy.
✦ Income Verification Detect fraudulently altered income documentation.
✦ E-Signatures Request electronic signatures for lease agreements and applications.
✦ Lease Builder Design leases with built-in document creator or upload PDF templates.

✦ Custom Listing Website Free personalized listing website with business logo.
✦ Lead Tracking Track potential tenant engagements with notes, call details, and activity timeline.

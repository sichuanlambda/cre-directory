About Privy

Privy is real estate investing software that consolidates live MLS listings, investor activity, rental details, and public records so investors can find and analyze deals without holding a license. It provides sold comps, price trends, and listing data across dozens of markets, and automatically calculates ARV, profit margins, and fix-and-flip potential against preset investment criteria. An investor activity heatmap shows where other investors are buying, and filters such as estimated equity, days on market, and ownership length help build targeted lead lists. The platform also generates comparative market analyses tailored to investment properties, serving investors, investor-friendly agents, and wholesalers.

✓ Pros

✓Live MLS-sourced data rather than delayed public-record scrapes

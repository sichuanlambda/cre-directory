About ParcelQuest

ParcelQuest is a cloud platform providing data on 13 million parcels across all 58 California counties, updated daily from county assessors. Users search by APN, address, or proximity, and get owner names and mailing addresses for every California property, zoning codes and maps matched to parcels, interactive assessor APN maps, and access to recorded documents. The platform supports exports, reports, and bulk data, integrates with GIS via an Esri Gateway, and includes a mobile app for field access. It serves real estate investors, appraisers, surveyors, engineers, utilities, construction firms, legal professionals, and government agencies.

ParcelQuest Features

✦ Owner Search Owner names and addresses for every California property, searchable by APN, address, or proximity.
✦ Recorded Documents Access recorded documents and transaction history reports on demand.
✦ Interactive APN Maps Assessor maps, book indexes, and county-wide indexes in an interactive viewer.
✦ Zoning Data Zoning codes and maps matched to parcels (Aviator plan).
✦ GIS Integration GIS property boundaries and Esri Gateway support.
✦ Exports & Reports Property record exports and reports, with bulk data capabilities.
✦ PQ Mobile App Mobile access to parcel data in the field.

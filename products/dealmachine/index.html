About DealMachine

DealMachine is a real estate lead generation and marketing platform designed for investors, wholesalers, and agents to find off-market properties. The platform offers a database of 150 million+ US properties with 700 data points and 70+ filters for building targeted lead lists. Key features include driving for dollars with GPS route tracking, an AI-powered cold-calling dialer with instant transcriptions and automated follow-ups, and personalized direct mail campaigns with handwritten letters. DealMachine provides instant property owner contact information from multiple reliable sources, making it a go-to tool for real estate prospecting.

DealMachine Features

✦ List Builder Build tailored lead lists from 150M+ properties with 70+ filters and 700 data points, updated daily.
✦ Driving for Dollars GPS-tracked route mapping to discover off-market properties while driving neighborhoods.
✦ Virtual Driving Explore and tag properties remotely from your computer without physical driving.
✦ Property Owner Info Instant access to property owner contact information from multiple reliable data sources.
✦ Direct Mail Campaigns Customizable direct mail with handwritten letters, property images, and automated follow-up sequences.
✦ AI-Powered Dialer Cold-calling system with instant transcriptions, AI-assisted scripts, and automated follow-ups.
✦ Mail Templates Stunning pre-designed templates or custom designs for direct mail marketing.
✦ Property Data Comprehensive property details including ownership, tax records, mortgage info, and equity estimates.
✦ Custom Highlights Create custom tags and highlights to organize and prioritize leads.
✦ Cross-Platform Access Seamless experience across mobile app and web platform for field and office use.

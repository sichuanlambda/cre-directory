About Valcre

Valcre is a modern commercial real estate appraisal platform that streamlines data gathering and report writing for appraisal teams. The platform enables appraisers to increase production and grow their business by focusing on analyzing real estate rather than manual report assembly. Valcre has facilitated 400,000+ property appraisals with 3.5 million+ comparables collected, saving appraisers 2+ hours daily. The platform features 100+ integrations with trusted data sources, highly customizable templates covering the most property types in the industry, and support for specialty practices including portfolios, eminent domain, and tax appeals.

About IBM TRIRIGA

IBM TRIRIGA (now IBM Maximo Real Estate and Facilities) is an enterprise integrated workplace management system (IWMS) for managing real estate portfolios, facilities, space, and sustainability. TRIRIGA has evolved by being repackaged into IBM Maximo Application Suite (MAS), retaining all TRIRIGA capabilities including real estate management, lease accounting, space management, facilities maintenance, capital projects, and environmental sustainability. IBM TRIRIGA serves Fortune 500 enterprises with complex global real estate portfolios requiring enterprise-grade IWMS capabilities.

IBM TRIRIGA Features

✦ Portfolio Management Manage global real estate portfolios with lease tracking, financial analysis, and strategic planning.
✦ Lease Administration Full lease lifecycle management with critical dates, payments, and compliance.
✦ Lease Accounting ASC 842 and IFRS 16 compliance with automated calculations and journal entries.
✦ Transaction Management Manage real estate transactions including acquisitions, dispositions, and renewals.
✦ Space Management Space allocation, utilization tracking, and dynamic space planning.
✦ Workplace Services Room booking, desk reservation, and workplace experience management.
✦ Move Management Plan and execute office relocations with resource coordination.
✦ Maintenance Management Work order management, preventive maintenance, and asset lifecycle tracking.
✦ Capital Project Management Manage construction and renovation projects with budgets and schedules.
✦ Environmental Sustainability Energy management, carbon footprint tracking, and sustainability reporting.

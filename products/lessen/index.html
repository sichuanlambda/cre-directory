About Lessen

Lessen is a property repair, maintenance, and capital projects platform serving commercial and residential real estate at scale. The platform combines technology, vetted operations teams, and a nationwide network of 30,000+ qualified vendors to handle work orders, capital projects, unit turns, and make-readies. Lessen serves 280,000+ properties across 100+ metro areas, completing 3M+ work orders annually with a 4.8/5 customer satisfaction rating. In 2025, Lessen launched Aiden, an AI-powered maintenance lifecycle platform that reshapes facilities management.

✓ Pros

✓Massive scale — 280K+ properties, 3M+ work orders, 30K+ vendors
✓AI-powered Aiden platform for intelligent maintenance management
✓4.8/5 customer satisfaction rating
✓Both commercial and residential coverage
✓Eliminates vendor sourcing headaches with pre-vetted network

✗ Cons

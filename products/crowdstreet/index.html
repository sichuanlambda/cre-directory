About CrowdStreet

CrowdStreet (now Crowd Street) is a private markets investment platform providing direct access to private equity, private credit, real estate funds, direct deal CRE, and venture capital. Originally a leading CRE crowdfunding marketplace, CrowdStreet has evolved into a broader alternative investments platform. The platform connects accredited investors with curated private market opportunities, enabling portfolio diversification beyond public markets. CrowdStreet also offers technology solutions for sponsors to manage fundraising and investor relations.

✓ Pros

✓Broad private markets access — not just CRE
✓Free for investors — fees paid by sponsors
✓Curated deal flow with due diligence
✓Track record in CRE crowdfunding space

✗ Cons

✗Faced regulatory scrutiny over Nightingale Properties fraud case in 2023
✗Minimum investment thresholds for many deals
✗Only for accredited investors
✗Less control over individual investments vs. direct ownership

CrowdStreet Features

✦ Direct Deal CRE Access individual commercial real estate projects with full due diligence materials.
✦ Real Estate Funds Curated portfolios of commercial properties managed by real estate professionals.
✦ Private Equity Access to funds investing in private companies for growth and transformation.
✦ Private Credit Funds providing private loans for company growth, acquisitions, or operations.
✦ Portfolio Dashboard Track investments, performance, distributions, and tax documents in one place.
✦ Due Diligence Materials Comprehensive offering documents, financials, and sponsor track records.
✦ Tax Document Management K-1s and tax documents organized and accessible through the platform.
✦ Fundraising Platform Technology for sponsors to raise capital from CrowdStreet's investor network.
✦ Investor Communications Tools for sponsors to communicate updates and reports to investors.
✦ Compliance Automation Automated accreditation, KYC, and subscription document processing.

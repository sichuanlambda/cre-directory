About Goby

Goby, now rebranded as Conservice ESG, is an ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) reporting and sustainability platform for commercial real estate. The platform helps CRE firms capture data, monitor progress, and disclose positive impact across their portfolios. Conservice ESG features AI-powered software to digitize portfolios, a hybrid platform combining SaaS with strategic expertise for end-to-end sustainable growth, and an intuitive interface making ESG simpler and accessible. The platform provides guidance through ESG reporting frameworks, strategies, and actionable insights for systematic creation of scalable, cohesive ESG programs.

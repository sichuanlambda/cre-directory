About Conservice ESG (formerly Goby)

Conservice ESG is the ESG and building compliance business of Conservice, the utility management company, built on Goby, which Conservice acquired in 2021 (gobyinc.com now redirects to esg.conservice.com). It captures utility bill data automatically through integrations with utility providers, fully automates the connection to ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager, and tracks benchmarking ordinance and compliance submissions, with in-house engineers who stamp and verify applications where an ordinance requires it. Its building performance standards pages cover laws including NYC Local Law 97, Boston BERDO 2.0, Washington DC BEPS, Energize Denver and Washington State Clean Buildings, and its benchmarking pages list about 70 US and Canadian ordinances. It is a GRESB Global Partner and supports LEED and Fitwel. Pricing is by quote.

About CMiC

CMiC is a construction ERP platform built on a single-database architecture that unifies financials and project management, giving construction firms real-time visibility without syncing between separate accounting and project systems. Financial modules cover accounting, job costing, equipment and inventory management, and opportunity management, while project management covers project controls, drawing and document management, procurement, and quality and safety. The platform adds workflow automation and analytics, and CMiC markets itself as the first AI-powered construction ERP. It serves general contractors, subcontractors, and civil/heavy-highway firms from small to large, with pricing available by quote and demo.

About Property Metrics

Property Metrics provides web-based commercial real estate analysis software and educational resources designed to make CRE valuation and marketing more accessible and affordable. The platform offers an alternative to traditional enterprise software like Argus and Excel with simple-to-use, 100% web-based tools for creating proformas, valuations, and professional marketing materials. Property Metrics serves both seasoned professionals and newcomers to the industry with intuitive software and comprehensive educational courses taught by industry experts.

About Back At You

Back At You is a SaaS platform for residential brokerages that combines four products in one system. Websites delivers AI-powered brokerage sites with natural language search and lead generation; Social automates listing marketing, AI listing videos, and social media posting; Office covers agent CRM, lead routing and nurturing, and paperless transaction management; and Financial handles commission splits, compliance tracking, and reporting. The company reports powering more than 30,000 websites and serving hundreds of brokerages and their agents. Pricing is not published; prospective customers book a demo.

✓ Pros

✓Websites, social, CRM, and financial back office from a single vendor

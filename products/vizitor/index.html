About Vizitor

Vizitor is a cloud-based workplace management system that unifies visitor check-in, desk and space booking, meeting room management, mailroom/package management, attendance tracking, queue management, ticketing, and contractor management in one platform. Visitor management includes badge printing, NDA signing, and watchlist screening, while space booking covers hot-desking with floor maps and real-time occupancy tracking. The platform supports multi-location management from a unified dashboard and integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Outlook, Azure AD, and Google Workspace. The site states GDPR compliance, AES-256 encryption, and ISO 27001 certification, and offers a free trial with no credit card required. Plans are priced per location per month (Starter and Premium tiers, plus custom Enterprise), with pricing displayed in regional currency.

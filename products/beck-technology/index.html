About Beck Technology

Beck Technology makes the DESTINI suite of preconstruction software, connecting takeoff, estimating, bid comparison, and analytics in one workflow. DESTINI Estimator produces detailed construction estimates using standardized cost structures, a centralized cost library, and historical data, while DESTINI AI Takeoff automates quantity generation from drawings with estimator oversight. DESTINI Bid Day supports side-by-side bid comparison and scope gap identification, and DESTINI Hub provides a centralized project workspace with AI-assisted dashboards and reporting. The cloud-native platform integrates with Autodesk, Procore, Power BI, and Join, and is used by preconstruction teams at commercial general contractors including Balfour Beatty, Gilbane, and Swinerton.

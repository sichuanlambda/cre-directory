About Archer

Archer is an AI platform that automates commercial real estate analysis for investors, brokers, lenders, and property managers. It parses deal documents automatically, underwrites assets, and benchmarks assumptions using proprietary market analysis models. Teams manage their deal pipeline and sourcing strategy in the same interface, generate broker opinions of value (BOVs), and use Excel integration to build custom data workflows. The product is cloud SaaS accessed at app.archer.re; pricing is not published and evaluation runs through a demo.

✓ Pros

✓Combines parsing, underwriting, market analysis, and pipeline in one platform
✓Serves multiple sides of a deal - investors, brokers, lenders, and managers
✓Excel integration preserves existing modeling workflows

✗ Cons

Archer Features

✦ Automated Document Parsing Auto-parses deal documents such as financials and rent rolls into structured data.
✦ Underwriting Automation Quickly underwrites assets with AI-generated models.
✦ Market Analysis Models Proprietary models benchmark assumptions and market conditions.
✦ Pipeline Tracking Track deals through the acquisition pipeline in one interface.
✦ Sourcing & Market Strategy Tools for defining market strategy and sourcing deals.
✦ BOV Generation Generate broker opinions of value from deal and market data.

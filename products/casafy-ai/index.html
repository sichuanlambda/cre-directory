About Casafy AI

Casafy AI is a property intelligence platform that uses computer vision on street-level imagery to identify distressed and value-add properties before they reach the market. It maintains AI condition assessments on more than 2 million scanned properties and over 159 million parcel records nationwide, combined with live MLS data, customizable filters, comparable-property analysis, and built-in skip tracing for owner contact information. It targets real estate investors as well as trades contractors, direct mail marketers, and insurance users who need property-condition data.

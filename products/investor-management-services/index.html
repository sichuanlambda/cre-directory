About Investor Management Services

Investor Management Services (IMS), now part of RealPage Investment Management, is the leader in investment management software for commercial real estate firms and funds. The platform serves 550+ customers, 85,000 investors in 98 countries, and manages over $50 billion in equity. IMS helps firms elevate investor engagement through streamlined communications, faster capital raises, and market insights. Features include efficient targeted communication tools, self-service performance dashboards with KPIs and analytics, pipeline tracking and fundraising automation, automated waterfall calculations, custom statement generation, and a modernized investor experience portal.

Investor Management Services Features

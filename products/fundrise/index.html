About Fundrise

Fundrise is a leading real estate investment platform that allows individuals to invest in private real estate through an online, low-cost model. The platform offers diversified portfolios of commercial and residential real estate projects including multifamily apartments, industrial properties, and single-family rentals. Fundrise uses technology to provide access to institutional-quality real estate investments with low minimums starting at $10. The platform manages billions in assets and has served over 2 million investors, offering features like auto-invest, dividend reinvestment, and detailed portfolio tracking through web and mobile apps.

✓ Pros

✓Very low $10 minimum investment makes real estate accessible to everyone
✓Diversified exposure to commercial and residential real estate
✓Strong historical returns averaging 8-12% annually
✓User-friendly platform with excellent mobile app
✓SEC-registered offerings with regulatory transparency

✗ Cons

✗Investments are illiquid with early redemption penalties
✗Limited control over specific property selection
✗Returns not guaranteed and subject to real estate market conditions

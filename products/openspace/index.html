About OpenSpace

OpenSpace is a visual intelligence platform for the construction industry that uses 360° cameras, smartphones, drones, and laser scanners to capture and analyze jobsite conditions. The platform automatically maps imagery to floor plans and BIM models, creating a searchable, timestamped visual record of construction progress. OpenSpace has captured over 60 billion square feet across 129 countries and is trusted by major general contractors, trade contractors, and building owners. Key products include OpenSpace Capture for site documentation, OpenSpace Field for image-based task management with AI Autolocation, and OpenSpace Progress Tracking for monitoring construction milestones.

OpenSpace Features

✦ 360° Capture Walk through jobsites with 360° cameras to automatically document conditions.
✦ Smartphone Capture Use any smartphone to capture site photos and videos with AI mapping.
✦ Drone Capture Integrate aerial drone imagery for exterior and large-area documentation.
✦ Laser Scanning Import point cloud data from laser scanners for precise as-built records.
✦ Auto-Mapping AI automatically maps captured imagery to floor plans and BIM with timestamps.
✦ AI Autolocation Automatically identifies and tags photo locations within the building.
✦ Progress Tracking Track construction progress against schedules with visual verification.
✦ BIM+ Integration Overlay captured imagery onto BIM models for comparison and validation.
✦ OpenSpace Field Image-based task management with AI-powered workflows for field teams.
✦ Voice Notes Add voice annotations to captures for quick field documentation.
✦ Issue Tracking Identify, tag, and track construction issues with visual context.
✦ Stakeholder Sharing Share visual progress reports with owners, architects, and stakeholders remotely.
✦ Platform Integrations Connect with Procore, Autodesk, PlanGrid, and other construction management tools.
✦ Timeline Views Compare site conditions across different dates with side-by-side views.

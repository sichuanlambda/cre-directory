About Equiem

Equiem is a tenant experience and smart building platform that brings building operations, flexible space management, and community engagement into one system, Equiem One, for commercial real estate owners and operators. Its modules span Engage for tenant community and events, Ops for property management automation, Flex for flexible space revenue, Data for analytics dashboards, Connect for third-party integrations and APIs, and Hybrid for hybrid workplace tools. The platform ships with tenant-facing iOS and Android apps. Equiem reports deployment across 800+ buildings and 145M+ square feet in 25+ countries, serving A-grade offices, business parks, B and C grade properties, and mixed-use developments.

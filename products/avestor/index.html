About Avestor

Avestor is an all-in-one platform for sponsors launching and managing private capital offerings, including SPVs, syndications, and its proprietary Customizable Fund structure that lets managers run multiple deals inside one multi-asset vehicle. The platform handles fund setup and administration, investor onboarding, capital collection, portfolio tracking, and tax document delivery, with CRM functionality for managing investor relationships. Beyond software, Avestor bundles legal and regulatory compliance partnerships, accounting and tax support, business coaching, and a mastermind community. The company reports 250+ active clients and more than $300M raised on the platform.

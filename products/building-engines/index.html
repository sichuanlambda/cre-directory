About Building Engines

Building Engines (by JLL Technologies) is a property operations platform for commercial real estate owners and operators that streamlines tenant service, building operations, risk management, and tenant experience. The platform covers work order management, preventive maintenance, vendor management, tenant communication, inspection tracking, and certificate of insurance management. Building Engines helps property teams deliver consistent operational excellence while reducing risk and improving tenant satisfaction across office, industrial, retail, and mixed-use portfolios.

Building Engines Features

✦ Work Order Management Create, assign, track, and resolve work orders with automated routing and SLA tracking.
✦ Preventive Maintenance Schedule recurring maintenance tasks with equipment tracking and compliance documentation.
✦ Inspections Digital inspection checklists with photo capture and issue escalation workflows.
✦ Vendor Management Vendor onboarding, performance tracking, and work order assignment.
✦ Certificate of Insurance Automated COI collection, tracking, and compliance monitoring for tenants and vendors.
✦ Incident Reporting Digital incident capture with photo documentation and escalation workflows.
✦ Compliance Tracking Track regulatory and safety compliance requirements across the portfolio.
✦ Tenant Portal Branded self-service portal for work orders, communications, and building information.
✦ Visitor Management Digital visitor registration, badge printing, and access management.
✦ Amenity Booking Online booking for conference rooms, amenities, and shared spaces.

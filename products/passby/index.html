About PassBy

PassBy is an AI-powered market intelligence platform for retail that analyzes foot traffic, spending patterns, and customer movement across store locations and brands. Its core intelligence layer, Almanac, combines brand, market, spend, and trip data so teams can tell operational issues apart from broader market trends. Real estate teams use it for site selection and portfolio optimization, while operations and marketing teams use it for performance diagnosis, benchmarking, and campaign measurement. The platform is delivered as a cloud dashboard with feeds and APIs, plus AI agents including an integration with Microsoft Copilot. Pricing is not published and access is arranged through the company.

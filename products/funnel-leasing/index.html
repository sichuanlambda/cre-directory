About Funnel Leasing

Funnel is a multifamily front-office platform combining a renter-centric CRM with agentic AI that engages prospects and residents 24/7 across email, chat, SMS, and voice. It covers the leasing lifecycle with online leasing and fraud detection, AI workflows for lead nurturing, voice AI with call answering and scoring, contact center software, and reporting dashboards, plus a resident portal (Nestio) for payments, maintenance requests, and renewals. Leasing agents work from a consolidated My Queue dashboard rather than per-property pipelines. The platform is PMS-agnostic, integrating with systems such as Yardi, RealPage, and Entrata, and is used by large multifamily operators and REITs.

Listing Services Software for Commercial Real Estate

Commercial listing platforms and marketing tools for advertising available spaces. Syndicate listings, generate lead traffic, and connect landlords with prospective tenants.

23 tools in this category

Listing services and platforms are where commercial real estate supply meets demand. These platforms help landlords and brokers market available spaces to potential tenants and buyers, while giving occupiers a way to search for properties that meet their requirements.

The CRE listing landscape is more fragmented than residential real estate — there's no single MLS that everyone uses. Instead, professionals rely on a mix of platforms (CoStar/LoopNet, Crexi, CREXi), brokerage websites, and specialized platforms for specific property types or deal sizes. Understanding which platforms reach your target audience is crucial for effective marketing.

Beyond just listing properties, modern platforms offer marketing tools (virtual tours, professional photography distribution, social media integration), lead tracking, and analytics that show you which listings generate interest and which need better positioning.

What to Look For in Listing Services Software

Audience & Market Reach

Where do your target tenants or buyers search? Different platforms dominate different markets and property types. LoopNet reaches the broadest audience; Crexi has grown rapidly; specialized platforms serve niche markets like retail or industrial.

Listing Quality & Presentation

Can you create compelling listings with photos, floor plans, virtual tours, and detailed specs? First impressions matter — platforms that limit how you present properties limit your marketing effectiveness.

Lead Management

When inquiries come in, how are they routed and tracked? Good platforms capture lead information, notify you promptly, and integrate with your CRM so nothing falls through the cracks.

Syndication & Distribution

Can your listing be distributed to multiple platforms from one entry point? Syndication reduces the work of maintaining listings across multiple sites and ensures consistency.

Analytics & Reporting

Views, inquiries, saved searches — understand how your listings perform so you can adjust pricing, improve presentations, or shift marketing spend to better-performing platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best platform to list commercial property?

There's no single best platform — it depends on your property type and market. LoopNet (owned by CoStar) has the largest audience for commercial listings. Crexi has grown significantly and offers competitive pricing. For specialized property types, platforms like Brevitas (investment sales) or Storefront (pop-up retail) may reach more qualified prospects.

How much does it cost to list commercial property online?

Basic listings on many platforms are free but have limited visibility. Premium listings on LoopNet/CoStar range from $200-1,000+/month per listing. Crexi offers tiered pricing with free basic listings. Buildout charges based on your total listing volume for marketing production.

Do I need to list on multiple platforms?

Generally, yes. Unlike residential real estate where MLS provides universal distribution, CRE buyers and tenants search across multiple platforms. At minimum, list on 2-3 major platforms plus your brokerage website. Syndication tools can help manage this without duplicate data entry.

💡 Buyer Tip: Track where your actual leads come from before committing to expensive listing packages. Many brokers pay for premium placements on platforms that don't generate meaningful inquiry volume for their property type or market. Run a 3-month test, track lead source carefully, and invest in the platforms that actually produce qualified prospects — not just impressions.

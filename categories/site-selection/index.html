Site Selection Software for Commercial Real Estate

Location intelligence and site analysis platforms for retailers, restaurants, and corporate occupiers. Evaluate demographics, traffic patterns, and competitive landscapes before signing a lease.

51 tools in this category

Site selection software helps retailers, restaurants, healthcare systems, and other CRE occupiers identify the best locations for their next facility. These platforms combine geographic data, demographics, consumer behavior, competition mapping, and real estate availability into analytical tools that take the guesswork out of location decisions.

The consequences of poor site selection are severe — a bad location can doom a business regardless of how well it's operated. That's why serious operators invest in data-driven site selection rather than relying on instinct or windshield surveys. Modern platforms can model trade areas, predict revenue, and score potential sites before you ever visit in person.

The category includes dedicated site selection platforms (like SiteZeus and Placer.ai), GIS tools with CRE applications, demographic data providers, and market research platforms that help with location strategy.

What to Look For in Site Selection Software

Data Coverage & Sources

The best tools aggregate data from multiple sources — demographics, foot traffic, consumer spending, zoning, and market trends. Look for platforms that cover your target markets and property types with current, reliable data.

Trade Area Analysis

Can you define and analyze custom trade areas? Drive-time polygons, walk-time radii, and custom shapes are more useful than simple radius rings. The tool should overlay demographics, competition, and traffic within these areas.

Predictive Analytics

Revenue forecasting, cannibalization analysis, and market potential scoring separate basic mapping tools from true site selection platforms. Look for models you can validate against your existing locations.

Mapping & Visualization

Interactive maps with customizable layers, heat maps, and the ability to visualize multiple data sets simultaneously. You should be able to create compelling presentations for internal stakeholders.

Competitive Intelligence

Understanding where competitors are located, how they perform, and where gaps exist in the market. Some platforms track competitor openings and closings in real time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CRE site selection software?

Site selection software helps businesses identify optimal locations for stores, offices, warehouses, or other facilities. It combines geographic analysis, demographic data, consumer behavior patterns, competition mapping, and available real estate into a platform that supports data-driven location decisions.

How much does site selection software cost?

Pricing varies widely. Basic demographic and mapping tools start at $100-300/month. Mid-range platforms with trade area analysis and some predictive capabilities run $500-2,000/month. Enterprise site selection platforms with custom modeling can cost $25,000-100,000+ per year.

Who uses site selection software?

Primarily retailers, restaurant chains, healthcare networks, financial institutions, and other businesses expanding their physical footprint. CRE brokers also use these tools to advise tenants and market properties. Economic development organizations use them to attract businesses to their communities.

💡 Buyer Tip: Before evaluating tools, validate their data against locations you already know well. Pick 5-10 of your existing sites and see if the platform's demographics, traffic data, and competitive landscape match your ground truth. This will quickly reveal which platforms have reliable data in your markets and which are extrapolating from thin sources.

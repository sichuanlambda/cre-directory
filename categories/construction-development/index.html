Construction & Development Software for Commercial Real Estate

Project management, budgeting, and coordination tools for ground-up development and major renovations. Track timelines, manage contractors, and keep builds on budget.

80 tools in this category · Read the buyer's guide →

Construction and development software helps CRE professionals manage the most complex and capital-intensive phase of real estate — taking a project from concept through entitlement, construction, and delivery. These tools bring visibility and control to projects where budget overruns and schedule delays are the norm, not the exception.

The category includes project management platforms like Procore, development feasibility tools, construction budgeting and draw management systems, and field documentation solutions. Many are designed to connect the office and the job site, giving developers real-time visibility into what's actually happening on the ground.

For developers and owners, the right construction software doesn't just save time — it protects margins. A 5% cost overrun on a $50M project is $2.5M, and most overruns start with poor visibility into change orders, schedule slippage, or vendor performance.

What to Look For in Construction & Development Software

Budget & Cost Tracking

Real-time budget tracking with change order management, draw schedules, and cost-to-complete projections. You need to see the financial picture clearly at every phase of construction.

Schedule Management

Gantt charts and critical path tracking are table stakes. Better tools also flag schedule risks, track dependencies, and integrate with financial projections so you can see how delays impact returns.

Field Documentation

Photo documentation, daily logs, punch lists, and RFIs from the field — ideally captured on mobile and automatically organized. OpenSpace and similar tools are adding AI-powered progress tracking.

Stakeholder Collaboration

Development projects involve owners, GCs, architects, engineers, and lenders. The platform needs to manage permissions, share documents, and facilitate communication across all parties.

Entitlement & Pre-Development

Some tools help with the pre-construction phase — feasibility analysis, zoning research, permitting workflows, and community engagement. This is often where deals are made or killed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between construction management and development software?

Construction management software (like Procore) focuses on the build phase — budgets, schedules, field docs, subcontractor management. Development software is broader, covering feasibility analysis, entitlements, financing, and project delivery from land acquisition through lease-up. Many developers use both.

Is Procore the best option for CRE construction?

Procore is the market leader in construction management and works well for most projects. However, it's primarily designed for general contractors. Owner/developers may want complementary tools for development-specific workflows like draw management, investor reporting, and feasibility modeling.

How much does construction management software cost?

Procore moved to a company-level annual license (typically $10,000-50,000+/year based on volume). Smaller tools start at $50-300/month. Development-specific platforms vary widely — some charge per project, others per seat.

💡 Buyer Tip: Think about who needs access. If it's just your internal team, a simpler project management tool might work. If you need GCs, architects, and lenders all collaborating in one place, you need a platform designed for multi-party construction workflows. Also consider whether you need pre-development tools (feasibility, entitlements) or just construction-phase management.

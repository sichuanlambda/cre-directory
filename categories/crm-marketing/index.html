CRM & Marketing Software for Commercial Real Estate

CRM systems and marketing platforms purpose-built for commercial real estate. Manage broker relationships, automate outreach, track deal pipelines, and nurture tenant and investor leads.

118 tools in this category · Read the buyer's guide →

In commercial real estate, relationships are everything — and CRM software is how you manage them at scale. CRE-specific CRMs go beyond generic tools like Salesforce by understanding deal structures, property types, and the unique workflow of commercial transactions.

Marketing tools in this category help brokerages and landlords generate leads, create professional property marketing materials, and maintain visibility in a competitive market. From automated email campaigns to offering memorandum generation, these platforms streamline the go-to-market side of CRE.

The best CRM and marketing platforms for CRE combine relationship management with deal pipeline tracking, so you can see not just who your contacts are, but where every deal stands and what's likely to close this quarter.

What to Look For in CRM & Marketing Software

CRE-Specific Deal Tracking

Generic CRMs don't understand CRE deal stages. Look for platforms that track pursuits, proposals, LOIs, due diligence, and closing — with fields for property type, square footage, and deal value built in.

Contact & Relationship Intelligence

The best CRMs map relationships between people, companies, and properties. You should be able to see every interaction, every deal, and every property connected to a contact in one view.

Marketing Automation & Collateral

Email campaigns, property flyers, offering memorandums, and market reports should be easy to produce. Look for built-in templates and the ability to track engagement (who opened, who clicked).

Pipeline Visibility & Reporting

Brokerage leaders need to see team-wide pipeline at a glance — deal volume, probability-weighted revenue, time in each stage, and conversion rates. Good dashboards replace weekly pipeline meetings.

Integration with Listing Platforms

Your CRM should connect to the listing platforms you use (CoStar, Crexi, LoopNet) and your property management system so data flows automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a CRE-specific CRM or can I use Salesforce?

You can use Salesforce — many large brokerages do — but you'll spend significant time and money customizing it for CRE workflows. CRE-specific CRMs like RethinkCRM, Apto, or RealNex come pre-built with CRE deal stages, property fields, and comp tracking. For most CRE firms, a purpose-built tool gets you productive faster.

What does CRE marketing software typically cost?

CRM tools range from $50-150/user/month for mid-market platforms to $200+ for enterprise solutions. Marketing-focused tools like Buildout charge based on listing volume and feature tier. Many offer bundled CRM + marketing packages.

How important is mobile access for CRE CRM?

Critical. CRE professionals spend significant time in the field — touring properties, meeting clients, attending events. A CRM that's painful to use on mobile means data doesn't get entered, which means your pipeline data is always stale.

💡 Buyer Tip: The biggest CRM failure in CRE isn't picking the wrong tool — it's poor adoption. Choose a platform your team will actually use daily. That means intuitive UI, fast data entry (especially on mobile), and visible ROI for individual brokers, not just management. Run a pilot with your most tech-skeptical team members before committing.

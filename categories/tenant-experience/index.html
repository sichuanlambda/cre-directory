Tenant Experience Software for Commercial Real Estate

Platforms that improve the tenant journey, from building access and amenity booking to community engagement and service requests. Increase retention and justify premium rents.

26 tools in this category · Read the buyer's guide →

Tenant experience platforms are the technology layer between landlords and tenants, designed to make buildings more attractive, retain tenants longer, and create new revenue streams. In a market where tenants have more choices than ever — including remote work — the in-building experience has become a competitive differentiator.

These platforms typically provide mobile apps for tenants to access building services: visitor management, room booking, maintenance requests, amenity reservations, community events, and communications from management. The best ones feel like a concierge service, not a corporate portal.

For landlords, tenant experience technology provides valuable data on how spaces are actually used, which amenities drive satisfaction, and which tenants might be at risk of not renewing. It's both a service improvement tool and a business intelligence platform.

What to Look For in Tenant Experience Software

Tenant-Facing Mobile App

The app needs to be genuinely useful and well-designed — tenants won't use something clunky. Look for intuitive UX, fast loading, and features tenants actually want like visitor pre-registration and room booking.

Communication & Community

Push notifications, event calendars, building news, and community features that create a sense of place. The platform should make it easy for property managers to communicate without spamming tenants.

Amenity & Service Management

If your building has a gym, conference center, rooftop, or other amenities, the platform should handle booking, access control, and utilization tracking.

Integration with Building Systems

The most useful platforms integrate with access control, HVAC, elevator systems, and parking. This enables features like mobile access, climate preferences, and smart parking.

Analytics & Engagement Metrics

Dashboards showing app adoption, feature usage, satisfaction scores, and engagement trends. This data helps justify the investment and identify what tenants value most.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do tenants actually use tenant experience apps?

Adoption varies significantly. Buildings that gate useful functions through the app (visitor access, room booking, mobile entry) see 60-80%+ adoption. Apps that are purely informational struggle to maintain engagement. The key is making the app genuinely useful for daily routines, not just a nice-to-have.

What's the ROI of tenant experience technology?

Direct ROI comes from higher tenant retention (even a small improvement in renewal rates has significant financial impact), ability to charge premium rents, and reduced operating costs through automation. Indirect benefits include better leasing velocity and competitive positioning in the market.

How much do tenant experience platforms cost?

Most charge per building or per square foot, typically $0.10-0.50/SF/year for commercial buildings. Some offer per-tenant pricing for residential. Setup and customization fees can add $5,000-25,000 per building. Enterprise deals for large portfolios are negotiable.

💡 Buyer Tip: Ask for references from buildings similar to yours — a platform that works great in a Class A trophy tower may be overkill for a suburban office park. Also, pay attention to the implementation process. The technology is only half the battle; you need a change management plan to get tenants actually using the app and property managers comfortable managing it.

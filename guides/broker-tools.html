BUYER'S GUIDE

Best Broker Tools for Commercial Real Estate (2026)

Updated August 2026 · 11 min read

TL;DR

There is no single "broker stack." Most producing brokers run a marketplace (Crexi), a CRM-plus-marketing platform (Buildout or RealNex), and one or two point tools for analysis or commissions. Almost nobody in this category publishes pricing, with TheAnalyst PRO ($699.99/year) as the honorable exception. Here's how the 7 that matter break down.

Broker tools are a strange category. Half the products are all-in-one platforms that promise to replace your entire workflow. The other half do exactly one thing — commission splits, property websites, investment flyers — and do it well. Both approaches work. What doesn't work is buying an all-in-one platform and then using 10% of it, which is what most brokerages actually do.

We went through the 33 broker tools in our directory and cut it down to 7. We dropped the platforms that have quietly gone dark (Apto's website is no longer accessible, and Rethink CRM's domain is now a gambling site — seriously), the ones that got absorbed into bigger companies, and the ones that are services firms rather than software.

A couple things to know before the list:

  • Pricing transparency is nearly nonexistent here. Six of the seven tools below make you talk to sales before you see a number. Budget for that time, and negotiate — quote-based pricing means the price is flexible.
  • Your specialty determines your stack. An investment sales broker needs analysis and offering memorandums. A tenant rep needs almost none of that. Don't buy your colleague's toolkit.
  • Watch for zombie products. CRE broker software has a graveyard problem. Before signing an annual contract with a smaller vendor, check that they're still shipping updates.

The 7 Best Broker Tools for CRE

1. Buildout

Best for: Brokerages that want one connected platform

Full Profile →

Buildout started as a marketing tool and has grown into the closest thing this category has to a default choice: CRM, marketing, prospecting data, and back-office automation in one connected platform, used by over 50,000 CRE brokers. Its 40M+ property database gives prospecting real teeth, and the modular structure means you can start with one product and expand instead of buying everything on day one.

The trade-off: it's built for brokerages, not landlords or investors, and every product is quote-priced. Its newer AI features (Rethink+, Showcase AI) are promising but still maturing.

What's good

  • Purpose-built for CRE brokers, not a generic CRM adapted for real estate
  • AI features across prospecting, marketing, and CRM that save real time
  • Modular — start with one product, expand as you grow
  • Large user base means active development and community

What's not

  • Quote-based pricing across all products — no published prices
  • Not built for landlords or investors
  • Newer AI features are still maturing
  • Less suited to enterprise portfolio management

Pricing: Modular — start with one product and expand. Contact for a custom quote.

2. Crexi

Best for: Listings and marketplace reach without CoStar money

Full Profile →

Crexi is a marketplace first and a broker tool second, but it earns a spot here because the free tier alone is useful: basic listings, property search, and limited data access at zero cost. With 500,000+ active listings, it has become the credible alternative to LoopNet, with a noticeably more modern interface. Paid tiers add enhanced listings, deal management, analytics, and lead tracking, up to Crexi Intelligence and API access at the enterprise level.

Be honest with yourself about your market, though. Crexi's inventory is still thinner than CoStar/LoopNet in some markets, and institutional players tend to default to the incumbents.

What's good

  • Free basic access — any broker can start today
  • Modern, intuitive interface vs. legacy platforms
  • Marketplace, analytics, and deal management in one place
  • Strong mobile experience

What's not

  • Smaller listing inventory than CoStar/LoopNet in some markets
  • Premium pricing not publicly disclosed
  • Data depth still maturing vs. CoStar
  • Less recognition among institutional players

Pricing: Free tier available. Pro and Enterprise (Crexi Intelligence, API): contact for pricing.

3. RealNex

Best for: Individual brokers who want everything in one suite

Full Profile →

RealNex is the most complete integrated suite in this category: CRM, financial analysis, presentation and marketing tools, transaction management with virtual deal rooms, and a marketplace, all in one platform. Its RX Data layer (property records, ownership, loans, tenants) feeds predictive analytics that flag likely buyers and sellers, and 60+ presentation templates keep your output looking consistent.

The knock on it is polish. The interface feels dated next to Buildout, and its market share and community are smaller. But for a solo broker or small team who wants one login instead of five, it's genuinely more affordable than assembling the equivalent from enterprise tools.

What's good

  • Most complete all-in-one suite: CRM, analysis, marketing, transactions
  • RX Data predictive analytics identify likely buyers and sellers
  • 60+ branded presentation templates
  • Affordable for individual brokers vs. enterprise platforms

What's not

  • Pricing not publicly listed
  • UI can feel dated vs. newer competitors
  • Smaller market share and community
  • Learning curve to use the full suite well

Pricing: Subscription, monthly or annual. Not publicly listed. Free trial available.

4. ClientLook (LightBox)

Best for: Brokers who want a CRM that just works, today

Full Profile →

ClientLook is the anti-Salesforce: a CRE-specific CRM you can be productive in on day one, with contact management, property tracking, deal pipeline, and activity logging in a deliberately simple interface. Brokers at CBRE, Lee & Associates, and Stirling use it, and since it's now part of LightBox, it plugs into that company's property data and the RCM deal-marketing platform.

Simplicity cuts both ways. It has less marketing and analytics muscle than Buildout, and being inside LightBox may nudge you toward the rest of that ecosystem. If your problem is "my CRM is empty because it's a pain to use," that trade is worth it.

What's good

  • Purpose-built for CRE brokers — simple, ready to use
  • Very quick setup, no elaborate configuration
  • Access to LightBox property data and RCM
  • Strong testimonials from major brokerages

What's not

  • Simpler than enterprise CRMs like Salesforce or Buildout
  • Pricing not publicly available
  • Limited marketing and analytics features
  • Ties you into the LightBox ecosystem

Pricing: Subscription, monthly or annual. Not publicly available. Free trial available.

5. TheAnalyst PRO

Best for: Investment analysis and OMs on a transparent budget

Full Profile →

TheAnalyst PRO combines investment analysis, lease analysis, demographics, offering memorandums, flyers, and brochures in one subscription, and — rare in this category — tells you exactly what it costs: $699.99/year, or $89.99/month plus a $49.99 setup fee. It handles 5- and 10-year cash flows before and after tax, lease-vs-own analysis, and demographic infographics, replacing the spreadsheet-and-InDesign grind with branded reports in minutes.

It is not ARGUS, and it doesn't pretend to be. Complex institutional valuations and portfolio-level analysis are out of scope, and there's no real CRM or deal management. For a broker producing BOVs and OMs every week, that's usually fine.

What's good

  • All-in-one analysis plus marketing in one subscription
  • Professional reports in minutes, not hours
  • Transparent, affordable pricing
  • 7-day free trial; cloud-based, works on any device

What's not

  • Less powerful than ARGUS for complex institutional work
  • Smaller user base than the big platforms
  • Limited CRM/deal management features
  • Not designed for portfolio-level analysis

Pricing: $699.99/year (no setup fee), or $89.99/month + $49.99 setup. Free trial available.

6. CommissionTrac

Best for: Brokerages drowning in commission spreadsheets

Full Profile →

CommissionTrac does one unglamorous thing: it tracks deals, calculates commission splits, manages invoices and installments, and generates broker statements. If your brokerage still reconciles splits in Excel — and most do — this is the kind of tool that pays for itself the first time it catches an error that a spreadsheet wouldn't have.

It's a niche product from a small Austin company, so don't expect it to run your whole brokerage or integrate with everything. It handles commissions. That's the pitch.

What's good

  • Purpose-built for CRE commission tracking — a real pain point
  • Eliminates spreadsheet-based commission errors
  • Automated calculations for complex split structures

What's not

  • Niche — commissions only, not full brokerage management
  • Small company, limited market presence
  • Pricing not publicly available
  • Limited integrations with major CRE platforms

Pricing: Subscription, monthly or annual. Not publicly available. Free trial available.

7. SharpLaunch

Best for: Property marketing and lead intelligence

Full Profile →

SharpLaunch handles the digital-marketing side of brokerage: professional property websites without a web developer, email campaigns, lead capture, and — its most underrated feature — document tracking that shows you exactly which prospects are actually reading your OM. That engagement data turns a cold follow-up list into a ranked one.

It is marketing-only. No CRM, no deal management, and its document portal is basic compared to a proper virtual data room. Pair it with a CRM rather than expecting it to be one.

What's good

  • Purpose-built for CRE marketing, not a generic tool
  • Document tracking reveals seriously interested prospects
  • Property websites without a developer
  • Engagement analytics surface hot leads

What's not

  • Pricing not publicly available
  • Not a CRM or deal management platform
  • Smaller company, limited integrations
  • Document portal is basic vs. virtual data rooms

Pricing: Subscription, monthly or annual. Not publicly available.

The split to notice: Buildout and RealNex want to be your whole stack, Crexi wants to be your marketplace, and ClientLook, TheAnalyst PRO, CommissionTrac, and SharpLaunch each solve one problem well. Most brokerages end up with one platform plus one or two point tools.

The bottom line

If you're building a brokerage stack from scratch, start with Buildout and add Crexi's free tier for marketplace exposure. Solo broker on a budget? RealNex or ClientLook plus TheAnalyst PRO covers CRM, analysis, and marketing for less. And if your back office is still calculating splits by hand, CommissionTrac is the least exciting and most immediately useful purchase on this page.

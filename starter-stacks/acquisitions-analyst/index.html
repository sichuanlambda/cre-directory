Starter Software Stack for an Acquisitions Analyst at a Small Firm

An analyst's week is screening deals, cleaning up rent rolls and T-12s, and backing up rent and value assumptions. Cheap tools now handle the tedious parts. The expensive data platforms are worth it only once deal volume or asset type demands them.

Start with

The essentials for day one. These make up the lean total.

A

Adventures in CRE A.CRE model library

Underwriting model

Free acquisition models for multifamily, office, retail and industrial in Excel, the format partners and lenders expect.

FreeFree models, per our listing record; the vendor site blocks automated checks.

Alternatives: TheAnalyst PRO ($699.99 per year or $89.99/mo plus setup); Property Metrics (web-based underwriting, pricing on request)

C

CRE Data Extractor Pro

Rent roll and T-12 extraction

Converts rent roll PDFs to spreadsheets, 200 pages a month. The free plan's 15 pages total is enough to test it on a real offering memorandum first.

$20/moPro $20/mo billed monthly, 200 rent roll pages a month. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Clik.ai (AI underwriting and extraction, pricing on request); Lobby CRE (AI deal screening, pricing on request)

R

RentCast Pro, billed annually

Rent comps

Residential rent estimates with 20 comps per search; enough to check a multifamily pro forma at screening stage.

$12/mo$12/mo billed annually ($19 billed monthly). Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Rentometer (rent comps by address; the vendor site blocks automated checks)

PropertyShark Pro, billed annually

Ownership and property records

Owners, sales history, property reports and list building, with deepest coverage in large metros.

$50/mo$599.95 per year billed annually = $50.00/mo, 1 seat; New York State price, other regions differ. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Reonomy (owner and debt data, pricing on request)

Add when

Worth buying once the trigger applies. These, minus anything they replace, make up the fuller total.

H

HelloData Standard, 1 user

Rent comps

Add when you underwrite multifamily most weeks and need unit-level comps and concessions. Replaces RentCast in the fuller total.

Unit-level multifamily rent surveys, trends and exports, with 10 saved surveys per user.

$300/mo$300 per user per month, billed monthly, 1 user. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: RentCast (keep it for single-family and small-property checks)

C

Compstak Enterprise

Lease and sale comps

Add when you buy office, industrial or retail and need verified lease comps for every deal.

Verified lease and sale comps by market. Firms that do not contribute comps to the free Exchange buy Enterprise access, which is priced by quote.

Pricing on requestEnterprise pricing on request, per our listing record. Not rechecked against the vendor site.

Alternatives: CoStar (the broadest comps database, pricing on request)

P

Plotzy Starter

Site and zoning screening

Add when your firm buys land, ground-up or value-add sites where zoning decides whether the deal works.

Screen sites by zoning designation, lot size and use before you build a model, and pull owner contacts for off-market outreach. Two-level zoning on every parcel answers the first question an investment committee asks about a development site. Caveats: it is in beta at pre-launch pricing, owner contacts are metered (25 a month on Starter, 100 on Production at $300/mo), and it has no comps or debt data.

$180/moStarter $180/mo billed monthly, about 1,000 parcel lookups and 25 owner contact lookups a month; 7-day free trial, no card. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Zoneomics (code-level zoning answers and reports, web plans from $92/mo); Regrid (nationwide parcel data with a zoning add-on)

Frequently Asked Questions

What software does an acquisitions analyst at a small firm need?

Free Excel models such as the A.CRE library, a rent roll extractor (CRE Data Extractor Pro, $20/mo), rent comps (RentCast Pro, $12/mo billed annually) and owner records (PropertyShark Pro, $50/mo billed annually). That lean stack is $82 a month at published prices.

Do I need ARGUS to underwrite deals?

Only for multi-tenant office and retail where lenders and partners expect ARGUS cash flows. Multifamily and simple net lease deals are underwritten in Excel almost everywhere, and ARGUS pricing is quoted.

What does the fuller analyst stack add?

Unit-level multifamily comps (HelloData Standard, $300 per user per month) in place of RentCast, and verified commercial lease comps from CompStak Enterprise, which is priced by quote and not totaled, plus Plotzy Starter ($180/mo) for zoning-led site screening if your firm buys land or development sites. The published-price total is $550 a month.

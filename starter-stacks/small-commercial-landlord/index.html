Starter Software Stack for a Small Commercial Landlord

Commercial leases are where small landlords lose money quietly: missed escalations, CAM that never gets reconciled, options nobody tracked. The first purchase is a system built around commercial leases and recoveries. Maintenance software and lease visibility tools follow when a building engineer, a lender or a broker asks for them.

Who this is for

An owner-operator of a few office, retail or light industrial buildings, roughly 20 to 50 tenant suites, keeping books in QuickBooks or similar, with a part-time building engineer. Totals assume 4 buildings and 30 suites.

Jobs the stack must cover

Leases, rent and CAM

Commercial lease terms, escalations, recoveries and rent collection.

Work orders and maintenance

Tenant requests and preventive maintenance with a history per building.

Lease visibility

Stacking plans, expirations and critical dates to share with brokers and lenders.

Frequently Asked Questions

What software does a small commercial landlord need?

A system built for commercial leases, escalations and CAM recoveries. StrataFolio Essential is $210 a month billed annually at 30 suites, which is the whole lean stack at $210. DoorLoop and Yardi Breeze commercial are published alternatives with $199 and $200 monthly minimums.

Can residential landlord software handle commercial leases?

Some can, but check CAM reconciliation, percentage rent and escalations before you commit. Tools built for single-family rentals often treat every lease as a fixed monthly rent, which is where commercial owners lose recoveries.

What does the fuller commercial landlord stack add?

Work order software for two users (UpKeep Essential, $48 a month) once a building engineer or several vendors are involved, and stacking plans for brokers and lenders (Stackplanner Starter, $99 a month billed annually). Together that is $357 a month.

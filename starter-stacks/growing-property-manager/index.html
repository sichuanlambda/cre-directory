Starter Software Stack for a Growing Property Management Company

At this size the property management system is the business: trust accounting, owner statements, leasing and maintenance all run through it, and switching later is painful. Choose it first and carefully. Add process automation and inspection software when growth and staff turnover make consistency the bottleneck.

Lean stack from $1,490/mo · Fuller stack from $4,174.50/mo · How the totals add up

Who this is for

A third-party residential manager with roughly 500 to 2,000 doors across single-family and small multifamily, a team of about 8 to 15, and plans to keep adding owners. Totals assume 1,000 doors.

Jobs the stack must cover

System of record

Trust accounting, owner and tenant portals, leasing and maintenance in one place.

Growth and processes

Owner leads, onboarding, renewals and turns run as tracked processes.

Inspections

Consistent move-in, move-out and routine inspections with photo evidence.

Showings

Self-guided and agent showings scheduled without phone tag.

Maintenance coordination

Triage, vendor dispatch and resident updates for work orders.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does property management software cost at 1,000 doors?

At published starting prices, AppFolio Core is from $1.49 per unit, or from $1,490 a month at 1,000 doors, before its minimum spend and onboarding. DoorLoop One works out to $1,600 a month at 1,000 doors on yearly billing, and Rentvine is advertised from $1.50 per unit. Get written quotes from at least two.

What should a growing manager add after the PM system?

Process automation for owner growth and onboarding (LeadSimple, $1,684.50 a month at 1,000 doors on an annual commitment) and consistent inspections (RentCheck, $1.00 per door). With both, the stack is from $4,174.50 a month, plus showing and maintenance tools that are priced by quote or unclear units and not totaled.

Should a 1,000-door manager move to Yardi Voyager or Entrata?

Usually not yet. Those platforms are built for institutional owners and on-site multifamily teams and are sold by quote with long implementations. Revisit when you manage large conventional or affordable communities, not scattered doors.

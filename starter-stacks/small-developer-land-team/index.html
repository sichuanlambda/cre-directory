Starter Software Stack for a Small Developer or Land Acquisition Team

Small development teams win or lose at sourcing: finding parcels that fit before anyone else, and killing bad sites quickly. Spend first on finding and qualifying land. Feasibility studies, verified zoning reports and project controls come later, one trigger at a time.

Who this is for

A developer or acquisitions team of one to three people sourcing infill or land sites in a handful of counties, with one project in design or construction at a time.

Jobs the stack must cover

Site sourcing

Searching parcels by zoning, size and location, and reaching owners.

Field parcel lookups

Parcel lines and ownership on a phone while you drive sites.

Development underwriting

Land residual, construction budget and returns in a model you control.

Project budget and payments

Budgets, contracts and payment tracking once a project starts.

Zoning verification

Code-level answers on uses, setbacks and height before an LOI.

Feasibility and massing

Fast test fits for unit counts and parking yields.

Start with

The essentials for day one. These make up the lean total.

P

Plotzy Starter

Site sourcing

Nationwide parcel search with zoning filters, owner contact lookup, folders, notes and CSV export, so you can sweep a county for sites that fit before you drive it. Caveats: it is in beta at pre-launch pricing, owner contacts are metered (25 a month on Starter), and it has no comps or debt data. Production ($300/mo) raises owner contacts to 100 a month and adds Zoning AI chat.

$180/moStarter $180/mo billed monthly, about 1,000 parcel lookups and 25 owner contact lookups a month; 7-day free trial, no card. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Regrid (nationwide parcel data app and API; plan prices load only after sign-in); LandID (parcel and owner maps from $7 per user per month billed annually, best for rural land)

L

Landglide Personal, billed annually, 2 users

Field parcel lookups

Parcel lines and owner names on your phone, with unlimited lookups. Cheap enough that everyone who drives sites should have it.

$16.67/mo$99.99 per year per user = $8.33/mo x 2 users ($9.99/mo each billed monthly). Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: LandID ($7 per user per month billed annually)

A

Adventures in CRE A.CRE model library

Development underwriting

Free development and land residual models in Excel. Lenders and equity partners will want to see your assumptions, and a spreadsheet keeps them visible.

FreeFree models, per our listing record; the vendor site blocks automated checks.

Alternatives: REFM (Excel development models, one-time purchase); Top Shelf Models (Excel models, one-time purchase)

M

Mastt Starter (free)

Project budget and payments

Your first project is free, with unlimited users, budgets and contracts and up to 10 tracked payments: enough to replace a budget spreadsheet before construction gets busy.

FreeStarter plan free, first project, up to 10 payments. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Northspyre (owner-side development management, pricing on request); JobTread (builder-side project management, $159/mo billed annually)

Add when

Worth buying once the trigger applies. These, minus anything they replace, make up the fuller total.

Z

Zoneomics Essentials

Zoning verification

Add when you need code-level zoning answers before an LOI more often than you can call the planning desk.

25 zoning searches a month and one analyst-verified zoning report. Advanced ($279/mo) gives 150 searches. Annual billing is advertised at 20% off, but the annual price is not shown.

$92/moEssentials $92/mo billed monthly, 1 user, 25 searches. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Gridics (zoning and parcel analytics, pricing on request)

T

TestFit Parking Solver

Feasibility and massing

Add when you test more than a couple of sites a month and need unit counts and parking yields fast.

Unlimited users at a monthly price. Site Intelligence (+$150/mo) and Pro Forma (+$170/mo) are add-ons; Site Solver, the full building configurator, starts at $15,000 a year.

$195/moParking Solver $195/mo billed monthly, unlimited users, no add-ons. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Alternatives: Forma Site Design ($185/mo or $1,500/yr per our listing; the vendor site blocks automated checks); Giraffe (feasibility and site analysis, self-serve price not published)

Mastt Professional

Project budget and payments

Add when a project moves into construction with contracts, change orders and payment claims to track. Upgrades the Starter (free) plan above.

Cost, risk, schedule and dashboard modules with flexible month-to-month licences.

$165/mo$165 per project per month on average, billed monthly, 1 active project. Checked October 25, 2026 on the vendor pricing page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What software does a small land acquisition team need first?

A way to search parcels by zoning and reach owners, a phone app for parcel lines in the field, and a model you trust. At published prices, Plotzy Starter ($180/mo), LandGlide for two people ($16.67/mo billed annually) and the free A.CRE models come to $196.67 a month, with Mastt's free plan for the first project budget.

Is TestFit worth it for a small developer?

Once you test more than a couple of sites a month. Parking Solver is $195/mo with unlimited users; before that, a civil engineer's test fit on your best site may be the better spend. With TestFit, verified zoning searches and project controls, the fuller stack is $648.67 a month.

Do I need Procore as a developer?

Usually not on the owner side. Procore is priced on annual construction volume and is typically the contractor's platform; owners get project access through the GC. Owner-side budget and payment tracking can live in a lighter tool.

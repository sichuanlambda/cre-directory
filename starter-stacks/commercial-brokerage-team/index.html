Starter Software Stack for a Commercial Brokerage Team

Once a team shares listings, splits and a marketing coordinator, spreadsheets start losing deals. The first money goes to a shared CRM and a back office that tracks deals and commissions. Owner data, deal rooms and premium listing placement come next, each when a specific workload justifies it.

Lean stack $1,135/mo · Fuller stack $1,810/mo · How the totals add up

Updated October 25, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Who this is for

A commercial brokerage or team of about 5 to 20 agents with a coordinator, doing leasing and investment sales, that wants shared records instead of each agent's own spreadsheet. Totals assume 10 agents.

Jobs the stack must cover

Shared CRM and pipeline

One record per owner, tenant, property and deal, visible to the whole team.

Deals and commissions

Deal records, splits, commission tracking and a shared listings database.

Listing marketing

Brochures, OMs and email campaigns produced by a coordinator for every agent.

Listing exposure

Syndicating listings to the marketplaces buyers and tenant reps search.

Ownership research

Owner lookups and call lists for systematic prospecting.

Deal rooms

NDAs, OM downloads and buyer activity for investment sales.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a small commercial brokerage team buy first?

A shared CRE CRM and a back office for deals and commissions. At published prices, ClientLook for 10 agents ($890/mo billed annually), TotalBrokerage Lite ($100/mo for 10 seats) and CREbuilder Pro for 3 publishing seats ($145/mo) come to $1,135 a month, with listings on Crexi and LoopNet at no cost.

Should every agent get their own data subscription?

Usually not at first. Two dedicated research seats, run by the people who build call lists, tend to get used more than ten seats that sit idle. The fuller stack adds two PropertyShark Pro seats, a FirmRoom deal room and one Plotzy seat for the land or development lead, for $1,810 a month in total.

Is Buildout worth it for a team?

It is the common choice for CRE marketing at larger shops and publishes a starting rate of $125 per user per month. For a team where one coordinator produces most materials, a tool that charges only publishing seats can cost less; compare both on your real seat count.

About CRE Software Directory

An independent directory of commercial real estate software: what each tool does, who it's for, and what it costs.

How listings are made

Every listing follows the same process, whether a vendor submits it or we add it ourselves. We verify the company exists and the product is live, research it from the product's own site plus independent sources, and write the listing: description, features, pricing as published, and honest pros and cons. We don't invent numbers. If pricing isn't public, the listing says so. If a product shuts down or gets acquired, the listing says that too, and unverifiable products don't get published.

What's paid and what isn't

Listings are free for every real CRE product, and corrections are always free. Two things generate revenue: Featured placement, which is clearly labeled paid positioning and never changes how a product is described, and partner links on some listings, which are disclosed on the page and never affect content. Comparisons, buyer's guides, and category pages are written the same way for every product, paid or not.

Corrections

Spot something outdated? Email hello@cresoftware.tech and we'll fix it, typically within two business days.

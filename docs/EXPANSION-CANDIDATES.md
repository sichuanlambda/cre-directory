against the 218 existing listings and across categories: **719 unique new candidates**.

Status: FOR REVIEW. Nothing here goes on the site until approved; the content
pipeline (verify company is real -> research -> draft listing) runs per product
after that, same as submissions.

Two proposed NEW categories are included: **Lending & Debt** and
**Energy & Sustainability** (both had 40+ real products and existing orphan
listings that fit them better than their current homes).

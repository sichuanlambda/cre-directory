# Vendor outreach templates

Contact emails live in Formspree/Gmail, not in this repo. Tone: short, plain,
no hype. Send from hello@cresoftware.tech or Nathan's address.

## 1. "Your listing is live" (to accepted submitters)

Subject: Your [Tool] listing is live on CRE Software Directory

Hi [name],

Thanks for submitting [Tool]. Your listing is live:
https://cresoftware.tech/products/[slug]/

A couple of things you can do with it:

- Add the "Listed on" badge to your site (embed code is at the bottom of your
  listing page). It links straight back to your listing.
- If anything in the listing is off (pricing, features, positioning), reply
  and we'll fix it. Free, always.

One more thing: if you want [Tool] pinned to the top of the
[category] category and featured on the homepage, Featured placement is
$49/month: https://cresoftware.tech/advertise.html
It's labeled as placement and doesn't change the listing content, it just
puts you where buyers look first.

Either way, glad to have [Tool] in the directory.

[sign-off]

## 2. "Claim your listing" (to vendors listed but who never submitted)

Subject: [Tool] is listed on CRE Software Directory

Hi,

We run CRE Software Directory (cresoftware.tech), a directory of 213
commercial real estate software tools. [Tool] has a listing:
https://cresoftware.tech/products/[slug]/

If anything is out of date (pricing, features, screenshots), reply and we'll
fix it, free. You can also grab the "Listed on" badge from the bottom of the
page.

If you'd like [Tool] featured at the top of its category, details are at
https://cresoftware.tech/advertise.html.

[sign-off]

## Notes

- Send template 1 within a day of a listing going live; it converts best while
  the submitter is paying attention.
- Follow-up once after ~1 week if no reply, then stop.
- Track who was pitched in a private sheet, not in this repo.

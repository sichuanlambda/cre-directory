# Affiliate programs among listed tools

Researched and link-verified 2026-08-30 (48 tools checked, agents fetched each program page).
Workflow: sign up → get tracking link → add `"affiliate_url"` to the product record in
`data/products.json` → `node scripts/build.js` → commit. The product page CTAs switch
automatically with `rel="sponsored"` and an on-page disclosure.

## Notes

- **dealmachine**: Confirmed: '20% to 50% lifetime commissions on every subscription you refer', recurring for as long as the subscriber stays. Application form on page, reviewed within 24 hours. Specific tier thresholds not published.
- **airdna**: Direct fetch returns HTTP 403 (bot blocking), but search results confirm the page is live and the claim matches: 30% commission on new accounts via your link plus 10% second-tier on sub-referrals. Program also surfaces at ambassador.upfluence.co/airdna-affiliates and us-rewards.airdna.co.
- **stessa**: Matches claim: 20% commission on eligible purchases (Stessa Pro subscriptions and RentPrep screenings) for a full 2 years from initial referral. Joinable via HasOffers; referrals have 30 days to create a free account to qualify as a lead.
- **rentec-direct**: Confirmed exactly as claimed: 25% of gross revenues for the referred customer's first 12 months plus a $10 lead bonus per completed free-trial signup. Join via electronic affiliate agreement form on the page.
- **innago**: Page live; states 'Earn Up To 25% Commission' with 60-day cookie and no reward limits. Per-transaction example (e.g. $6.88 per $27.50 tenant fee, annualized) implies ongoing earnings, though 'ongoing while active' is not stated verbatim.
- **doorloop**: Matches claim exactly: $50 per completed demo + $200 per new subscribing account, up to $250 combined per referral. Runs via Impact; referred customers get 10% off first year. No paid search/social ads, no self-referrals.
- **hemlane**: Page exists and states a flat $250 per customer (within the claimed $200-250 range); targets realtors/brokers, coaches, and creators, and says over $160k paid out to affiliates. The separate $500-per-landlord agent figure is not on this page — the page mentions a separate agent referral option for REALTORS/brokers without stating its amount.
- **turbo-tenant**: Direct fetch returns HTTP 403 (bot blocking), but search results confirm the page is live ('Get Paid to Refer TurboTenant') and describes a joinable affiliate program run through Impact. Commission rates are not publicly disclosed (custom/tiered structures), so 'unknown' is accurate. 90-day click cookie, monthly payouts via Impact.
- **rentredi**: Page exists and is accepting applications. States 'Earn 20% in commission for each qualified subscription' — clarified as up to 20% of the first-time purchase (not recurring). Free to join, 30-day cookie, no minimum threshold.
- **mashvisor**: Page exists; program runs through Awin and is free to join. Page states 20% commission when visitors sign up for a paid plan — worded as 20% on first payment, not recurring. The 40-50% network-listing figures are not on the page.
- **avail**: Active affiliate program page with Join Now button (via Commission Junction). Page says only 'Generous commissions' and 'Earn money for each qualified new Avail user that you refer' — the $20-25 directory figure is not on the page and could not be verified.
- **tenantcloud**: Page live and describes a joinable program, but payouts are slightly higher than claimed ($18/$35/$60 vs $17/$32/$55). Affiliates must be existing TenantCloud landlords or property managers. No referral limit.
- **buildium**: Page exists and describes a joinable affiliate program (for websites/blogs referring traffic) plus a customer referral program, but the claimed $10/lead + 25% first-year terms appear nowhere on it. Only stated figure: 'up to $400 in account credits per referral' for existing customers. Affiliate terms live behind a separate 'Learn more' link.
- **fundrise**: affi.io directory page exists and confirms the Fundrise affiliate program is open and run exclusively on Impact.com, but the page itself states no commission terms. Third-party sources (LinkClicky, CommissionDex) corroborate roughly $50-$100 per funded account with a ~30-day cookie, consistent with the claim's caveat that the exact rate is set inside Impact. Note this is a third-party directory, not a Fundrise-owned page.
- **tensorpix-real-estate-video-maker**: Page live and program joinable, but claimed terms are wrong on two counts: commission applies to every payment for 12 months, not just the first purchase, and the minimum payout is $30, not EUR 50.
- **stratafolio**: Confirmed: $100 per friend who successfully signs up, explicitly 'no limit, no questions asked'. Flow is form -> referred person demos and signs up -> $100 paid.
- **re-leased**: Original URL (re-leased.com/company/partner) returns 404. Live partner page at /partnerprogram states a 10% referral reward if the referred company becomes a customer, and the partner referral agreement (global.re-leased.com/re-leasedpartnerreferralagreement) confirms 10% of first-year signed subscription value — matches the claim.
- **oncomply**: Page live and describes a joinable partner program that pays 'when a qualified introduction becomes a customer', but publishes no commission rates or amounts, so 'unknown' is accurate. Terms presumably shared after applying.
- **leasequery**: FinQuery (formerly LeaseQuery) referral-partner page exists and is joinable via a partner form; it promises 'another recurring revenue stream' for referral partners but states no commission rates or amounts anywhere. Commission remains unknown.
- **covercy**: Matches claim exactly: $500 per referral when the referral becomes a paying Covercy One customer and makes their first payment; no cap. Open to GPs, sponsors, and family offices who aren't already Covercy customers or in prior sales conversations.

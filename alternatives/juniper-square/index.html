Best Juniper Square Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Juniper Square is an investment management and fund administration platform for private markets GPs. It handles fundraising, investor onboarding, the investor portal, reporting, treasury and compliance, and it pairs the software with an in-house fund administration service that oversees more than $300 billion in assets. Its JunieAI tools and a new admin oversight agent automate review work for CFOs and controllers. The platform serves more than 2,000 GPs, and that scale explains most of the reasons people look elsewhere. Pricing is quote-based and tied to assets under management, which gets expensive for syndicators raising a few million per deal. Implementation is complex, especially for firms with legacy systems, and adoption demands real change management. It is also fund-centric, so operators who mainly want a clean investor portal and distributions engine pay for depth they will not use.

Think about which slice of Juniper Square you rely on. If it is the investor portal, CRM and distributions, several purpose-built platforms cover that at lower cost with faster setup. If it is outsourced fund administration, you are choosing a service provider as much as software, and switching costs are real. If the core need is raising capital rather than managing it, a marketplace approach can substitute for owning the investor relationship. Match the replacement to the workflow, not to the feature list.

Top Juniper Square Alternatives

1.
I

InvestNext

Real estate investment management platform for GPs and sponsors.

Why pick it over Juniper Square: InvestNext is the most direct replacement for GPs and syndicators. It covers the full capital lifecycle: fundraising with capital calls and co-sponsor structures, an investor portal and CRM, distributions and cap table management, K-1 delivery and KYC/AML checks. It serves firms from small to enterprise, which makes it more accessible than Juniper Square for emerging sponsors. Pricing still requires a demo, and its enterprise market share is smaller.

2.
A

Agora Real Estate

Real estate investment management software with accounting services.

Why pick it over Juniper Square: Agora combines investor management software with real estate accounting services, which is the closest match to Juniper Square's software-plus-service model for mid-market sponsors. It includes fundraising, investor onboarding in minutes, an investor portal and built-in ACH and cross-border payments, and it has more than 900 customers. Pricing is not published, and it is focused on investor management rather than property operations.

3.
I

Investor Management Services

Investor reporting and communication software.

Why pick it over Juniper Square: IMS, now part of RealPage Investment Management, is an established alternative for institutional firms and larger syndicators. Its strengths are automated waterfall calculations, custom statement generation, fundraising pipeline tracking and self-service investor dashboards, and it serves 550+ customers and 85,000 investors. Pricing is enterprise-level and quote-based, and being inside RealPage means some firms will face a platform transition to use it fully.

4.
C

Covercy

Investment management platform with embedded banking for CRE.

Why pick it over Juniper Square: Covercy is the pick for sponsors whose main pain is moving money. It embeds banking in the investment management platform, so capital calls, waterfall distributions and payments run from one place without manual wires. A free tier lets small operators start without a contract. It is a smaller vendor with less market recognition, and firms with entrenched banking relationships may not want to shift accounts to use its core feature.

5.
C

CrowdStreet

Direct access to private market real estate investing.

Why pick it over Juniper Square: CrowdStreet is a different approach: a private markets marketplace rather than software you run. Sponsors list deals to a pool of accredited investors and use CrowdStreet's sponsor tools for fundraising and investor relations, which can replace the capital-raising part of Juniper Square. Investors pay nothing; sponsors carry the fees. CrowdStreet faced regulatory scrutiny after the 2023 Nightingale Properties fraud case, and you do not own the investor relationship in the same way.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best alternative to Juniper Square for syndicators?

InvestNext is the most common choice because it covers fundraising, investor portals, distributions and compliance for sponsors of all sizes, with pricing set by demo rather than assets under management.

How is Juniper Square priced?

Pricing is quote-based and depends on assets under management and the services required, including whether you use its fund administration team. No public price list is available.

Is there a free alternative to Juniper Square?

Covercy offers a free tier for basic investment management, which is enough for a small sponsor to run an investor portal. Full-featured alternatives such as InvestNext and Agora require paid subscriptions.

Does Juniper Square manage properties?

No. Juniper Square manages funds, investors and capital. Property operations, leasing and accounting need a property management platform such as Yardi or MRI.

Best Propertyware Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Propertyware is RealPage's property management software for single-family rental management companies, built around customization: custom fields, custom dashboards and unlimited custom reports, portfolio-level accounting, multi-location management, owner and tenant portals, and an open API for two-way data exchange. Pricing is published per unit: Basic $1, Plus $1.50 and Premium $2 per unit per month, with monthly minimums of $250, $350 and $450, a one-time implementation fee of twice the monthly subscription, units billed in 50-unit increments, and API access as a $1 per unit add-on. That structure explains most departures: small managers pay the minimum regardless of door count, the implementation fee, API surcharge and transaction fees on screening and payments add to the real cost, the customization demands configuration time a small team lacks, and some managers prefer a vendor focused on their segment over one product among many at RealPage.

Decide by size and by what you value in Propertyware. Professional single-family managers who chose it for accounting depth and an open API should look at the newer per-unit platforms built for third-party management, which publish pricing and include integrated trust accounting. Smaller managers paying the $250 minimum for a modest portfolio will save with flat-rate or low-minimum tools. And if your portfolio is shifting toward multifamily, commercial or associations, pick a system that covers those asset types natively rather than another single-family specialist.

Top Propertyware Alternatives

1.
R

Rentvine

Property management software for professional managers: trust accounting, portals, leasing, AI, and open API.

Why pick it over Propertyware: Rentvine is the closest modern match for professional single-family managers: fully integrated trust accounting, an open API and MCP server, AI-drafted owner statements, maintenance coordination between tenants and vendors, unlimited custom fields and next-day ACH funding. Pricing starts as low as $1.50 per unit per month with a 30-unit minimum for new accounts, plus a one-time setup fee covering migration and a dedicated onboarding specialist. There is no free trial, and the setup fee amount is not published.

A

AppFolio

AI-native property management platform that delivers real performance through unified data and agentic AI.

Why pick it over Propertyware: AppFolio is the scale alternative with published per-unit pricing: Core from $1.49, Plus from $3.20 and Max from $5.00 per unit per month, with a 50-unit minimum on Core and minimum spend on every tier. Plus adds custom fields and a read-only API; Max adds a leasing CRM and read/write API. It suits growing third-party managers who want AI automation across accounting, leasing and maintenance, but the upper tiers cost more per unit than Propertyware and there is no free trial, only a demo.

Why pick it over Propertyware: Rentec Direct suits smaller managers paying Propertyware's minimum for a modest door count. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Propertyware cost?

Propertyware publishes per-unit pricing: Basic $1, Plus $1.50 and Premium $2 per unit per month, with monthly minimums of $250, $350 and $450. Implementation costs twice the monthly subscription, Enterprise/API access adds $1 per unit per month, units are billed in 50-unit increments, and screening and payments carry transaction fees.

Who owns Propertyware?

Propertyware is a RealPage company. RealPage, which is backed by Thoma Bravo, also sells the OneSite property management system and a wide range of rental housing software, while Propertyware stays focused on single-family property management companies.

What is the cheapest Propertyware alternative for a small portfolio?

Rentec Direct starts at $50 a month for property managers (Rentec PM, with trust accounting), and Yardi Breeze starts at $1 per unit with a $100 monthly minimum on an annual agreement. Both cost less than Propertyware's $250 minimum for a small portfolio.

Does Propertyware offer a free trial?

Yes, a limited-access trial with no credit card: you request login credentials through a form and Propertyware follows up. Buildium and Rentec Direct offer self-serve trials of 14 days and two weeks, while Rentvine, AppFolio and Rent Manager sell through demos and quotes.

Best BatchLeads Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

BatchLeads is a list building, skip tracing and outreach platform for residential investors and wholesalers, built by BatchService and acquired with BatchDialer by PropStream, a Stewart Information Services company, in July 2025. It still sells as its own product, now branded "powered by PropStream": 10,000 to 75,000 leads a month on published plans of $119, $349 and $749, owner phone numbers and emails included on every plan, BatchRankAI lead scoring, comps, and direct mail, with a 7-day trial for individual plans. People leave for predictable reasons. The AI pieces cost extra (Dialer AI at $89 a month, Reia AI at $19), full BatchRankAI signals start on the $349 plan, every tier caps leads and exports with 4 cent overages, and annual prepay renews automatically unless you cancel before the anniversary. The ownership change matters too: buyers who left PropStream for BatchLeads now find the same parent company behind both, and the data model is still built for residential motivated-seller lists rather than commercial or land work.

Pick a replacement by the job BatchLeads does for you. If it is a single seat pulling lists and calling owners, compare all-in-one tools on how many contacts each plan really includes. If it is driving neighborhoods, choose the strongest field app. If you need raw skip trace and property data for your own CRM at volume, buy a feed instead of seats. And if your targets are commercial buildings or land sites, switch to a tool built around ownership entities or zoning rather than residential distress flags.

Top BatchLeads Alternatives

1.
P

PropertyRadar

160M+ property records with owner contacts, 30+ distress signals, and built-in multi-channel marketing.

Why pick it over BatchLeads: PropertyRadar is the strongest independent all-in-one alternative: 160M+ properties and 318M+ mortgages across 3,000+ counties, 30+ distress signals, automated alerts when a property's status changes, and campaigns by phone, text, email, mail and online ads, with API access on every paid plan. Solo costs $119 a month or $99 on annual billing, with a 5-day trial. Included contacts are modest (250 phones and emails a month on Solo, 4 to 8 cents for more), so heavy skip tracers should price the Team or Business tier.

D

DealMachine

Nationwide property and owner data with skip tracing, a Driving for Dollars app, direct mail and API access for investors.

Why pick it over BatchLeads: DealMachine is the alternative for field-first investors, with the Driving for Dollars app, owner lookups, skip tracing and pay-per-piece postcards on every plan, plus API and CLI access for teams that automate. Plans are per seat with monthly data credits from $119 a month, and a free account lets you explore with results locked. Its current plans do not list a built-in dialer, so BatchLeads users who live in Dialer AI will need a separate calling tool.

3.
P

PropStream

AI-enhanced nationwide property data, lead generation, comps, skip tracing, and marketing tools for investors.

Why pick it over BatchLeads: PropStream is the larger sibling: 160M+ property records, 308M+ deeds, 185M+ mortgages, 165+ filters and 20 pre-built lists, plus comps, mobile apps and a marketing suite, from $99 a month or $81 on annual billing with a 7-day trial. It suits users who want deeper record history than BatchLeads offers. Skip tracing is extra on Essentials (12 cents per contact) and it shares an owner with BatchLeads, so it is not an escape from that company.

4.
P

Plotzy

AI parcel search with two-level zoning, owner contacts and exports across 150M+ US parcels.

Why pick it over BatchLeads: Plotzy is the pick if your deals are land or commercial sites where zoning decides value. It searches 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties by permitted use, lot size, land use, owner type and sale date, shows two-level zoning on every parcel, and adds owner contact lookup, folders and CSV export, month to month at $180 to $300 with a 7-day trial and no card. It does not replace BatchLeads for wholesaling: owner contacts are metered (25 a month on Starter, 100 on Production), there are no skip-traced lists at scale, no dialer, direct mail, comps or mortgage and equity data, and it is still labeled beta.

5.
B

BatchData

Bulk property data and APIs: ownership, AVMs, mortgages, permits, skip tracing, and contact enrichment.

Why pick it over BatchLeads: BatchData is the data business BatchService kept when BatchLeads was sold: property data, skip tracing, reverse skip tracing and contact enrichment by API or bulk delivery, with published plans from $1,000 a month for 100,000 property records and $2,000 a month for 100,000 skip traces. It is self-funded and independent of PropStream. Choose it for high-volume teams with an engineer and their own CRM; it has no list-building interface for a solo investor.

6.
C

Casafy AI

Computer-vision platform that scans street-level imagery to surface distressed, off-market properties for investors.

Why pick it over BatchLeads: Casafy AI replaces virtual driving with computer vision: it scores the condition of properties from street-level imagery to surface visibly distressed houses, and layers on live MLS data, comps, owner information and skip tracing. It suits investors whose best leads come from what a house looks like rather than a public-records flag. Pricing is not published on the site, and condition scores depend on the areas it has already processed.

7.
R

Reonomy

CRE intelligence platform combining exclusive data partnerships with machine learning.

Why pick it over BatchLeads: For commercial prospectors, Reonomy, an Altus Group business, covers 53M+ US commercial properties with 200+ filters, owners resolved behind LLCs and trusts, ranked phone and email contacts, and sales and debt history. Monthly and annual plans include 1,000 exports a month and a trial is available on request. Plans start at $400 a month on an annual subscription, and it has no dialer, mail or residential distress lists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BatchLeads owned by PropStream?

Yes. PropStream, a Stewart Information Services company, acquired BatchLeads and BatchDialer in July 2025. BatchLeads remains a separate product with its own subscription, and its site now reads "powered by PropStream". BatchData was not part of the sale.

Does BatchLeads include skip tracing?

Yes. Every plan enriches saved leads with owner phone numbers and emails. Leads, exports or imports beyond the plan's monthly limit cost 4 cents each, and Dialer AI ($89 a month) and Reia AI ($19 a month) are paid add-ons.

What is the cheapest BatchLeads alternative?

PropertyRadar starts at $99 a month on annual billing ($119 monthly), and PropStream Essentials at $99 a month, though skip tracing costs extra there. DealMachine offers a free account to explore before paid plans from $119 a month.

Does BatchLeads require an annual contract?

No. Growth, Professional and Scale can be paid monthly and cancelled before the next cycle, with no partial refunds. Annual prepay saves 40% and renews automatically unless cancelled before the anniversary; Enterprise is annual only.

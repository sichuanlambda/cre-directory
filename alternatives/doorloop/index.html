Best DoorLoop Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

DoorLoop is cloud property management software for landlords, small managers and investors, founded in Miami in 2019. It bundles rent collection, TransUnion screening, lease e-signatures, maintenance tracking, accounting with QuickBooks sync, listing syndication and an AI assistant, and it supports residential, commercial, HOA and mobile home portfolios. Its published pricing is the main reason people compare alternatives. Starter is $69 per month on annual billing, or $99 month to month, and covers only 10 units, with additional units billed at $3 each. Pro, which unlocks QuickBooks sync, workflows and AI features, is $149 per month on annual terms. That structure is expensive for a landlord with three doors and adds up quickly past a few hundred units, and Premium carries onboarding fees. The most useful automation also sits behind the Pro plan.

To pick a replacement, decide whether you are a self-managing landlord or a professional manager. Self-managers with a handful of units are well served by free or flat-rate tools that cover screening, leases and rent collection. Managers who need trust accounting, owner statements and a professional feature set should look at established mid-market platforms with published per-unit or tiered pricing. Only mixed commercial portfolios need something heavier.

Top DoorLoop Alternatives

1.
B

Buildium

Property management software.

Why pick it over DoorLoop: Buildium is the professional-grade alternative for third-party managers. It starts at $62 per month with a 14-day trial and adds owner reporting, trust accounting, community association tools and Lumina AI for communications and bill scanning, and it scales past 15,000 units. It is residential-first with limited commercial support, and its $400 Premium tier is where the open API and full automation suite live.

2.
R

Rentec Direct

Property management software for landlords and property managers.

Why pick it over DoorLoop: Rentec Direct offers the lowest-cost professional plan. Starter is $25 per month for up to 10 properties, and the Pro and PM editions start at $50 per month with unlimited properties, free ACH for tenants, and trust accounting and owner portals on the PM edition. Support is US-based and award-winning. The interface looks dated compared with DoorLoop and the mobile app is more limited.

3.
T

TenantCloud

Cloud-based property management software.

Why pick it over DoorLoop: TenantCloud is the closest budget match for landlords with fewer than 60 leases. Starter is $15 per month on annual billing for up to 10 leases, Growth is about $29 for 30 leases and Pro is $50 for 60, with unlimited properties on every plan and a 14-day trial. Plans are capped by lease count rather than units, and QuickBooks sync and property manager tools only appear on Pro and above.

Not sold on TenantCloud either? See its alternatives →

4.
T

TurboTenant

Free property management software for landlords.

Why pick it over DoorLoop: TurboTenant is the free option for self-managing landlords. Its free plan covers listing syndication, applications, TransUnion screening, rent collection and maintenance tracking, and more than 500,000 landlords use it. Paid tiers add state-specific leases, unlimited e-signatures and faster payouts and are billed annually. It is not designed for managers handling third-party properties, and accounting depth is thin next to DoorLoop.

5.
I

Innago

Free property management software for landlords.

Why pick it over DoorLoop: Innago is free with no monthly fee, setup fee or unit limit, and it covers rent collection, screening, leases, maintenance and basic accounting with a mobile app. Support is consistently praised in reviews. The trade-offs are a narrower feature set, few third-party integrations and no equivalent to DoorLoop's workflow automation, so it fits landlords and small managers rather than growing management companies.

6.
H

Hemlane

Property management software for remote landlords.

Why pick it over DoorLoop: Hemlane takes a different approach: software plus optional human services. Its higher tiers add 24/7 repair coordination, tenant placement and eviction mediation, which suits remote or out-of-state owners who want DoorLoop-style tools without doing the work themselves. Pricing is per unit plus a platform fee and climbs quickly on the full-service tier, approaching traditional management costs, and it is a smaller vendor than DoorLoop.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to DoorLoop?

TurboTenant and Innago both offer free plans covering listings, screening, leases, rent collection and maintenance. Innago has no unit limit, while TurboTenant reserves state-specific leases and unlimited e-signatures for annual paid tiers.

How much does DoorLoop cost?

Starter is $69 per month on annual billing ($99 monthly) for up to 10 units, with additional units at $3 each. Pro is $149 per month and Premium $209 per month on annual terms, and portfolios over 300 units need a custom quote.

Which DoorLoop alternative is best for professional property managers?

Buildium, from $62 per month, is the usual step up for third-party managers who need trust accounting and owner statements. Rentec Direct's PM edition, from $50 per month, is the lower-cost alternative.

Is TenantCloud cheaper than DoorLoop?

Yes for small portfolios. TenantCloud's Starter plan is $15 per month on annual billing for up to 10 leases, versus DoorLoop's $69 for up to 10 units, though TenantCloud caps plans by lease count.

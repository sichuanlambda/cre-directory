Best PropertyRadar Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

PropertyRadar is a property data and marketing platform that has tracked foreclosures since 2007 and now covers 160M+ properties, 318M+ mortgages and 1.1M+ listings across 3,000+ counties, with 30+ distress signals, automated alerts when a property changes status, and outreach by phone, text, email, mail and online ads. API access comes with every paid plan and an MCP server connects AI assistants. Published plans are Solo at $119 a month ($99 on annual prepay), Team at $249 ($199) and Business at $599 ($549), after a 5-day trial that requires a card and mobile number and does not allow exports or contact appends. The reasons people look elsewhere sit in the allowances: Solo includes 250 phones and emails a month, extra contacts cost 4 to 8 cents each, the dialer ($59 to $149) and SMS ($49 and up) are separate purchases, postcards are billed per piece, and extra users cost $25 to $50 a month. It also leans toward investors, agents and home-service businesses, so institutional commercial teams and land buyers find it thin.

Start with the part of PropertyRadar you depend on. If it is the contact volume behind your campaigns, compare how many phones and emails each alternative includes before comparing sticker prices. If it is field prospecting, a tool with a driving app fits better. If it is the API feeding your own systems, a data provider will scale further. And if your targets are commercial owners or land sites, choose a platform built around ownership entities or zoning rather than residential distress signals.

Top PropertyRadar Alternatives

1.
P

PropStream

AI-enhanced nationwide property data, lead generation, comps, skip tracing, and marketing tools for investors.

Why pick it over PropertyRadar: PropStream is the most direct swap for residential list building: 160M+ property records, 308M+ deeds, 185M+ mortgages, 165+ filters, 20 pre-built lead lists, comps and a marketing suite, from $99 a month ($81 on annual billing) with a 7-day trial and 50 free leads. Pro at $199 includes skip tracing through PropStream Connect. Essentials does not, at 12 cents a contact, and every plan caps monthly saves and exports.

BatchLeads

Property list building, skip tracing, AI dialing, and direct mail for high-volume investor prospecting.

Why pick it over PropertyRadar: BatchLeads fits users who outgrow PropertyRadar's contact allowances: every plan includes owner phones and emails on saved leads, starting at $119 a month for 10,000 leads, 20,000 exports and three users. AI lead scoring is built in and Dialer AI is an $89 monthly add-on. It is owned by PropStream since July 2025, residential in focus, and overages run 4 cents a record.

DealMachine

Nationwide property and owner data with skip tracing, a Driving for Dollars app, direct mail and API access for investors.

Why pick it over PropertyRadar: DealMachine adds what PropertyRadar lacks for field investors, a Driving for Dollars app with route tracking and instant owner lookups on every plan, alongside 150M+ properties, 240M+ people records, skip tracing, postcards and API access. Per-seat plans with monthly data credits start at $119 a month, and a free account lets you explore first. Contact reveals and exports draw down credits, and there is no built-in dialer listed on current plans.

Plotzy

AI parcel search with two-level zoning, owner contacts and exports across 150M+ US parcels.

Why pick it over PropertyRadar: Plotzy is the pick if your deals are land or commercial sites where zoning decides value: search 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties by permitted use, lot size, land use, assessed value and owner type, see two-level zoning on every parcel, then look up owner contacts and export to CSV. It is month to month at $180 to $300 with a 7-day trial and no credit card. It does not cover PropertyRadar's marketing side: owner contacts are metered (25 a month on Starter, 100 on Production), there are no distress signals, mortgage data, dialer, texting or mail, and it is still labeled beta.

CRE intelligence platform combining exclusive data partnerships with machine learning.

Why pick it over PropertyRadar: Reonomy, an Altus Group business, is the step up for commercial agents: 53M+ US commercial properties, 200+ filters, machine-learning ownership that resolves LLCs and trusts, ranked owner contacts and sales and debt history across 68M+ transactions. Monthly and annual plans include 1,000 exports a month, and a trial is available on request. Plans start at $400 a month on an annual subscription, and there is no built-in outreach.

National property data on 160M+ US properties via API, bulk files, and cloud shares, with AVMs and risk analytics.

It suits teams building their own prospecting or CRM workflows. Pricing is quote-based and it has no list-building or marketing interface.

PropertyShark

Comprehensive real estate data and analytics for CRE research.

It suits brokers and investors who need occasional ownership research rather than campaigns. Coverage is deepest in New York City and major metros, and it has no texting, dialer or automated alerts like PropertyRadar's.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does PropertyRadar cost?

Solo is $119 a month or $99 a month prepaid annually, Team is $249 or $199, and Business is $599 or $549. Extra phones and emails cost 4 to 8 cents each depending on plan, and the dialer and SMS are separate purchases.

Does PropertyRadar have a free trial?

Yes, a 5-day trial on all plans. It requires a credit card and mobile number, includes 5 phone numbers and 5 email addresses, and does not allow exports, document images or contact appends.

Which PropertyRadar alternative includes the most owner contacts?

BatchLeads includes phone and email skip tracing on every saved lead within its monthly limits, starting at 10,000 leads for $119 a month. PropStream Pro at $199 includes skip tracing through PropStream Connect.

What is the best PropertyRadar alternative for commercial prospecting?

Reonomy is built for commercial buildings, with owners behind LLCs, contacts and debt history. For land and development sites chosen by zoning, Plotzy searches parcels by permitted use and lot size with owner contact lookup, though its contacts are metered.

Best AirDNA Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

AirDNA is the most widely used short-term rental analytics platform, tracking Airbnb and Vrbo listings worldwide with market-level occupancy, daily rate and revenue data, a Rentalizer that estimates income for any address, and comp sets for benchmarking. It has a free tier, a Research plan at $34 per month billed annually or $125 monthly, a Host plan at $50 per month annually or $150 monthly, and a custom-priced Property Manager plan. It fits investors screening markets and hosts pricing against competitors. People look for alternatives because monthly billing costs three to four times the annual rate, accuracy drops in smaller and emerging markets, the manager tier has no published price, and Rentalizer estimates are only a starting point for underwriting.

Match the alternative to the decision you are making. If you are screening a purchase, a free address-level calculator may be all you need before you pay for anything. If you are comparing short-term against long-term rental returns, an investment analytics tool that covers both is more useful than deeper STR data alone. If you manage a portfolio, benchmarking built on verified reservation data from property management systems is more reliable than scraped calendars. And if the goal is pricing and channel performance, that lives in your property management software.

Top AirDNA Alternatives

1.
M

Mashvisor

Real estate investment property data and analysis platform.

Why pick it over AirDNA: Mashvisor is the closest replacement for investors: short-term and long-term rental estimates side by side with cap rates and cash-on-cash returns, a Market Finder and Property Finder across the US, AI dynamic pricing for hosts, and a data API. It publishes tiered plan prices. Accuracy thins in small and rural markets, API access sits on the expensive plans, and coverage is US-only.

2.
R

Rabbu

Free Airbnb calculator and market data with occupancy, ADR, and RevPAN projections for any US address.

Why pick it over AirDNA: Rabbu is the free option: an Airbnb calculator with occupancy, ADR, RevPAN and seasonalized monthly revenue for any US address, market pages with an ROI Score, and a Chrome extension that overlays projections on listings. It is enough for first-pass screening before paying for AirDNA. It is STR-only, US-only, and its projections are listing-based estimates that need validation.

3.
K

Key Data

Short-term rental and lodging benchmarking from direct PMS reservation data, hotel comps, and OTA coverage.

Why pick it over AirDNA: Key Data builds its benchmarks from verified reservation data via 80-plus property management system integrations, not scraped listing calendars, and layers in hotel comps and OTA market coverage. Choose it if you run a portfolio and need trustworthy pacing and occupancy comparisons. It is quote-based, sales-led, and aimed at managers and destination organizations rather than individual buyers.

4.
R

Rentometer

Rental pricing data and analysis for property owners and investors.

Why pick it over AirDNA: Rentometer answers the long-term rental side that AirDNA ignores: hyperlocal rent estimates, comparable rentals, batch processing across a portfolio, branded reports and an API. It fits investors deciding whether a property should be a traditional rental at all. Free access is very limited, plans are contact-for-pricing, and it does not cover short-term rental performance.

5.
G

Guesty

AI-powered short-term rental PMS with 60+ channel sync, automation, payments, and trust accounting.

Why pick it over AirDNA: Guesty is a different approach: a short-term rental property management platform with dynamic pricing, revenue management, a 60-plus channel manager, payments and trust accounting, starting at $9 per month for 1 to 3 listings. Choose it if your AirDNA use is really about pricing your own listings. Pro and Enterprise tiers are quote-based and the module breadth brings a learning curve.

6.
H

Hostaway

Vacation rental channel manager and PMS with automation, AI CoHost, and 100+ OTA integrations.

Why pick it over AirDNA: Hostaway suits professional managers who want pricing and revenue tools inside a full PMS: a channel manager across 100-plus OTAs, unified messaging, task automation and an AI CoHost for guest communication. It replaces the operational half of AirDNA's Property Manager plan rather than its market research. Pricing is quote-based by listing count, and single-listing hosts are routed to a separate path.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free alternative to AirDNA?

Rabbu offers a free Airbnb revenue calculator and market data pages for US addresses, and AirDNA's own free tier includes limited Rentalizer and Market Insights access.

How much does AirDNA cost?

AirDNA's Research plan is $34 per month billed annually or $125 month to month, the Host plan is $50 per month annually or $150 monthly, and the Property Manager plan is custom priced.

Which AirDNA alternative compares short-term and long-term rental returns?

Mashvisor provides both Airbnb and traditional rental estimates with cap rate and cash-on-cash calculations for the same property, while Rentometer covers long-term rent comps only.

Is AirDNA data accurate?

AirDNA compiles data from millions of Airbnb and Vrbo listings and is widely used, but accuracy can vary in smaller or emerging markets. Key Data uses verified reservation data from property management system integrations, which is more reliable for benchmarking a managed portfolio.

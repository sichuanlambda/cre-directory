Best Buildout Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Buildout is purpose-built for CRE brokerages, but shoppers hit a few consistent friction points in the data: quote-based pricing across all products with no published prices, a product best suited to brokerages rather than landlords or investors, newer AI features (Rethink+, Showcase AI) that are still maturing, and a deal-focused design less suited to enterprise portfolio management. For solo brokers and small shops, the inability to see a price before talking to sales is often the deciding annoyance.

When comparing alternatives, separate the three jobs Buildout bundles: CRM, marketing production, and prospecting data. Some competitors bundle them differently, others do one job well at a visible price. Solo brokers should prioritize transparent, affordable pricing and quick setup; larger brokerages should compare AI maturity, data quality, and how well each platform's workflow matches how their teams actually win and market listings.

Top Buildout Alternatives

1.
R

RealNex

Real estate solutions for professionals.

Why pick it over Buildout: The closest all-in-one rival: CRM, financial analysis, marketing, and virtual deal rooms in one suite, positioned as affordable for individual brokers versus enterprise platforms. Its interface can feel dated compared to Buildout, which is the acknowledged trade-off.

2.
S

SharpLaunch

Digital marketing platform for commercial real estate.

Why pick it over Buildout: Choose SharpLaunch if marketing is the only Buildout module you use: purpose-built CRE property websites, document tracking, and lead engagement analytics without buying a full CRM suite. It is marketing-only, with no CRM or deal management.

3.
C

ClientLook (LightBox)

CRE CRM purpose-built for commercial real estate brokers.

Why pick it over Buildout: A simpler CRM for brokers who find Buildout's suite heavy: very quick setup with no elaborate configuration, plus LightBox data access, with endorsements from CBRE and Lee & Associates teams. It has fewer marketing and analytics features than Buildout by design.

4.
T

TheAnalyst PRO

CRE investment analysis and marketing platform for brokers.

Why pick it over Buildout: Transparent pricing Buildout lacks: $699.99/year (7-day trial) for investment analysis plus marketing presentations. Good for solo brokers who need deal analysis and branded reports more than a full brokerage operating system.

5.
C

Crexi

Comprehensive CRE marketplace connecting buyers, sellers, brokers, and lenders.

Why pick it over Buildout: If listing exposure matters more than back-office software, Crexi's free marketplace tier plus paid promotion puts inventory in front of buyers directly, with deal management included. It does not replace a brokerage CRM or marketing production.

6.
P

Property Capsule

Digital asset management for real estate.

Why pick it over Buildout: For retail-focused teams, Property Capsule automates flyer generation and interactive site plans with an iPad tour app and a free 30-day trial. It is a retail-leasing niche tool rather than a full brokerage platform.

7.
B

Brevitas

Find off-market commercial real estate deals.

Why pick it over Buildout: Adds a free marketplace and network of 200,000+ members with built-in email marketing and landing pages, useful marketing reach at zero cost alongside or instead of Showcase. Listing quality on the open network varies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Buildout?

No free product replaces Buildout's CRM-plus-marketing suite. For free marketing reach, Brevitas lets you list and network at no cost with 200,000+ members, and Crexi's free tier covers basic listings and search. For the CRM side there is no strong free option in this space; the budget route is an affordable paid tool like TheAnalyst PRO at $699.99/year for analysis and presentations.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Buildout for solo brokers?

Buildout does not publish prices, which makes comparison hard, but two alternatives are explicitly positioned for individual brokers: RealNex markets its all-in-one suite as affordable versus enterprise platforms, and TheAnalyst PRO publishes transparent pricing at $699.99/year with a 7-day free trial. ClientLook is another simpler, faster-to-set-up CRM option, though its pricing is also unpublished.

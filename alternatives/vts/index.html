Best VTS Alternatives (2026)

VTS unifies leasing, marketing, tenant experience, and market data across 13B+ square feet, but it is built for large landlords, and the data says so: enterprise pricing is not accessible for small brokerages or individual brokers, quote-based pricing means no transparency until you talk to sales, teams coming from spreadsheets face a steep learning curve, and the platform is primarily office and commercial focused, with multifamily features that are newer.

Alternatives to VTS are mostly unbundlings of it. Few products replicate the whole leasing-to-tenant-experience stack, so the practical move is to identify which module you actually use, leasing pipeline, lease data, building operations, or tenant experience, and buy that piece from a focused vendor. Smaller landlords should also weigh whether a full property management platform with leasing features covers enough, since those are often easier to buy and implement than an enterprise leasing suite.

Top VTS Alternatives

1.
D

Dottid

Leasing workflow management software for CRE.

Why pick it over VTS: A focused alternative to VTS Lease: purpose-built leasing workflow management with dynamic portfolio projections, ARGUS integration, and a modern UX that replaces email-and-spreadsheet processes. It is leasing-only, without VTS's marketing and tenant experience modules.

2.
P

Prophia

AI-powered lease abstraction and management for commercial real estate.

Why pick it over VTS: If lease data accuracy is your VTS use case, Prophia's AI lease abstraction (with a free instant abstraction tool) and self-updating stacking plans attack the problem that 53% of rent rolls contain material errors. It is not a full leasing pipeline platform.

3.
H

HqO

First CRM purpose-built for CRE, delivering exceptional tenant experiences and measurable outcomes.

Why pick it over VTS: Replaces the tenant experience side (VTS Activate): a CRM purpose-built for CRE tenant experience with AI-powered analytics across single buildings to global portfolios. It is enterprise-focused and does not cover leasing pipeline management.

4.
B

Building Engines

AI-powered property operations platform built for CRE.

Why pick it over VTS: For day-to-day building operations, Building Engines (backed by JLL) covers work orders, COI tracking, inspections, and tenant experience. Note the JLL ownership may not suit firms working with competing brokerages, and it lacks leasing pipeline tools.

5.
O

Occupier

Lease management and transaction management for corporate tenants.

Why pick it over VTS: The tenant-side mirror of VTS: lease management and transaction workflows for corporate occupiers with ASC 842 compliance built in. Choose it if you sit on the occupier side of the table rather than the landlord side.

6.
R

Re-Leased

Cloud-based commercial property management software.

Why pick it over VTS: For smaller commercial landlords priced out of VTS, Re-Leased delivers cloud-native commercial property management with automated rent reviews and strong Xero and QuickBooks integrations. It is property management with lease tracking, not a leasing intelligence platform.

7.
M

MRI Software

Open and connected property management platform for commercial and residential real estate.

Why pick it over VTS: An enterprise alternative that bundles property management, lease intelligence, and investment management on an open platform with 200+ integration partners. It shares VTS's enterprise pricing and learning curve, so pick it for broader scope, not simplicity.

8.
Y

Yardi

The global leader in AI-enabled property management and real estate investment software.

Why pick it over VTS: For landlords wanting leasing inside a full management-and-accounting stack, Yardi covers the whole lifecycle, and Breeze gives smaller operators an entry point VTS does not offer. Voyager brings its own complexity and lock-in considerations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to VTS?

There is no free equivalent to VTS's leasing and asset management platform. The only notable free entry point in this space is Prophia Abstract, a free instant lease abstraction tool that covers one slice of the workflow, understanding what is in your leases. Everything comparable to VTS's core modules is quote-priced enterprise software, so small teams should look at right-sized paid tools instead.

Is there a cheaper alternative to VTS for small landlords?

VTS is explicitly enterprise-priced, so smaller landlords have options that, while mostly quote-based themselves, target smaller portfolios: Dottid for leasing workflow, Re-Leased for commercial property management with strong accounting integrations, and Prophia for lease data (with a free abstraction tool to start). None publishes pricing, but all are scoped, and typically priced, for narrower needs than a 13B-square-foot enterprise platform.

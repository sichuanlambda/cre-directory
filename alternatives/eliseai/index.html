Best EliseAI Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

EliseAI is an AI platform for housing operators (and, separately, healthcare practices) that handles prospect and resident conversations across text, email, chat and voice. Its housing products include LeasingAI for prospect management and AI-guided tours, ResidentAI for move-in, maintenance, renewals and delinquency, EliseCRM, VoiceAI and lease audits; voice runs in seven languages and written replies in 51. Named customers include Equity Residential and LeFrak. Pricing is not published and starts with a demo. People look at alternatives for practical reasons: the enterprise sales motion and quote-based pricing suit large portfolios better than small ones, many operators already pay for a CRM or property management system that now ships its own AI agents, running a separate AI layer adds another integration to maintain, and single-family or scattered-site managers often need showing logistics and self-guided tours more than conversational depth.

Start with where the AI needs to live. If you want one front-office system with AI built in, a multifamily CRM with a native leasing agent replaces both EliseAI and your current CRM. If you are committed to your property management system, its native AI is the least disruptive option. If marketing and lead generation are part of the problem, a platform that bundles ads, websites and an AI leasing agent covers more ground. And if you lease single-family homes, a showing automation tool with an AI add-on fits the workflow better.

Top EliseAI Alternatives

1.
F

Funnel Leasing

Multifamily front-office platform: renter-centric CRM, online leasing, resident portal, and 24/7 agentic AI.

Why pick it over EliseAI: Funnel is the closest all-in-one alternative: a renter-centric CRM with Fenix, its AI agent across email, phone, text and chat, plus online applications with fraud checks and a resident portal. Funnel says it powers 2 million units and integrates with major property management systems. Choosing it means replacing your CRM, not just adding AI, and pricing is quote-based.

2.
R

Respage

Multifamily lead-gen, AI leasing agent, chatbot, CRM, and reputation tools in one platform.

Why pick it over EliseAI: Respage bundles an AI leasing agent, chatbot, CRM and reputation management with paid social, Google Ads, SEO and apartment websites, and says it serves 10,000 apartment communities. It suits operators who want marketing and AI leasing from one vendor. Pricing is not published, and teams that already have an agency for ads and websites will pay for overlap.

3.
K

Knock

RealPage-owned multifamily leasing CRM: multi-channel messaging, self-scheduled tours, attribution and AI solutions.

Why pick it over EliseAI: Knock is a multifamily leasing CRM owned by RealPage since 2022, with multi-channel messaging across email, text, voice and chat, Knock Now self-scheduling, attribution and AI solutions. Its API-first design connects to any property management system. It fits operators that want a mature leasing CRM with AI added, though some Yardi or Entrata shops prefer to avoid a RealPage-owned front office.

4.
E

Entrata

Property management platform for multifamily, student, affordable and military housing with native Leasing, Maintenance and Payments AI.

Why pick it over EliseAI: Entrata is the option for operators already on its property management platform: Leasing AI, Maintenance AI, Payments AI and Renewals AI run natively alongside 100+ built-in agents, so there is no separate integration to maintain. It covers multifamily, student, affordable and military housing. It only makes sense if you run Entrata as your system of record, and pricing is by demo.

Not sold on Entrata either? See its alternatives →

5.
V

Venn

Resident management system and intelligence layer covering leasing, onboarding, daily living, concierge, and renewals.

Why pick it over EliseAI: Venn pairs an AI leasing agent, which it says replaces CRM, lead nurture and tour tools, with a resident app that includes an AI concierge, amenities, maintenance and packages. It suits large owner-operators focused on resident experience and retention, with customers such as Bozzuto and Related. It is built for big portfolios, and pricing is on request.

6.
S

ShowMojo

Leasing automation for property managers: syndication, scheduling, self-guided tours, and screening.

Why pick it over EliseAI: ShowMojo is the pick for single-family and scattered-site managers: listing syndication, showing scheduling, self-guided tours with its own lockboxes, screening, an AI Virtual Agent add-on and an AI outbound voice leasing agent. It publishes pricing from $1.30 per unit on annual Pro plans or $50 per listing. The AI add-on price is not published, and it does not cover resident communications after move-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is EliseAI priced?

EliseAI does not publish pricing. Quotes come through a demo and depend on the products and portfolio involved.

What is the cheapest EliseAI alternative?

ShowMojo is the only option here with published pricing, from $1.30 per unit on annual Pro plans or $50 per listing, with an AI Virtual Agent available as an add-on. The multifamily platforms on this list are all quote-based.

Should I use my property management system's AI instead?

If you run Entrata, its native Leasing, Maintenance, Payments and Renewals AI avoids a separate integration. Operators who want AI that works across several property management systems usually choose a standalone layer such as EliseAI or a PMS-agnostic CRM like Funnel or Knock.

Which alternative is best for single-family rentals?

ShowMojo is built around single-family and scattered-site leasing, with self-guided tours, lockboxes, screening and an AI Virtual Agent add-on. The other options focus on multifamily communities.

Best Buildium Alternatives (2026)

Buildium is a strong residential property management platform with transparent pricing, but its own trade-offs push some users to shop around. It is primarily residential with limited commercial property management features, pricing scales up significantly with portfolio size, and key capabilities are gated: several advanced features sit behind the $400/month Premium tier, and API access is only available on the highest plan. Small landlords paying $62/month for the Essential plan may find they use a fraction of it, while growing firms hit the tier walls.

When switching, sort yourself into one of three groups. Very small landlords can get core rent collection and screening free elsewhere. Managers with commercial or mixed portfolios should prioritize platforms that treat commercial as first-class rather than an afterthought. And larger operations comparing Growth or Premium pricing should test whether per-unit or flat-rate models come out cheaper at their size.

Top Buildium Alternatives

1.
A

AppFolio

AI-native property management platform that delivers real performance through unified data and agentic AI.

Why pick it over Buildium: The step up for growing residential firms: an AI-native unified platform proven with customers managing 14,000+ units, with automation that goes beyond Buildium's Lumina features. Pricing is quote-based and premium, so it is a capability upgrade rather than a savings play.

2.
D

DoorLoop

All-in-one property management software for residential and commercial properties.

Why pick it over Buildium: Covers both residential and commercial properties, unlike residential-focused Buildium, with published tiers from $69/month and migration measured in days. Its Zapier and open API integrations are not locked to the top plan the way Buildium's API is.

3.
I

Innago

Free property management software for landlords.

Why pick it over Buildium: Completely free with no hidden fees or unit limits, making it the obvious move for small landlords who find even Buildium's $62/month Essential plan more than they need. The feature set is lighter than paid competitors, which is the trade.

4.
T

TenantCloud

Cloud-based property management software.

Why pick it over Buildium: A free starter plan for up to 10 leases plus dedicated mobile apps for landlords, tenants, and owners. Good for small portfolios testing whether they need paid software at all; paid-tier pricing is less transparent than Buildium's.

5.
R

Rentec Direct

Property management software for landlords and property managers.

Why pick it over Buildium: At $2-2.50/unit/month with no minimum, a 100-unit portfolio costs far less than Buildium's Growth tier, and tenants get free ACH payments. The interface feels more dated, but US-based support is award-winning.

6.
R

RentRedi

Streamlined property management for landlords.

Why pick it over Buildium: Flat-rate pricing from $19.95/month (annual) with unlimited units means costs do not scale with portfolio size, directly addressing Buildium's scaling-price complaint. Accounting is lighter than a full suite, so pair it with your bookkeeping tools.

7.
H

Hemlane

Property management software for remote landlords.

Why pick it over Buildium: Built for remote and hands-off landlords, with 24/7 repair coordination available and tiers from $2/unit/month. Choose it if what you really want from software is fewer landlord tasks, not more accounting features.

8.
R

Rent Manager

Robust property management software.

Why pick it over Buildium: Supports residential, commercial, HOA, and self-storage in one system with 450+ reports and an open API, solving Buildium's limited-commercial and top-tier-API constraints in one move. Pricing requires a consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Buildium?

Innago is the strongest free option: completely free with no unit limits and consistently praised support, covering rent collection, screening, and basic operations. TenantCloud's free starter plan (up to 10 leases) and TurboTenant's free landlord plan are also solid for very small portfolios. Expect a lighter feature set than Buildium, especially in accounting and owner reporting.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Buildium for small teams?

Yes. Rentec Direct at $2-2.50/unit/month with no minimums undercuts Buildium for most small portfolios, and RentRedi's flat rate from $19.95/month covers unlimited units. Both are cheaper than Buildium's $62/month entry plan for small landlords, though Buildium remains competitive if you need its deeper accounting and its published, predictable tiering.

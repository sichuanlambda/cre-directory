Best Rent Manager Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Rent Manager is property management and accounting software from London Computer Systems, a Cincinnati company that has built it since 1986. It is known for range: residential, commercial, HOA, self-storage and mixed portfolios run on one system, with dual cash and accrual accounting, more than 450 built-in reports, an rmAppSuite Pro mobile app, Orion AI and an open API. That breadth is also why people look at alternatives. Pricing is not published and requires a consultation, so smaller operators cannot budget without a sales call. Setup is involved because nearly everything is customizable, and the interface feels dated next to cloud-native competitors. Teams that only manage residential units often find they are paying for flexibility they never touch.

The right replacement depends on which part of Rent Manager you actually use. If it is core residential management with solid accounting, a modern all-in-one platform with published pricing will feel simpler and cheaper to run. If you are a small landlord or a manager under 100 units, per-unit tools with monthly plans avoid a consultation entirely. If your portfolio is single-family heavy, a purpose-built SFR system may fit better. Only mixed or commercial-heavy portfolios need to stay in the enterprise tier.

Top Rent Manager Alternatives

1.
A

AppFolio

AI-native property management platform that delivers real performance through unified data and agentic AI.

Why pick it over Rent Manager: AppFolio is the most direct modern replacement for professional managers from roughly 50 units up. It covers leasing, screening, online payments, maintenance, accounting and owner reporting in a unified interface, with AI agents that automate communications and workflows, and it serves customers past 14,000 units. Pricing is quote-based like Rent Manager's, it skews residential, and the AI-native approach means retraining staff on new workflows.

2.
Y

Yardi

The global leader in AI-enabled property management and real estate investment software.

Why pick it over Rent Manager: Yardi Breeze is the option with published pricing for smaller operators leaving Rent Manager: $1 per residential unit and $2 per commercial unit per month, with $100 and $200 monthly minimums on an annual agreement. Breeze Premier adds job costing and custom reports. Larger or more complex portfolios move to Voyager, which is quote-based, takes months to implement and is harder to leave later.

Not sold on Yardi either? See its alternatives →

3.
B

Buildium

Property management software.

Why pick it over Rent Manager: Buildium suits residential managers who want transparent pricing and a fast start. Plans are published at $62, $192 and $400 per month with a 14-day free trial, and it covers leasing, screening, rent collection, maintenance, accounting and owner statements, plus community associations. It is residential-first with limited commercial support, and API access is restricted to the top tier, so it will not replicate Rent Manager's integration flexibility.

Not sold on Buildium either? See its alternatives →

4.
D

DoorLoop

All-in-one property management software for residential and commercial properties.

Why pick it over Rent Manager: DoorLoop is the lighter all-in-one for landlords and small managers who find Rent Manager heavy. Starter is $69 per month on annual billing for up to 10 units, with extra units at $3 each, and Pro adds QuickBooks sync, workflows, CAM charges and AI features. It supports residential, commercial and HOA portfolios. Per-unit charges add up beyond a few hundred units, and portfolios above 300 units need a custom quote.

Not sold on DoorLoop either? See its alternatives →

5.
P

Propertyware

Professional property management software for residential properties.

Why pick it over Rent Manager: Propertyware is the pick for single-family rental managers. It is built around SFR operations with custom fields, dashboards, multi-location management and an open API with two-way data exchange, and it is part of RealPage. Published pricing runs $1 to $2 per unit per month, but monthly minimums of $250 to $450 plus an implementation fee make it costly under about 200 units, and it is a poor fit for multifamily.

6.
R

ResMan

Property management software for multifamily and commercial properties.

Why pick it over Rent Manager: ResMan is the alternative for multifamily-only operators who want something simpler than Rent Manager. It covers accounting, reporting, leasing and resident communication with an interface that is easy to learn, and it handles conventional, affordable and student housing. Pricing is quote-based, and it does not attempt commercial, HOA or self-storage, so it only works if you can give up Rent Manager's mixed-portfolio range.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best alternative to Rent Manager?

AppFolio is the most common choice for professional property managers, and Buildium for smaller residential operators who want published pricing. Both are residential-first, so mixed commercial portfolios should compare Yardi or MRI.

How much does Rent Manager cost?

Rent Manager does not publish pricing. Costs are set by consultation based on unit count and modules, so budgeting requires a sales call.

Which Rent Manager alternative supports commercial and mixed portfolios?

Yardi is the strongest option, with Breeze covering commercial at $2 per unit per month and Voyager serving large mixed portfolios. DoorLoop also supports commercial properties with CAM charges at a lower price point.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Rent Manager for small landlords?

Yes. DoorLoop starts at $69 per month on annual billing, Buildium at $62 per month, and Yardi Breeze at a $100 monthly minimum, all with published pricing and no consultation required.

Best Entrata Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Entrata is a property management operating system for multifamily, student, affordable, military and manufactured housing operators, with more than 2.5 million units on the platform. It covers accounting, leasing, marketing, resident services, maintenance and utilities under a single login. Its recent releases lean hard into AI, with more than 100 operational workflows running as embedded agents, an OpenAI partnership, and an IPO filing that signals where investment is going. People shop for alternatives for a few specific reasons. Pricing is quote-based with no public rate card, which makes budgeting hard for operators under a few thousand units. The platform is broad and the learning curve is steep. It is built for multifamily first, so commercial features are less mature, and its closed ecosystem limits third-party integrations compared with vendors that publish open APIs.

Start by naming the workflow that matters most. If you want the same all-in-one scope with a bigger partner marketplace, the enterprise incumbents are the like-for-like options. If your team is 500 to 5,000 units and finds Entrata heavy, a multifamily-focused mid-market platform will be easier to run. If your portfolio mixes commercial, HOA or self-storage assets, look at systems that treat those as first-class property types rather than add-ons.

Top Entrata Alternatives

1.
Y

Yardi

The global leader in AI-enabled property management and real estate investment software.

Why pick it over Entrata: Yardi Voyager is the closest like-for-like replacement at enterprise scale, covering every property type Entrata does plus commercial, senior living and coworking with deeper support. Its RentCafe marketing and leasing suite matches Entrata's front end, and Yardi Breeze offers a published entry point from $1 per residential unit per month for smaller portfolios. Voyager implementations take months, the interface feels dated in places, and data lock-in makes leaving hard.

2.
R

Realpage

Commercial real estate software platform.

Why pick it over Entrata: RealPage is the other enterprise multifamily stack, and its revenue management and market data are stronger than Entrata's. It integrates leasing, spend management, utilities, resident services and marketing, with its Lumina AI automating routine tasks. It is quote-based and priced for large operators, and it has faced Department of Justice antitrust scrutiny over its rent-pricing algorithms, which some operators now factor into vendor selection.

Not sold on Realpage either? See its alternatives →

3.
R

ResMan

Property management software for multifamily and commercial properties.

Why pick it over Entrata: ResMan is the pick for mid-market multifamily operators who find Entrata heavy. It covers accounting, reporting, leasing and resident communication in an interface widely described as easy to navigate, and it supports conventional, affordable and student housing. Pricing is quote-based, its market presence is smaller than Yardi or RealPage, and it is not suited to commercial or single-family portfolios.

4.
A

AppFolio

AI-native property management platform that delivers real performance through unified data and agentic AI.

Why pick it over Entrata: AppFolio suits operators from roughly 50 units up to the low tens of thousands who want AI-driven automation in a cleaner interface than Entrata's. It unifies property management, investor reporting and workflow automation through its Realm-X engine, with leasing, screening, payments and maintenance included. Pricing is quote-based and considered premium, and moving to an AI-native workflow requires training and change management.

Not sold on AppFolio either? See its alternatives →

5.
R

Rent Manager

Robust property management software.

Why pick it over Entrata: Rent Manager is the alternative for mixed portfolios. It treats residential, commercial, HOA and self-storage properties as first-class, runs cash and accrual accounting simultaneously, ships 450+ reports and exposes an open API, which directly addresses Entrata's closed ecosystem. London Computer Systems has built it since 1986, so the platform is mature. Pricing is quote-based, and setup takes work because of how customizable it is.

Not sold on Rent Manager either? See its alternatives →

6.
M

MRI Software

Open and connected property management platform for commercial and residential real estate.

Why pick it over Entrata: MRI is the choice when commercial assets matter as much as apartments. It covers commercial and residential property management, lease intelligence, investment management and facilities, with an open platform of 200+ integration partners and both cloud and on-premise deployment. It is enterprise-priced and complex, and product sprawl from years of acquisitions can make the experience inconsistent across modules.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the closest alternative to Entrata?

Yardi Voyager and RealPage are the two enterprise multifamily platforms with comparable scope. Yardi covers more property types, while RealPage is stronger in revenue management and market data.

Does Entrata publish pricing?

No. Entrata pricing is quote-based and depends on unit count and the modules selected. Competitors such as Yardi Breeze and Buildium do publish starting prices for smaller portfolios.

Is Entrata good for commercial real estate?

Entrata does support commercial properties, but it is designed around multifamily and its commercial features are less mature. Operators with significant office, retail or industrial assets usually prefer MRI, Yardi or Rent Manager.

Which Entrata alternative works best for mid-sized multifamily operators?

ResMan and AppFolio are the usual choices for portfolios from a few hundred to several thousand units, since both are simpler to run than Entrata and still cover accounting, leasing and resident services.

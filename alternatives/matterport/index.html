Best Matterport Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Matterport is the default platform for 3D digital twins in real estate, used for listing tours, floor plans, facilities documentation and construction coordination. It has a free tier, smartphone capture, paid plans from $11.49 per month for 5 active spaces up to $277.99 for 100, and integrations with LoopNet, Zillow, VTS and Autodesk. People look for alternatives for a few consistent reasons. Per-space pricing grows quickly across a large portfolio, the best results still require a Matterport camera costing $3,000 or more, the models are cloud-dependent and need good bandwidth, and CoStar Group's acquisition of Matterport, completed in early 2025, has some users rethinking vendor concentration.

Which Matterport feature you rely on decides the replacement. If it is measured floor plans and reports, a capture system built for accuracy will beat it. If it is simple 360 tours for listings, a lower-cost tour builder that works with any 360 camera is enough. If it is jobsite documentation, construction-specific capture tools do that job better. And if it is polished listing media in general, a per-image editing service may cost less than a subscription you rarely use.

Top Matterport Alternatives

1.
I

iGUIDE

Scanning camera plus platform for measured floor plans, 3D tours, and property reports, priced per project.

Why pick it over Matterport: iGUIDE is the closest replacement for the measurement side of Matterport: one capture with its PLANIX camera produces a 3D tour, ANSI-compliant floor plans with a stated 0.5 percent uncertainty, and exports to DWG, CAD and Revit. Pricing is per project with no subscription, and users own their data. The catch is buying the proprietary camera at $3,499 CAD or hiring an iGUIDE Pro.

2.
C

CloudPano

Drag-and-drop 360° virtual tour software with hotspots, live video chat, white labeling, and website embeds.

Why pick it over Matterport: CloudPano is the budget route for listing tours: upload 360 photos from any camera, link scenes with hotspots, add dollhouse floor plans, and publish or embed, from $22.50 per month billed annually with a free tier. It suits agents and property managers who need tours, not digital twins. The entry plan caps you at 3 published projects and team features sit on the pricier Commercial tier.

3.
R

RICOH360 Tours

Cloud 360° tour software with AI image enhancement, virtual staging, floor plans, and MLS-compliant sharing.

Why pick it over Matterport: RICOH360 Tours pairs a free tier and a $39 per month Pro plan, billed annually, with unlimited tours, AI image enhancement, virtual staging and a floor plan generator. It is a good fit if you already shoot with a THETA camera. Floor plans cost $20 each beyond the first order and extra team seats are paid add-ons, so it is best for small teams.

4.
B

BoxBrownie.com

Pay-per-image platform for listing photo enhancement, virtual staging, day-to-dusk edits, and floor plan redraws.

Why pick it over Matterport: BoxBrownie is a different approach: instead of software, you order per-image edits such as virtual staging, day-to-dusk, floor plan redraws, 3D renders and 360 image editing, priced from 2 euros per image with no subscription. Choose it when your Matterport plan is really paying for listing media. Human editors mean a 24 to 48 hour turnaround rather than an instant model.

5.
O

OpenSpace

Construction site monitoring with AI.

Why pick it over Matterport: OpenSpace replaces Matterport specifically for construction progress capture: 360 cameras, phones or drones walk the site and imagery is auto-mapped to floor plans and BIM models with timestamps, plus integrations with Procore and Autodesk. It fits general contractors and owners tracking builds, not marketing. Pricing is quote-based and it needs a consistent capture routine to pay off.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Matterport still an independent company?

No. CoStar Group completed its acquisition of Matterport on February 28, 2025, in a deal valued at about $1.6 billion, and Matterport now operates as part of CoStar Group.

What is the cheapest alternative to Matterport for virtual tours?

RICOH360 Tours and CloudPano both offer free tiers, with paid plans at $39 and $22.50 per month respectively when billed annually. Both build tours from 360 photos rather than a dedicated Matterport camera.

Do I need a special camera to replace Matterport?

Not always. CloudPano and RICOH360 Tours accept 360 photos from consumer cameras, and Matterport itself supports smartphone capture. iGUIDE, by contrast, requires its PLANIX camera or a hired iGUIDE Pro because its floor plans depend on measured scans.

Which Matterport alternative produces accurate floor plans?

iGUIDE produces ANSI/RMS-compliant floor plans with a stated measurement uncertainty of 0.5 percent and exports to DWG, CAD and Revit formats, which makes it the usual pick for appraisal, insurance and construction workflows.

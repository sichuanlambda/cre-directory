Best Compstak Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

CompStak's crowdsourced exchange produces lease comp detail, effective rents, TI, free rent, lease terms, that is hard to find anywhere else, and it is free for contributing brokers and appraisers. The reasons teams look elsewhere are in its own data: comp quality depends on member contributions and can be uneven in smaller markets, enterprise pricing is not public, there is limited property-level data beyond transaction comps (no building operations data), and the overall dataset is smaller than CoStar's, making it better for specific deals than broad market overviews.

When switching, name the gap precisely. Thin coverage in your market points to researcher-driven or larger databases. Needing ownership, debt, or building-level context around comps points to property-intelligence platforms. Portfolio-level benchmarking and forecasting is a different product category again. Many firms keep CompStak for granular deal data and add a second source for breadth rather than replacing it outright.

Top Compstak Alternatives

1.
C

CoStar

The leading commercial real estate information, analytics, and online marketplace platform.

Why pick it over Compstak: The coverage answer: a 1,000+ person research team maintains the most comprehensive CRE database, giving consistent data in markets where CompStak's member contributions run thin. CompStak's own data notes CoStar lacks its granular effective-rent detail, and the price is premium with annual contracts.

2.
M

Moody's REIS

CRE market analytics, forecasting, and risk assessment by Moody's.

Why pick it over Compstak: Choose Moody's for market-level analytics rather than deal-level comps: gold-standard forecasting with proprietary econometric models and deep historical series. It is expensive, enterprise-priced, and less useful for property-level decisions.

3.
R

Reonomy

CRE intelligence platform combining exclusive data partnerships with machine learning.

Why pick it over Compstak: Complements or replaces CompStak when you need context around the deal: true ownership behind LLCs, debt maturity, and AI-connected property profiles, with a free trial. Its lease data is explicitly weaker than CompStak's, so this is a different lens, not a superset.

Not sold on Reonomy either? See its alternatives →

4.
P

PropertyShark

Comprehensive real estate data and analytics for CRE research.

Why pick it over Compstak: At $60/month, a budget research option for owner lookup, sales records, and list building, versus CompStak's undisclosed enterprise pricing for non-contributors. Strongest in NYC and major metros, and it has nothing like CompStak's effective-rent granularity.

Not sold on PropertyShark either? See its alternatives →

5.
M

MSCI Real Capital Analytics

Global commercial property transaction data and analytics.

Why pick it over Compstak: For institutional investors benchmarking sales activity, MSCI Real Capital Analytics offers the most comprehensive global transaction database and 80+ indexes. It is institutional-only, very expensive, and backward-looking rather than deal-support data.

6.
C

CRED iQ

CRE and CMBS loan data, analytics, and valuation platform for investors, lenders, and brokers.

Why pick it over Compstak: Fills the debt-side gap: CMBS and CRE loan analytics with property-level financials, delinquency, and maturity tracking that CompStak's comps do not cover. It is focused primarily on CMBS, so coverage of other debt is limited.

7.
C

Crexi

Comprehensive CRE marketplace connecting buyers, sellers, brokers, and lenders.

Why pick it over Compstak: A free starting point for market awareness: marketplace search plus Crexi Intelligence analytics on 500,000+ active listings. Its data depth is still maturing, so treat it as breadth on a budget rather than an appraisal-grade comp source.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to CompStak?

CompStak is unusual in that it already has a strong free path: brokers, appraisers, and researchers who contribute their own comps get exchange access at no cost. No competitor offers comparable lease-comp data for free. Crexi's free tier provides basic market visibility, but if you qualify as a contributor, CompStak's own exchange is generally the best free lease-comp option available.

Is there a cheaper alternative to CompStak for small teams?

If your team includes deal-doing brokers or appraisers, contributing comps to CompStak's exchange is effectively cheaper than any subscription. For teams that cannot contribute, PropertyShark at $60/month is the most affordable adjacent research tool, covering ownership and sales records, though not effective-rent lease detail. Full lease-comp coverage without contributing generally means quote-based enterprise pricing at CompStak or CoStar.

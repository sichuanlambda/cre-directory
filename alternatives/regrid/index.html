Best Regrid Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Regrid sells standardized land parcel data: 100% of US parcels in one schema, plus Canadian parcels and coverage in 22 countries in total, enriched with matched building footprints, standardized zoning, daily ownership updates and secondary addresses. It is delivered by API and tiles, an Esri-compatible feature service, bulk files, a self-serve Data Store and an MCP server, with a web and mobile Property App on top that is free to start. The reasons people shop around come from that data-first design. The app is built for looking up and saving parcels, not for running a prospecting workflow with owner contacts. Self-serve API plans cover modest volumes, and anything above 10,000 records a month moves to enterprise licensing. The API terms bar using the data to train AI models. And teams that only need one state, or a phone app in the field, pay for national depth they do not use.

Start with the slice of Regrid you actually use. If it is the parcel feed for your own GIS or software, compare other licensed data providers on coverage, schema, delivery and contract terms. If it is the Property App for finding sites and their owners, an application with zoning search and owner contacts will save more time than raw data. If it is a quick parcel check on a phone, a low-cost mobile app does the job. And if your work stops at the California state line, a California-only service is deeper for less money.

Top Regrid Alternatives

1.
L

LightBox

Commercial property data and workflows: parcels, structures, zoning, true owners and environmental due diligence, via apps, APIs and bulk data.

Why pick it over Regrid: LightBox is the closest like-for-like on the data side: parcels, structures, zoning, land use and true-owner records tied together by the LightBox ID, delivered as bulk data, APIs, GIS feature services, Snowflake shares or MCP. Its LightBox Live workspace covers 156M parcels for investors working deals, and environmental due diligence sits in the same company. Choose it when you need enterprise parcel data with commercial workflows around it. Pricing is quote-based and the buying process is enterprise-shaped, so it is rarely the cheaper option.

2.
P

Plotzy

AI parcel search with two-level zoning, owner contacts and exports across 150M+ US parcels.

Why pick it over Regrid: Plotzy is the app-first answer for brokers, land acquisition teams and developers who use parcel data to find sites rather than to feed a GIS. It searches 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties by permitted use, lot size, land use, assessed value, owner type and sale date, with two-level zoning on every parcel, owner contact lookup, folders and CSV export, at $180 to $300 a month with a 7-day trial and no credit card. It is still labeled beta, sub-level zoning depth varies by municipality, owner contacts are metered and API access sits on the custom Scale plan, so it does not replace Regrid as a bulk data source.

3.
A

ATTOM

National property data on 160M+ US properties via API, bulk files, and cloud shares, with AVMs and risk analytics.

Why pick it over Regrid: ATTOM suits data teams that want parcels plus everything recorded against them: ownership, deeds, mortgages, foreclosures, building permits, boundaries and AVMs on 160M+ US properties, delivered by API, bulk files, Snowflake, Databricks or MCP. A 30-day API trial lets you test coverage before talking price. Licensing is negotiated per use, and zoning is not part of its published dataset list, so it complements a parcel geometry layer more than it replaces one.

4.
L

Landglide

Mobile and web parcel app: boundaries and owner info on 157M+ parcel records in 3,200+ counties, from $9.99 a month.

Why pick it over Regrid: LandGlide is the low-cost field companion: tap a parcel on your phone to see boundaries, owner, mailing address, acreage and sale data across 157M+ parcel records in 3,200+ counties, with offline access on iOS and Android. It costs $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year after a 7-day free trial, which makes it the obvious swap for people who mainly use Regrid's mobile app. It is a viewer, not a data feed, zoning search or prospecting tool.

5.
L

Land id

Land and rural mapping: parcel and owner info in all 50 states, comps, soil reports, branded maps and 3D tours.

Why pick it over Regrid: Land id, formerly MapRight, is built for land and rural brokers: parcel and owner info in all 50 states, 40+ data layers, comparable sales, soil reports and mailing lists, plus branded maps and 3D tours for marketing listings. Plans start at $7 per user per month billed annually, after a 7-day free trial with no card required. It is a mapping and marketing tool rather than a data product, and it is thin for dense urban commercial work.

6.
P

ParcelQuest

Daily-updated California parcel, ownership, and zoning data covering 13M parcels in all 58 counties.

Why pick it over Regrid: ParcelQuest is the better choice if your work is in California only: about 13 million properties across all 58 counties, updated daily from county assessors, with owner details, APN maps, recorded documents and zoning data. Access starts at $99.95 for 7 days, then $199.95 to $249.95 a month per user, with bulk data and an ArcGIS gateway on quote. Per-item fees apply for documents and exports beyond plan allowances, and it has nothing outside California.

7.
P

PropertyRadar

160M+ property records with owner contacts, 30+ distress signals, and built-in multi-channel marketing.

Why pick it over Regrid: PropertyRadar fits residential investors, agents and service businesses who use parcel data to find owners and contact them: list building, owner phones and emails, email sends, property monitoring and integrations in one account. Plans run $99 to $549 a month billed annually, or $119 to $599 monthly, with a 5-day trial. Phone and email quotas are modest on lower tiers and do not roll over, and it is a marketing tool rather than a source of standardized parcel geometry or zoning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Regrid free to use?

Partly. The Regrid Property App lets you look up parcels and view boundaries without a credit card, and a free account unlocks more; paid Starter, Pro and Team plans and volume-priced exports sit above that. API access runs on self-serve monthly plans with a 30-day Interactive API Sandbox, and needs above 10,000 records a month go to enterprise licensing.

What is the cheapest Regrid alternative?

For parcel and owner lookups, LandGlide costs $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year and Land id starts at $7 per user per month billed annually. Both offer 7-day free trials. Neither sells a data feed, so they replace the Property App rather than the API.

Which Regrid alternative is best for finding development sites by zoning?

Plotzy is built for that job: search by permitted use and lot size with two-level zoning on every parcel, then look up owners and export the shortlist. It starts at $180 a month with a 7-day trial and no credit card, and it is still labeled beta.

Which alternative replaces Regrid's API or bulk parcel files?

LightBox and ATTOM both license nationwide data by API and bulk delivery, including Snowflake and MCP options, on quote-based terms; ATTOM offers a 30-day API trial. ParcelQuest sells bulk data for California only.

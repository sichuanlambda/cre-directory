Best DealMachine Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

DealMachine started in Indianapolis in 2017 as a driving-for-dollars app and is now a property and owner data platform from DealMachine Operations, Inc.: 150M+ US properties and 240M+ people records, AI search in plain language, 800+ filters, list building, skip tracing and enrichment, a Driving for Dollars app on every plan, per-piece postcards, and API, CLI, webhook and MCP access. Pricing moved to per-seat plans with monthly data credits: Basic at $119 a month per seat with 10,000 credits, Pro at $179 a month for a three-user bundle with 20,000 credits, and Scale packages of 100,000 records for $599 a month through sales, with 17% off on annual billing. The time-limited trial is gone; a free account lets you search, but owner and contact details stay locked until you pay. The reasons people look elsewhere follow from that model. Credits are spent on contact reveals and exports, so list-heavy users need more seats or a Scale package; mail is billed per piece on top; the current plans do not list a built-in dialer; and the data and filters are built for residential motivated-seller work, not commercial or land analysis.

Choose a replacement by the part of DealMachine you rely on. If it is driving neighborhoods, look for a field app or an imagery-based alternative that finds distress without the miles. If it is list building and owner contacts, compare how many contacts each tool includes per dollar. If it is outreach, pick a platform that bundles calling, texting and mail in one place. And if you buy land or commercial sites, you need parcel, ownership and zoning data first, with owner contacts as the last step.

Top DealMachine Alternatives

1.
P

PropStream

AI-enhanced nationwide property data, lead generation, comps, skip tracing, and marketing tools for investors.

Why pick it over DealMachine: PropStream is the deepest residential database in this group: 160M+ property records, 308M+ deeds, 185M+ mortgages, 41M+ pre-foreclosures, 165+ filters and 20 pre-built lead lists, with comps, a free mobile app and a marketing suite. Plans start at $99 a month ($81 on annual billing) with a 7-day trial and 50 free leads. Skip tracing is extra on Essentials at 12 cents a contact and included from Pro at $199, and monthly saves and exports are capped.

BatchLeads

Property list building, skip tracing, AI dialing, and direct mail for high-volume investor prospecting.

Why pick it over DealMachine: BatchLeads suits DealMachine users who want calling and contacts bundled: owner phones and emails come with every plan, from $119 a month for 10,000 leads and three users, with AI lead scoring and an $89 a month Dialer AI add-on. Direct mail is built in with plan-based discounts. PropStream has owned it since July 2025, and leads and exports above the cap cost 4 cents each.

PropertyRadar

160M+ property records with owner contacts, 30+ distress signals, and built-in multi-channel marketing.

Why pick it over DealMachine: PropertyRadar is the outreach-heavy alternative: public records on 160M+ properties across 3,000+ counties, 30+ distress signals, automated alerts when a property changes, and campaigns by phone, text, email, mail and online ads with API access on every paid plan. Plans run from $119 a month or $99 on annual billing after a 5-day trial. Solo includes only 250 phones and emails a month, and the dialer and SMS are add-ons.

Not sold on PropertyRadar either? See its alternatives →

4.
P

Plotzy

AI parcel search with two-level zoning, owner contacts and exports across 150M+ US parcels.

Why pick it over DealMachine: Plotzy is the pick if your deals are land or commercial sites where zoning decides value. Instead of driving for distress, you search 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties by permitted use, lot size, land use, owner type and sale date, see two-level zoning on every parcel, then look up owners and export to CSV, month to month at $180 to $300 with a 7-day trial and no card. It is not a house-wholesaling tool: owner contacts are metered (25 a month on Starter, 100 on Production), there are no skip-traced lists at scale, no driving app, dialer or mail, no comps or mortgage and equity data, and it is still labeled beta.

5.
C

Casafy AI

Computer-vision platform that scans street-level imagery to surface distressed, off-market properties for investors.

Why pick it over DealMachine: Casafy AI is driving for dollars without the car: computer vision scores property condition from street-level imagery to flag visibly distressed houses, with live MLS data, comps, owner information and skip tracing alongside. It suits investors who valued DealMachine for spotting distress rather than for its mail. Pricing is not published, and condition scores cover only areas the platform has already processed.

6.
L

Landglide

Mobile and web parcel app: boundaries and owner info on 157M+ parcel records in 3,200+ counties, from $9.99 a month.

Why pick it over DealMachine: LandGlide is the low-cost field companion for land investors: GPS shows which of 157 million parcels you are standing on, with owner, mailing address, acreage and sale data, online or offline, for $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year after a 7-day trial. It suits rural and land prospecting where DealMachine's house-focused driving routes add little. It has no skip tracing, lists or mail, so outreach happens elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does DealMachine cost?

Basic is $119 a month per seat with 10,000 data credits, Pro is $179 a month for a three-user bundle with 20,000 credits, and Scale is $599 a month per package of 100,000 records and 10 seats through sales. Annual billing saves 17%, and postcards cost $0.70 to $0.89 each depending on size and plan.

Does DealMachine have a free trial?

No time-limited trial. DealMachine offers a free account with no credit card that lets you run searches, but owner names, contact details and property data stay locked, and lists, Driving for Dollars, mail and exports require a paid plan.

What is the best DealMachine alternative for driving for dollars?

Casafy AI replaces the drive with computer vision on street-level imagery to flag distressed properties. For land and rural parcels, LandGlide is a $9.99 a month field app that shows parcel lines and owners from your GPS position. PropStream also offers a free mobile app with its subscription.

Is DealMachine good for land or commercial deals?

It is built for residential motivated-seller work. Land investors often pair a field app like LandGlide with a parcel search tool, and Plotzy fits land and commercial sites where zoning decides value, with metered owner contacts rather than bulk skip tracing.

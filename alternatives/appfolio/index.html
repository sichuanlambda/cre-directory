Best AppFolio Alternatives (2026)

AppFolio positions itself as an AI-native performance platform, and that is exactly why some teams look elsewhere. Its cons are the flip side of its ambitions: the platform can be complex for smaller property management companies, the AI-native approach requires real change management and training, and its enterprise-level AI capabilities carry premium pricing. Pricing is also quote-based with no published plans, which makes budgeting harder for small operators who just want to know the monthly cost.

When comparing alternatives, decide whether you want less platform or just more predictable pricing. Small landlords can move to free or flat-rate tools and give up little they actually use. Mid-sized managers should weigh transparent per-unit or tiered pricing against AppFolio's automation depth, and confirm any replacement covers the accounting, screening, and maintenance workflows they rely on today.

Top AppFolio Alternatives

B

Buildium

Property management software.

Why pick it over AppFolio: The most direct alternative with transparent published pricing from $62/month and a free trial, versus AppFolio's quote-based model. It is purpose-built for residential management and scales to 15,000+ units, with Lumina AI covering communications and bill scanning.

2.
D

DoorLoop

All-in-one property management software for residential and commercial properties.

Why pick it over AppFolio: Published tiers ($69/$139/$199), setup and data migration in days, and a 98.6% customer satisfaction rating make it a lower-friction switch than an AI-native replatforming. It covers both residential and commercial properties.

3.
Y

Yardi

The global leader in AI-enabled property management and real estate investment software.

Why pick it over AppFolio: Yardi Breeze starts around $1/unit/month, giving smaller operators a simpler, cheaper entry point than AppFolio's premium AI platform, with Voyager available if you later outgrow it. Be aware the enterprise tier brings its own complexity.

4.
R

Rentec Direct

Property management software for landlords and property managers.

Why pick it over AppFolio: At $2-2.50/unit/month with no minimums, free ACH payments, and US-based support, it is one of the most affordable full property management options for cost-conscious managers. The interface is more dated than AppFolio's, which is the trade-off for the price.

5.
T

TenantCloud

Cloud-based property management software.

Why pick it over AppFolio: A free starter plan (up to 10 leases) makes it the easy off-ramp for small landlords who find AppFolio complex or expensive, with autopay that the company says cuts late payments by 90%. Paid-tier pricing is less transparent.

6.
R

Rent Manager

Robust property management software.

Why pick it over AppFolio: Choose Rent Manager for customization: 450+ built-in reports, dual cash/accrual accounting, an open API, and support for every property type including commercial, which AppFolio's residential focus does not emphasize. Pricing requires consultation.

7.
E

Entrata

Commercial real estate software platform.

Why pick it over AppFolio: For larger multifamily operators, Entrata offers a true single-login platform proven at 3.2M+ units with strong AI leasing. Like AppFolio it is quote-based, so this is a move for capability fit rather than price transparency.

8.
T

TurboTenant

Free property management software for landlords.

Why pick it over AppFolio: Free for landlords on the core plan and trusted by over 500,000 of them, with a Premium tier at $12.42/unit/month. It suits DIY landlords, not professional managers running third-party portfolios.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to AppFolio?

For small landlords, Innago is completely free with no unit limits, TurboTenant's core plan is free for landlords, and TenantCloud's starter plan is free up to 10 leases. These cover rent collection, screening, and basic operations, but none approach AppFolio's automation, accounting depth, or investor reporting, so they fit self-managing landlords rather than professional management companies.

Is there a cheaper alternative to AppFolio for small teams?

Yes. AppFolio does not publish pricing, but several alternatives do: Buildium starts at $62/month with a free trial, DoorLoop at $69/month, and Rentec Direct at $2-2.50/unit/month with no minimums. For very small portfolios, RentRedi's flat rate (from $19.95/month billed annually) covers unlimited units.

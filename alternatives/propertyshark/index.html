Best PropertyShark Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

PropertyShark is a property research and prospecting database owned by Yardi, priced from $60 per month with a free trial. It suits individual brokers, investors and appraisers who need owner lookup, comparable sales, permits, zoning and foreclosure tracking without a CoStar contract. The reasons people look elsewhere follow from its cons: coverage is uneven outside New York City and a few major metros, the dataset is smaller than CoStar's, the interface feels dated, and there are no transaction or property management features. Teams that prospect nationally, or that need verified owner phone numbers and emails rather than names and mailing addresses, tend to outgrow it fastest.

Start by naming the job PropertyShark does for you. If it is finding true owners behind LLCs and building call lists, a dedicated ownership-intelligence tool covers that better and nationally. If it is parcel maps and zoning, a map-first platform will be cleaner. If you need bulk data feeding a model or CRM, a licensed data provider with an API is the right shape, even if it costs more. And if you actually need lease comps and market analytics across property types, the honest answer is a CoStar subscription, which PropertyShark was never meant to replace.

Top PropertyShark Alternatives

1.
R

Reonomy

CRE intelligence platform combining exclusive data partnerships with machine learning.

Why pick it over PropertyShark: Reonomy, now part of Altus Group, is the closest like-for-like upgrade for prospecting: national coverage, AI-connected ownership records that surface the people behind LLCs and trusts, and debt maturity data for finding motivated sellers. A free trial lets you compare coverage in your market first. Pricing is not published, and like PropertyShark its data is strongest in major metros with limited lease detail.

2.
P

PropTracer

PropTracer is the top U.S. skip-tracing platform that provides commercial and residential real estate professionals with accurate owner phone and email data in seconds.

Why pick it over PropertyShark: PropTracer is built for one job: verified owner contact details for commercial and residential parcels, with map-based search, bulk CSV export and dynamic lists that refresh as records change. Choose it if PropertyShark's owner lookup gets you a name but not a working phone number. Pricing is quote-based, and accuracy can lag on very recently transferred properties.

3.
P

Plotzy

AI parcel search with two-level zoning, owner contacts and exports across 150M+ US parcels.

Why pick it over PropertyShark: Plotzy covers 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties, which fills PropertyShark's gaps outside New York and the major metros, and adds two-level zoning filters plus owner contact lookup for outreach. It costs more, from $180 a month, and is still labeled beta, so a single-market user who only needs occasional owner lookups will find PropertyShark cheaper.

4.
L

LandID

Owner info; parcel boundaries. Basic research.

Why pick it over PropertyShark: Land id is the low-cost option, with a free plan and paid plans from $9.99 per month, covering parcel lines, ownership and comparable sales in all 50 states with branded interactive maps. It fits land, rural and suburban work far better than dense urban commercial, so NYC-focused PropertyShark users will find it thin for their core market.

5.
L

Landvision

Comprehensive location mapping software; analysis; & management for CRE. Source new deals; rule in & out parcels; do market & owner research.

Why pick it over PropertyShark: LightBox Vision, formerly LandVision, is a nationwide map-first platform with parcel boundaries, building footprints, ownership and zoning data, plus in-platform report ordering. It suits site selection and ownership research teams that want consistent coverage rather than PropertyShark's metro-by-metro depth. It is enterprise-oriented with contact-for-pricing plans, so budget for a sales process.

Not sold on Landvision either? See its alternatives →

6.
A

ATTOM

National property data on 160M+ US properties via API, bulk files, and cloud shares, with AVMs and risk analytics.

Why pick it over PropertyShark: ATTOM is the choice when you need data as a feed rather than a search screen: deeds, mortgages, foreclosures, permits and AVMs on more than 160 million US properties, delivered by API, bulk files or Snowflake and Databricks shares. It is right for brokerages and investors building their own tooling. Pricing is negotiated per license and it requires technical integration to be useful.

7.
C

CoStar

The leading commercial real estate information, analytics, and online marketplace platform.

Why pick it over PropertyShark: CoStar is the step up rather than sideways: the largest US commercial database, with lease comps, sales transactions and submarket analytics at a depth PropertyShark does not attempt. Choose it when you have outgrown owner lookup and need market intelligence across property types. The cost is real: premium pricing, annual contracts and a steep learning curve that smaller firms often cannot justify.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PropertyShark good outside New York City?

PropertyShark covers major US metros, but its data is deepest in New York City and coverage varies noticeably by market. Users outside its core markets often pair it with, or replace it with, a nationwide platform such as Reonomy or LightBox Vision.

What is the cheapest alternative to PropertyShark?

Land id offers a free plan and paid plans from $9.99 per month for parcel, ownership and comparable sales data across all 50 states, though it is strongest for land and rural property rather than urban commercial buildings.

Which PropertyShark alternative is best for contacting property owners?

PropTracer and Reonomy both specialize in identifying true owners behind LLCs. PropTracer focuses on verified phone numbers, emails and mailing addresses with bulk export for CRMs and direct mail, while Reonomy adds debt maturity and transaction history to help prioritize who to call.

Is PropertyShark cheaper than CoStar?

Yes. PropertyShark publishes a starting price of $60 per month with a free trial, while CoStar is enterprise-priced on annual contracts. The trade-off is a smaller dataset with no transaction or property management features.

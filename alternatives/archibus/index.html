Best Archibus Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Archibus is a long-established Integrated Workplace Management System, now sold as part of Eptura's worktech portfolio alongside the former iOFFICE and SpaceIQ products. It covers space planning, asset and facility management, preventive maintenance, visitor management and employee experience, with Autodesk integration and FedRAMP authorization for government clients. It fits large enterprises and public agencies with complex portfolios. The reasons people look elsewhere follow from its scope: quote-based enterprise pricing, a platform that is far more than most mid-size organizations need, and product-line confusion after the Eptura rebrand.

The right replacement depends on which Archibus modules carry your workload. If you need the whole IWMS, the direct competitors are other enterprise suites, and switching is a multi-year project. If maintenance and work orders are the core, a modern CMMS is cheaper and faster to deploy. If space planning and test-fitting for corporate real estate is the job, a focused workplace design tool covers it without the rest of the suite. Be honest about how many modules you actually run before you buy another all-in-one system.

Top Archibus Alternatives

1.
P

Planon

Market-leading smart sustainable building management software.

Why pick it over Archibus: Planon is the most direct like-for-like IWMS: portfolio, space and workplace management, facilities and maintenance, lease accounting, sustainability and field services, recognized by Verdantix, Gartner and IDC. It suits global enterprises and universities that want a single platform from an independent vendor. It is EMEA-centric with a smaller US footprint, and implementation needs specialized consulting.

2.
I

IBM TRIRIGA

Intelligent real estate and facilities management by IBM.

Why pick it over Archibus: IBM TRIRIGA, now packaged as IBM Maximo Real Estate and Facilities, matches Archibus module for module and adds Maximo's broader asset management. It is the pick for Fortune 500 occupiers already standardized on IBM. Costs are IBM-scale, implementations usually involve IBM consultants, and the fold into Maximo Application Suite adds transition risk.

3.
A

Accruent

Facilities, asset, and lease management software for the built environment.

Why pick it over Archibus: Accruent offers IWMS capabilities across space, lease administration and accounting, capital planning, maintenance and IoT monitoring, with strong verticals in healthcare, retail, education and government. Choose it over Archibus if lease compliance or multi-industry support matters more than workplace experience features. The portfolio sprawls from acquisitions and some modules feel dated.

4.
T

Tango

Real estate solutions for streamlining portfolio operations.

Why pick it over Archibus: Tango covers the property lifecycle from site selection to sustainability, with lease management, space utilization, projects, maintenance and energy tracking, and unusually strong retail features such as store performance prediction. It fits corporate and retail occupiers with many locations. It offers little for investment or brokerage use, and enterprise pricing applies.

5.
F

Facilio

AI-powered connected CMMS and building operations platform for commercial portfolios, from work orders to energy.

Why pick it over Archibus: Facilio is the focused alternative if Archibus is mostly running your work orders: an AI-powered CMMS with preventive maintenance, inspections, vendor management, energy monitoring and a mobile app for field teams, across commercial portfolios. It drops the space and workplace modules, so it suits operations teams rather than corporate real estate planners. Pricing is quote-based with no free trial.

6.
S

Saltmine

Workplace design and management platform.

Why pick it over Archibus: Saltmine replaces the space planning slice of Archibus for corporate real estate teams: space programming, test fits, visualization and project delivery with unlimited users, built by architects. It is the choice when planning speed matters more than facilities operations. It is not an IWMS, implementation runs 4 to 8 weeks, and results depend on clean data inputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Archibus the same as Eptura?

Archibus is one product within Eptura, the company formed by combining iOFFICE and SpaceIQ. Eptura sells Archibus alongside its other workplace and asset management tools.

What is the closest alternative to Archibus for a full IWMS?

Planon, IBM TRIRIGA (now IBM Maximo Real Estate and Facilities) and Accruent are the closest full-suite competitors, covering space, facilities, maintenance, leases and sustainability for enterprise portfolios.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Archibus for maintenance management?

Facilio and other CMMS platforms cover work orders, preventive maintenance, inspections and vendor management without the full IWMS footprint. Facilio's pricing is quote-based, so cost still needs to be confirmed against your portfolio size.

Does Archibus publish pricing?

No. Archibus pricing is quote-based through Eptura and depends on modules and portfolio size, which is also true of Planon, IBM TRIRIGA, Accruent, Tango, Facilio and Saltmine.

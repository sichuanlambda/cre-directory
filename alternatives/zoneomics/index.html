Best Zoneomics Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Zoneomics is the zoning reference most CRE teams reach for first. It digitizes zoning and land use codes from thousands of cities into the largest nationwide zoning dataset, layers AI analysis on top, sells reports from $25 and certified zoning letters for closings, and exposes everything through an API. The reasons people look elsewhere are consistent: coverage is uneven, since not every city is fully digitized, the free search shows little without buying a report, per-report and per-letter costs add up for teams that check zoning every day, and it stops at the answer. Zoneomics tells you what a parcel allows; it does not help you find the next twenty parcels or reach their owners.

So start with the job. If zoning is the deliverable, you need a certified letter or a bulk code feed, the alternatives are thin and Zoneomics usually stays. If zoning is a filter on the way to a site or a prospect list, a parcel search platform with zoning attached to every record replaces it for most daily work, and a data vendor covers the pipeline case. Most land and development teams end up with one sourcing tool and Zoneomics on standby for confirmation.

Top Zoneomics Alternatives

1.
P

Plotzy

AI parcel search with two-level zoning, owner contacts and exports across 150M+ US parcels.

Why pick it over Zoneomics: The most direct replacement for daily zoning research: every one of Plotzy's 150M+ parcels carries a two-level zoning designation, so you filter a corridor, county or polygon by permitted use, lot size and owner type in one pass, then pull owner contacts and export the list. A Zoning AI chat covers the code questions Zoneomics reports answer. Flat pricing from $180 a month replaces per-report spend, with a 7-day trial and no card. It does not issue certified zoning letters, it is still labeled beta, and sub-level zoning depth varies by municipality.

2.
G

Gridics

Zoning intelligence platform - parcel-level zoning data, 3D development capacity analysis, and municipal code tools.

Why pick it over Zoneomics: Gridics converts ordinances into parcel-level development-allowance data with a patented rules engine, and its PropZone product wraps that in property research and site selection tools with 3D development capacity. Pick it when you need buildable-envelope math, not just the zoning label. Depth depends on which municipalities have been onboarded, pricing is quote-based, and feasibility reports carry a services component.

3.
R

Regrid

Standardized nationwide parcel data - boundaries, buildings, zoning, ownership - via API, bulk files, and apps.

Why pick it over Zoneomics: Regrid sells standardized zoning as an enrichment on complete US parcel data, delivered by API, tiles or bulk files. Choose it when you have GIS analysts or engineers and want zoning inside your own tools rather than a website. It is a data product, not an analysis application, and zoning is a paid add-on with no published pricing.

4.
L

Landvision

Comprehensive location mapping software; analysis; & management for CRE. Source new deals; rule in & out parcels; do market & owner research.

Why pick it over Zoneomics: LightBox Vision, the rebranded LandVision, puts zoning layers, parcel boundaries, ownership and in-platform report ordering in one enterprise mapping tool backed by LightBox datasets. Choose it for multi-seat teams already buying from LightBox. Pricing is annual and on quote, and the feature set is heavier than most brokers need.

Not sold on Landvision either? See its alternatives →

5.
A

Archistar

AI site feasibility: zoning analysis plus generative design that produces compliant 3D building concepts.

Why pick it over Zoneomics: Archistar reads zoning rules, overlays and constraints and then generates code-compliant 3D massing options, so it answers what can be built rather than what the zoning says. Choose it for early feasibility on a shortlisted site. Planning-data depth is strongest in Australia and coverage varies elsewhere; pricing is not published.

6.
T

Testfit

Site feasibility & planning. Understand if you can make a deal make sense.

Why pick it over Zoneomics: TestFit is the feasibility step after zoning: map the parcel, generate building configurations and see the financial takeoffs in hours. Choose it when the zoning answer is already known and the question is whether the deal pencils. It is not a zoning lookup and needs a sales conversation for pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free alternative to Zoneomics?

Zoneomics itself offers a free basic zoning search. Among alternatives, Plotzy provides a 7-day free trial with no credit card, and most other zoning and parcel platforms are paid or quote-based.

Which Zoneomics alternative also provides owner contacts?

Plotzy combines zoning filters with owner contact lookup, folders and CSV export, so a zoning search becomes a prospect list. LightBox Vision includes ownership data as well; Regrid provides ownership as a data enrichment.

Who else provides certified zoning letters?

Certified zoning verification letters remain a Zoneomics strength. Gridics offers expert-prepared zoning feasibility reports; the parcel search platforms in this list do not issue certified letters.

How does Plotzy compare to Zoneomics on coverage?

Plotzy covers 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties with a two-level zoning designation on every parcel; Zoneomics has the largest digitized code coverage but not every city is fully digitized. Check your target markets on both before committing.

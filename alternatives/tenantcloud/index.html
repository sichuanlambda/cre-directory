Best TenantCloud Alternatives (2026)

Updated September 3, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

TenantCloud is cloud property management software for landlords and small managers, with separate apps for landlords, tenants, owners and service providers. It covers online rent collection, screening, listing syndication to Apartments.com and Realtor.com, QuickBooks accounting, maintenance requests and e-signatures. Its pricing has changed. The free Starter plan that made it popular is gone; Starter is now $15 per month on annual billing, or $18 monthly, and it is capped at 10 leases. Growth allows 30 leases at about $29 per month, Pro allows 60 at $50, and Business is custom from $100, all with a 14-day trial. Those lease caps are the main reason landlords look elsewhere: a growing portfolio is forced up a tier even if it needs no new features. The lease builder, inspections and QuickBooks sync are also reserved for higher plans.

The good news is that this segment has plenty of options. Landlords who mainly want screening, leases and rent collection can get that free with no unit limit. Owners who care most about bookkeeping and tax time should choose an accounting-first tool. Flat-rate platforms remove the lease-count math entirely, and per-unit professional systems make sense once you manage for other owners.

Top TenantCloud Alternatives

1.
T

TurboTenant

Free property management software for landlords.

Why pick it over TenantCloud: TurboTenant is the most direct swap for landlords who used TenantCloud's old free tier. Its free plan includes listing syndication, applications, TransUnion screening, rent collection and maintenance tracking with no lease cap, and paid tiers add state-specific leases and unlimited e-signatures on annual billing. It is aimed at self-managing landlords, so professional managers handling other owners' properties will outgrow it.

2.
I

Innago

Free property management software for landlords.

Why pick it over TenantCloud: Innago is free for landlords with no monthly fee, setup fee, contract or unit limit, which removes TenantCloud's lease-count problem entirely. It covers rent collection, screening, lease management, maintenance and accounting, with a mobile app and support that reviewers consistently rate highly. Integrations are limited and the feature set is lighter than paid platforms, so it fits landlords and small managers rather than larger companies.

3.
R

RentRedi

Streamlined property management for landlords.

Why pick it over TenantCloud: RentRedi is the flat-rate choice: $29.95 per month, or $12 per month on annual billing, for unlimited units, tenants and team members. It includes ACH, card and cash rent payments at 90,000+ retail locations, TransUnion screening and maintenance requests with photo and video, and it is the official software for BiggerPockets members. Accounting is thinner than TenantCloud's QuickBooks-connected books.

4.
L

Landlord Studio

Mobile-first accounting, rent collection, screening, and tax reports for DIY landlords and small portfolios.

Why pick it over TenantCloud: Landlord Studio is the accounting-first alternative. Its free Go tier covers up to 3 units, and Pro is $12 per month plus $1 per additional unit, adding bank feeds, AI bookkeeping, more than 15 reports including Schedule E preparation and Xero integration, alongside listings, screening and rent collection. It is built for self-managing landlords and small investors, not for third-party managers or commercial assets.

5.
R

Rentec Direct

Property management software for landlords and property managers.

Why pick it over TenantCloud: Rentec Direct is the step up for landlords who are becoming professional managers. Starter is $25 per month for up to 10 properties, Pro and PM editions start at $50 per month with unlimited properties, and the PM edition adds trust accounting, owner portals and statements. Tenants get free ACH payments and support is US-based. The interface is older-looking and the mobile app is more limited than TenantCloud's.

6.
D

DoorLoop

All-in-one property management software for residential and commercial properties.

Why pick it over TenantCloud: DoorLoop is the fuller all-in-one for landlords who want more automation than TenantCloud offers, including an AI assistant, custom workflows, CAM charges for commercial units and listing syndication. Starter is $69 per month on annual billing for up to 10 units with $3 per extra unit, and Pro is $149. It costs considerably more than TenantCloud at the same portfolio size, and the AI features require the Pro plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TenantCloud still free?

No. TenantCloud replaced its free plan with paid tiers. Starter is $15 per month on annual billing ($18 monthly) for up to 10 leases, with a 14-day trial.

What is the best free alternative to TenantCloud?

Innago is free with no unit limit, and TurboTenant's free plan covers listings, screening, applications and rent collection. Landlord Studio's free tier covers up to 3 units with an accounting focus.

Which TenantCloud alternative has no lease or unit caps?

RentRedi charges a flat $29.95 per month, or $12 per month on annual billing, for unlimited units, and Innago is free with no limits.

What should a landlord choose when moving into managing properties for others?

Rentec Direct's PM edition, from $50 per month, adds trust accounting and owner statements, and Buildium from $62 per month is the common next step for full third-party management.

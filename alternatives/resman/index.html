Best ResMan Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

ResMan is a cloud property management platform for conventional multifamily and affordable housing operators, part of Inhabit (formerly Inhabit IQ) since August 2021 and still based in Plano, Texas. It combines property and corporate accounting, leasing, payments, screening, BudgetsPro budgeting and a BoardRoom KPI dashboard with AI tools for invoice entry, leasing and resident questions, and its affordable offering supports HUD, Rural Development and tax credit programs. Pricing is not published; Get Pricing leads to a demo request. People look elsewhere because quote-only pricing slows comparison, owners with commercial or single-family assets need coverage the current site does not describe, enterprise operators may want the wider ecosystems of the largest vendors, and some owners consolidate on the system their third-party managers already use.

Start from the reason you are leaving. If it is breadth, the large multifamily suites cover leasing, resident experience, AI agents and accounting under one contract, at the cost of longer implementations. If it is a mixed portfolio, choose a platform that runs commercial and residential in one system. If it is budget visibility for a smaller operator, a vendor with published per-unit pricing lets you price the move before the first sales call. Affordable housing operators should have every finalist demonstrate the specific compliance programs they run before signing.

Entrata

Property management platform for multifamily, student, affordable and military housing with native Leasing, Maintenance and Payments AI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns ResMan?

ResMan is part of Inhabit, a property management software company formerly known as Inhabit IQ, which added ResMan in August 2021. Inhabit's other brands include ResidentIQ, Streamline and LiveRez. ResMan remains based in Plano, Texas.

How much does ResMan cost?

ResMan does not publish pricing. Its pricing page routes to a demo request, so expect a quote based on your portfolio and modules. AppFolio and Yardi Breeze are the main multifamily-capable alternatives with published per-unit pricing.

What is the best ResMan alternative for affordable housing?

Yardi, RealPage, MRI Software and Entrata all serve affordable housing. Ask each to demonstrate the exact programs you run, such as HUD, tax credit or Rural Development compliance, because depth varies by program and that is where ResMan is strongest.

Does ResMan handle commercial properties?

ResMan's current site focuses on conventional multifamily and affordable housing and does not describe commercial property management. Operators with office, retail or industrial assets usually look at MRI Software, Yardi or Rent Manager, which run commercial and residential portfolios together.

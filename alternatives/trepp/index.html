Best Trepp Alternatives (2026)

Updated November 2, 2026. Independent editorial; how we research.

Trepp provides data, analytics and technology for structured finance, commercial real estate and banking, and it is the long-standing reference source for CMBS deal and loan surveillance. Its products span CMBS analytics and cash flow modeling, TreppLoan for managing and valuing CRE loan portfolios, TreppCRE for property-level ownership, lease, tax and financial data, bulk data feeds and MCP servers that connect Trepp data to AI assistants. In April 2026 Fitch Group agreed to buy Trepp from Rothermere Continuation Holdings, the parent of DMGT, with Trepp to become a subsidiary of Fitch Solutions. Pricing is quote-based and sold on enterprise terms. The reasons people look elsewhere are mostly about fit and cost: licenses priced for bond desks and large lenders are hard to justify for a broker who wants loan maturity leads, a lender that needs origination workflow, or an investor who mainly needs bond cash flow models, and a change of ownership gives some clients a natural moment to review vendors.

Decide which part of Trepp you actually use. If it is loan-level CMBS and agency data for sourcing refinance and distress opportunities, a newer platform with published pricing covers that at lower cost. If it is bond cash flow modeling, the independent deal library standard is the direct substitute. If it is debt alongside transactions and market research, an institutional data house fits. And if you are a lender who needs origination and underwriting workflow with CMBS data built in, a lending suite is the right shape.

Top Trepp Alternatives

1.
C

CRED iQ

CRE and CMBS loan data, analytics, and valuation platform for investors, lenders, and brokers.

Why pick it over Trepp: CRED iQ is the most direct alternative for loan-level research: CMBS, SASB, CRE CLO and agency multifamily loans joined to ownership, sales and comps, with maturity pipelines, valuation and refinance models and distress alerts. Its pricing is published, from $625 a month billed annually for one seat, with a free view-only trial and API access from the Professional plan. It is a younger company founded in 2020, and bulk feeds and its MCP server sit on the custom Enterprise plan.

2.
I

Intex

Independent cashflow model library and analytics for CMBS and structured finance investors.

Why pick it over Trepp: Intex is the standard independent cash flow model library for securitizations, with more than 50,000 deals modeled across CMBS, RMBS, ABS and CLOs and delivery through desktop software, APIs and Excel plug-ins. Choose it if you use Trepp mainly for bond cash flows and scenario analysis. It is not a property or loan research tool, and pricing is quote-based.

3.
M

MSCI Real Capital Analytics

MSCI's Real Capital Analytics: global commercial property transaction, investor and loan data, plus indexes and debt intelligence.

Why pick it over Trepp: MSCI's Real Capital Analytics pairs global transaction data with Mortgage Debt Intelligence, which covers securitized and non-securitized property loans and links lenders to deals. It suits institutional investors and lenders who want debt in the context of $50 trillion+ of recorded transactions. It is not a CMBS bond analytics tool, and pricing is enterprise and quote-based.

Not sold on MSCI Real Capital Analytics either? See its alternatives →

4.
M

Moody's Lending Suite for CRE

Moody's CRE lending suite: origination, underwriting and stress tests, covenant tracking, early warnings, refinance risk.

Why pick it over Trepp: Moody's Lending Suite for CRE is built for lenders: deal screening, sizing, rent roll and cash flow analysis, credit memos and decisioning, plus CMBS and securitized loan data, property and ownership data and early warning monitoring. Choose it when origination and portfolio monitoring matter more than bond surveillance. Pricing is by consultation.

5.
G

Green Street

Subscription CRE research, pricing data, REIT analytics, and news for institutional investors.

Why pick it over Trepp: Green Street combines independent CRE research with data products that include debt data, sales comps for deals of $5M and up and the Commercial Property Price Index. It suits investors who want debt alongside valuation and market research. It is not a loan-level surveillance system, and subscriptions are quote-based and institutional.

6.
B

Backshop

CRE loan origination, deal-stack modeling, asset management, and servicing tools, plus a low cost CMBS PRO tier for borrowers.

Why pick it over Trepp: Backshop from CMBS.com is an underwriting and asset management system for CRE lenders with an integrated CMBS library of loans and bonds. Its Professional plan for individual users costs $20 per user per month or $200 per user per year, the lowest published price on this list. Analytics are lighter than Trepp's, and the full Backshop platform is licensed by quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns Trepp?

Trepp has been part of DMGT since 2004. In April 2026 Fitch Group announced an agreement to buy it from Rothermere Continuation Holdings, DMGT's parent, with Trepp to become a wholly owned subsidiary of Fitch Solutions, subject to regulatory clearance.

What is the cheapest Trepp alternative?

CMBS.com's Professional plan costs $20 per user per month or $200 per user per year. For a full loan research platform, CRED iQ starts at $625 a month billed annually and offers a free view-only trial.

Which alternative is best for CMBS bond cash flow modeling?

Intex is the independent standard for securitization cash flow models, with more than 50,000 deals modeled and access through APIs and Excel plug-ins.

Does Trepp publish its pricing?

No. Trepp sells on quote-based, enterprise terms. CRED iQ and CMBS.com are the alternatives here that publish prices.

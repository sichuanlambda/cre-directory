RealPage integration FAQ

Which CRE software integrates with RealPage?

9 tools in the directory list a RealPage integration, including CoStar, HappyCo, Entrata, Propertyware, Rently, Knock, Property Meld, Brivo, and 1 more.

Which tools with a RealPage integration offer a free trial or free plan?

None of the 9 tools publish a free trial or free plan. Expect to request a demo or a quote.

What kinds of CRE software connect to RealPage?

By primary category: Property Management (7), CRM & Marketing (1), Data & Analytics (1).

Does "integrates with RealPage" mean a two-way sync?

Not necessarily. Vendors use the phrase for anything from a certified two-way sync to a scheduled file import. Ask each vendor which records sync, in which direction, how often, and whether the connector costs extra.

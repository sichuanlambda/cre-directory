Our Verdict

Matterport and iGUIDE both turn a physical walkthrough into a 3D virtual tour with floor plans, but they start from different priorities. Matterport is a spatial data platform sold as a subscription, with capture from its own cameras, supported 360 cameras, or a smartphone, and outputs aimed at marketing, facilities documentation, and design coordination. iGUIDE, made by Planitar, pairs its proprietary PLANIX lidar camera with a per-project processing service whose core output is a measured floor plan, with the tour as a companion.

Matterport wins on reach and flexibility. It is the recognized standard for listing tours and integrates with LoopNet, Zillow, Realtor.com, and VTS, so a broker's marketing team can publish without extra steps. The free tier covers one active space, and paid tiers scale by active spaces, with Starter listed from $9.99 per month billed annually on Matterport's plans page. Enterprise plans add SSO, an API, and unlimited spaces for corporate portfolios. The caveats: the best results still call for dedicated camera hardware, hosting fees accumulate across a large portfolio, and models are cloud-dependent.

iGUIDE wins when the deliverable is the floor plan. Captures produce ANSI-compliant plans with a stated 0.5 percent measurement uncertainty, plus room-by-room measurements, and export to DWG, RVT, ESX, and Xactimate formats used by appraisers, insurers, and architects. Pricing is per project, with iGUIDE Instant at $7.99 USD and Standard packages from $48 CAD, with no subscription and no hosting fee, and users own their data. The trade-off is hardware: you either buy the PLANIX camera, listed at $3,499 CAD, or hire an iGUIDE Pro, which makes it a poor fit for occasional users.

Pick Matterport for marketing-led tours, broad camera support, and a portfolio you want to keep online indefinitely. Pick iGUIDE for appraisal, insurance, or construction workflows where measured drawings and one-time project pricing matter more than the tour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does iGUIDE work with any camera?

No. iGUIDE captures require the PLANIX camera system or a trained iGUIDE Pro operator. Matterport supports its own cameras, selected third-party 360 cameras, and smartphone capture.

Is Matterport free?

Matterport has a free plan limited to one active space. Paid plans are priced by the number of active spaces, with Starter listed from $9.99 per month billed annually.

Which produces more accurate floor plans, Matterport or iGUIDE?

iGUIDE states a measurement uncertainty of 0.5 percent and exports plans in CAD, Revit, and appraisal formats. Matterport generates schematic floor plans on paid tiers that are suited to marketing rather than appraisal work.

Does iGUIDE charge hosting fees?

No. iGUIDE charges per project with no subscription or hosting fees, and users retain ownership of their data. Matterport charges a recurring subscription based on active spaces.

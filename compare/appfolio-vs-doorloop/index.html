Our Verdict

AppFolio and DoorLoop cover the same ground, accounting, leasing, maintenance, payments and tenant and owner portals, but they are sized for different businesses. AppFolio is a public company with a mature platform sold in three plans (Core, Plus and Max), built around its Realm-X AI and workflow engine and aimed at professional management companies. DoorLoop is a younger, Miami-based product that sells self-serve plans to landlords and small to mid-sized managers across residential, commercial, HOA, student and affordable portfolios.

The clearest difference is how you buy. DoorLoop publishes its prices: Starter is $69 a month billed yearly ($99 month to month) for up to 10 units, Pro is $149 and Premium is $209 on yearly billing, with portfolios over 300 units pushed to a demo. AppFolio lists its plans but not its prices, and its Core plan carries a 50 unit minimum plus a minimum monthly spend. If you manage 12 doors, AppFolio is not really on the table.

AppFolio wins once the operation is large enough to need standardized processes. Its workflow automation, AI leasing and communication tools, investment management add-on and reporting depth are built for teams with dedicated accounting, leasing and maintenance staff. The caveats are quote-based pricing, onboarding that takes real effort, and features that a small team will pay for without using.

DoorLoop wins on speed and transparency. Setup is quick, QuickBooks sync and listing syndication come with the Pro plan, and support is a selling point. The caveats: AI features and workflows sit on Pro and above, the base plans cover a limited number of units before per-unit charges apply, and it has less depth for very large or institutional portfolios.

Choose AppFolio if you run a management company with 50 or more units, expect to grow, and want automation and reporting that scale. Choose DoorLoop if you are a landlord or a smaller manager who wants to see the price up front, be live in days and keep the software simple. Managers sitting between 50 and 200 units should demo both and compare the total quoted cost against DoorLoop Pro or Premium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DoorLoop cheaper than AppFolio?

For small portfolios, almost certainly, and it is the only one of the two you can price without a sales call. DoorLoop publishes plans from $69 a month on yearly billing for up to 10 units. AppFolio does not publish prices and applies minimum spend and unit requirements.

What is the minimum number of units for AppFolio?

AppFolio states that its Core plan has a 50 unit minimum and a minimum monthly spend, and that minimums also apply to Plus and Max. DoorLoop has no comparable minimum and sells a Starter plan for up to 10 units.

Does DoorLoop handle commercial properties?

Yes. DoorLoop markets itself to residential, commercial, mixed-use, HOA, student and affordable housing portfolios, and its Pro plan includes CAM charges. AppFolio also supports commercial properties, though both are used primarily for residential management.

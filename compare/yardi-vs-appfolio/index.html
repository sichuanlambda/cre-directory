Our Verdict

Yardi and AppFolio both aim to run your entire property operation, but they come at it from opposite directions. Yardi is the 40-year incumbent with the broadest single-stack platform in the industry: Voyager for enterprise portfolios, Breeze (from $1/unit/month) for smaller operators, plus investment management, energy, and procurement suites that cover multifamily, commercial, affordable housing, senior living, and more across 80+ countries. AppFolio is the modern challenger: a cloud-only, AI-native platform built around agentic AI and its Realm-X workflow engine, serving residential managers from roughly 50 units up to 14,000+.

Yardi wins on breadth and property-type coverage. If you run commercial assets, affordable housing, or need fund accounting and an investor portal in the same stack, AppFolio simply does not go that deep. Yardi's trade-offs are real, though: Voyager has a steep learning curve, implementations can take months, and some modules feel dated. AppFolio wins on user experience and automation, with AI built into the core rather than bolted on, at the cost of quote-based pricing and a change-management lift for traditional teams.

Bottom line: diverse or institutional portfolios that need commercial and investment management pick Yardi; growing residential managers who value modern UX and AI automation pick AppFolio; very small landlords should compare Yardi Breeze against both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Yardi cheaper than AppFolio?

It depends on your size. Yardi Breeze publishes pricing from $1/unit/month ($2 for Breeze Premier), which is a genuinely affordable entry point, while Yardi Voyager and AppFolio are both quote-based, so you will need sales conversations to compare enterprise costs. AppFolio is generally positioned as premium pricing for its AI capabilities.

Can AppFolio handle commercial properties like Yardi does?

AppFolio lists commercial among its supported property types, but its platform is built primarily for residential property managers. Yardi is the stronger choice for commercial portfolios, with dedicated commercial leasing tools, plus coverage for affordable housing, senior living, and coworking that AppFolio does not match.

Which is easier to implement, Yardi or AppFolio?

AppFolio, generally. Yardi Voyager implementations are known to take months and the platform can be complex to configure, while AppFolio is a single cloud platform with a unified interface. That said, AppFolio's AI-native approach still requires training and change management for teams used to traditional software.

Our Verdict

VTS and Yardi solve different problems that happen to overlap. VTS is a leasing and asset management platform: deal pipeline tracking, AI proposal generation, tenant relationship intelligence, digital marketing, and market data built on 13B+ square feet under management. Yardi is the operational and accounting backbone, covering property management, full general ledger accounting, investment management, and fund accounting across multifamily, commercial, affordable housing, and senior living. Tellingly, VTS lists Yardi as an accounting integration; many large landlords run both.

VTS wins on leasing workflow and market intelligence. Its Proposal AI, lease roll management, and VTS Data products are built for landlords and brokers who live in the deal cycle, and nothing in Yardi's stack matches its office demand data. Yardi wins on breadth and operational depth: it is a single stack for operations, accounting, and investment management, serves every property type, and Yardi Breeze starts at $1/unit/month, giving smaller operators an entry point VTS's enterprise, quote-based pricing does not.

Bottom line: choose Yardi if you need core property management and accounting, especially across mixed portfolios. Choose VTS if your system of record is settled and you need to modernize leasing, asset management, and tenant experience. Large office landlords often justify both; small operators should start with Yardi Breeze, since VTS is not priced for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can VTS replace Yardi?

Generally no. VTS covers leasing, asset management, marketing, and tenant experience but is not a full accounting and property management system, while Yardi provides the general ledger, AP/AR, and operational core. VTS actually integrates with Yardi, and many large landlords run the two together.

Which is better for a small landlord or brokerage?

Yardi, through Yardi Breeze, which starts at $1/unit/month and is built for smaller portfolios. VTS is enterprise-focused with quote-based pricing and is not accessible for small brokerages or individual brokers.

How do VTS and Yardi pricing compare?

Yardi publishes entry pricing for Breeze ($1/unit/month, $2 for Premier) while its enterprise Voyager platform is quote-based. VTS does not publish pricing at all; expect a sales conversation and enterprise-level contracts for either flagship product.

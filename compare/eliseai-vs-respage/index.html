Our Verdict

EliseAI and Respage both sell an AI leasing assistant to multifamily operators: software that answers prospect questions at any hour, follows up and books tours. Beyond that shared core they are different kinds of company.

EliseAI is an AI platform first. It handles conversations by text, email, chat and voice, with voice in seven languages and written replies in 51, and extends from leasing (prospect management and AI-guided tours) into resident operations: move-ins, maintenance, renewals, delinquency, lease audits and fee transparency. It includes EliseCRM and integrates with systems such as Rent Manager, ResMan, Funnel and Bluemoon. It serves conventional, student, affordable and single-family rental operators and says more than 900 property management companies use it. Pricing is not published.

Respage is a multifamily marketing company with an AI assistant at the centre. ResMate answers prospects across web chat, forms, email, text, phone, Facebook Messenger and ILS leads and books tours, AI Voice handles inbound calls, and a resident chatbot and a CRM built as an extension of ResMate round out the leasing side. Around that, Respage runs paid search and social ads, SEO, apartment websites, Google Business Profile and reputation management. Its integrations list covers Yardi, RealPage, Entrata, MRI, AppFolio, ResMan, Rent Manager, AMSI and Knock, and it serves multifamily, senior living and affordable communities. It does not publish prices but explains the structure: the chatbot is a flat rate, the full leasing assistant is per unit, AI phone is an add-on, and the CRM and resident chatbot are included with the AI license.

The caveat for Respage is that its value is highest if you also buy its marketing services; teams with an agency already may only want ResMate. The caveat for EliseAI is the enterprise sales motion, which can be heavy for small portfolios.

Choose EliseAI if you want AI to take on resident operations as well as leasing, across several housing types. Choose Respage if you want one vendor for apartment marketing and AI leasing, with broad PMS integrations and senior living support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ResMate?

ResMate is Respage's AI leasing assistant. It answers prospects across chat, email, text, phone, Facebook Messenger and ILS leads and books tours.

How is Respage priced?

Respage does not publish prices. It says the chatbot is a flat rate, the full leasing assistant is priced per unit, AI phone is an add-on, and the CRM and resident chatbot are included with the AI license.

Does EliseAI handle maintenance requests?

Yes. EliseAI's resident features cover maintenance requests, move-ins, renewals and delinquency as well as leasing conversations.

Which supports senior living?

Respage lists senior living alongside multifamily and affordable housing. EliseAI lists conventional, student, affordable and single-family rental housing.

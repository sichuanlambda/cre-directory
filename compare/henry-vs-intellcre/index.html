Our Verdict

Henry and IntellCRE are both aimed at commercial real estate brokers, and both promise to compress the work between winning a listing and taking it to market: underwriting, broker opinions of value, pitch decks and offering memorandums. They go about it differently.

Henry works like an AI analyst embedded in a brokerage. It builds custom Excel underwriting models from a deal description and rent roll using the firm's own assumptions, drafts pitch decks on the firm's templates, runs market research and comp selection with cited sources, profiles sponsors and builds buyer lists from the firm's CRM and past closings. It works through email, Excel and chat rather than a separate app. The company was founded in 2024, raised a $16.5M Series A led by FirstMark in July 2026, is SOC 2 Type II certified, and says it is used at more than 150 firms. The caveats: pricing is by sales call, it is a young company, and its output is only as good as the templates, assumptions and CRM data a team gives it.

IntellCRE is a platform for producing marketing materials with underwriting attached. It generates offering memorandums, BOVs, decks, brochures, property websites and flipbooks, and its underwriting starts from an address or from rent rolls, T-12s and operating statements read by its parsers, with sensitivity analysis and equity waterfalls. It draws comps from 150 million residential and multifamily records, offers deal sourcing tools, lists twelve property types, and has a concierge service that will produce materials for you. A testimonial from Lee & Associates San Diego appears on its site. The caveats: pricing is not published, its security page relies on its hosting providers' certifications rather than its own, and teams that only need analysis may not use most of the product.

Choose Henry if you run a brokerage team that wants an AI analyst working inside its existing email, Excel and CRM, with enterprise security. Choose IntellCRE if your bottleneck is producing polished marketing materials, from OMs to property websites, and you want the option to hand production to a concierge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Henry and IntellCRE publish pricing?

No. Henry is sold through a booked call, and IntellCRE offers sign-up and a booked call without listing prices.

Which generates more marketing formats?

IntellCRE, which produces offering memorandums, BOVs, decks, brochures, property websites and flipbooks. Henry focuses on underwriting, pitch decks, market research and buyer lists.

Does either work inside Excel?

Henry builds custom Excel underwriting models and offers an Excel add-in that is free for its customers. IntellCRE imports Excel and PDF files into its own platform.

Which has stronger security credentials?

Henry states SOC 2 Type II certification. IntellCRE does not name a certification of its own and points to its hosting providers.

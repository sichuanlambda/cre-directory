Our Verdict

Both of these platforms chase residential operators with heavy AI investment, but at different altitudes. AppFolio serves property management companies from about 50 units to 14,000+, wrapping property management, investment management, and agentic AI automation into one unified cloud experience. RealPage is aimed at mid-market and enterprise multifamily: OneSite property management, Knock CRM, G5 advertising, DemandX demand forecasting, utility and spend management, and its flagship AI-driven revenue management, used by some of the world's largest apartment operators.

AppFolio wins on unified experience and accessibility for professional management firms; one platform, one login, with AI woven through leasing, accounting, and maintenance rather than assembled from acquisitions. RealPage wins on enterprise multifamily firepower: no one in this matchup matches its revenue optimization, market analytics footprint, and marketing stack. The trade-offs are steep on both sides. RealPage is enterprise-priced, complex to learn, and its revenue management algorithms carry active DOJ antitrust scrutiny. AppFolio is quote-based premium pricing and lacks RealPage's revenue management and market data depth.

Bottom line: property management companies in the tens-to-thousands of units pick AppFolio for its unified AI platform; large institutional multifamily operators who live and die by occupancy and rent optimization pick RealPage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AppFolio or RealPage better for a mid-sized property management company?

AppFolio targets exactly that range, roughly 50 to 14,000+ units, with a single unified platform. RealPage is built for mid-market and enterprise multifamily operators and is not suitable for smaller portfolios. Most mid-sized third-party management firms will find AppFolio the more natural fit.

How much do AppFolio and RealPage cost?

Neither publishes pricing; both are quote-based with no free trial. AppFolio is positioned as premium pricing for its AI capabilities, and RealPage as enterprise pricing. Expect sales-led purchasing processes with both, and get quotes scoped to your unit count and modules.

Does AppFolio have revenue management like RealPage?

No. RealPage's AI-driven revenue management, which optimizes pricing against supply and demand, is its signature capability, alongside market analytics and the DemandX forecasting platform. AppFolio's AI focuses on automating operational busywork and performance insights rather than algorithmic rent pricing. Note that RealPage's pricing algorithms have drawn DOJ antitrust scrutiny.

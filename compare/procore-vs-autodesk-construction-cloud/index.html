Our Verdict

Procore and Autodesk Construction Cloud are the two platforms most general contractors and owners end up comparing. Both cover document control, RFIs and submittals, quality and safety, cost management, bidding and field apps. The difference is where each started. Procore was built as a project management system for contractors and reports more than 3 million individual users. Autodesk assembled its platform around design and model data, and is now presenting the products under its Forma brand, including Forma Build and Forma Takeoff.

Procore wins on field and financial workflows and on its commercial model. Pricing is based on the products you choose and your annual construction volume, and every plan includes unlimited users, unlimited storage and round-the-clock support. That makes it easy to invite subcontractors, architects and owner representatives without counting seats. Its cost management and its large marketplace of accounting and ERP integrations are the usual reasons contractors pick it. The caveats: there are no published prices, the volume-based fee can feel steep as a firm grows, and its model coordination tools are less deep than Autodesk's.

Autodesk Construction Cloud wins when design data drives the job. Native connections to Revit, AutoCAD and Navisworks, model coordination with clash detection, and model-based takeoff and estimating mean less translation between design and construction teams. Autodesk describes its pricing as flexible across user, project and account-based options, and cites more than 400 integrations. The caveats: pricing is also by quote, the product names are changing as the Forma brand rolls out, and firms that do not use BIM get less from its main advantage.

For commercial real estate owners and developers, the question is often which platform their contractors already run. Owners who mandate one system across projects tend to favour Procore for its unlimited-user model, while design-build firms and those with in-house VDC teams lean toward Autodesk.

Choose Procore if you are a general contractor or owner who wants strong project financials and easy collaboration with every party on the job. Choose Autodesk Construction Cloud if your teams already work in Autodesk design tools and model coordination is central to how you build.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Procore priced compared with Autodesk Construction Cloud?

Neither publishes prices. Procore charges an annual fee by product based on your annual construction volume, with unlimited users. Autodesk offers user, project and account-based options by quote.

Which is better for BIM and model coordination?

Autodesk Construction Cloud. It connects directly to Revit, AutoCAD and Navisworks and includes model coordination and model-based takeoff. Procore supports models and integrates with design tools, but that is not its core strength.

Is Autodesk Construction Cloud being renamed?

Autodesk is presenting its construction products under the Forma brand, with names such as Forma Build and Forma Takeoff. The underlying capabilities remain, but expect to see both names during the transition.

Does Procore limit the number of users?

No. Procore states that all plans include unlimited users, so collaborators such as subcontractors and consultants can be added without per-seat fees.

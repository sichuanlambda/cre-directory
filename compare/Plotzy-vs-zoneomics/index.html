Our Verdict

Plotzy and Zoneomics both digitize zoning, but they sell different jobs. Zoneomics is a zoning data company: it aggregates codes from thousands of cities into the largest nationwide zoning dataset, sells reports from $25 and certified zoning letters, offers a free basic search, and exposes the data through an API. Plotzy is a parcel research platform: 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties, each carrying a two-level zoning designation, with filters for permitted use, lot size, owner type and sale date, plus owner contact lookup, folders and CSV export. Plotzy publishes flat monthly pricing of $180 or $300 with a 7-day trial.

Zoneomics wins when the question is what exactly this code allows at this address. Its city-level depth, report format and certified letters are what lenders, attorneys and closing checklists expect, and the API suits teams that want zoning inside their own systems. Coverage is the caveat on both sides, and Zoneomics remains the reference point that any parcel platform's sub-level zoning is measured against.

Plotzy wins when zoning is a filter, not the deliverable. Find every parcel zoned for a use inside a corridor or county, screen by lot size and owner type, then pull contacts and export the list; Zoneomics does none of the list building or outreach. For a team running searches daily, a flat subscription is cheaper than per-report spend, while an occasional single-report buyer pays less on Zoneomics. Plotzy is still labeled beta and its sub-level zoning depth varies by municipality.

The rule: source with Plotzy, confirm with Zoneomics. Many land teams keep both, using Plotzy to build the list and a Zoneomics report or letter on the one parcel that goes to contract.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Plotzy provide certified zoning letters?

No. Plotzy attaches two-level zoning to every parcel and answers code questions through its Zoning AI chat, but certified zoning verification letters remain a Zoneomics product.

Which is cheaper, Plotzy or Zoneomics?

For occasional lookups Zoneomics is cheaper: basic search is free and reports start at $25. For daily research Plotzy's flat $180 or $300 a month usually costs less than accumulating per-report fees.

Can Plotzy replace Zoneomics for site selection?

For finding and screening parcels by zoning, yes; Plotzy filters 150M+ parcels by permitted use and adds owner contacts and exports. For formal zoning confirmation on a parcel under contract, most teams still order a Zoneomics report or letter.

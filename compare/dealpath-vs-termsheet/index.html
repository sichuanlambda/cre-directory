Our Verdict

Dealpath and TermSheet both replace the spreadsheet-and-email approach to tracking real estate deals, and both now lead with AI that extracts data from offering memoranda. They differ in scope and in who stands behind them. Dealpath is an independent, real-estate-only vendor that reports more than 300 firms as customers. TermSheet was acquired by Intapp and is now sold as Intapp Properties, part of a larger software company serving professional and financial services firms.

Dealpath wins for investment teams whose main problem is the pipeline. It covers sourcing, screening, underwriting, due diligence workflows and investment committee approvals across acquisitions, development, debt and dispositions, with reporting on top. Dealpath Connect adds a private channel through which brokers send listings directly into the pipeline, and its comps tracking builds a proprietary market database as deals are logged. The caveats: pricing is quote-based, it is designed for teams rather than solo investors, and it does not try to be a construction cost tracker or a portfolio accounting consolidation tool.

TermSheet wins for firms that want one system from site selection through project delivery and into the hold period. Intapp Properties combines deal evaluation with GIS mapping across more than 152 million parcels, two-way Excel syncing for pro formas, schedule and line-item cost tracking with a Procore integration, and portfolio intelligence that consolidates actuals from Yardi, MRI and RealPage. Its target list includes developers, retailers rolling out stores and corporate real estate teams as well as private equity real estate. The caveats: pricing is not published, the TermSheet name is being retired in favor of Intapp branding, and a broader platform usually means a longer rollout.

Choose Dealpath if you are an institutional investor, lender or asset manager that wants the deepest pipeline, approval and broker-connectivity tooling from a vendor focused only on that. Choose TermSheet (Intapp Properties) if you are a developer, operating company or multi-site occupier that needs deal tracking, project execution and portfolio reporting in one place, or if your firm already uses Intapp products.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TermSheet now Intapp Properties?

Yes. Intapp acquired TermSheet and sells the product as Intapp Properties. The TermSheet name still appears in places such as the login page, but new marketing uses the Intapp brand.

Do Dealpath or TermSheet publish pricing?

No. Both are sold by demo and quote, with pricing dependent on team size and modules.

Which is better for real estate developers?

TermSheet (Intapp Properties) has more development-specific tooling, including schedule automation, line-item cost tracking and a Procore integration. Dealpath supports development pipelines too, but its center of gravity is deal sourcing, underwriting and approvals.

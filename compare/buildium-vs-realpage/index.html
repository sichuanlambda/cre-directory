Our Verdict

This is the widest gap of any pairing here; these products serve almost entirely different buyers. Buildium is residential property management software with published pricing from $62/month to $400/month, a free trial, and an all-in-one toolkit for landlords, property managers, and community association managers running anywhere from a few doors to 15,000+ units. RealPage is an enterprise multifamily platform, quote-based and sales-led, offering OneSite property management, Knock CRM, AI-driven revenue management, DemandX demand forecasting, and utility and spend management for some of the largest apartment operators in the world.

Buildium wins on everything a small or mid-sized operator cares about: transparent pricing, fast self-serve setup, community association support, and features like Lumina AI bill scanning without an enterprise contract. RealPage wins on everything an institutional multifamily operator cares about: revenue optimization, market benchmarking data, fraud detection, and a marketing stack. RealPage is explicitly not suitable for small landlords, carries a steep learning curve, and its revenue management algorithms have drawn DOJ antitrust scrutiny. Buildium, for its part, cannot offer revenue management or enterprise analytics.

Bottom line: landlords, small-to-mid property managers, and HOA managers pick Buildium without hesitation; institutional multifamily portfolios optimizing thousands of units pick RealPage. Few buyers should genuinely be torn.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Buildium cheaper than RealPage?

Almost certainly, for the buyers who could use either. Buildium publishes plans at $62, $192, and $400 per month with a free trial. RealPage is quote-based enterprise pricing with no published numbers and no trial, and it is explicitly not aimed at small landlords or single properties.

Can Buildium do revenue management like RealPage?

No. RealPage's AI-driven revenue management and market analytics are enterprise multifamily tools with no equivalent in Buildium. Buildium's automation focuses on operations: rent collection, late fees, communications, bill scanning, and workflow rules. If algorithmic rent optimization is the goal, RealPage is the only option of the two.

When should a growing property manager move from Buildium to RealPage?

Buildium supports portfolios up to 15,000+ units, so unit count alone rarely forces a move. The switch makes sense when you become an institutional multifamily operator needing revenue management, market benchmarking, and demand forecasting, and can absorb enterprise pricing and a complex implementation.

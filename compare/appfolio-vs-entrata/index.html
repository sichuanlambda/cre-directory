Our Verdict

AppFolio and Entrata are both single-platform property management systems with heavy AI investment, but they grew up serving different customers. AppFolio built its base among third-party managers running mixed portfolios of single-family homes, small multifamily, community associations and some commercial, and now sells Core, Plus and Max plans that stretch from 50 units into the enterprise. Entrata built its base among large apartment and student housing operators, and positions itself as an operating system for multifamily, student, affordable, military, commercial and manufactured housing.

Entrata wins for large, site-staffed communities. Its platform bundles accounting, leasing, marketing websites, resident portals, payments and utilities, and its Entrata Layered Intelligence (ELI) agents cover leasing, maintenance, payments and renewals. Student housing features such as by-the-bed leasing are a long-standing strength. The caveats: pricing is entirely quote-based, the platform takes time to learn, and it is more than a manager with a few hundred scattered units needs.

AppFolio wins for fee managers and mid-sized operators. Owner statements, owner portals, maintenance coordination across scattered properties and its Realm-X automation are designed for companies managing on behalf of many owners. It is generally regarded as quicker to adopt. The caveats: prices are not published, minimum spend and unit counts apply, and operators with large lease-up communities, student housing or centralized leasing teams may find Entrata goes deeper.

Neither vendor publishes pricing, so the real comparison happens in the quotes. Ask each for the total cost including payments, screening, websites and AI modules, because both sell meaningful functionality as add-ons.

Choose AppFolio if you are a third-party manager with a varied residential portfolio and want fast adoption with strong owner reporting. Choose Entrata if you own or operate large multifamily or student communities and want leasing, marketing, resident experience and accounting in one enterprise stack. Portfolios in the low thousands of units that sit between those descriptions should run both through a structured demo with their own leasing and accounting leads in the room.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for student housing, AppFolio or Entrata?

Entrata is the more common choice for purpose-built student housing and lists student as a core segment alongside multifamily. AppFolio supports student housing too, but its strength is third-party residential management.

Do AppFolio or Entrata publish pricing?

No. AppFolio lists its Core, Plus and Max plans without prices and notes minimum spend and unit requirements. Entrata sells by demo and quote. Budget for add-on modules with either vendor.

Do both platforms include AI tools?

Yes. AppFolio builds AI into its platform through Realm-X for workflows, leasing and communications. Entrata offers Entrata Layered Intelligence (ELI), with agents for leasing, maintenance, payments and renewals. In both cases, confirm which AI features are included in your quote.

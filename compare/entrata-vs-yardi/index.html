Our Verdict

Entrata and Yardi both aim to run an entire property operation from one system, but Yardi spans far more of the real estate industry. Yardi's Voyager platform covers multifamily, commercial, affordable, senior living, coworking, and investment management, with clients in over 80 countries and a choice of cloud or on-premise deployment. Yardi Breeze serves smaller operators at a published price from $1 per unit per month, or $2 for Breeze Premier. Entrata is a multifamily-first operating system serving student, affordable, military, commercial, and manufactured housing as well, with one login across accounting, leasing, marketing, resident services, and maintenance, and its ELI AI layer throughout.

Yardi wins on breadth and flexibility. If the portfolio includes office, retail, or industrial assets, or needs fund accounting and an investor portal in the same stack, Entrata's commercial features are not as mature. Yardi's own ecosystem, including RentCafe, COMMERCIALCafe, and Yardi Matrix market data, extends the core, and Breeze gives small operators a genuine entry point. The costs are well documented: Voyager has a steep learning curve, implementations take months, some modules look dated, and migrating away later is hard.

Entrata wins on cohesion for multifamily operators. Everything is native to one platform with no third-party dependencies, the AI capabilities are integrated rather than added on, and it is proven at scale with over 32,000 properties. The trade-offs mirror Yardi's in places: quote-based pricing with no public tiers, a steep learning curve, and a closed ecosystem with fewer third-party integrations than some competitors.

Pick Yardi if the portfolio is mixed, international, or commercial-heavy, if on-premise deployment matters, or if you are small enough for Breeze. Pick Entrata if you run residential rental housing at scale and want a single modern platform without assembling modules. For multifamily-only operators the decision usually comes down to implementation experience and total cost from the sales process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Entrata or Yardi better for commercial properties?

Yardi. Yardi Voyager has dedicated commercial leasing tools and covers office, retail, and industrial portfolios. Entrata supports commercial but its features there are less mature than its multifamily core.

Does Yardi publish pricing?

Only for its small-operator products. Yardi Breeze starts at $1 per unit per month and Breeze Premier at $2 per unit per month. Yardi Voyager and Entrata are both quote-based.

Can Yardi be installed on-premise?

Yes. Yardi offers cloud, on-premise, and mobile deployment. Entrata is cloud-only.

Which is harder to implement, Entrata or Yardi?

Yardi Voyager implementations are known for taking months and requiring significant configuration. Entrata also has a learning curve, but it is delivered as a single cloud platform rather than a set of modules.

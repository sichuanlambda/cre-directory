Our Verdict

Buildout and SharpLaunch overlap on property marketing, which is where most buyers compare them, but they are different kinds of product. Buildout, used by more than 50,000 brokers, is a connected brokerage platform spanning prospecting, CRM (including Rethink by Buildout), marketing materials and deal management through to commissions. SharpLaunch is a marketing platform for commercial property: property websites, a searchable listings engine for your own site, lead management, document portals, flyers, interactive maps and analytics. It reports more than 400 CRE teams as customers.

Buildout wins for brokerages that want one system. A listing entered once feeds brochures, offering memoranda, websites and syndication, and the same data flows into the CRM and back office. Its newer AI features write property descriptions, structure data and track follow-ups. For a multi-office brokerage standardizing on one login, that integration is the main attraction. The caveats: every product is priced by quote, the breadth takes time to roll out, and the platform is designed around brokers rather than owners.

SharpLaunch wins for teams whose priority is how listings and portfolios look online. It serves building owners and leasing teams as well as full-service, leasing and investment sales brokers, which matters for landlords marketing their own space. Its document portal controls access to due diligence files and tracks who viewed what. SharpLaunch also publishes a starting price, with plans from $399 a month on annual billing. The caveats: it is not a CRM or commission system, so brokers will still need those elsewhere, the full price list is only shared on a call, and it is a much smaller company than Buildout.

The two can also be used together. Some firms keep a CRM and back office in one system and run their public-facing property sites in another.

Choose Buildout if you are a brokerage that wants prospecting, CRM, marketing and deal management connected, and you are willing to commit to a platform. Choose SharpLaunch if you are an owner, a leasing team or a broker who mainly needs polished property websites, listing search, controlled document sharing and marketing analytics, with a known entry price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Buildout and SharpLaunch cost?

SharpLaunch publishes plans from $399 a month on annual billing, with details quoted on a call. Buildout does not publish prices: its products are sold modularly by demo and quote.

Is SharpLaunch a CRM?

No. SharpLaunch handles property websites, listings search, lead capture, document portals and marketing analytics. Buildout includes CRM products alongside its marketing tools.

Which is better for property owners and landlords?

SharpLaunch explicitly serves building owners and leasing teams as well as brokers. Buildout is built around brokerage workflows, from prospecting through to commissions.

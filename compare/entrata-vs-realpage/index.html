Our Verdict

Entrata and RealPage are both enterprise property management platforms rooted in multifamily, but they differ in how they are assembled. Entrata is a single-vendor operating system: accounting, leasing, marketing, resident services, maintenance, and utilities run under one login, with its ELI layer adding AI across the platform. It reports over 32,000 properties and 3.2 million units across multifamily, student, affordable, military, commercial, and manufactured housing. RealPage is an integrated suite plus a data business. Its leasing, spend, utility, and resident tools sit alongside revenue management, DemandX demand forecasting, and the Lumina AI workforce, and it is used by some of the largest multifamily operators in the world.

Entrata wins on platform coherence and property-type reach. Operators tired of stitching together point solutions get one system without third-party dependencies, and the student, affordable, and military housing coverage is broader than RealPage's residential focus. The trade-offs: pricing is quote-based, the platform has a steep learning curve, commercial features are less mature than the multifamily core, and the closed ecosystem means fewer third-party integrations.

RealPage wins on analytics and revenue optimization. Its revenue management is the most established in the industry, its market data footprint supports benchmarking that Entrata cannot match, and Lumina automates tasks at scale. The trade-offs: enterprise-only pricing that excludes small landlords, a complex platform, a strictly multifamily orientation, and ongoing antitrust scrutiny from the Department of Justice around its revenue management pricing algorithms. That legal overhang is a real consideration for any operator making a long-term commitment.

Pick Entrata if you want one unified stack, especially with student, affordable, or military assets in the portfolio. Pick RealPage if revenue management and market analytics drive your operating model and you are comfortable with the regulatory questions around it. Both require sales conversations to establish pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does RealPage work for commercial properties?

RealPage is focused on multifamily rental housing and has limited commercial features. Entrata lists commercial among its supported types, though its commercial tools are less mature than its multifamily core.

Why is RealPage facing antitrust scrutiny?

The US Department of Justice has scrutinized RealPage's revenue management software over concerns that its pricing algorithms could coordinate rents across competing landlords. Operators should factor this into long-term platform decisions.

Is Entrata cheaper than RealPage?

Neither vendor publishes pricing. Both are quote-based and aimed at mid-market and enterprise operators, so cost depends on portfolio size and modules.

Can Entrata and RealPage work together?

Entrata lists RealPage among its analytics integrations, so some operators use RealPage data products alongside Entrata's operating platform. Entrata's ecosystem is otherwise relatively closed to third parties.

Our Verdict

This comparison is really about how big and complicated your operation is. Buildium is purpose-built residential property management software with transparent pricing: published plans at $62, $192, and $400 per month, a free trial, and a deep feature set covering leasing, screening, accounting, maintenance, and community associations for portfolios from a handful of doors up to 15,000+ units. Yardi is a full real estate platform spanning every property type, with Breeze (from $1/unit/month) at the low end and enterprise-grade Voyager, investment management, and energy suites at the top.

Buildium wins on simplicity and transparency. You can see the price, start a free trial, and be running without an implementation project; its Lumina AI handles bill scanning and communications, and it covers niches like community associations and student housing well. Its limits: it is primarily residential with thin commercial capability, and API access is locked to the $400/month Premium tier. Yardi wins the moment you need commercial management, fund accounting, investor portals, or a platform that scales into institutional territory, though Voyager brings a steep learning curve and lengthy implementations.

Bottom line: residential managers and landlords who want transparent pricing and fast setup pick Buildium; mixed or institutional portfolios that will grow into commercial and investment management pick Yardi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Buildium cheaper than Yardi?

Buildium is the more transparent of the two, with published plans at $62, $192, and $400 per month and a free trial. Yardi Breeze starts at $1/unit/month, which can be cheaper for small unit counts, while Yardi Voyager is quote-based enterprise pricing. Actual cost depends on your portfolio size and the features you need.

Can Buildium handle commercial properties?

Only in a limited way. Buildium supports mixed portfolios and lists commercial among its property types, but it is purpose-built for residential management and its commercial features are thin. Yardi has dedicated commercial management capability and is the better choice if commercial assets are a meaningful part of your business.

Which is better for a small landlord, Buildium or Yardi?

Both are viable. Buildium's Essential plan at $62/month with a free trial is easy to evaluate, while Yardi Breeze's $1/unit/month can be very cheap for small portfolios. Buildium offers the deeper residential feature set; Breeze offers simplicity and a growth path into the larger Yardi ecosystem.

Our Verdict

Both are enterprise platforms with quote-based pricing and real learning curves, so this decision comes down to what you own. RealPage is a multifamily specialist: OneSite property management, Knock CRM, G5 advertising, DemandX forecasting, and the industry's best-known AI revenue management, all built for apartment operators at mid-market and enterprise scale. MRI Software is a generalist with deep commercial roots: 50+ years in market, 50,000+ clients, commercial lease administration, AI lease abstraction, investment and fund management, and the Angus facilities platform, delivered on an open architecture with 200+ integration partners.

RealPage wins for multifamily operators, full stop. Its revenue optimization, market data footprint, and Lumina AI workforce target apartment economics in a way MRI does not, though buyers should weigh the DOJ antitrust scrutiny around its pricing algorithms. MRI wins for commercial, mixed-use, and investment-driven organizations: lease intelligence, investor reporting with waterfall modeling, and facilities management are core strengths, and its open platform plays well with tools like VTS and ARGUS. MRI also covers residential, making it the safer pick for genuinely mixed portfolios, though product sprawl from acquisitions can mean uneven user experiences.

Bottom line: enterprise multifamily operators pick RealPage; commercial, mixed-use, and investment management organizations pick MRI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does RealPage work for commercial real estate?

Not really. RealPage is focused on multifamily and rental housing, with limited commercial CRE features. MRI offers full-stack commercial management including lease administration, AI lease abstraction, and facilities management, and it handles residential too, making it the choice for commercial or mixed portfolios.

How do RealPage and MRI compare on price?

Both are quote-based enterprise platforms with no published pricing, free trials, or free tiers, so you will need scoped quotes from each. Neither is accessible to small firms; both target mid-market and enterprise organizations.

Which platform integrates better with other software?

MRI is explicitly built as an open platform with 200+ integration partners, including VTS, ARGUS, and DocuSign, so best-of-breed stacks are a design goal. RealPage's strength is the breadth of its own ecosystem (Knock, G5, DemandX) rather than openness, though it does integrate with partners like Plaid and even Yardi.

Our Verdict

Buildium and Rent Manager are both long-established systems for small and mid-sized property managers, and they tend to appear on the same shortlists. Buildium, a RealPage company, is a cloud product purpose-built for residential managers and community associations, with a clean interface and three published plans. Rent Manager, from London Computer Systems in Cincinnati, is a highly configurable platform that handles residential, commercial, HOA, self-storage and mixed portfolios, and is known for accounting depth and customization.

Buildium wins on ease and price clarity. Its Essential, Growth and Premium plans start at $62, $192 and $400 a month, with a 14-day trial that needs no credit card. Most teams can run it without a dedicated administrator. The caveats: it is primarily residential, the open API sits on the Premium plan, live phone support starts at Growth, and per-transaction fees for eSignatures and bank transfers are higher on the lower plans.

Rent Manager wins on flexibility. It runs cash and accrual books at the same time, ships with more than 450 reports, lets you add user-defined fields and custom workflows, and offers an open API and a large integration network. Commercial features such as CAM handling and its support for storage and manufactured housing make it a better fit for mixed portfolios. The caveats: pricing is by quote across its Basic, Plus and Premium bundles, setup takes longer because there is more to configure, and the interface feels more traditional than newer cloud products.

A useful rule of thumb is how much your processes deviate from the norm. If you manage standard residential leases and associations and want software that works out of the box, Buildium will be faster to value. If you have unusual fee structures, several property types, or an accounting team that wants control over the general ledger and reporting, Rent Manager rewards the extra setup.

Choose Buildium if you are a residential or association manager who values a short learning curve and published prices. Choose Rent Manager if you manage commercial, storage or mixed portfolios, or if customization and accounting control matter more than simplicity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Buildium and Rent Manager cost?

Buildium publishes three plans starting at $62 (Essential), $192 (Growth) and $400 (Premium) per month, with the price rising by unit count. Rent Manager does not publish prices: its Basic, Plus and Premium bundles are sold by quote.

Which is better for commercial property management?

Rent Manager. It supports commercial, self-storage and mixed portfolios alongside residential, while Buildium is designed for residential rentals and community associations.

Is Rent Manager harder to learn than Buildium?

Generally yes. Rent Manager offers far more configuration, including custom fields, reports and workflows, so implementation and training take longer. Buildium trades some of that flexibility for a simpler setup.

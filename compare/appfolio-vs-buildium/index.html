Our Verdict

AppFolio and Buildium are the two most common shortlist rivals for residential property managers, and the split is fairly clean. Buildium is the accessible option: published pricing from $62/month up to $400/month for Premium, a free trial, and an all-in-one feature set spanning leasing, screening, accounting, maintenance, community associations, and student housing, serving everyone from individual landlords to 15,000+ unit portfolios. AppFolio positions itself upmarket as an AI-native 'Performance Platform' for managers from roughly 50 units to 14,000+, with agentic AI and its Realm-X Flows engine automating work across leasing, accounting, and maintenance.

Buildium wins on transparency and low-end accessibility: you know the price, you can trial it, and small landlords are squarely in its target market. Its AI (Lumina) is solid for bill scanning and communications, though API access requires the Premium tier. AppFolio wins on automation ambition and unified workflow for growing professional operations, and it adds investment management tools Buildium lacks. The trade-offs are quote-based premium pricing, no published numbers, and a platform that may be more than small operators need.

Bottom line: individual landlords and small-to-mid residential managers pick Buildium; professional firms at 50+ units betting on AI-driven operations and investor reporting pick AppFolio.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AppFolio more expensive than Buildium?

AppFolio does not publish pricing; it is quote-based and generally positioned as premium for its AI capabilities. Buildium publishes plans at $62, $192, and $400 per month with a free trial. For small portfolios Buildium is the more predictable spend; at larger scale you will need an AppFolio quote to compare.

Can I use AppFolio or Buildium as a small landlord with a few units?

Buildium is the better fit; it explicitly serves solo landlords and its Essential plan starts at $62/month with a free trial. AppFolio targets professional managers starting around 50 units, so very small portfolios are outside its sweet spot.

Which has better AI features, AppFolio or Buildium?

Both invest heavily in AI. AppFolio's differentiator is agentic AI built into the platform core with the Realm-X Flows automation engine, aimed at automating whole workflows. Buildium's Lumina AI covers communications, writing assistance, and bill scanning. AppFolio's automation is more ambitious; Buildium's is more accessible, though its full AI suite sits in higher tiers.

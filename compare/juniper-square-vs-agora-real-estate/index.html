Our Verdict

Juniper Square and Agora both combine investor management software with back-office services, which sets them apart from portal-only tools. Juniper Square is the larger and more institutional of the two, reporting more than 2,300 GPs and $1 trillion in investor equity, across real estate, private equity, venture capital, fund of funds and private credit. Agora is focused on real estate firms and syndicators, and reports more than 1,400 customers worldwide.

Agora wins on transparency and on fit for mid-sized real estate sponsors. Its Essential plan is published as starting from $749 a month and includes the investor portal, CRM, fundraising suite, document and K-1 management, cap table, waterfall automation and integrated payments. The Pro plan adds an institutional-grade data room, open-end fund accounting, free embedded ACH and single sign-on, and Enterprise adds custom integrations and data warehouse exports. Tax operations and bookkeeping are sold as separate services, and cross-border payments are supported, which is useful for sponsors with overseas investors. The caveats: Pro and Enterprise prices are not published, services are quoted separately, and the platform is real estate specific.

Juniper Square wins when investors are institutions and reporting requirements are heavy. Its fund administration practice, AML and KYC, due diligence questionnaire tooling, data rooms and investor CRM with institutional data are built for managers answering to pension funds and endowments. The caveats: there is no published pricing, onboarding is a substantial project, and a sponsor with a handful of syndications may be paying for scale it does not use.

Both vendors will happily run a migration from spreadsheets or a competitor, so the decision usually turns on investor profile. Ask each how they would handle your most complicated structure, for example a fund with side letters or a deal with a multi-tier waterfall.

Choose Agora if you are a real estate sponsor who wants a known starting price, a modern portal, and the option to add tax and bookkeeping services later. Choose Juniper Square if you raise from institutional LPs, need full fund administration, or manage strategies beyond real estate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Agora cost compared with Juniper Square?

Agora publishes a starting price of $749 a month for its Essential plan, with Pro and Enterprise quoted. Juniper Square does not publish pricing at all, so you will need a quote based on your funds, investors and services.

Does Agora handle K-1s and tax work?

Agora's platform includes document and K-1 management for distributing forms to investors. Preparing them is a separate matter: Agora sells tax operations and bookkeeping as additional services priced after a consultation.

Which is better for a first-time syndicator?

Agora is the more natural fit, with a published entry price and a product designed around real estate sponsors. Juniper Square is built for established managers and is likely to be more platform than a first deal requires.

Our Verdict

EliseAI and Funnel are two of the names multifamily operators shortlist when they want AI to answer prospects, book tours and handle resident requests around the clock. The useful distinction is that EliseAI starts from the AI agent and adds a CRM, while Funnel starts from the CRM and adds AI. They are not always either-or: Funnel appears on EliseAI's own list of integrations.

EliseAI handles conversations by text, email, chat and voice, with voice in seven languages and written replies in 51. On the leasing side it manages prospects and AI-guided tours; on the resident side it covers move-ins, maintenance requests, renewals and delinquency, plus lease audits and fee transparency. It ships with EliseCRM but is designed to sit on top of other systems, and its integrations page names Rent Manager, ResMan, Funnel, Bluemoon and Zillow Rentals among others. It serves conventional multifamily, student, affordable and single-family rental operators, says it is used by more than 900 property management companies, and names Equity Residential and LeFrak among them. The caveats: pricing is not published, the sales motion is enterprise, and it also sells into healthcare, so not every roadmap item is housing.

Funnel is a renter-centric CRM for multifamily, organized around the renter rather than each property's pipeline. Its AI, branded Fenix, engages prospects by email, SMS, chat and voice and books tours, and on the resident side handles requests, rent reminders and renewals and turns maintenance calls into work orders. It is sold as an Agent Platform (CRM, communications and Fenix) with an optional Renter Platform for applications, screening, fraud detection, payments and renewals. It works on top of Yardi, RealPage, Entrata and ResMan, and Funnel says it powers 2 million units for operators including Camden, Essex, MAA and UDR. The caveats: it is multifamily only, pricing is not published, and you still need a property management system underneath.

Choose EliseAI if you want an AI agent that covers leasing and resident operations across several housing types and slots into the systems you already run. Choose Funnel if you want to replace your leasing CRM and centralize your front office around one renter record, with AI built into that platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can EliseAI and Funnel be used together?

Yes. Funnel is listed among the integrations on EliseAI's site, so some operators run EliseAI's AI agent alongside Funnel's CRM.

Do EliseAI or Funnel publish pricing?

No. Both are sold through demos and quotes.

Which property types do they support?

EliseAI serves conventional multifamily, student housing, affordable housing and single-family rentals. Funnel focuses on multifamily.

Which PMS platforms do they work with?

Funnel names Yardi, RealPage, Entrata and ResMan. EliseAI's integrations page names partners such as Rent Manager, ResMan, Funnel and Bluemoon.

Our Verdict

AirDNA and Mashvisor both sell rental market data to investors, but they answer different questions. AirDNA is a short-term rental intelligence platform built on Airbnb and Vrbo listing data worldwide, with tools for market research, revenue estimation, comp sets, and host benchmarking. Mashvisor is a US-focused investment analytics platform that scores properties for both short-term and long-term rental strategies and layers in a property search, market finder, and data API.

AirDNA wins on depth and coverage for anyone whose business is short-term rentals. Rentalizer produces address-level revenue estimates, Market Insights carries historical and forward-looking occupancy, rate, and revenue data, and the Host tier adds benchmarking and competitor rate calendars. There is a free tier for browsing. Paid plans are listed at $34 per month for Research and $50 per month for Host when billed annually; month-to-month prices are much higher, at $125 and $150. The caveats are that an annual commitment is effectively required for sensible pricing, the Property Manager tier is quote-based, and data thins out in small markets.

Mashvisor wins for investors deciding what to buy and how to operate it. Its analysis shows cap rate, cash-on-cash return, and occupancy for the same property under both rental strategies, and the nationwide property search and heatmaps help narrow markets and neighborhoods. Mashvisor's pricing page lists Lite at $49.99 per month, Standard at $99.99 monthly or $74.99 billed annually, and Professional at $119.99 monthly or $99.99 annually, with API access reserved for an Enterprise plan on quote. The trade-offs are US-only coverage, rental estimates that get less reliable in rural markets, and a monthly entry price above AirDNA's annual rate.

Pick AirDNA if you already run or plan to run short-term rentals and need market-grade STR data. Pick Mashvisor if you are still choosing between STR and long-term rental strategies and want deal-level return analysis on listed properties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AirDNA or Mashvisor cheaper?

AirDNA's Research plan is $34 per month billed annually, or $125 month to month. Mashvisor's Lite plan is $49.99 per month. AirDNA is cheaper on an annual commitment, while Mashvisor's monthly pricing is lower if you do not want to prepay for a year.

Does AirDNA cover long-term rentals?

No. AirDNA focuses on short-term and vacation rental data from Airbnb and Vrbo. Mashvisor analyzes both short-term and long-term rental performance for the same property.

Does Mashvisor have an API?

Yes. Mashvisor offers a real estate data API, but access is bundled into its Enterprise plan, which is priced on request.

Do AirDNA and Mashvisor cover markets outside the US?

AirDNA covers short-term rental listings worldwide. Mashvisor focuses on the United States.

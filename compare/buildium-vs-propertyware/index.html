Our Verdict

Buildium and Propertyware are sister products inside RealPage, which makes the choice mostly about portfolio type rather than vendor. Buildium serves residential managers and community associations of almost any size, starting with small portfolios. Propertyware is built specifically for single-family rental managers running hundreds or thousands of scattered homes, often across several markets, and leans into customization and an open API.

Pricing shows who each is for. Buildium publishes Essential, Growth and Premium plans starting at $62, $192 and $400 a month, with a 14-day free trial. Propertyware publishes per-unit pricing of $1, $1.50 and $2 per unit per month for Basic, Plus and Premium, but with monthly minimums of $250, $350 and $450 and an implementation fee of twice the monthly subscription. A manager with 40 doors pays far less on Buildium. A manager with 600 homes should price both.

Buildium wins for smaller and more varied residential businesses. It is quicker to set up, handles associations as well as rentals, and includes resident services and AI-assisted communications on higher plans. The caveats: the open API is limited to Premium, eSignature and bank transfer fees are higher on Essential, and it offers less scope for custom fields and portfolio-level structures than Propertyware.

Propertyware wins for single-family specialists at scale. Custom fields, dashboards and reports, two-way API access, multi-location management and add-ons such as maintenance and leasing contact centers are designed around the operational pattern of dispersed homes and many owners. The caveats: the minimums and implementation fee make it poor value for small portfolios, the interface is older in feel, and it is not aimed at multifamily communities or associations.

Choose Buildium if you manage under a few hundred units, run associations, or want to be live quickly at a predictable entry price. Choose Propertyware if single-family rentals are your core business, you have several hundred homes or more, and you want to shape the system around your own processes. Because both sit under RealPage, it is reasonable to ask the sales team which they would recommend for your unit count and why.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Buildium and Propertyware owned by the same company?

Yes. Both are RealPage products. Buildium targets residential and association managers broadly, while Propertyware focuses on single-family rental management companies.

What is the minimum cost of Propertyware?

Propertyware publishes monthly minimums of $250 for Basic, $350 for Plus and $450 for Premium, at $1, $1.50 and $2 per unit per month, plus an implementation fee of twice the monthly subscription price.

Which is better for a small landlord?

Buildium. Its Essential plan starts at $62 a month and has a free trial. Propertyware's monthly minimums mean it only makes financial sense once you manage a few hundred single-family homes.

Our Verdict

Buildout and RealNex are the two vendors that try to cover most of a commercial broker's day in one suite, so this is a true head-to-head. Buildout positions itself as an AI brokerage platform taking a team from contact to commission, with prospecting, CRM, marketing and deal management on one data layer, and counts more than 50,000 brokers as users. RealNex sells Navigator, an all-in bundle, or individual modules: a CRM, MarketEdge for financial analysis and presentations, Bridge for transaction management, a marketing module and MarketPlace for listings.

Buildout wins on marketing and on firm-wide adoption. Its document generation for brochures, flyers, offering memoranda and proposals is a long-standing strength, and listings flow through to websites and syndication. National and regional brokerages standardize on it, which helps with brand consistency and reporting across offices. The caveats: there is no published pricing, the lineup includes several CRM options that need explaining during a demo, and it has no built-in investment analysis tool comparable to MarketEdge.

RealNex wins on flexibility for individuals and small teams. It is priced per user, you can subscribe to a single module or to everything in Navigator, and billing is monthly or annual. MarketEdge produces investment and lease analyses that feed straight into presentations, which matters to brokers who underwrite for clients. MarketPlace has a free tier for listing and searching, with a paid Pro tier. The caveats: a one-time per-user implementation fee applies on most plans, the interface is often described as less modern than Buildout's, and its market share is smaller. RealNex shows rates per product on its pricing page, so check there for current figures.

Choose Buildout if you run a brokerage and want consistent, branded marketing with CRM and back office connected across the firm. Choose RealNex if you are an independent broker or small team that wants to buy only the modules you need, values built-in financial analysis, and prefers per-user pricing you can start month to month. In both cases, test the CRM with your own contact data before signing, since that is where daily use is won or lost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does RealNex include financial analysis?

Yes. MarketEdge is RealNex's investment and lease analysis and presentation tool, available on its own or within Navigator. Buildout concentrates on marketing documents, CRM and deal management and does not offer an equivalent analysis module.

How are Buildout and RealNex priced?

Buildout is modular and quote-based with no published prices. RealNex is priced per user by module or as the Navigator bundle, with monthly or annual billing and a one-time implementation fee on most plans.

Can a solo broker use either platform?

Yes, though RealNex is easier to buy as an individual because modules are sold separately per user. Buildout serves solo brokers as well, but its strongest fit is brokerages rolling it out across a team.

Does either include a listing marketplace?

RealNex operates MarketPlace, which has a free tier for listing and searching properties. Buildout publishes listings to brokerage websites and syndicates them to third-party listing sites.

Our Verdict

These two rarely serve the same buyer, so the choice usually makes itself. AppFolio is a cloud-only, AI-native platform for residential property managers, from roughly 50 units to 14,000+, with agentic AI, the Realm-X Flows workflow engine, and a unified interface covering property and investment management. MRI Software is an enterprise real estate platform with 50+ years of history and 50,000+ clients, built for commercial and mixed-use portfolios: commercial lease administration, AI lease abstraction, fund and investment management, and the Angus facilities platform, all on an open architecture with 200+ integration partners.

AppFolio wins for residential operators who want modern software that reduces busywork; its AI is native rather than retrofitted, and everything lives in one interface on any device. It has little to offer a commercial landlord, though. MRI wins for commercial and institutional buyers: lease intelligence, facilities management, and investor reporting at a depth AppFolio does not attempt, plus the freedom to plug in best-of-breed tools like VTS and ARGUS. The costs are complexity, quote-based enterprise pricing, lengthy implementations, and some inconsistency across acquired products.

Bottom line: residential property management firms pick AppFolio; commercial, mixed-use, and institutional portfolios pick MRI. If you genuinely straddle both, MRI covers residential too, but AppFolio cannot cover serious commercial.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AppFolio manage commercial real estate like MRI?

Not at MRI's level. AppFolio lists commercial among supported property types but is built for residential managers. MRI offers full-stack commercial management including lease administration, AI-powered lease abstraction, and facilities management via MRI Angus. Commercial-focused buyers should look to MRI.

How do AppFolio and MRI pricing compare?

Both are quote-based with no published prices, so you will need sales conversations either way. AppFolio is positioned as premium pricing for residential portfolios; MRI is enterprise pricing aimed at mid-market and large organizations. Neither offers a free trial or free tier.

Which is easier to adopt, AppFolio or MRI?

AppFolio, for its audience. It is a single cloud platform with a unified interface, though its AI-native approach still requires some change management. MRI is a broad enterprise platform with a steep learning curve, potentially lengthy implementations, and user experiences that vary across its acquired products.

Our Verdict

Yardi and RealPage are both enterprise heavyweights, but they are not really solving the same problem. Yardi is a general-purpose real estate platform: property management, accounting, and investment management across multifamily, commercial, affordable, senior living, and coworking, with Breeze (from $1/unit/month) giving smaller operators a way in. RealPage is a multifamily specialist: its OneSite platform, Knock CRM, DemandX demand forecasting, and industry-leading revenue management are all purpose-built for apartment operators, and it does not seriously compete outside rental housing.

RealPage wins on multifamily depth. Its AI-driven revenue management, market analytics footprint, and Lumina AI workforce give large apartment operators optimization tools Yardi does not match, and the two even integrate, so some operators run RealPage tools on top of Yardi accounting. The caveats: RealPage is enterprise-priced with no small-portfolio option, has a steep learning curve, and its revenue management pricing algorithms have drawn DOJ antitrust scrutiny, which buyers should factor into diligence. Yardi wins on breadth, accounting depth, investment management, and having options at every portfolio size.

Bottom line: large multifamily operators focused on revenue optimization and leasing performance pick RealPage; anyone with mixed property types, investment management needs, or a smaller portfolio picks Yardi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RealPage only for multifamily properties?

Effectively, yes. RealPage's entire suite, from OneSite to revenue management to DemandX, targets multifamily and rental housing, and it has limited commercial CRE features. Yardi covers multifamily plus commercial, affordable housing, senior living, and coworking, making it the clear choice for mixed portfolios.

Can small landlords use RealPage or Yardi?

RealPage is enterprise-priced and not suitable for small landlords or single properties. Yardi serves small operators through Yardi Breeze, which starts at a published $1/unit/month. If you have a small portfolio, Yardi Breeze is the realistic option between these two.

What is the concern with RealPage's revenue management?

RealPage's revenue management pricing algorithms have faced DOJ antitrust scrutiny. The product itself is considered industry-leading for optimizing multifamily rents, but buyers should weigh the legal and reputational risk as part of due diligence before adopting it.

Our Verdict

Plotzy and LightBox Vision, the platform formerly sold as LandVision, both put nationwide parcels, ownership and zoning on a map so you can find sites and the people who own them. LightBox Vision comes from LightBox, a large CRE data company, and ties parcels to its wider datasets through the LightBox ID, with report ordering inside the platform and annual contracts priced on quote. Plotzy is a younger, AI-first platform covering 150M+ parcels across 3,200+ counties with two-level zoning on every record, owner contact lookup, folders and CSV export, priced month to month at $180 or $300 with a 7-day trial and no credit card.

LightBox Vision wins for enterprise teams. If you already buy environmental, valuation or other LightBox products, want multi-seat administration, or need authoritative datasets and in-platform report ordering for due diligence, its breadth and ecosystem are the reason to accept the price and the procurement cycle. Its feature set is deep, and that depth is what large brokerages and lenders pay for.

Plotzy wins on speed and clarity. Pricing is published, there is no annual contract, and a broker or land team can be searching within minutes of starting the trial. Zoning is a first-class filter rather than a layer, so finding parcels by permitted use is a single query, and owner contacts and exports live in the same workflow. The trade-offs are honest: Plotzy is still labeled beta, sub-level zoning depth varies by municipality, and API access and custom integrations sit on the custom Scale plan.

Pick LightBox Vision if you are an enterprise buyer who values the ecosystem more than the price. Pick Plotzy if you are a broker, developer or land team that wants to find parcels and owners this week at a cost you can see.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LandVision still available?

LandVision is now sold as LightBox Vision. It remains a nationwide parcel mapping and property research platform from LightBox, priced on quote.

How much does Plotzy cost compared to LightBox Vision?

Plotzy publishes month-to-month pricing: $180 for Starter and $300 for Production, with a custom Scale plan. LightBox Vision does not publish pricing and is typically sold on annual terms.

Which has better zoning data?

Both include zoning. Plotzy attaches a two-level designation to every parcel and adds sub-level zoning filters and a Zoning AI chat; LightBox Vision offers zoning layers and report ordering backed by LightBox datasets. Coverage depth varies by municipality on both.
